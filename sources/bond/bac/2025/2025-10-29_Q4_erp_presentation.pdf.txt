 BRIEF HISTORY
• 2020 - Bond passage for Tech Modernization
• 2021 - Gartner assessed readiness and built recommendations for a cloud-based ERP

 CORE TRANSFORMATION PRINCIPLES
1.   Strong Data Strategy & Governance is the di erence between success and failure

2.   Change Management is a non-negotiable - involved early and often

3. “Jobs To Be Done”- people, process, and data required to be successful

4. Well documented Business Capabilities drive focus, order of operation, and degree of change

5.   What gets measured gets done: KPI’s that are committed, monitored, and celebrated

6. Everything is baselined - document and align on what is “better” and “best”

7.   Work organized into two week Program Sprints - goals, decisions, deliverables

8. Parkinson’s Law - constraints and standardization to build a durable infrastructure moving forward

9. This is someone else’s money - let’s use this fact to anchor responsible decisions
