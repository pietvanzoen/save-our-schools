                                             A. INTRODUCTION

In May 2025, the voters of Portland, OR approved a $1.83B bond program for Portland Public Schools
(PPS). The primary purpose of this Program Management Plan (PMP) is to provide an overview of the
PPS 2025 Bond Program and provide updates from the Program Manager (PgM), PROCEDEO, whom is
responsible for overseeing the day-to-day operation to all bond program projects.

This PMP will effectively outline the typical sequence of activities for the design and construction phases
in a project, provide updates to the schedule and budget models for all of the projects in this program,
and supporting information for the management tools that will be utilized.

As per Exhibit A – Statement of Services Article B.1 from the agreement between PPS and PgM, this PMP
was first submitted on March 9th, 2026 and is updated to PPS every six months (January and June).
