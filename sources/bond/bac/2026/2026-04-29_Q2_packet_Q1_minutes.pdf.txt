Risks and Mitigation Strategies (applicable to all modernizations):

Discussion:
   ●​ Potential risks to project schedules and budgets include delays in securing necessary
       permits, which could postpone the start of construction. To address this, PPS is
       collaborating with the City of Portland through a Major Project Group to collectively refine
       design details during the design phase. The City of Portland has agreed to a 16-week
       review and approval timeline.
   ●​ Another identified risk is the availability of mass timber. Mitigation for this involves
       diversifying the supply chain to ensure the District's simultaneous projects do not create
       internal competition for the same production resources.
