Programmatic Risks and Mitigation
Cost
Value Engineering (VE) - Consistent effort from design team at every submission to
reconcile costs and VE as needed.
Early work assignment - CMGCs releasing bid packages early to gain best pricing
ahead of escalating market pricing.
All budgets include an allowance for cost escalations to mitigate future unforeseen
cost increases.




                                                                                      30
Programmatic Risks and Mitigation
Schedule (Impacts)
Lookahead scheduling: Proactive identiﬁcation of potential risks in regular design
and construction meetings.
Recovery schedules: Working closely with CMGCs in proactive development of
recovery schedules when needed.
Proactive schedule review as needed to identify activity relationships that can achieve
potential time savings




                                                                                      31
Programmatic Risks and Mitigation
Design (Scope Creep)
Cost Reconciliation: Consistent effort from design team at every submission to
reconcile cost estimates and VE as needed to maintain budgets.
Constructability reviews during each design phase to ensure design is progressing in
alignment with budget constraints
Weigh the cost of must have vs. nice to have design elements




                                                                                       32
Programmatic Risks and Mitigation
Permitting (Delays)
Major Projects Group (MPG): Collaborative design review meeting to expedite review
and permitting process.
Increased communication: Multiple points of contacts from professional partners
reaching out to permitting agency for status updates.


Site Security (Break-ins/theft prior to turnover to GC)
Enhanced security measures: Patrols, continuity in district property insurance prior
to GC turnover.
Early release of the GC site security plan and measures prior to full site turn over

Deﬁnitions
● Capital Improvements: work that adds value, results in betterment of the facility,
  extends the property’s life, or adapts it for new use. Capitalization threshold is $25k

● Repair/Maintenance: work that restores the property to its original condition

● Categories of Capital Improvements:
  -Construction and Renovation
  -Systems and Infrastructure
  -Fixtures and Equipment
  -Sites and Grounds
