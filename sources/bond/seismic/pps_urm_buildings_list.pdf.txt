          PPS Buildings on City of Portland Unreinforced Masonry (URM) Building List
                                         As of Oct 2024

Several buildings that were previously on the City of Portland’s URM Building List have since
been fully seismically reinforced by PPS. PPS is working with the City of Portland to update
their list, however the City’s list may still include some sites for which full seismic reinforcement
has recently been completed or is in the process of completion.

The following sites have had some incremental seismic improvements but are not yet fully
seismically reinforced as of 2024. PPS continues to seek funding to complete full seismic
reinforcement of these sites.

   1.​ Ainsworth Elementary - Some interior seismic upgrades. Seismically strengthened roof.
   2.​ Beach Elementary - Some interior seismic upgrades.
   3.​ Buckman Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   4.​ Capitol Hill Elementary
   5.​ Cleveland High School* - Seismically strengthened roof.
   6.​ Creston Elementary - Some interior seismic upgrades. Seismically strengthened roof.
   7.​ Fernwood (Beverly Cleary) Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   8.​ George Middle School
   9.​ James John Elementary - Some interior seismic upgrades. Seismically strengthened roof.
   10.​Jefferson High School** Will be rebuilt starting in fall 2025
   11.​Kelly Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   12.​Markham Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   13.​Marysville Elementary*** Undergoing a full seismic retrofit from 2024-2025
   14.​Mt. Tabor Middle School - Seismically strengthened roof.
   15.​Richmond Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   16.​Rieke Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   17.​Rigler Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.
   18.​Rose City Park Elementary
   19.​Sabin Elementary - Some interior seismic upgrades. Seismically strengthened roof.
   20.​Smith Building (non-school site)
   21.​Vernon K-8 - Seismically strengthened roof.
   22.​Wilcox K-8
   23.​Winterhaven Elementary - Seismically strengthened roof.

*Cleveland High School was allocated funding in the 2020 School Improvement Bond to
complete master planning and design for a full modernization. However funding for construction
has not yet been secured. PPS anticipates securing that funding in a future bond.

**Jefferson High School was funded for full modernization in the 2020 School Improvement
Bond and scheduled to begin work in 2025 and be completed in 2028.

***Marysville Elementary is undergoing a full seismic retrofit. Phase one was completed in the
summer of 2024. The final phase is scheduled to be completed in the summer of 2025.
