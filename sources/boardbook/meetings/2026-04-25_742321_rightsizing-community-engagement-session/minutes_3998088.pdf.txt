                  Community Engagement Session: School Rightsizing

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Portland Public Schools Board of Education Community Engagement Session was held at
Benson High School at 546 NE 12th Ave, Portland, OR 97232, and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Board Members Present: Michelle DePass, Rashelle Chase-Miller, Virginia La Forte, Stephanie
Engelsman.

Staff: Jon Franco - Senior Chief of Operations; Margaret Calvert - Assistant Superintendent; Nichole
Watson - Senior Director of Family and Community Engagement; Emily Glasgow - Senior Director - P-K
Academics; Richard Smith - Senior Director, Data and Accountability.

Call to Order
The meeting was called to order at 2:16 pm.

Welcome + Grounding

Nicole Watson welcomed attendees, oriented them to materials and the agenda for the session. Dr. John
Franco provided a welcome and reiterated that there was not already a list of identified schools for
closure.

Board members introduced themselves and shared what they hoped to hear from the attendees.

Historical Context

Jon Franco provided the context for the need to consolidate schools, citing declining enrollment and
creating well resourced schools in a budget deficient.

Learning Lab Rounds

Participants were able to ask questions of subject matter experts: Emily Glasgow for Academics, and
Richard Smith and Margaret Calver for Data. Common themes expressed by participants for what a
well-resourced school looks like, included strong and relevant curriculum, adequate adult-to-student
ratios, special education supports, schools with librarians along with music and art. There was also a
desire for safe and functional facilities, and after-school programming. Participants also expressed the
need to increase trust, examine focus option schools and the impact on enrollment, consider expansion of
pre-K classrooms in underutilized buildings, concerns about the decision making process, and calls to
address state level funding formulas.

Board Remarks
Board Members recapped the themes they heard from attendees and encourage people to attend an
upcoming school funding forum.
