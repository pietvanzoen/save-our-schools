SUBJECT:         Preliminary Financial Update for the Quarter Ended December 31, 2025


This Preliminary Financial Update is an overview of Portland Public School’s financial operations
for the Quarter Ended December 31, 2025.
This report provides an overview of financial operations of District funds for the period of July 1,
2025 through December 31, 2025. Amounts reported are from the financial records of the
District and are unaudited.


As you review the Second Quarter Financial Schedules, please keep the following in mind:

         The “Annual Forecast” column for each fund includes known activity through December
          and projected revenues and expenditures based on historic annual averages, current
          year trends, as well as co-created projections with operational departments.
         The District operates on a modified accrual basis of accounting, which means most
          revenues and expenses are recorded as received and expended.
         Typically, the final audited financial reports for the prior fiscal year are completed in
          December, however due to a government shutdown and delayed issuance of OMB
          guidance on federal grant reporting requirements this year, the annual audit report filing
          timeline has been extended into early February 2026.
         Beginning balances are reported, however, they remain unaudited draft numbers until
          the final adjustments are recorded based on the recently completed Fiscal Year 2025
          financial report. Updated values will be reflected in the January 2026 Financial Reports.
         This information is utilizing the best information available at this time.
         Additional reports are included with this report to provide additional context and a high level
          summary of the financial update.

                                 Financial Report Summary for the Quarter Ending December 31, 2025

           • Revenues: Current Revenues collected include a double payment of the State School Fund for July. Beginning Fund Balance has been updated based on 2025 fiscal year end
General      adjustments. Projections have been updated based on November and December tax collections and enrollment updates.
           • Expenses: Labor costs include updated PERS Employer rates and four months of 10-month staff wage expenses as 10-month staff are paid September through June.

           • Grant Revenues: Most grants are on a reimbursement basis, with first quarter claims completed and revenues recorded. Second quarter claims recorded include
 Special     reimbursements for October and November expenditures while December claims will record in January.
           • Grant Expenses: Most grant labor expenses involve 10-month staff and school year contract services.
Revenue    • Nutrition Services: Revenues reflect July through November activity with December meal claims recording in January. Expenditures reflect summer program work, four
             months of 10-month staff wage expenses, and food inventory needs through the end of the second quarter.




 Debt      • Quarterly revenues include PERS UAL rates charged against monthly payroll, property tax revenues, and a transfer from the Construction Excise Tax Fund for QZAB loan
             repayment.
Service    • Expenses reflect the annual debt service payments through December.




 Capital   • Project expenses for the quarter include ongoing Bond capital projects and updated projections for estimated FY2026 project spending. Bond sale proceeds have also been
Projects     projected based on current planning for a spring bond sale for partial 2020 and 2025 bond authorizations.




           • Second quarter projections are based on actual expenditures through December following month end close and are informed through operational department expenditure
             projections. Projections will be updated monthly based on new information from local and state entities as well as from our actual monthly expenditures and departmental
Items of     projections.
  Note     • Current operational spending is projected to exceed revenues. Although actuals fluctuate from the adopted budget for both revenue and expenses, there are a few key
             contributing factors: FTE Additions/Vacancy Savings Gap ($10M), Nutrition Services anticipated transfer, and Integrated Operations costs exceeding budget.
           • Enrollment: Student enrollment has declined from the prior year, however State School Fund revenues are tied to the 2024-25 school year enrollment actuals.




           • Beginning Fund Balances have been recorded and are pending final audit.
           • 2025-26 State School Fund revenues are tied to 2024-2025 enrollment. Enrollment will be monitored for any further impacts on revenues.
Bottom     • Fiscal Year 2025 financial reporting close out anticipated for December was delayed through late January to early February due to a federal shut down and a lack of OMB
             federal grant audit guidelines. Audited financial data will be updated where necessary with the Q3 reports.
 Line      • Initial Spending freeze remains in place to reduce expenditures and will be enhanced throughout the close of the year to achieve additional savings to close the fiscal year.
           • At the close of Quarter 2, current operational spending is projected to exceed revenues. If left unaddressed, it is anticipated that this will deplete our 5% fund balance by
             more than $22 million.
