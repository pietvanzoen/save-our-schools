Renaming Committee Work
● The Jason Lee Renaming Committee was established in the spring of 2024, after
  the Board of Directors approved the request to allow the school to pursue a name
  change.
● The committee was made up of parents, staff, students and community members.
● The committee worked for two years to set parameters for the search of a new
  name, focusing on the goal of ﬁnding a name that inspired our diverse community
  of learners and their families.
● A total of 7 names of individuals and two symbolic names were put up for a vote
  after many discussions, lessons and community conversations.
● We held our voting process from Feb. 18 to March 18, and 438 votes were cast
  from a community of current students, alumni, family members, neighbors and
  staff.

           Why Sunrise Elementary?
“This name came to me while I was driving to school one morning.
Watching the sunrise over Rocky Butte I thought was something so
special. Then over the next few weeks I really noticed the sun rising
behind our school and across our playground.
I thought about the symbolism of a sunrise. It's the beginning of a new
time. A new day. There's a lot of hope in a sunrise.”
                                         - Ms. Dar, kindergarten teacher
