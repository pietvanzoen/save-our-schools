Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 4:34 pm by Committee Chair Virginia La Forte. She provided an
overview of the meeting agenda, noting that there was a request to postpone the Bond Accountability
Committee (BAC) Charter. The Committee discussed the purpose of the BAC Charter item and whether
to postpone the topic, ultimately deciding to hold it until the next meeting of the Committee.

Public Comment

Time: 4:40 pm

    ●   John Dwyer: Thanked staff for the additional seismic materials and requested clarity on how
        schools were selected, and requested strong oversight of all bond projects.
    ●   Elijah Probst: Requested the development of a seismic plan for every school in the event of a
        major earthquake and to pursue outside funding sources.
    ●   Effie Greathouse: Stated that temporary cooling measures still need improvement, including
        additional portable air conditioners and allowing upper-level windows to remain open overnight.
    ●   Sasha Pollack: Requested that the operations team explore ways to regulate building
        temperatures while reducing reliance on fossil fuels.
    ●   Shannon Kittrick: Shared concerns that student-led environmental projects funded through the
        Portland Clean Energy partnership face approval barriers and urged prioritization of student
        projects in order to maintain the partnership.

Update: Grant Bowl

Time: 4:55 pm

Staff: Dr. Jon Franco and Tom Odgers

Staff provided an update on the Grant Bowl project, noting that city permitting has been submitted,
coordination is underway with utility partners, and that the project remains on track. There was a question
regarding the status of the Powell Park field project. Staff indicated ongoing discussions with Portland
Parks and Recreation related to shared space use and long-term planning for Powell Park.

Bond Audit Implementation Update

Time: 4:59 pm

Staff: Sarah Norman and Darren Lee

Staff provided a status update on the implementation of four of the bond audit recommendations,
identifying which items are complete and which are ongoing. Staff noted that there has been improved
executive leadership involvement, continued tracking of recommendations, and efforts to address website
issues affecting public access to audit-related materials after the implementation of a new web host
districtwide.

Procedeo Report

Time: 5:08 pm

Procedeo Representatives: Brian Johnson, TJ Watkins, Sarah Norman

Procedeo representatives provided an overview of the previously shared Procedeo Report, including
Jefferson High School modernization options, and the process, timelines, and build scenarios considered.
Board Members asked questions, with discussion focused on the feasibility of each of the options. It was
noted that permitting, design, and athletic planning continue assuming Option 1.

Review of the Bond Accountability Committee Charter

Topic moved to the next Facilities Improvement and Oversight Committee Meeting.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 5:26 pm by Committee Chair Virginia La Forte.
