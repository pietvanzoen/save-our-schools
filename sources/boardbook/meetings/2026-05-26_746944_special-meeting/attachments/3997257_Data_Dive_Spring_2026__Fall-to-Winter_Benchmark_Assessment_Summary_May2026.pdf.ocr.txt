Responding to the Data: Schools and
Central Oﬃce
● Schools continue to:
  ○ Provide Grade Level and Standards-Aligned Instruction with supports
     and scaffolds
  ○ Provide student academic support via WIN blocks, intervention
     courses, and personalized instruction

● Central Oﬃce continues to:
  ○ Monitor the mathematics academic program implementation via
     instructional walks with senior directors of schools to ensure better
     calibration of instruction across school sites
  ○ Provide guidance and professional learning to principals on providing
     practice-changing feedback to strengthen instruction                    18
