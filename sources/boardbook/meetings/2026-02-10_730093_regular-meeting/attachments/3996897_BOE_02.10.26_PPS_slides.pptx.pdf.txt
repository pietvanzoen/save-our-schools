Technology
Portland Public Schools has selected MicroK12 as our partner for
the upcoming Chromebook refresh to replace student devices which
no longer meet instructional needs. This work includes investments
in updated student laptops and classroom technology across the
district.

The refresh is part of our broader modernization eﬀorts and is
aligned with August start of school.

This approach diﬀers from past cycles by co-designing the rollout
with school-based leaders to reduce risk of disruption to students
and instruction. Building principals, senior directors of schools, and
school business managers are helping shape processes for a 1:1
Chromebook model at high schools, cart-based models for grades
6–8, and age-appropriate access in earlier grades so that device
distribution and management ﬁt each school’s operations and
student needs.

OTIS is dedicated to reﬁning logistics with the appropriate
stakeholders, to fully develop a go forward plan by end of February.
