2025-26 Champion PPS Schools
Program Investments
Champion PPS Schools is our districtwide model to support staff positions and
creating wider systems of support for students.

This school year, the following investments are impacting students districtwide:

High Impact Tutoring for K-3rd grade students     $660,000
Math Supports for 9th-10th grade students         $ 70,000
Food Pantries in 20 Community Schools             $200,000
Total Investment in PPS for 2025-26               $930,000

The PAC also allocated $100,000 for The Fund to use as
“Seed Funding for Future Fundraising.”
High Impact Tutoring is a Proven
Literacy Strategy Benefitting K-3 Students
With our investment of $660,000, 45 schools are receiving in
person High Impact Tutoring (HIT) in literacy skills this school
year.

 ●   30 schools have tutors from Reading Results
 ●   15 schools have Educational Assistants, including bilingual
     tutor in Spanish DLI
 ●   Tutoring is 3x/week - 30 minutes per session - 30 weeks
 ●   Students are more likely to attend school on tutoring days
