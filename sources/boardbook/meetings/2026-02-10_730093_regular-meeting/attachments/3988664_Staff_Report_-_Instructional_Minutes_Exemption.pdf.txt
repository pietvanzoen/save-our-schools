2026–27 Instructional Minutes Exemptions

Staff is submitting the recommended 2026–27 School District Calendar for Board
consideration and approval at the February 10, 2026 meeting.

In alignment with the proposed calendar, staff assumes the continuation of current bell
schedules and instructional minute frameworks. The proposal also includes three days of
parent/teacher conferences as instructional minutes for 2026–27, consistent with historical PPS
practice. This approach is also required under the Portland Association of Teachers (PAT)
collective bargaining agreement, which specifies that instructional minutes and parent-teacher
conference days are counted toward meeting state-required instructional time (PAT Contract
6.8.4.5). These elements ensure PPS provides the required instructional time under OAR
581-022-2320, barring unforeseeable circumstances:

      (1)(a) Except as allowed under subsections (2) and (4), each school district shall
      ensure that at least 92% of all students in the district and at least 80% of all
      students at each school operated by the district are scheduled to receive annually
      the following minimum hours of instructional time: (A) Grade 12 — 966 hours; (B)
      Grades 9–11 — 990 hours; and (C) Grades K–8 — 900 hours.

Staff recommends adopting this proposed calendar and approving the allowable exemption also
per 581-022-2320 Required Instructional Time (6)(e) as noted below:

      (6) Upon approval by the local school board, a district may include in its calculation
      of instructional time required by subsection (1) of this rule the following: ... (e) Up to
      30 hours for parent teacher conferences;

Recommendation for Exemption 4

Staff further recommends the Board approve Exemption 4 in alignment with the proposed
calendar to ensure district compliance with OAR 581-022-2320. This request is specific to the
following alternative program:

   ●​ Alliance High School: To accommodate a four-day instructional week.
