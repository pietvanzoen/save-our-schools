2026-27 District Calendar

Major Religious Holidays and Cultural Events 2026-27

Schools work to avoid scheduling special school events on major religious holidays and cultural events to be inclusive of all
students. This includes the scheduling of field trips, back-to-school night, outdoor school, assemblies, major tests, PTA and site

council meetings, student performances, etc. This list of holidays/events does not include all students’ traditions and there
may be other dates to avoid scheduling conflicts for students.

Sep 11 - 13 Rosh Hashanah* Feb 10 Ash Wednesday
Sep 20-21 Yom Kippur* Feb 8 - Mar 9 Ramadan*
Oct 12 Indigenous Peoples’ Day Mar 22-23 Purim*

Nov 8 Diwali Mar 26 Good Friday

Nov 27 Native American Heritage Day Mar 28 Easter

Dec 4 - 12 Hanukkah* Mar 9 - 10 Eid al-Fitr*
Dec 25 Christmas Apr 21 - 29 Passover*
Dec 26 - Jan 1 Kwanzaa May 16 -17 Eid al-Adha*
Feb 6 Lunar New Year June 19 Juneteenth
*Observance of Jewish and Muslim holidays begin at sundown on the first day listed and end at
nightfall on the last date specified.
