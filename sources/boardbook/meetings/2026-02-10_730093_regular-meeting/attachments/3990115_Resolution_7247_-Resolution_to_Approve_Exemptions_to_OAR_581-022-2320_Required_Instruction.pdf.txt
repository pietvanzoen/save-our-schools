                                          RESOLUTION No. 7247

          Resolution to Approve Exemptions to OAR 581-022-2320 Required Instructional Time:
                     2026-27 Parent-Teacher Conferences and Alliance High School

                                                 RECITALS

A.    OAR 581-022-2320 Required Instructional Time requires all school districts to ensure that at least
      92% of all students in the district and at least 80% of all students at each school operated by the
      district are scheduled to receive annually the minimum hours of instructional time:

                 a. Grade 12 - 966 hours;
                 b. Grades 9-11 - 990 hours; and
                 c. Grades K-8 - 900 hours.

B.    Instructional time in Portland Public Schools’ alternative education programs, specifically within the
      Department of Multiple Pathways to Graduation, is designed to meet the unique needs of students.
      To remain in compliance with Division 22, the Board must annually approve exemptions under OAR
      581-022-2320(4). For the 2026-27 school year, Alliance High School requires an exemption to
      facilitate a four-day-per-week instructional model.

C.    For the 2026-27 school year and consistent with the District’s historical practice and relevant
      collective bargaining agreements, Portland Public Schools is seeking approval to include up to 18
      hours of Parent-Teacher Conferences as instructional time as permitted by OAR 581-022-
      2320(6)(e).

                                               RESOLUTION

The Board of Education hereby approves the following exemptions to OAR 581-022-2320 Required
Instructional Time:

     1. Inclusion of up to 18 hours of Parent-Teacher Conferences as instructional time; and

     2. A specific exemption for Alliance High School as an alternative education program under
        subsection (4).

This approval supports the District on a path toward compliance with Division 22 standards for the 2026-
27 school year.
