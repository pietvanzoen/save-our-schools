                                        Resolution No. 7167

              Directing the Superintendent to Provide an Analysis of Outdoor School Costs
                      and Options to Preserve a Five-Day Outdoor School Program

                                            RECITALS

A.   Outdoor School provides valuable, hands-on learning experiences that support students’ academic
     growth, social-emotional development, and connection to the natural environment.

B.   In Oregon, Outdoor School is a required experiential learning program for middle school students,
     providing an integral part of the curriculum designed to meet state science learning standards.

C.   Outdoor School is, for many students, their only childhood access to immersive, nature-based
     education and outdoor experiences, making it a critical component of educational equity.

D.   Measure 99, passed by Oregon voters in 2016, allocated funding to ensure all Oregon students have
     access to a full week of Outdoor School.

E.   The cost of providing Outdoor School may have increased since the passage of Measure 99, and it
     is essential to understand these changes in order to ensure the program’s sustainability.

F.   The Board of Education is committed to preserving a five-day Outdoor School experience for all
     eligible students.

G.   The Board of Education recognizes the efforts made by the Superintendent working with
     superintendents around the state of Oregon, and with the Superintendent of the Multnomah
     Education District, to continue this important educational program.

                                               RESOLVED

Now, therefore, be it resolved that, to the maximum extent permitted under law, the District shall:

1.   Provide an analysis of the per-student and total cost of Outdoor School before and after the passage
     of Measure 99, adjusted for inflation, and identify any major cost drivers contributing to changes over
     time.

2.   Present options to preserve a five-day Outdoor School experience for all eligible students for the
     2025–26 school year that do not impact direct student services, including but not limited to:

            a. Identifying opportunities to reduce program costs without reducing services or
               access.
            b. Leveraging alternative funding sources.

3.   Deliver the analysis and recommendations to the Board no later than the October 14, 2025 Regular
     Board meeting, so the Board may review and take any necessary action during Fall 2025.
