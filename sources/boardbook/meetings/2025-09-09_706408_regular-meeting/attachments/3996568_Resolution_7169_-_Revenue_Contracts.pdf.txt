                                                RESOLUTION No. 7169

                      Revenue Contracts that Exceed $150,000 for Delegation of Authority

                                                        RECITAL

Contracts; Delegation of Authority to Superintendent”) requires the Board of Education (“Board”) enter
into contracts, except as otherwise expressly authorized. Contracts exceeding $150,000 per contractor
are listed below.

                                                     RESOLUTION

The Superintendent recommends that the Board approve these contracts. The Board accepts this
recommendation and by this resolution authorizes the Deputy Clerk to enter into the following
agreements.
