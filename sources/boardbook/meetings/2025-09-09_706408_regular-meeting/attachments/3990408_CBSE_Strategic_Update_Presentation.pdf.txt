Purpose
The Center for Black Student Excellence (CBSE) is
both a place and a systems-level approach, born out
of Portland Public Schools’ 2018 visioning process
to reimagine public education and aﬃrmed by 75%
of Portland voters through the 2020 bond.

We honor and express gratitude to the many
community members, educators, and leaders who
shaped this moment, engaging in authentic and
courageous conversations that made CBSE
possible.

CBSE represents a bold response to Portland’s
legacy of racial exclusion and displacement,
particularly impacting Black communities from
Albina. While open to all students, the Center is
rooted in cultural aﬃrmation and designed to
integrate joy, healing, and excellence as essential
conditions for learning.

The Current Moment
A Site Identiﬁed: Due Diligence in Motion
PPS has identiﬁed a potential site for the Center for
Black Student Excellence headquarters, selected for its
cultural signiﬁcance, physical readiness, accessibility,
and alignment with the community’s vision.

This is not a rushed decision. It is the product of ﬁve
years of engagement and the 194-page community
vision document that continues to guide our steps.

We are now in the due diligence phase to assess
feasibility, operational needs, and programmatic ﬁt
before ﬁnal commitment.

CBSE is not just a building. It is a systems-level
redesign and a living resource designed to advance
student success and strengthen community
connection.
