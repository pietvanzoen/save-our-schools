  BACKGROUND

  In November 2020, voters approved bonds that included $60 million for investment in the Center
  for Black Student Excellence (CBSE). Following a multi-year community engagement effort, the
  CBSE Vision was created in 2023. (See 194-page Vision:
  https://drive.google.com/file/d/1M5FpAmLwpx4-p6y3gPXpNMPOKf623hMH/view.) Following the
  development of the Vision, PPS engaged in a search for properties in the Albina neighborhood
  to serve as the home for the CBSE where students, educators, and community members can
  convene and realize the Vision.

  PPS has identified the One North property to serve as the CBSE home. It is located in the
  Albina neighborhood at 3506-3514 N. Vancouver Avenue and 25 N. Fremont, St. It was built in
  2015 and comprises two buildings, together containing approximately 72,000 square feet of
  space. The negotiated purchase price for the property is $16 million. (PPS obtained an
  appraisal of the property in May 2025, estimating its value at $15.2 million.)

  Staff seeks Board approval to enter into a Purchase and Sale Agreement (PSA) for the One
  North property, attached as Exhibit A.

  ANALYSIS OF SITUATION

  The proposed purchase of One North represents over four years of work to develop the Vision
  and programming for the CBSE. It is critical that the CBSE home be in Albina, and this building
  is centrally located in the Albina neighborhood. PPS could not build a building like the One
  North property for anywhere close to $16 million, and PPS expects the building to be largely
  move-in ready for many of its component uses. Other options explored by PPS, working with its
  commercial real estate broker, did not yield a better, more cost-effective option.

  Once PPS executes the PSA, it will formally enter a robust due diligence process that will
  include assessment of:

     ●   Physical and environmental inspections
     ●   Property survey
     ●   Suitability for PPS’s intended use, including extent of remodeling required
     ●   Data about building operations and existing tenants rent streams
              ○ As of 1/1/26, approximately 29% of the building will be occupied under current
                 leases.
     ●   Transportation and parking options
     ●   Operating agreement for the common areas
     ●   Title report

  PPS will engage experts in the relevant areas, as needed, to assist with and advise on the due
  diligence. PPS's efforts will include (1) assessing requirements for anticipated uses of the
building in line with the CBSE Vision; (2) development of operating budgets and staffing plans
for the building maintenance; (3) planning for collaborative space for community partners
serving PPS students; and (4) review of title, surveys, and physical inspections.

After a 90-day due diligence period, the Board will vote on whether to proceed with the
purchase based on the results of the extensive due diligence. In advance of that vote, the
results of the due diligence assessments will be shared with the Board and community.
Entering into the PSA at this time does not create a binding obligation for PPS to purchase the
property. It does allow for PPS to conduct thorough due diligence and for the building to not be
sold to any other purchaser during this time.

FISCAL IMPACT

The negotiated purchase price is $16 million, which will be funded by 2020 bond funds. In
addition, PPS will incur transaction-related expenses as needed in consultant and legal fees to
support the due diligence and negotiate and document the transaction. Costs of any needed
improvements are not known at this time, but will be assessed during the due diligence period.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT (IF APPLICABLE)

The community was engaged for multiple years to create the Vision for the CBSE, which
informs the selection and uses of the building. PPS will continue to seek input on the One North
property throughout the due diligence process through public Board and Committee meetings
and other channels.
