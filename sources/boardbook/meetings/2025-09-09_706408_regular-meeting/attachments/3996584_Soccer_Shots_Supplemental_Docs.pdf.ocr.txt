Coach Impact

The partnership between Soccer Shots and Head Start was something | really looked forward to. | was so excited
to be given the opportunity to coach classes during both sessions of Head Start programs this season and to
extend our reach to a community | care greatly about. We strive to be inclusive in all we do, and this was an
experience | felt honored to be a part of. Coaching in Spanish is new to me, as | haven’t always had to use my
native language on the field, and it’s been so much fun to incorporate phrases and guidance to ensure | am
doing my best to meet each player on their level. There is an abundance of kindness and helpfulness at every
location from the players and teachers. | have enjoyed getting to see the players’ smiling faces each week and
watching them grow personally and athletically through our character concepts and the skills we teach.

- Coach Amy, Coach for Whitman and Grout classes

As a proud product of Head Start, these classes have meant so much to me. Our program centers around
futbol—a sport that, for many, is a familiar language from home. In our Hispanic community, sports like
futbol are more than just a game — they are a bond that brings us together and keeps us grounded.
Having the opportunity to coach these classes in Spanish, my first language (and, for many of them, their
first language) helps create a space for both of us where we feel comfortable and at home. |! am proud to
coach these classes and show the children that they are represented and truly belong.

- Coach Ash, Coach for Clarendon Early Learning Academy classes

These classes have allowed me to gain even more experience as a coach by teaching the
children in a different language. These Head Start classes have been such a pleasure becuase |
get to watch these kids grow while having fun and learning something new. Teaching in Spanish
was something that | was afraid of at first, but as time has passed, | have come to love teaching
these classes and spending time with these kids. Sometimes | believe they are teaching me more
than | am teaching them!

- Coach Daniel, Coach for Clarendon Early Learning Academy and Sacajawea classes

My classes at Head Start have been nothing but amazing. The children are always respectful and
inclusive — checking in on their friends and making sure to include them in the fun. They bring
positive energy to class, rain or shine, which helps me become a better coach!
- Coach Edwin, Coach for Jason Lee, Lent, Marysville, and Faubion classes

My Head Start experience has reinforced my belief in our Soccer Shots statement: We Care, We
Are Inclusive, and We Own It! Coaching these kids each week has been a blast — not just with
their progression within our curriculum, but their positive and joyful energy is contagious! My
Head Start classes have been some of my best of the season, and | believe it's because their
values match ours. Each week, my kids surprise me with their respect, kindness, and confidence!
- Coach Orion, Coach for Whitman, Grout, and Faubion classes

Coaching Head Start classes has been such a pleasure and has quickly become one of my
favorite parts of being a coach at Soccer Shots. The kids bring curiosity, enthusiasm, and
imagination to every practice. They’re always ready to learn and have a great amount of respect
for their peers and coaches. Their energy makes class so fun and allows me to let loose and have
fun with them too! In addition, being able to teach classes in Spanish brings me so much joy. It
allows the Spanish-speaking students to get the most out of each practice, makes them feel
more comfortable, and has resulted in a deeper connection between the kids and me.

- Coach Sofia, Coach for Rosa Parks, Sitton, and Sacajawea classes

Working with Head Start has been a life changing experience. Each class is about much more
than soccer- we support each other as a team, give each child a chance to shine on the field, and
build an environment that is safe and fun for everyone. The kids are so passionate and caring,
they look out for each other and cheer for one another every class. It is my first experience
participating in Spanish-led classes and that has been so meaningful. It has pushed me to learn
more of a second language to be able to connect better with the kids, and seeing their faces as
they learn from coaches in their primary language is heartwarming. | love being in such a caring,
inclusive environment and always get so much from working with Head Start!

- Coach Michael, Coach for Creston Annex and Clarendon Early Learning Academy classes
