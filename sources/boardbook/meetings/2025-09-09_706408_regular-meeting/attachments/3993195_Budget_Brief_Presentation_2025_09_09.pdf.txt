“Prepare and plan, not panic.”
“The forecast does not mean there are imminent state budget cuts.
Today’s news means it is time to prepare and plan, not panic.”
Key differences between this forecast and the Great Recession, include:
 ● The State has relatively healthy reserves ($3.419 billion) compared to the
   past.
 ● This is the first of eight revenue forecasts and there are still 21 months left
   in the current budget cycle – a lot can change during that time period.
 ● The Governor and Legislature have time to plan a response to the budget
   news - there isn’t sufficient evidence to rush straight to budget cuts.
