Public Comment

Public Comment was offered during the related agenda topic.

Policies in the Public Comment Period - Policy Revisions: 1.20.010-P Board of Education and
5.10.066-P Ethics and Conflict of Interest Policy

Time: 4:26 pm

Chair Brim-Edwards noted that the Board of Education Policy 1.20.010-P and Ethics and Conflict of
Interest Policy 5.10.066-P received a first reading at the March 18, 2025 Board Meeting. She shared that
additional language has been proposed for Policy 5.10.066-P and provided a summary of the proposed
amendment, which would allow an exemption for board members wishing to enter the hiring process
during the last six months of their term for the following year, and read the new language aloud. The
Committee discussed the proposed amendment. The policy was unanimously referred for a second first
reading.

Draft Proposed New Policy x.xx.xxx-P Solicitation Registration and Reporting

Time: 4:34 pm

Committee Chair Brim-Edwards introduced the draft of a new policy for consideration related to
transparency in district interactions, adding that the draft policy is not yet ready for action.

It was noted that the Director of Purchasing and Contracting sent some information to Committee
members regarding the district procurement processes. Discussion topics included contract types that do
not prompt a Request for Proposal (RFP) process, potential influence in non-competitive contracting, the
proposed registration process and what type of entities would be required to register under the proposed
policy. There was emphasis on keeping the system simple and focused on transparency, not restricting
engagement.

Committee Chair Brim-Edwards requested that committee members compile their questions and send
them to her directly before the next meeting.

4.10.020-P Compulsory Enrollment: Age and Grade Level at

Time: 4:57 pm

Staff: Dr. Emily Glasgow, Dana Stiles, Judy Brennan

Committee Chair Brim-Edwards introduced the policy, confirming that no action on the policy would be
taken at this time. It was noted that the Early Learners department provided written responses to
previously submitted questions at the start of the meeting. There was discussion on the proposed
revisions.

The discussion focused on the implications of maintaining or revising the September 1 kindergarten entry
cutoff date. Members acknowledged that while a strict cutoff is the most equitable, no date serves all
families equally, and school readiness varies widely. Concerns were raised about the reliability of
assessing readiness, with data showing readiness assessments are accurate only about half the time.
The committee also discussed how many current first-grade students would have been affected by a
more flexible entry date, specifically those with September birthdays. Additionally, there was discussion
about whether to revise the policy that currently allows students who completed kindergarten at
accredited institutions to transfer into Portland Public Schools, with maintaining consistency noted as a
reason to retain that option.

The Committee heard public comment on the policy.

        •   Jessica Colby, TAGAC: In support of a later kindergarten entry cutoff date to provide flexibility
            for families and better meet individual student readiness.
