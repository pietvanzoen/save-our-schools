Jefferson High School Enrollment Scenario Proposal Listening Session

Time: 6:10 pm

Staff: Margaret Calvert – Assistant Superintendent, School Planning and Modernization

The meeting was called to order at 6:10 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang. Interpreters introduced
themselves, and Principal Drake Shelton welcomed families to Jefferson. Chair Wang provided comments
on behalf of the Board in reflection of the recent Regular Meeting, and highlighting the Board’s
commitment to professional conduct and indicating the culture the new Board seeks to establish.

Superintendent Armstrong explained the timing of her proposal, noting that additional opportunities for
public comment and Board discussion will continue, with a final Board decision anticipated in January.
Staff presented an overview of the Superintendent’s enrollment scenario, including the planning and
engagement process, a phased implementation timeline, projected enrollment and utilization data,
visualizations of school boundaries and distances, and the anticipated benefits of boundary changes.
