Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR)

A brief update on the status of the Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR) was provided. It was
noted that the Federal Office of Management and Budget typically releases supplemental guidelines for
the Single Audit’s report on spending of federal funds each year in May, however the updated guidelines
have not yet been released, which may lead to delays in the completion of the report. An additional status
update is scheduled for the Board on November 6, 2025. Committee Chair Sullivan noted that the
financial auditors may reach out to Committee Members to schedule a meeting with them and provided a
summary of her experience meeting with them, adding that it’s an informal conversation.

Office of Internal Performance Audit (OIPA) 2025-26 Audit Plan and Discussion on New Audit
Topics

Time: 5:45 pm

Staff: Janise Hansen

Staff provided a status update on approved audit topics and reviewed progress on several audits currently
underway or in development, including audits related to English Language Learners, Measure 98,
building security, and Career and Technical Education programs. There was discussion regarding
anticipated timelines, coordination with state-level audit work to avoid duplication, incorporation of student
feedback where appropriate, and potential adjustments to audit scope to align with recent strategic
planning efforts.

The Committee also discussed the process for identifying and prioritizing future audit topics, reviewed
areas of interest raised by board members, students, and community members, and considered how
audits might align with board goals and areas of risk. The Committee narrowed the list of potential topics
down to three audits topics: leave time practices, systems of support for principals, and student
performance measure. Staff will review the selected audit topics and bring more details on each back to a
future meeting to be narrowed further.
