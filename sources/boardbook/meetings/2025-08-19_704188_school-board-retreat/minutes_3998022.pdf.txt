                                Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a School Board Retreat of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at
Lincoln High School, 1750 SW Salmon St., Portland, OR 97205 and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Purpose & Introduction of Facilitator, Norms & Ice Breaker

The meeting was called to order at 1:08 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.

Chair Wang introduced the meeting facilitator Barbara Jenkins. Ms. Jenkins shared meeting norms, and
attendees participated in an ice breaker activity.

TTI Assessments Review

Time: 1:30 pm

Dr. Shawn Joseph introduced himself and presented the TTI assessment results, highlighting the balance
of personality styles among Board Members, their strengths, and how these traits influence their work
together. He emphasized the importance of clearly defining success at the outset of major initiatives,
maintaining focus to avoid initiative overload, and holding regular check-ins on progress.
Recommendations included identifying a few high-level goals, establishing timelines and outcomes, and
using public dashboards for accountability. The Board discussed the assessment results, including
managing workload and avoiding staff and superintendent burnout.

Governance

Time: 2:22 pm

Chair Wang summarized the purpose of the meeting. Ms. Jenkins provided an overview of the
governance relationship, highlighting which responsibilities fall under the Board’s work versus the
Superintendent’s. Attendees then reviewed and discussed a Harvard case study, analyzed behaviors and
suggested ways the situation could have been handled more effectively.

Break: 3:19pm - 3:29pm

Ms. Jenkins provided an overview of Board best practices based on research, emphasizing the
importance of coherence, including a shared vision. Attendees reviewed the National School Board
Association’s Effective School Boards guidelines and participated in a group activity to highlight key
takeaways and identify areas where the PPS Board excels and faces challenges.

Chief Legal Officer Sharon Toncray reviewed the Board’s legal duties, covering key rules, including serial
meetings and the role of committees. Discussion focused on serial meeting laws, public comment at
committees, how agenda items are added, the Board’s Protocols, and meeting materials timelines. A
request was made to include a discussion of public comment at committee meetings in a future Board
meeting.

Communications

Attendees participated in an activity regarding protocols for communication, noting what communication
protocols they would like to start and stop on a sticky note and then took time to read what others wrote.

Additional Protocols

Superintendent Armstrong provided an overview of the Portland Public Schools Vision and Strategic Plan.
She noted that a Continuous Improvement Plan is already in place with four major goals focusing on
attendance, literacy, math, and 9th grade success. Planning is underway for the development of the
district’s 2030 Strategic Plan.

Chair Wang reviewed past practices for the superintendent evaluation and potential approaches for the
future. Discussion included the evaluation tool options and required timelines outlined in the
superintendent’s contract. The superintendent noted that she would appreciate clarity on her evaluation
criteria.

It was noted that the board has not conducted a self-evaluation in recent years but that there is interest in
completing one in the near future, potentially using the Oregon School Board Association (OSBA)
template.

Debrief and Next Steps

Board Members reflected on the day by sharing pluses and deltas.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 6:08 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.
