The meeting was called to order at 4:05 pm by Chair Eddie Wang

Bond Accountability Committee (BAC) Report Presentation

Time: 4:06 pm

BAC: Norman Dowty

Mr. Dowty presented the quarterly Bond Accountability Report, covering progress and financial updates
for the 2012, 2017, and 2020 bonds, noting that projects are generally on time and within budget, with
contingency funds holding. He highlighted that no funds have been spent on the Center for Black Student
Excellence, and that bond revenues remain in compliance with legal requirements. Board members
asked questions about project delays, the Benson High School budget, workforce equity goals, and
reporting practices, including a request for cost change reports to be shared with the Board.
Office of School Modernization (OSM) Update

Time: 4:36 pm

Staff: Stormy Shanks

Staff presented an update on bond projects from the Office of School Modernization, highlighting
remaining funds, project statuses, reallocation of the remaining 2017 bond funds to additional health and
safety improvements, the progress of the Jefferson High School design, curriculum funding adjustments
from the 2020 bond, and modernization efforts at Harrison Park. It was noted that ADA and special
education upgrades have been completed at 90 schools. Reporting on the 2025 bond is expected to
begin in July.

2025 Bond Seismic Improvements Program Bimonthly Update

Time: 4:52 pm

Staff: Stormy Shanks

Staff provided an update on seismic improvement planning, noting that the initial assessment identifying
deficiencies has been completed. The next steps include prioritizing the work on the identified needs. A
prioritization report is expected in July, followed by developing recommendations that incorporate district-
specific considerations.
