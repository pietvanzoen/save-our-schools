1. Successful completion (C or better) of 3 or more Advanced
   Placement courses,
2. Successful completion (C or better) of 3 or more International
   Baccalaureate courses,
3. Successful completion (C or Better) of 3 or more Dual Credit
   courses,
4. Successful completion of a Career and Technical Pathway (3 or
   more courses in the same pathway), or
5. Successful achievement of the Seal of Biliteracy.
6. Successful attainment of a postsecondary readiness score on the
   PSAT, SAT, or ACT.
