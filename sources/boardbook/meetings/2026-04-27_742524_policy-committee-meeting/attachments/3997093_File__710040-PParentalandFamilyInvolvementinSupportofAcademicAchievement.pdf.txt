7.10.040-P                Parental and Family Involvement in Support of                                         B
                          Academic Achievement
                                                                                                                O
 I. Introduction.
    (1)       It is the policy (7.10.010-P) of the Portland Public School Board of                              A
              Education to encourage and support family and community involvement in
              education, at home, in our schools and communities, and through                                   R
              school/parent groups. The Board respects families as important decision-
              makers for their children’s education.                                                            D
    (2)       The Board believes that parental engagement is critical to the success of
              each student. In the pursuit of this goal, the Board is committed to ensure
              that Portland Public Schools develop and implement practices and                                  P
              procedures that are broadly inclusive and that provide opportunities for all
              parents and families to become involved in their students’ education                              O
              regardless of economic, social or cultural circumstances.
    (3)       The Board expects the District to establish a culture of best practices for                       L
              parental and family involvement in support of academic achievement for
              all students.                                                                                     I
                                                                                                                C
II. The Superintendent shall oversee the implementation of this policy.
    (1)       The Superintendent shall develop administrative directives, make program
                                                                                                                Y
              changes, if necessary, and integrate staff and program resources to
              support the implementation of this policy, consistent with relevant federal,
              State and local programs and requirements.
    (2)       Implementation of this policy shall be consistent with existing policies on
              citizen and parent group involvement.
    (3)       Implementation of this policy shall include regular assessment of current
              practices; development, implementation and review of a District parental
              and family involvement plan; and coordination with relevant federal, state
              and local programs and requirements.
    (4)       Parental and family participation will be solicited in the development of
              both the District and respective schools’ parental and family involvement
              plans.
    (5)       The Superintendent shall report to the Board of Education (via the
              appropriate standing committee) with a regular report of progress under
              the policy.
    (6)       As required by Portland Public School Board policy (1.70.021-AD) the
              Superintendent shall make available public information related to the
              implementation of this policy.
Legal References: Sections 1112-to-1118 of the Elementary and Secondary Education Act (ESEA), and related
NCLB mandates. History: Adopted 4/16/07
