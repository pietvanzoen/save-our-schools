            schools can provide valuable assistance to the instructional program, to
            school personnel and to the educational enrichment opportunities of                             $"

            under the direction of a licensed teacher or administrator. The Board                           %"
            actively encourages volunteer participation by individuals and groups in
            local schools, district and region level capacities and various specialized                     &"
            educational programs of the district.
        (2) All volunteers shall complete a Criminal History Verification Form and a                         "
            district or school Volunteer Information Form prior to rendering volunteer
            services.                                                                                       '"

            potential contributions of volunteers, the district has provided for the                        #"
            coordination of a volunteer program by establishing a position of
            Coordinator of Volunteers in Portland Schools. The superintendent or
            designee shall have responsibility for developing detailed procedures for
