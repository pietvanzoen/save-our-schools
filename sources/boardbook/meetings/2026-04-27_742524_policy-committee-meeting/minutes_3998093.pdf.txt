                  Policy Committee Meeting


                                Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Policy Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at
Roosevelt High School , 6941 N Central St, Portland, OR 97203and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Committee Members: Directors Michelle DePass (Committee Chair), Stephanie Engelsman, Nora Isles,
Eesa Taylor

Other Attendees: Sharon Toncray - Chief Legal Officer; Emily Courtnage - Senior Legal Counsel;
Rosanne Powell - Senior Board Manager


Opening

Committee Chair DePass called the meeting to order at 4:46 pm.

Call to Order / Introductions

Committee members and staff introduced themselves.

Public Comment

There was no public comment.

1.70.020-P Policies and Administrative Directives

The Committee recommended moving the Policy to the full Board for a First Reading with the addition of
language that explains the change from word ‘shall’ to ‘will’.

Review of 7.00.000 Policies - Community

Stephanie shared her concerns about removing language about the requirements for volunteer
background check while the section 5.00.000 policies have not been updated, and made a motion to
amend: 7.20.020-P Volunteers to add the note that contingent moving this to the appropriate place in the
5:00.000 at a later date.

The Committee recommended additional language to the 7.10.010-P School-Site Councils to encourage
student membership on Site Councils. There was also a discussion to add baCk language that requires
an annual evaluation to the District Foundation Policy.

Review of 2.00.000 Policies - District Administration

There was a request to wait to discuss this section until the next meeting in order to include each
Committee member. There were questions about the recommendation to remove reporting requirements
from the 2.10.010-P Racial Educational Equity Policy

The Committee reviewed the recommended revisions to the 2.50.020-P Student Education Records
Policy and decided to add back the word “federal” which had been removed from the draft redline version.

1.60.040-P District Performance Auditing

There were questions about the process for making changes to the District Performance Auditing Policy.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

The Committee expressed interest in further discussion on the Foundation Policy, as well as clean air and
screen usage policies.

Adjourn

Committee Chair DePass adjourned the meeting at 5:48 pm.
