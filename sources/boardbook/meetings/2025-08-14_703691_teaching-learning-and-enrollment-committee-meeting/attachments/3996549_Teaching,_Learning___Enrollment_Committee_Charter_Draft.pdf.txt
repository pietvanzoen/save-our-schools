Draft Teaching, Learning & Enrollment Committee Charter


Purpose

The Teaching, Learning & Enrollment Committee will provide focused review and recommendations on
matters that directly impact student learning programs, including program quality, equitable access to
educational opportunities, and enrollment planning.


Committee work can include:


               Review proposed curriculum changes prior to consideration by the full Board.


               Review enrollment and school boundary recommendations prior to consideration by the
               full Board.


               Review topics and initiatives related to the Board Goals and Post-Secondary Readiness,
               engaging deeper learning on such topics.


               Review education-related state grants.


               Receive updates and monitor the district’s charter schools, as required by statute.


               Receive updates and monitor Special Education programs, as required by statute.


               Discuss other topics aligned with the Superintendent and Board priorities.


The Committee operates in compliance with PPS policy and applicable public meeting laws.
