Welcome and Introduction

The meeting was called to order at 4:47 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller. Attendees
introduced themselves.

Public Comment

None

Committee Purpose and Desired Outcomes

Time: 4:50 pm

Committee Chair Chase-Miller provided a summary of her background and interests in teaching and
learning, and the general purpose of the committee.

Committee Charter

Time: 4:52 pm

Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller introduced the draft charter and walked through it line by line with
committee members, who provided feedback on the language and scope of the committee’s work. It was
emphasized that the charter is still a draft and would not be approved at this meeting.

Discussion focused on clarifying the committee’s purpose and role, including its involvement in curriculum
oversight, enrollment and boundary processes, and accountability for board goals. Members
acknowledged the importance of maintaining flexibility to amend the charter, ensuring decisions are
reviewed through an equity lens, and distinguishing between equity and equality in resource allocation.

There was also interest in the committee receiving updates on charter schools, grants, legislative matters,
the Talented and Gifted (TAG) program, Special Education, and the Continuous Improvement Plan.

Outdoor School

Time: 5:45 pm

The Committee reviewed a draft resolution directing the superintendent to analyze the total cost of
outdoor school before and after the passage of Measure 99 and to provide financial options for preserving
a five-day program this year. It was noted that some members of the legislature have expressed they
didn’t believe they had cut funding, and that specific details regarding the funding and costs will be
needed for state-level advocacy.

Concerns were raised about covering costs within the district’s deficit budget, as any reductions would
likely affect student programs. Members emphasized the importance of Outdoor, explored the possibility
of creative fundraising, and requested clarity on whether costs increased after Measure 99. There was
discussion regarding whether a resolution was needed.

Staff will revise the draft Resolution based on discussion feedback. The Committee referred the revised
draft Resolution to the Board for consideration.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 6:24 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller. The next meeting is
scheduled for September 18, 2025.
