Center for Black Student Excellence

Time: 4:58 pm

Staff: Joanne Evenson and Liz Large

Staff provided an update on the due diligence progress for the purchase of the Center for Black Student
Excellence (CBSE) building and the next steps, noting that there were no material concerns found in the
survey nor the title report, and that staff is working on creating mitigation plans for any areas that may
need them. Staff provided an overview of the initial review of operating costs, and next steps. Committee
members asked questions, with topics including the road that goes between the two buildings and rental
income.

Athletics

Time: 5:08 pm

Staff: Joanna Evenson

Staff provided an update on athletics capital projects, including the development of athletic hubs at
Marshall High School and Jackson Middle School and planned improvements at several high schools,
such as seating upgrades, field work, and lighting. Committee members asked questions about project
timelines for Grant Bowl lighting installations. Staff provided an overview of the steps that need to be
taken and vendor constraints. There was a request for more detailed athletic project information, with staff
noting that a dashboard is being developed to provide more timely updates as schedules change.

Ida B. Wells-Barnett High School Revised Master Plan

Time: 5:20 pm

Stefee Knudsen – Bora Architects

Ms. Knudsen provided an overview of the Ida B Wells modernization budget summary, noting that the
costs estimates have come in within the 1.5% range of hard costs after value engineering. The
comprehensive plan will go to the Board in November. Committee members asked questions, including
about the green energy tech costs, which are primarily for solar panels

Health and Safety Work Orders

Time: 5:27 pm

Staff: Frank Leavitt

Staff provided a summary of the district’s maintenance operations, including staffing and scheduling,
open work orders, and the maintenance priority matrix. Committee members asked questions, with topics
including how to address low level maintenance needs.

Seismic Report Update

Time: 5:44 pm

Staff: Dr. Jon Franco

Staff provided an update on completed and ongoing seismic work, outlined plans for future
improvements, and reviewed the prioritization formula that will inform a forthcoming recommendation to
the Board. Staff noted plans to apply for a seismic improvement grant in November and shared that, while
earlier starts were explored, current timelines point to two major sites beginning work in 2027, with some
seismic upgrades being completed alongside current roofing projects through a hybrid approach of full
retrofits and targeted improvements. Committee members asked questions about the request for proposal
timeline, anticipated summer projects, and how seismic planning aligns with broader facility planning and
right-sizing efforts.

Status Updates

Time: 5:57 pm

Staff: Dr. Jon Franco and Deborah Kafoury

Staff provided an update on several initiatives. Staff are working to ensure that they consider lessons
learned in previous enrollment rebalancing initiatives to apply to the upcoming School Right Sizing
project, noting that timing matters. Requests for Proposals are out for seismic upgrade projects. Staff
plans to meet with representatives from the Ida B Wells Farmers Market to address safety concerns,
potential delays, and cost impacts.

Other Business

Chair Wang requested that each committee add 10 minutes at the end of the meeting to talk about future
agenda topics. The committee shared ideas for future topics, including a community lift project which
would coordinate with outside partners with small maintenance projects that need to be completed

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 6:14pm by committee chair La Forte.
