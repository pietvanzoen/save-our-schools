Maintenance Priority Matrix
• Emergency – Immediate threat to safety, health, or building operations same day after
  immediate triage (e.g., boiler failure in winter, major water leak).

• High – Impacts safety, instructional delivery, or critical services if not addressed soon
  and preventative maintenance.

• Medium – Important, but does not immediately threaten safety or instruction.

• Low – Cosmetic or discretionary requests; lowest impact on students/staff.
