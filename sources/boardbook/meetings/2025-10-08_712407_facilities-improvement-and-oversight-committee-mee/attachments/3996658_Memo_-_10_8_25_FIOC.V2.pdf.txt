IDA B WELLS
During the last FIOC meeting on September 16, the Ida B Wells High School project team reported a
$5.6M cost savings, which remains unchanged in this report. Since then, the team has received and
reconciled two professional cost estimates, one from the CM/GC and another from a third-party cost
estimator. The initial reconciled estimate showed the project was $24.8M over the hard cost target of
$345M. However, following a successful value engineering process, the overage has been
significantly reduced to $4M, which is considered well within industry standards at the 100%
Schematic Design phase.

This marks a critical milestone, providing a clear and attainable Pathway to Budget as the team
concludes a Design Phase Approval and transitions into the Design Development phase. The team
remains focused on identifying further cost-saving opportunities throughout Design Development and
through ongoing collaboration with the CM/GC on construction logistics planning.

ATHLETICS
The PPS Program Vision for District Athletics is described in the PPS Long Range Facility Plan
(LRFP). The high priority athletic improvements identified in the LRFP include three athletic hubs
and improvements at four middle schools. The high priority program supports for the middle schools
include a multipurpose turf field with a track, an appropriately sized main gym and an auxiliary gym.
The high priority program support for high schools includes access to turf baseball and softball fields.

Grant High School (Lights and New Seating)

The Grant Bowl Lighting Project is progressing, with Musco LED lights procured through the KCDA
cooperative purchasing program. Land Use review was approved in 2024, and the current focus is
securing City permits and coordinating new electrical service with Pacific Power. The project remains on
track contingent on timely utility coordination. This work is essential to meeting PPS’s Title IX
commitments and will provide Grant High School and the community with equitable, safe, and
energy-efficient athletic facilities.


Jackson Middle School (Fields, Lights)

The Jackson Middle School project is progressing with Land Use approval completed in 2024. The fields
will support Ida B Wells activities during their Modernization project and will ultimately become a hub to
support district wide athletics. The Wells Modernization team is reviewing options for expedited
implementation to align with student program needs. More detailed construction cost and permit
application schedule information are currently in development.
