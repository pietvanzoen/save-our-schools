​
Committee Chair Invited Comment: Safe Indoor Air Quality for Oregon Schools - McDaniel High
School Air Quality Report

Dr. Effie Greathouse (Safe Indoor Air for Oregon Schools) presented an independent air quality report
using airflow data. She highlighted the variation in air purifier usage across the district and recommended
expanded deployment and required use of existing purifier inventory.

Air Quality Standards, Modernization Design, and Deployment

Brad Wilson and Corey Squire presented on indoor air quality design standards for the Cleveland, Ida B.
Wells, and Jefferson modernization projects, including target CO2 levels, MERV-13 filtration, and
operable windows in every classroom. Joanna Tobin reviewed the district's air purifier deployment history
and current inventory, noting over 6,000 units delivered since 2020 and approximately 2,500 units
remaining in storage.

Board members had questions about school-level education on purifier use, the distinction between
particulate filtration and CO2/VOC reduction, window functionality and maintenance in older buildings,

Portland Clean Energy Fund (PCEF) Grant Award and Projects

Staff provided an update on two PCEF grants: the PCEF Strategic Program 16 Climate Friendly Public
Schools (SP16), and the Collaborating for Climate Action grant, covering student-led climate projects,
HVAC and infrastructure upgrades, and green schoolyard initiatives.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

There was a request to follow up on outstanding questions regarding purifier deployment communication
and air quality monitoring processes for a future meeting.

Adjourn

Committee Chair La Forte adjourned the meeting at 5:37 pm.
