Subject: ​     Resolution Reaffirming the Appointment of a Deputy Clerk



Staff recommend adopting a resolution reaffirming the appointment of a Deputy Clerk for the
2025-26 fiscal year and enumerating, in detail, the role and authority of the Deputy Clerk. The
Board previously appointed Michele Morrison as the Deputy Clerk for the 2025-26 fiscal year on
July 22, 2025. That resolution did not, however, enumerate the role and authority of the Deputy
Clerk. The Oregon statute that authorizes an appointment of a deputy clerk, ORS 332.515, also
does not enumerate the role or authority of a deputy clerk.

In the course of negotiating real property or other legal transactions, the District is occasionally
asked to provide evidence of the Deputy Clerk’s legal authority to execute legal documents on
behalf of the District. It will be helpful for the Board to specifically enumerate the role and
authority of the Deputy Clerk to provide necessary proof of the Deputy Clerk’s legal authority.

For these reasons, we recommend adoption of Resolution 7242.
