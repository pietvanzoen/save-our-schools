                                          RESOLUTION No. 7242

                                  A Resolution Appointing a Deputy Clerk

                                                 RECITALS

A.​   ORS 332.515 requires the board of directors of a school district to appoint the chief administrative
      officer of the district as the district school clerk and authorizes the board to appoint a qualified
      deputy clerk or clerks to perform the functions of the school clerk.

B.​   Given the volume of records, contracts, and other legal documents that must be managed and
      executed on behalf of the District each year, the District Board of Education (the “Board”) deems it
      advisable to appoint a deputy clerk to assist the Superintendent.

C.​   On July 22, 2025, the Board appointed Chief Financial Officer Michelle Morrison as Deputy Clerk of
      Portland Public Schools for the 2025-26 fiscal year.

D.​   The appointment resolution, Resolution No. 7144, did not enumerate, in detail, the role and
      authority of the Deputy Clerk.

E.​   Neither statute nor policy enumerates the role and authority of a deputy clerk.

F.​   The Board therefore deems it advisable to clarify the deputy clerk’s role and authority in the
      appointment resolution.

                                                  RESOLVED

1.​   The Board reaffirms the appointment of Chief Financial Officer Michelle Morrison as Deputy Clerk of
      Portland Public Schools for the 2025-26 fiscal year.

2.​   The Deputy Clerk is empowered to exercise the authority of the Superintendent to execute all legal
      documents on behalf of the District, including without limitation contracts, grant agreements,
      transactional documents relating to the acquisition or disposition of an interest in real property, and
      any other legal document that the Superintendent has authority to execute on behalf of the District
      under statute, Board policy, administrative regulation, Board resolution, or as otherwise authorized
      by the Board or Oregon law.
