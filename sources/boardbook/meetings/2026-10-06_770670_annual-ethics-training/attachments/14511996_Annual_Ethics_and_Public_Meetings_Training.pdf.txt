Oregon       Gifts

Ethics Law      • May not accept gift with value of $50.01 or more
                     from a source with a legislative or administrative
                     interest

                • Gift – something of economic value not offered on
                     the same terms as to the general public

                • Many exceptions – from food or beverage at
                     reception with broad attendance to honorariums
                     for professional engagement unrelated to official
                     position
