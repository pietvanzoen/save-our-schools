SUBJECT:        Financial Update for the Month Ended April 30, 2026


This Financial Update is an overview of Portland Public School’s financial operations for the
Month Ended April 30, 2026.
This report provides an overview of financial operations of District funds for the period of July 1,
2025 through April 30, 2026. Amounts reported are from the financial records of the District and
are unaudited.


As you review the April 2026 Financial Schedules, please keep the following in mind:

         The “Annual Forecast” column for each fund includes known activity through April and
          projected revenues and expenditures based on historic annual averages, current year
          trends, as well as co-created projections with operational departments.
         Projections are updated monthly in consultation with operational departments and are
          estimated plus or minus 10%.
         State School Fund revenue updates from the Oregon Department of Education are
          received throughout the year and the State does not close a fiscal year until the following
          May. As such, revenue projection updates occur on a more frequent basis during the
          spring when prior year reconciliations impact current year revenue collections.
         The District operates on a modified accrual basis of accounting, which means most
          revenues and expenses are recorded as received and expended.
         Beginning balances reflect audited numbers and all final adjustments have been
          recorded based on the completed Fiscal Year 2025 financial report.
         This information is utilizing the best information available at this time.
       Additional reports are included with this report to provide additional context and a high
          level summary of the financial update.
