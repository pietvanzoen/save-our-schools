Call to Order and Introductions

Committee Chair Chase-Miller called the meeting to order at 4:36 pm.

Public Comment

    ●​ Beth Cavanaugh - Expressed concerns about the impact of Senate Bill (SB) on electives.
    ●​ Erika Freundlich - Provided comments on the Social Studies Curriculum that is being considered
       for adoption and the reference to the history of Israel, and urged the Committee to consider
       McGraw Hill as an alternative.
    ●​ Katharine Young- A member of the curriculum selection committee would like to have clear
       content searchability prior to the final adoptions.

Annual Charter School Report

Korinna Wolfe and Sunita Sandoz presented the Annual Charter School Report and shared that Kairos
PDX is on an Improvement Plan due to low performance. They shared that Kairos now has new
leadership and this should help them stabilize. There were questions about their staffing, Special
Education Services, and what statutory requirements the sponsor district needs to follow. There were
questions about the pass-through rates for state funding.

Interim Assessment Tool Selection Update

Renard Adams presented an update on the District's selection of a new interim assessment tool,
Curriculum Associates' iReady. Committee members raised questions about family outreach for feedback,
the difference between norm- and standards-based reporting, the definition of computer-adaptive testing,
and the distinction between achievement and growth measures. Members also expressed concern about
the added instructional and screen time required with the assessment, and stated a need to continue
monitoring OSAS outcomes.

6-12 Social Studies Instructional Resource Recommendation

Filip Hristic presented the recommendation resulting from the District's 6-12 social studies curriculum
adoption process. Staff recommend TCI for grades 6-8, based on strong student and teacher feedback,
and a combination of Savvas/National Geographic and TCI for grades 9-12. There were questions about
whether the curriculum adequately covers current events, and a request for more detailed high school
teacher feedback data. The Committee recommended forwarding the curriculum to the full Board.

Artificial Intelligence in Portland Public Schools

Loretta Benjamin Samuels, Melissa Lim, Russel Adamson, and Kristen Moon, presented a progress
update on the District's AI planning work, which covered the District's AI readiness assessment, an AI
vision statement developed with parent and educator input, a living "AI guidebook," and the decision to
adopt Google Gemini as the District's governed AI tool. They also shared results of a new student AI
perception survey conducted with Panorama.

Committee members raised questions about the "profile of an AI-ready graduate" framework, the
appropriateness of AI tools for elementary-age students, and the low response rate to the survey. There
was also an expressed desire to slow down until staff and educators feel ready. Committee members
asked that future updates to the Board include input from the Climate Crisis Response Committee and
consideration of related policy updates.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

Committee members were asked to email Chair Chase-Miller with topics for future meetings.

Adjourn

Committee Chair Chase Miller adjourned the meeting at 6:48 pm.
