PPS Interim Assessment Choice
i-Ready meets all PPS preferred feature categories
(see previous slide) and aligns with the following
stakeholder feedback:

• Have a single assessment across all grades (K-8)
  and subjects (reading and mathematics).

• Have a system with robust reporting features that
  includes both achievement and growth.

• Keep the primary focus on the usefulness of the
  assessment results to inform instruction and
  individual student learning.
