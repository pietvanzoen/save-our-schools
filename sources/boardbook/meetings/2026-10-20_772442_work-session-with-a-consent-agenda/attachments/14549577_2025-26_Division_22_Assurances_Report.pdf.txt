Administration of State Assessments
• Added SEED Survey as a required OSAS component

  a new requirement)

New TAG Requirements for the 2025-26 SY
(OAR 581-022-2325)
• Any person may recommend a student to be screened for TAG identification
   during the school year
• A definition of “screen” was added to the rule:

   “Screen” means to use district policies and procedures that have the purpose of
   identifying which students are eligible to receive talented and gifted services,
   supports, or programs.
Preserving Student Instructional Time
(OAR 581-022-2323 and Executive Order 26-06)
• Districts that reduced student instructional time levels during the 2025-26 or
    2026-27 school years must submit to ODE either:
     • a plan to restore student instructional time by the 2027-2028 school year, or
     •   evidence of the district school board approving the decision to not restore
         student instructional time and a public posting of the justification for that
         decision
•   A district may not reduce student instructional time during the 2026-2027 school
    year.
