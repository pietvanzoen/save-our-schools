Background: Annually, this report is provided to the Board to review status on meeting the Division 22
Required Instructional Minutes (581-022-2320) for the prior school year. The requirements for
instructional time in grades K-12 are: Grade 12 — 966 hours; Grades 9–11 — 990 hours; and Grades K–8
— 900 hours, with allowable exemptions.

The state-provided exemptions include:

(6) Upon approval by the local school board, a district may include in its calculation of instructional time
required by subsection (1) of this rule the following:
        (a) For kindergarten programs offering 900 hours or more of instructional time, up to 60 hours of
        recess;
        (b) For kindergarten programs offering less than 900 hours of instructional time, up to 30 hours
        of recess;
        (c) For grades 1–3, up to 60 hours of recess;
        (d) Up to 30 hours for staff professional development;
        (e) Up to 30 hours for parent teacher conferences; and
        (f) For the 2015–16 school year, up to 14 hours for emergency school closures due to adverse
        weather conditions and facilities failure.

There are essentially two key components to calculating the Required Instructional Time with the
additional consideration of state-provided exemptions.

    1.​ Each school district shall ensure that at least 92% of all students in the district and at least 80%
        of all students (who are not exempted from the calculation) at each school operated by the
        district are scheduled to receive annually the following hours of instructional time. This part of
        the rule primarily applies to the scheduling of high school students.
        (A) Grade 12 — 966 hours;
        (B) Grades 9–11 — 990 hours; and
        (C) Grades K–8 — 900 hours.
    2.​ Review of actual instructional time achieved during the school year per grade level.
    3.​ Review of and determination of exemptions.

Clarification on Senior Schedules and Board Approval

A senior’s schedule that includes less than a full school day does not, by itself, require individual Board
approval. Under OAR 581-022-2320(2)(a), Board approval is required when the district requests an
instructional time exemption for eligible students. These exemptions allow students to be excluded from
the calculations used to determine whether the district meets state instructional time requirements. The
exemption process requires annual Board approval following a public hearing.

Under PPS’s current practice, seniors who are not fully scheduled are reported to the Oregon
Department of Education as “not fully scheduled.” These students remain included in the district’s
instructional time calculations. Because no exemption is being requested for these students, the
approval and public hearing requirements under this provision do not apply.

This distinction concerns when formal Board action is required. The Board retains its oversight role in
reviewing senior scheduling practices, students’ access to instruction, and compliance with state
requirements. Any request to exclude eligible students from instructional time calculations through this
exemption process would require Board approval following a public hearing.
