                                         RESOLUTION NO. 7390

    Seismic Construction Projects: Exemption from Competitive Bidding and Authorization for Use of
                              Requests for Proposals Contracting Method

                                                RECITALS

A.​ The Board of Directors of Portland Public Schools ("District") is the Local Public Contract Review
    Board ("Board") pursuant to ORS 279A.060.

B.​ ORS 279C.335(2) authorizes the Board to exempt certain public contracts or classes of contracts
    from the standard competitive bidding process otherwise required by the Public Contracting Code and
    Rules upon certain findings.

C. In a duly and legally held election on May 20, 2025, general obligation bonds were approved by a
    majority of the qualified voters of Portland Public Schools voting at the election (“2025 Capital
    Improvement Bonds”).

D.​ The District intends to perform seismic upgrades at five schools: Beverly Cleary – Fernwood,
    Richmond, Capitol Hill, Ainsworth, and Kelly as part of the 2025 Capital Improvement Bond work.
    Capitol Hill and Richmond Roofs also include funding from the 2017 approved bond.

E.​ Staff has determined that use of the Request for Proposal (“RFP”) alternative contracting method is
    the preferred method of delivery for these critical seismic projects. This determination is supported by
    draft Findings of Fact ("Draft Findings") presented to the Board pursuant to ORS 279C.335.

F.​ These Draft Findings specify the advantages gained through use of the RFP contracting method.

G. On September 18, 2026, the District issued a public notice in the Daily Journal of Commerce
   announcing the District’s intent to utilize the RFP alternative contracting method for the specified
   seismic projects. The notice was issued in compliance with ORS 279C.335 and the PPS Public
   Contracting Rules. The Draft Findings were made available for public review on the date of
   publication. The notice included instructions for requesting a public hearing on the request for
   exemption from competitive bidding.

H.​ The District received no requests for a public hearing.

I. ​ The Superintendent recommends approval of the exemption from Competitive Bidding and approval
     of the RFP alternative contracting method for solicitation and completion of the five seismic projects
     named above.

    1.​ The Board hereby adopts the Draft Findings in support of use of the RFP alternative contracting
        method for the specified seismic projects (“Findings”).

    2.​ The Board hereby exempts the specified seismic projects from competitive bidding requirements
        as provided in ORS 279C.335 and PPS Public Contracting Rules -49-0600 through
        PPS-49-0690. The exemption is based upon the Findings pursuant to ORS 279C.335(2).

    3.​ Pursuant to these Findings and decision, the Superintendent or her designee is hereby
        authorized to conduct a RFP alternative contracting process for the specified seismic projects.
