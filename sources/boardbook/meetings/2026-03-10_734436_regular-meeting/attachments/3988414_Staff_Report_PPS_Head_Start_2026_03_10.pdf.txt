1. PPS Head Start 2023 – 2025 OPK Amended Budget Workbook

As you know, our district currently faces rising costs and limited funding across our system. In
order to balance the Early Learners Department budget while maintaining preschool access to
the same number of children and families, PPS Head Start requests board approval of our
Federal Head Start Change in Scope Application. The Executive Summary here.

The Change in Scope process – managed by the Office of Head Start – allows programs to adjust
program funding without Congressional approval. It is a necessary step in PPS Head Start’s
budget balancing for SY26-27. The process allows us to request to serve fewer children while
retaining the same federal funding to increase our per pupil funding rate. The justification for
this need is higher staffing costs, changes in community need, and the flat funding through our
federal Head Start grant. Reducing the number of Head Start seats will allow our program to
better match funding with actual enrollment, operating costs, and personnel costs to ensure
long-term program stability. Following the approval of the Change in Scope, our program will
have the opportunity to increase preschool seats from other grantors in order to continue to
serve the same number of students and families in our community. No currently enrolled
children or families will be affected by this plan, and the change will take place for the 2026–27
school year. The Parent Policy Council approved the Head Start Change in Scope as of
February 2026. Board approval is required. A Board Letter of Approval and Resolution are
requested.

2. 2025 – 2026 PPS Head Start Program Self-Assessment Timeline
The annual Self-Assessment is a central tenet of continuous quality improvement for Head Start
programs. The goal of the Self-Assessment is to meet Head Start Program Performance
Standards and move toward program excellence in serving children and families. The process
provides programs the means to regularly assess their own management systems and program
operations in order to continually strengthen the program and the services and supports
delivered to children and families. The Parent Policy Council approved the Self-Assessment
Timeline in December 2025. A Board Letter of Approval and Resolution are requested.

3. Eligibility, Recruitment, Selection, Enrollment, and Attendance (ERSEA) Selection Criteria
for SY26-27
PPS Early Learners enrolls students in Head Start according to Section 645 of the Head Start Act.
Federal Poverty Guidelines are used to determine income eligibility for participation in Head
Start programs. The poverty guidelines are adjusted for families of different sizes and may be
used to determine financial eligibility for certain federal programs such as Head Start services.
The guidelines are issued each year in the Federal Register, and as such, the program must
provide an annual training to the Parent Policy Council and board on their ERSEA procedures
and Enrollment Selection Criteria for the following school year. The Parent Policy Council
received training and approved the SY26-27 Enrollment Selection Criteria in February 2025.
Board approval is required. A Board Letter of Approval and Resolution are requested.
4. Updated PPS Head Start Policy & Procedures

       1.A. Child Abuse & Neglect – PPS Head Start has revised Child Abuse & Neglect policy
       and procedures in accordance with the Head Start Program Performance Standard 45
       CFR §1302.90(c) which mandates that staff protect children's safety and well-being. The
       procedure reflects Early Learner’s current mandated reporter practices, partnership
       with ODHS, and staff expectations for need-to-know communication. The Parent Policy
       Council approved these revised procedures in January 2026. Board approval of new
       policies/procedures is required. A Board Letter of Approval and Resolution are
       requested.

       1.B. Transportation – PPS Head Start has revised the Transportation policy and
       procedures to align the Head Start Program Performance § 1303 Transportation. The
       revisions to this policy clarified the responsibilities of the PPS Early Learners Department
       and PPS Transportation Department, as well as procedures for McKinney-Vento
       transportation referrals. PPS Head Start Parent Policy Council approved these revised
       procedures on December 11, 2024. Board approval of new policies/procedures is
       required. A Board Letter of Approval and Resolution are requested.

CONNECTION TO BOARD GOALS
The goal of PPS Head Start is to prepare the city’s diverse and resilient young children for
successful transitions to kindergarten and beyond by leveraging each child’s strengths, building
their social competence, and developing school readiness skills.

In June 2022, the PPS School Board established a new set of goals. “It remains the core mission
and responsibility of our school system to ensure that every student has opportunities to thrive
and experience success. We are dedicated to the continuous improvements necessary to drive
the system shifts that will better support our students, educators, and leaders and result in
improved student performance.” The Board adopted the following four priorities for the 2022-
2027 time period to move the school district toward the above aspirations:

   ●   Improve Third Grade Reading achievement
   ●   Improve Fifth Grade Mathematics achievement
   ●   Improve Eighth Grade Readiness
   ●   Improve High School Graduation rates

The above items support the district’s and School Board’s vision and priorities.
