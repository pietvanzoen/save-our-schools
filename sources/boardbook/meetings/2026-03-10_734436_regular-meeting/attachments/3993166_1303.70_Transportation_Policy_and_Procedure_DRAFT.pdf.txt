 § 1303 Transportation.
  (a) Applicability. This rule applies to all agencies, including those that provide transportation services, with the
  exceptions and exclusions provided in this section, regardless of whether such transportation is provided directly on
  agency owned or leased vehicles or through arrangement with a private or public transportation provider.

  (b) Providing transportation services.
     (1) If a program does not provide transportation services, either for all or a portion of the children, it must provide
     reasonable assistance, such as information about public transit availability, to the families of such children to arrange
     transportation to and from its activities, and provide information about these transportation options in recruitment
     announcements.
  AF (2) A program that provides transportation services must make reasonable efforts to coordinate transportation
     resources with other human services agencies in its community in order to control costs and to improve the quality
     and the availability of transportation services.
     (3) A program that provides transportation services must ensure all accidents involving vehicles that transport
     children are reported in accordance with applicable state requirements.
  (c) Waiver.

Purpose
To ensure all standards are met, when applicable, and children are transported safely when in the care
of PPS Early Learners.

Policy
Portland Public Schools (PPS) Early Learners does not provide transportation services. Federal Head
Start safety standards for preschool transportation are different from the requirements followed by the

Procedure
In some cases, children who qualify under the McKinney-Vento Homeless Assistance Act may be
eligible for transportation provided by the PPS Student Transportation Department. When this occurs,
families may choose to use district transportation services. In this instance, caregivers will sign an
Authorized Release form giving permission for Transportation department staff to sign their children
into the care of PPS Early Learners staff.




    T
When Early Learner staff receive notification that a child has qualified for transportation support
through McKinney Vento, the following steps will be taken to support communication between
departments.
    ●​ McKinney Vento team will submit the transportation request for enrolled students who qualify,
  AF   including Early Learner (EL) Program Manager so qualifying children can be tracked
       appropriately.
    ●​ Transportation Department will process the request, including EL Program Manager on all
       update notifications.
    ●​ EL Program Manager will notify Family Advocate and Pod Administrative Assistant at the site
       that the child will be starting transportation and they will get an update once it is approved.
    ●​ Family Advocate will complete the authorized release form with the parent/caregiver and
       scan/email the signed form to the EL Program Manager to upload in ChildPlus.
    ●​ Once final approval notification is sent, EL Program Manager will notify Family Advocate and
       Pod Administrative Assistant. The Transportation Department will communicate the information
