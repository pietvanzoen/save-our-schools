Office of Head Start – West Region

March 10, 2026

Subject: Head Start Change in Scope


The Portland Public Schools Board of Education approves the submission of a Change in Scope
request to the Office of Head Start to adjust funded enrollment for the PPS Head Start program
effective September 2026. This action supports alignment of program design with current
community demand, workforce capacity, and available resources.

The Board affirms that the proposed changes are data-informed and intended to maintain high-
quality services for children and families while ensuring long-term program sustainability.
