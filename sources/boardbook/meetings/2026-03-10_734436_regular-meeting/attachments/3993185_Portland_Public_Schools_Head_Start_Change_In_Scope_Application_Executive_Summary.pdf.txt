                         PPS Head Start Change in Scope Executive Summary

●​ What is changing: Portland Public Schools Head Start plans to reduce the number of federally funded Head Start
   slots by 90, from 297 to 207, beginning in September 2026 for the 2026–27 school year.

    The program proposes 90 slots through the following calculation:

        ○​ 65 slots currently filled by over income families;

        ○​ 20 unfilled slots;




    T
        ○​ 5+ for waitlist variance.

●​ Why this change is needed: The adjustment reflects higher staffing costs, changes in community need, and the
   amount of funding available through the federal Head Start grant.

●​ Community trends: Fewer families now qualify for Head Start based on income, and more publicly funded early
  AF
   learning options are available across the community. Population shifts, including lower birth rates and rising
   housing costs, have also reduced demand.

●​ Enrollment efforts: PPS Head Start has successfully met enrollment requirements; however, waitlists have
   remained small and, at times in recent years, insufficient to remain fully enrolled every month.

●​ Financial sustainability: Rising personnel costs with the current level of funding cannot sustainably support the
   existing number of slots. A Change in Scope is a means to increase program funding allocations per slot (revenue
   increase).

●​ How this change helps: Reducing the number of slots allows the program to better match funding with actual
 R
   enrollment, operating costs and personnel costs to ensure long-term program stability.

●​ Impact on families: No currently enrolled children or families will be affected. The change will take place for
   the 2026–27 school year.

●​ Program quality: Classroom models, schedules, and services will remain the same. PPS Head Start will continue
D

   providing high-quality, comprehensive services to children and families.

●​ Looking ahead: This change supports staff stability and ensures PPS Head Start can continue serving families
   effectively throughout the remainder of the grant period.

●​ Tendencias de la comunidad: Ahora, menos familias califican para Head Start según sus ingresos, y hay más

   listas de espera se han mantenido pequeñas y, a veces en los últimos años, insuficientes para mantener la
   inscripción completa cada mes.
