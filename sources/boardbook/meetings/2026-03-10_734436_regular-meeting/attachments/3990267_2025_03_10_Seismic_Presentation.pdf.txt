Resolution 7109
Resolution 7109 establishes the criteria and intent for prioritizing seismic
retrofits. It contemplates that all high-risk schools will not be addressed within a
single bond cycle. Specifically, it directs Portland Public Schools (PPS) to use
current seismic assessment data to identify and prioritize schools that pose the
greatest risk of injury or death in a significant earthquake, and to clearly
communicate the criteria used to determine the sequence of retrofits.
We acknowledge that discussion of a significant earthquake can cause concern
and anxiety. We affirm at all times that we are focused on ensuring the safety of
all of our children and staff in our buildings and are working to devise a plan to
prioritize the work across our many buildings to do that.
