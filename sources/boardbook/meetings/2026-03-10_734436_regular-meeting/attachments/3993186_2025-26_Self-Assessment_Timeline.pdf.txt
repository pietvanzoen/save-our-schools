What is the purpose of the Self–Assessment Committee?
The self-assessment is conducted once each year and helps Head Start leaders understand the
effectiveness of program operations. The process provides the mechanism for ensuring
programs are doing the right things, as well as to identify and make necessary course
corrections. Overall, the self assessment uses data to monitor progress toward program goals,
compliance with the program requirements, and effectiveness in promoting school readiness.

Who is on the Self-Assessment Committee?
The committee is made up of program Management Team, staff from all classifications, and
caregivers. This grouping allows for several perspectives as data is presented and reviewed.
