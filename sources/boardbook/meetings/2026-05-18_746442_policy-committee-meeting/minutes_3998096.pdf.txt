                  Policy Committee Meeting


                                        Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Policy Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at
the Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center, 501 N. Dixon Street, Portland, OR 97227 and streamed live
at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Committee Members: Directors Michelle DePass (Committee Chair), Rashelle Chase-Miller, Nora Isles
(District Student Representative)

Other Attendees: Patte Sullivan - Board Member; Emily Courtnage - Senior Legal Counsel; Rosanne
Powell - Senior Board Manager


Opening

Committee Chair DePass called the meeting to order at 4:34 pm.

Public Comment

    ●​   Chris Schweizer - Shared concerns about the need for an AI Policy.
    ●​   Holly Hofkamp - Shared concerns about harmful effects of screen usage in classrooms.
    ●​   Amanda Uerlings - Shared support for restriction of screen usage in classrooms.
    ●​   Allison Wall - Shared concerns about the impacts of education technology on children.
    ●​   Jason Honl - Shared support for varsity baseball at Jefferson.



Review of 2.00.000 Policies - District Administration

The Committee referred the policies to the full Board for consideration at the June 9th board meeting.

Screen Time in Schools

Chair DePass introduced the topic and shared that more discussion would be forthcoming, committing to
getting input from across various stakeholders. She stated she has submitted an initial draft to the Legal
Department for review and she would also like to collaborate with the Teaching and Learning Department.

Revisions to the District Student Council Operating Bylaws - Committee Review

This was postponed to a future Committee meeting.

District performance Auditing

The Committee agreed to schedule this for a full board discussion when looking at all the 1.00.000
policies to review.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

No topics were raised.

Adjourn

Committee Chair DePass adjourned the meeting at 5:03 pm.
