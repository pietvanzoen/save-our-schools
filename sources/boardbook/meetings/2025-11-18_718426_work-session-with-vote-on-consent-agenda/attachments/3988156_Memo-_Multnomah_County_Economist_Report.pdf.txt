                                          MEMORANDUM
DATE​ ​        November 18, 2025

FROM​ ​        Michelle Morrison, Chief Financial Officer
CC ​   ​       Dr. Kimberlee Armstrong​        ​        ​
SUBJECT​       Property Taxes and the PPS Local Option Levy presented by Jeff Renfro, Multnomah ​
​              County Economist

Portland Public Schools is fortunate to have a strong partnership with Multnomah County. One of the
benefits of this partnership includes access to and voluntary support from the Multnomah County
Economist, Jeff Renfro.

At the meeting this evening, Economist Renfro will be sharing information regarding the PPS Local
Option Levy Imposed Tax History, residential values, property tax mechanism, downtown office values
and regional inflation.

Multnomah County is home to many other districts and is the main local revenue source for Portland
Public Schools. This information is provided to give Directors and the community a greater
understanding of resources that support students in PPS schools.
