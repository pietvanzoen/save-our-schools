WHAT IS AN “ERP”
• An Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system is an integrated software platform that manages core
  business processes— inance, human resources, procurement, payroll, operations, and inventory—
  within a single, centralized system.
• The goal of an ERP is to streamline operations, improve data accuracy, and enable better decision-
  making by providing real-time visibility across all departments.
• The ERP is the digital backbone —connecting people, processes, and data - to drive smarter, faster,
  and more e icient operations.
• Our goal is to replace fragmented legacy systems and spreadsheets with a uni ied platform that:
   •    Automates work lows, streamlines review and approval cycles, and reduces manual e ort
   •    Provides a single source of truth across the district - inancial, operational, student, employee data
   •    Supports compliance, security, reporting, and analytics
   •    Scales with organizational growth and evolving business needs

PHASE ONE: DATA PLATFORM
1. Purchase a cloud data warehouse ($300K for 4 years)
   • Simplify data management and accelerate analytics and reporting
   • Secure, scalable, fully managed service from an industry product leader
   • Store, integrate, and analyze data across all PPS district functions
   • Complies with all government requirements for personal data security
   • Used by Oregon DoE and Multnomah County along with thousands of PK-12 school districts
2. Professional services to support data migrations, user transitions and platform optimization ($136K)
   • Ensure data is migrated onto the new platform safely and securely
   • Validate all integrations are working as designed
   • Identify PPS applications and integrations that can be deprecated
   • Build and deploy best practices to optimize usage for the long-term
   • Implement system controls to ensure usage does not exceed purchased capacity

APPENDIX
 BRIEF HISTORY
• 2020 - Bond passage for Tech Modernization includes ERP modernization
• 2021 - Gartner assessed readiness and built recommendations for a cloud-based ERP
• 2022 - 2023 - RFP planning, response reviews, and vendor selection process (no decision)
• 1st half of 2024
  • TekSystems began process documentation for HR and Finance operations
  • Data Strategy & Governance recommendations developed but ultimately paused
• 2nd half of 2024 - 1st half of 2025
  • Multiple key leadership and program changes
  • Statement of work from AST to implement Oracle ERP (ultimately declined)
  • 2025 Bond measure for continued Tech Modernization
    REASONS TO BELIEVE
    • Leveraging prior assessments and recommendations - the prior
      work is highly valuable and is being used for planning

    • Simpli ied program governance with multiple levels of support and
      leadership across Finance, HR, RAA, and Ops

    • Emphasis on process standardization and elevated change control

    • Implementing data management best practices in parallel

    • Dedicated Senior Director with experience in multi-year
      transformational change and large scale implementations

    • Priority focus on change management and consistent data quality

    • Funding from 2020 and 2025 bond measures
