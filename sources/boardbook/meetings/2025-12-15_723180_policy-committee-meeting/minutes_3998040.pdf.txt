Called to Order/Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 4:31 pm by Committee Chair Michelle DePass; attendees introduced
themselves.

Policies for Potential Review

Time: 4:34 pm

Vice-Chair DePass stated that the Committee would like to review all Board Policies to identify those
requiring revision. Staff recommended reviewing policies by section, noting that policies within each
section are related, and suggested beginning with Sections One and Seven. Staff further noted that they
would review the selected sections and bring forward recommendations on which policies should be
considered for revision. The Committee discussed the recommendation and agreed to begin with section
one, with section seven to follow. Due to their related nature, staff will review both sections concurrently.
