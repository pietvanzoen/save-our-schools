Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR)

Time: 5:08 pm

Staff: Michelle Morrison
Talbot Korvola & Warwick, LLP (TKW): Dan Miley

The external auditor from Talbot Korvola & Warwick, LLP (TKW) presented the results of the Annual
Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR) audit, outlining the audit process, relevant federal and
accounting standards, and issuing an unmodified opinion, indicating that the district’s financial statements
fairly represent its financial position. The audit identified several procedural findings; however, it was
noted that none of the findings involved loss or misuse of funds. Staff have submitted a corrective action
plan.

Committee members asked clarifying questions regarding the findings, long-term implications, internal
controls, and future reporting timelines. Staff explained that the issues were related to internal processes,
that corrective measures and strengthened oversight systems have been implemented, and that the

findings are not expected to affect the district’s bond sales. The Committee agreed that they are in
support of the draft ACFR acceptance resolution that is posted with the February 10, 2026 Regular
Meeting materials.

Multilingual Learners / English Language Development - Compliance Audit Report Presentation

Time: 6:21 pm

Staff: Janise Hansen and Mary Catherine Moore

The Senior Internal Auditor presented an overview of the Multilingual Learners audit, highlighting findings
from the executive summary that identified opportunities to strengthen internal controls and improve
processes, while noting that no instances of fraud or negligence were found. Management agreed with all
nine recommendations, and staff will return at a future meeting to present an implementation plan.

Committee members provided comments. It was noted that the report is now considered final and the
report is included in the supplemental materials for the next Regular Meeting.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

A list of future agenda topics was shared with the Committee. Staff requested the committee provide
feedback on the agenda directly to the Office of Internal Performance Audit.
