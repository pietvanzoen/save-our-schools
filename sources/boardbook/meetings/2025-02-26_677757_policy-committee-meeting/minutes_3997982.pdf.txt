Update on Access to Advanced Placement Courses

Time: 4:38 pm

Staff: Chris Brida and Dr. Filip Hristić

Director Sullivan provided a summary of her concerns regarding 9th-grade student access to Advanced
Placement (AP) classes. Staff shared that they completed a review of AP practices, noting their findings
on alignment and inconsistencies across the district. Staff’s next steps include stakeholder engagement,
potential revisions to the Administrative Directive, improved student supports, and clearer communication
for eligible families. The anticipated timeline is to complete the work by the end of the year to inform
course planning. The discussion also touched on efforts to expand equitable access to Career and
Technical Education (CTE) programs, with a question about whether a policy exists to support this vision.

Draft Revisions to Policy 5.10.066-P Ethics and Conflict of Interest Policy - Potential revision
pertaining to the district employment of Board Members. Potential Action

Time: 5:04 pm

Committee Chair Brim-Edwards provided a summary of the proposed policy changes, highlighting
changes that were made based on the Committee’s discussion at the previous meetings. There was
discussion regarding whether the policy should include hiring restrictions. It was noted that the proposed
revisions put in place guardrails in case a situation comes up. The policy was referred to the board for a
first reading, with Director DePass voting no, stating she would raise her questions with the full board.

4.10.020-P Compulsory Enrollment: Age and Grade Level at Entrance - Discussion, Potential
Action

Time: 5:19 pm

Staff: Dr. Emily Glasgow and Judy Brennan

The committee reviewed the recent history of early entry to kindergarten policy changes and discussed
proposed revisions. The discussion focused on whether an exception process should exist for students
with birthdays after September 1. It was noted that September 1 is a state-defined date, however not a
requirement. There was discussion regarding public preschool and kindergarten options available to
students who are not 5 by the cut-off date. Considerations included staff capacity for reviewing exception
requests and the importance of clear messaging. Staff will gather additional data and revisit the topic at a
future meeting.

Draft Proposed New Policy x.xx.xxx-P: Solicitation Registration and Reporting - Discussion only

Time: 5:56 pm

Committee Chair Brim-Edwards introduced the proposed new policy on solicitation registration and
reporting, which is aimed at establishing transparency through high-level guardrails for staff to then create
detailed protocols. Committee members should consider the policy and submit questions to the
Committee Chair for future discussions. Initial questions were considered, including defining thresholds
for reporting, minimizing administrative burden, clarifying language for existing district contractors,
determining how the policy applies to nonprofits and schools, and specifying what constitutes
"engagement" under the policy.

Adjourn
