               PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
               Board of Education
               Monday, January 13, 2025

               Policy Committee Meeting



                                Informal Meeting Minutes
Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
public meeting notice mailing list, a Policy Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of
Education was held at Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center - Board Auditorium, 501 N. Dixon St,
Portland, OR 97227 and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance:

Director Julia Brim-Edwards:    Present

Director Michelle DePass        Present

Director Patte Sullivan:        Present



Call to Order / Introductions

Committee Chair Julia Brim-Edwards called the meeting to order at 4:51 pm.



Public Comment - 5 Two-Minute slots

None



Draft Revisions to Policy 1.20.010-P Board of Education - Board vacancy revision to align with
statue - Discussion Only

4:52 pm

Staff: Liz Large

Staff provided an overview of the proposed changes to Policy 1.20.010-P Board of Education, noting that
the revisions would bring the policy into alignment with the current statutes. The policy will be brought
back to the Committee at a future meeting for consideration and potential referral to the full Board.



Draft Revisions to Policy 5.10.066-P Ethics and Conflict of Interest Policy - Potential revision
pertaining to the district employment of board members. Discussion only

4:54 pm

Director Brim-Edwards introduced the policy, noting that the proposed language revisions are regarding
board member employment with the District after their term has ended. The Committee discussed the
potential revisions. Discussion included exceptions for re-employment of former district employees, such
as certified teaching positions, the perception of a potential power imbalance when board members apply
for positions while serving, potential legal considerations. Differing views on the necessity of a waiting
period versus prohibiting applying or lobbying while actively on the board were expressed. The policy will
be brought back to the Committee for further consideration.
Draft Proposed New Policy: Lobbying Registration and Reporting - Discussion only

5:39 pm

Director Brim-Edwards introduced the idea of a new policy on lobbying registration and reporting, noting
that a draft policy is not yet available. The Committee discussed the concept, including examples of
situations and the need for such as policy. There was a request for concrete problems that need to be
solved by the policy. Director Brim-Edwards requested that staff prepare a draft policy for consideration.



Draft Revision to Policy 1.20.010-P Board of Education - Consideration of zone voting for board
members - Discussion only

5:23 pm

Director Brim-Edwards provided a summary of current board election practices, proposing that the Board
consider zone voting for elections. The discussion focused on topics including the impact of city-wide
elections on board diversity, the potential for zoned elections to reduce barriers for prospective
candidates, logistical challenges of implementation due to differing boundaries of board zones and other
electoral zones, the lack of distinct demographic clustering in neighborhoods in Portland, the financial
burdens of city-wide elections, and the broader representation responsibilities of at-large board members.
Director Brim-Edwards will bring data to a future meeting.



Other Business

5:46 pm

Director Patte Sullivan requested that the Committee review the policy regarding Advanced Placement
classes at an upcoming meeting.



Adjourn

Committee Chair Julia Brim-Edwards adjourned the meeting at 5:51 pm.
