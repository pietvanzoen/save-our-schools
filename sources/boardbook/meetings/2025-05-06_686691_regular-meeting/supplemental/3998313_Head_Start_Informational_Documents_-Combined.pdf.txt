1. Service Goals
In Section I.B of the grant agreement, service goals are outlined for each program
component. Considering service data through December 31st, is the program on
track to meet the service goals (number of participants to be served and amount of
service to be offered) for each program component? If the program is not on track to
meet annual goals, explain why that is the case.

Our program is working to meet the 2024-25 service goals. The average enrollment rate across
all four classrooms is 83%. In our two Spanish Dual-Language classrooms, our current enrollment
rate is 87% (31/36 students). In our two Neuroeducation classrooms, our current enrollment rate
is 81% (29/36). The average program attendance rate is 83.3%. Family engagement
(parent/child) is on track.

2. Implementation
Describe any implementation issues experienced during the first six months of the
reporting period. What modifications (e.g. curriculum, service location, times,
outreach, staffing model, staff training) have been made or are planned to be made
to address these issues?

An implementation challenge this year has been the shift from site-based enrollment to
central-enrollment. As with any system change, there has been growing pains; however, at this
point in the school year we are quickly filling all classrooms. We have identified gaps within the
new system and addressed those. Moving forward we will continue to assess the system prior to
Spring enrollment for the next school year.

The program has retained 4 coaches to increase curricular implementation and staff
training/coaching. We are implementing Tools of the Mind (TOTM) and launched Heggerty in all
PreK classrooms this year, including Spanish Heggerty in our DLI classrooms. Finding sufficient
time for staff training staff is a consistent challenge. We’ve been able to offer extended training
sessions at Preservice and Winter Institute, and monthly curricula training on Early Release
Wednesdays. We offered a specific Neuroeducation and Spanish DLI training at the institute.
