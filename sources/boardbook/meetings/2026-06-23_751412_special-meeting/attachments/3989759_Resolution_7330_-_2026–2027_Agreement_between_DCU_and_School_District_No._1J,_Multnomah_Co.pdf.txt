                                       RESOLUTION No. 7330

                     2026–2027 Agreement between District Council of Unions and
                          School District No. 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon

                                              RECITALS

The 2026-2027 Collective Bargaining Agreement between School District No. 1J, Multnomah
County, Oregon (Portland Public Schools) and the District Council of Unions (DCU) expired on
December 31, 2025.

                                            RESOLUTION

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education (Board) adopts and approves the 2026–2027 Collective
Bargaining Agreement between the Portland Public Schools and DCU, attached hereto as Exhibit A. The
Superintendent or designee is authorized to make technical corrections to the Agreement in order to
correct typographical and spelling errors, make formatting changes, or renumber provisions, so long as
the intended meaning of the Agreement is not thereby altered.
