                                    Budget Amendment No. ___

                                 Removal of Discretionary Travel
                                 Submitted by Director La Forte

I move to amend the FY 2026-27 Proposed Budget to implement a one-year freeze on discretionary
travel for the Board of Education and Portland Public Schools (PPS) Leadership, as defined in the
attached Proposed 2026-27 PPS Leadership Organization Chart. Please remove all travel expenditures
that are not legally required, grant-funded, or fully reimbursed by an external funding source.

The resulting budget savings shall be reallocated to classroom-based FTE positions.
