                                           Resolution No. ____


                                     Budget Amendment (Engelsman)
                                                 RECITALS


    A.​ WHEREAS, Portland Public Schools (PPS) is committed to student success and equitable
        access to ensure every student can reach their full potential; and


    B.​ WHEREAS, the Board of Education has established specific priorities of 3rd Grade Reading, 5th
        Grade Math, 8th Grade Readiness and High School Graduation; and


    C.​ WHEREAS, PPS is committed to providing rigorous, high-quality academic learning experiences
        that are inclusive and joyful, while disrupting racial inequities to create vibrant environments for
        every student to demonstrate excellence; and


    D.​ WHEREAS, PPS is committed to providing safe, welcoming and healthy learning environments
        that are conducive to student success and well-being; and


    E.​ WHEREAS, PPS is committed to well-resourced sustainable schools so that every PPS student
        has equitable access to a robust and high-quality educational experience.


                                               RESOLUTION


NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Board of Education directs the Superintendent to reinstate
funding ($13,120,000) for staffing in schools in student facing positions.
