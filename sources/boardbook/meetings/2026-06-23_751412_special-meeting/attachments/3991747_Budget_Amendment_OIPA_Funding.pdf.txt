                                     Budget Amendment No. ___

                  Restoration of Office of Internal Performance Auditor Funding

I move to amend the FY 2026-27 Proposed Budget to restore funding for the Office of Internal
Performance Auditor within the Board of Education budget sufficient to maintain the Office at its FY
2025-26 staffing and operational level.

Funds restored through this amendment shall be dedicated exclusively to the Office of Internal
Performance Auditor and shall not be reallocated without prior approval of the Board of Education.
Further, no other modifications to the Board of Education budget shall be implemented without the
Board of Education's prior approval.
