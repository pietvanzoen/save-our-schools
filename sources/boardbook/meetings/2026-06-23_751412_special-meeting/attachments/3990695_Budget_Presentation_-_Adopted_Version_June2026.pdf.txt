The Central Oﬃce Roles
●   Central oﬃce positions represent the Board’s investment in school support to
    achieve the Board's priorities.
●   Central oﬃce teams develop instructional resources, provide professional
    learning, support continuous improvement efforts, and implement strategies to
    accelerate student achievement.
●   Many positions support students receiving special education services,
    interventions, multilingual learners, and other supports.
●   These teams advance equity and meet legal obligations by ensuring compliance
    with state and federal requirements.
●   Changes to these positions affect the district's capacity to support schools, build
    educator effectiveness, monitor student outcomes, and achieve the Board's
    adopted goals.
In District and Out of District Travel
●   Over 95% of “In District Travel” expenses are for Special Education staff, IT
    technicians, and student service workers, and other support staff who are driving
    between our campus buildings to deliver direct support to kids.
●   “Out of District Travel” is distributed across departments and includes the
    Superintendent and School Board. This discretionary area has been restricted
    during the spending freeze.
●   During non-restricted spending periods, “Out of District” funds provide
    professional development and networking opportunities for recruitment, legal
    updates, certiﬁcation, compliance training, student transportation for athletics,
    and safety training.
Contract Services Increase
●   The increase to contract costs are in the Capital Project funds as we begin 3 high
    school modernization projects and other bond projects.
●   There are also signiﬁcant increases in the general fund to student transportation
    and other property services- areas that were under budgeted in 2025-26.
●   Some services are either itinerant or require specialized training, equipment and
    warranty.
●   Budget holders continue to evaluate contract services as they are invoiced and
    paid to ensure best value.
