                                              Resolution No. ________

     Establishing a Temporary Pause on the Use and Expansion of Generative Artificial Intelligence Systems
                    Pending Development of Board-Approved Policy and Oversight Framework




                                                     RECITALS

    A.​ WHEREAS, Portland Public Schools (“PPS”) has not yet drafted and adopted a Board Policy governing
        the use and approval of generative artificial intelligence (“GenAI”);

    B.​ WHEREAS, GenAI technologies present significant risks related to academic integrity, intellectual
        property, data privacy, and compliance with federal and state law; and

    C.​ WHEREAS, PPS has a responsibility to protect personally identifiable student data, employee data,
        and other confidential information; and

    D.​ WHEREAS, the Board finds that a deliberate and transparent review process is necessary before the
        PPS expands use of GenAI technologies or enters into additional contractual commitments related to
        such technologies.




                                                   RESOLUTION:

​
For purposes of this Resolution, the following definitions apply:

Generative AI: Artificial Intelligence systems, software, and tools that generate text, code, images, audio,
video, or other content in response to prompts or other use input. Example: Lumi AI (students input text
and the software creates images based on student text input).

Agentic (non-generative) AI: Software and tools with Autonomous AI systems that do not just provide information,
but can orchestrate multi-step actions and execute tasks on a user's behalf. Example: Synergy (uses information
uploaded or entered into the software to create reports).

The Board directs the Superintendent to:

     1) ​ Pause the execution of any new contracts, contract amendments, subscriptions, pilot programs, software
    licenses, or procurement actions primarily related to GenAI technologies;

    2) ​ Pause the expansion of existing GenAI technology implementations beyond their current scope of work;

    3) ​ Prepare an inventory of existing student-facing GenAI technology implementations for the Board.

    4) ​ Work to amend all existing educational technology contracts and data-sharing agreements to prohibit
    third-party vendors from collecting or sharing personally identifiable student data unless expressly required
    by law.


    5) ​ Provide, within 90 days of adoption of this Resolution, a comprehensive districtwide assessment of all AI
        systems, contracts, expenditures, pilot programs, data-sharing agreements, risk mitigation measures, and
        educational impacts; that includes, at a minimum:

        a) ​ An inventory of all GenAI tools, platforms, software products, pilots, and services currently utilized
             throughout PPS;
        b) All associated contracts, costs, funding sources, and renewal dates;
        c) A description of all student, employee, and PPS data shared with associated vendors;
        d) Recommendations regarding future implementation, limitations, or expansion of GenAI
        technologies within PPS.

This pause shall not apply to cybersecurity, accessibility, or software maintenance functions that do not materially
expand GenAI capabilities and/or are not student-interactive.

Due to significant public interest, future GenAI-related contracts shall require Board authorization,
regardless of the Board approval threshold.

The Board affirms that decisions regarding PPS adoption of GenAI technologies should be made through
a transparent public process that prioritizes student outcomes, data privacy, and educational
effectiveness.
