Background Information
 This memo provides an update to the Board of Education and Superintendent Armstrong on the Racial
 Equity and Social Justice (RESJ) Support Services RFP partnerships for the 2025–26 school year.

2025–26 Community Partner Scope of Work
 The process for tracking goals and activities was revised to create a more equitable and consistent
 approach aligned with District expectations. Community partners established goals connected to the
 District’s Continuous Improvement Plan and Board goals in four priority areas: attendance, literacy,
 mathematics, and 9th-grade success.

 Partners’ strategies have been written as theories of action and aligned with the Ideal Student
 Experience to demonstrate measurable impact and illustrate how their work supports these priority
 areas. RESJ continues to review data from the PPS dashboard to assess partner performance and
 student outcomes in attendance, academic achievement, discipline, and graduation rates for high
 school students.

Data Observations
 We are pleased to report that the majority of community partners demonstrated improvements in
 student attendance and on-track-to-graduation indicators.

 There was, however, a noticeable increase in discipline incidents due to the addition of several
 categories that had not been tracked during the previous four years. These included Minor Defiance,
 Minor Disruptive Conduct, Minor Failure to Attend Class, Minor
 Insubordination/Defiance/Disobedience, Minor Not Following Directions, Minor Personal Electronic
 Device Violations, Minor Play Fighting, and Talking Too Loudly.

 The inclusion of these categories significantly contributed to the overall increase in discipline incidents
 reported this year. The inclusion of these categories significantly contributed to the overall increase in
 discipline incidents reported this year. In response to this trend, the RESJ team will partner with
 Conduct and Discipline staff this summer to further examine this data, identify patterns that may be
 emerging across schools, and disaggregate incidents by race and other focal student groups. Using
 the District’s Equity Lens, the teams will explore potential root causes, assess whether any disparities

 to address identified needs and promote equitable outcomes for students.

Looking Ahead
 For the 2026–27 school year, RESJ staff collaborated with community partners to develop
 standardized pre- and post-program surveys to gather feedback from students and families regarding
 their experiences and the impact of partner programming. The survey data will provide valuable
 insights into participant engagement, sense of belonging, and overall program effectiveness. Findings
 from these surveys will be included in the Board memo presented in June 2027 to complement
 existing attendance, achievement, discipline, and graduation data.

 Beginning on page 3, you will find detailed information about each organization, including the
 organization name, program description, RESJ strategy(ies) being implemented, schools served, and a
 link to the community partner performance review prepared by RESJ staff.

 Community partners will also be submitting an end-of-year narrative to provide additional context and
 highlight outcomes; however, those reports are not due until June 19, 2026.
