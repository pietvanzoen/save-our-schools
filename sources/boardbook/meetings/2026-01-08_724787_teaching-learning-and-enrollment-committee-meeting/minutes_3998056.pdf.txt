Career Technical Education (CTE) / College Career Readiness

Time: 4:40 pm

Staff: Dr. Renard Adams, Christopher Brida, Yeng Dhabolt, Dr. Filip Hristić

Staff presented an overview of the district’s Career and Technical Education and College and Career
Readiness programs, highlighting program scale, expanded access to advanced coursework, growth in
student participation, and strong outcomes in graduation, credential attainment, and college credit
accumulation. The presentation emphasized increased enrollment in career pathways, rising completion
rates, expanded opportunities for dual credit, Advanced Placement, and International Baccalaureate
coursework, growth in access for historically underserved students, and the development of a nationally
                                               Page 1 of 2
unique literacy initiative embedded within CTE instruction to strengthen technical reading, writing, and
knowledge acquisition.

Board members asked questions and shared feedback related to access, student outcomes, staffing
capacity, and long-term sustainability of the programs. Discussion reflected support for the district’s
approach to preparing students for a range of postsecondary options, including college, workforce entry,
and industry pathways, while noting the importance of maintaining staffing, expanding equitable access,
and continuing to strengthen program quality and alignment.

Attendance Overview

Time: 5:13 pm

Staff: Naomi Orem and Chandra Wilson-Cooper

Staff provided an overview of districtwide attendance trends and efforts to address chronic absenteeism,
highlighting the importance of engaging instruction, positive school climate, and coordinated school-
based supports. The presentation outlined the district’s overall approach to improving attendance,
including the use of data, targeted strategies for identified student groups, and partnerships to support
students and families.

Board members asked questions and provided comments on attendance patterns, communication with
families, differences across schools and student groups, and the effectiveness of current strategies.
There was discussion regarding barriers to attendance and the need for continued focus on targeted
supports, data-informed decision making, and family engagement to improve student attendance
outcomes.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 5:48 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller.
