Call to Order / Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 4:30 pm. Board Members and staff at the dais introduced themselves.

Public Comment

Time: 4:32 pm

    ●   Elizabeth Super: Raised concerns regarding safety at Powell Park on behalf of a student athlete
        and urged for progress on the field renovations and for the presentation materials to reflect
        needed softball improvements.
    ●   Brian Kinion: Requested clarity on progress of West Sylvan athletic improvements, including

        toward modernization goals.

Jefferson High School Update
Time: 4:46 pm

Staff: Joanna Evenson, TJ Hopkins, Sarah Norman

Colas-Hoffman Representatives: Andrew Colas and Stephanie Coyle

Staff and representatives from Colas-Hoffman provided a summary of the feasibility study for Jefferson
High School, which examined the potential use of a shell option as part of the modernization project. It
was reported that while the shell option may have resulted in modest timeline savings, it was ultimately
not selected due to safety considerations and an increased cost. Staff reviewed project timelines and
capacity needs, noting that early occupancy would not be feasible, though some services may be
available sooner, and that temporary classroom spaces would be required to accommodate the
anticipated increase in student enrollment during construction. Board members asked questions and
expressed appreciation for the analysis, noting that the feasibility study provided value in informing
decision-making and identifying efficiencies.

Bond Athletic Projects Update

Time: 5:01 pm

An update was provided on athletic facilities projects, including current status’ and anticipated timelines. It
was noted that multiple projects remain in progress, with timelines influenced by planning requirements
and external coordination. Updates included ongoing work at several sites, with specific challenges such
as utility coordination, land use requirements, and continued negotiations with partners. It was also noted
that project plans remain subject to change based on these factors, and that implementation is being
addressed as each project advances.

Bond Audit—Status Update

Time: 5:17 pm

Staff: TJ Hopkins

Staff provided an update on the status of bond audit recommendations #7 and #8 from Year 2, and
recommendations #1 and #6 from Year 4, indicating that all recommendations have been fully
implemented. Information was shared regarding the implementation process, including prior work
completed before the current team, and efforts to standardize reporting practices. Board members asked
questions and requested additional materials related to evaluation tools developed through the process.

Procurement and Land Use Updates

Time: 5:26 pm

Staff: TJ Hopkins

Staff provided an update on procurement timelines and land use processes for multiple school projects,
including for the modernization of Cleveland, Ida B. Wells, and Jefferson High Schools. Recent progress
was noted, including the completion of a historic resource review for Jefferson High School, and next
steps in project planning and approvals were outlined.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

Time: 5:30 pm

Committee Chair Virginia La Forte highlighted the anticipated agenda for the next meeting of the
Committee in April, which is anticipated to include the districtwide seismic improvement plan and indoor
air quality in schools. It was noted that the topic of indoor air quality is also being considered in the Policy
Committee as part of the Climate Crisis Response Policy.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 5:31 pm by Committee Chair Virginia La Forte.
