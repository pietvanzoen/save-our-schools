Committee Protocols

Time: 4:38 pm

Committee Chair Michelle DePass highlighted the process by which Committee Members can suggest a
policy for review. She noted that public comment will always be scheduled at the top of the meeting, and
that each commenter will have a strict time limit of two minutes.

Proposed Policy Revision: Field Trips, Foreign Travel, and Other Off-campus Activities Policy
6.50.010-P

Time: 4:40 pm

Board Chair Eddie Wang provided an overview of the proposed policy revisions, noting that the current
policy is overly prescriptive and does not align with best practices. Committee members discussed
balancing Board oversight with operational efficiency, emphasizing that either way risk management
procedures and district approvals will remain in place. Suggestions included focusing Board attention on
out-of-state or overnight trips, providing annual reporting for transparency, and streamlining approval for
recurring trips. The Committee will work with staff to summarize the changes, share key elements of the
current administrative directive, and involve Risk Management and school administrators in the policy
review process.
