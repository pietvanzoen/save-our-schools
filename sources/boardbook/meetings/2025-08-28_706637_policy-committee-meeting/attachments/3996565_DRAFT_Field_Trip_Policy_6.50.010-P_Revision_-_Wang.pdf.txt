DRAFT Field Trip Policy                                        Policy 6.50.010-P


Field Trips, Foreign Travel, and Other Off-Campus
Activities
Field trips and experiences outside of the classroom provide unique opportunities and
enrichment for students. The District shall strive to make these experiences equitably
accessible and inclusive for all students. The District is responsible for overseeing the
educational value of field trips and for the safety and welfare of all students involved.



  I.   Field Trip Approval Process

          A. The District shall determine the appropriate approval process in the
             subsequent Administrative Directive and/or in the appropriate Risk
             Management procedures.

          B. Field trip and risk management approval procedures should be developed
             with consultation from school administrators. School administrators may
             also request exceptions or alternative procedures in order to account for
             special or unique circumstances.

          C. Field trips and adult supervision must be in accordance with Board Policy
             5.10.064‐P Professional Conduct between Adults and Students.



 II.   District Sponsored Activities, Non-District Sponsored Activities, and Travel Study

          A. The Administrative Directive may authorize separate requirements for
             Non-District Sponsored Activities and Travel Study Programs.

          B. The Board of Education may request an annual report of Field Trip
             activities.
