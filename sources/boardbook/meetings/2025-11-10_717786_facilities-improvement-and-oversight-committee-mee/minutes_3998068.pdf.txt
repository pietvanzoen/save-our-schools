Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the

Oversight Committee was held at the Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center (501 N Dixon St. Portland,

Call to Order and Introductions

Director DePass requested that the update on the Center for Black Student Excellence due diligence be
moved off of the agenda, and instead have it be a full Board discussion at the November 18th Work
Session, where it is on the agenda. The Committee discussed the reasons behind the topic being added
late to the agenda which resulted in inadequate notice to interested community members who would want
to attend. Committee Chair La Forte stated she would consider the request.

Public Comment

    •   Collin Harris: A parent in the southwest community who shared concerns about the safety of the
        schools in their catchment area and the proposed plan outlined in the Holmes Report.
    •   Daniel LoFaro: Reike parent who shared he has concerns for the seismic safety of schools and
        asked that the district move quickly to identify a plan to reinforce schools.
    •   Sylvan Cambier: Rose City Park parent who stated she wants to be included in planning for
        seismic upgrades.
    •   Per Olstad: Safe Structures PDX would like visibility to the formula for identifying the schools for
        seismic upgrades.
    •   Terrell Garrett: Advocated to require waste recycling into the contract language for school
        modernizations.
    •   Arielle Tozier de la Poterie: Cesar Chavez parent who would like visibility to the formula for
        identifying the schools for seismic upgrades
    •   Aryn Frazier: Executive Director for the Center for Black Excellence shared she was excited the
        due diligence period for the One North building is complete. She asked the Board to please move
        forward with the building.

Seismic Update

Jon Franco provided an update on the framework for prioritization using the hybrid approach to updating
buildings, including full and partial retrofits. Jennifer Eggers shared the formula for the weighted scoring
model. The Committee had questions about how enrollment and equity were considered. Vice-Chair
Michelle DePass asked if geological data such as liquefaction zones could be considered and Director
Rashelle Chase-Miller asked whether the seismic safety of a building will be considered when considering
consolidations. There were questions about the differences in safety between 9 and 9.9.

Facilities Maintenance Staff Analysis

Tom Odgers provided an update on staffing for maintenance positions and shared we are currently 36
short of the recommended number of custodians per square feet. He shared district comparators on work
order completion rates. There were questions about what kind of work orders fall to the bottom of the
priority list and whether there are ways to engage students or the community to be able to tackle some
projects if appropriate.

Grant Bowl Field Lighting Update

Tom Odgers shared that the permit is almost complete and will be finalized and submitted, the equipment
is ordered and is scheduled for delivery in January, and the lights will be up in March. He stated the
project is 100% bond funded, and Director La Forte stated that the community has fundraised to
contribute and it needs to be used for that purpose.

Update on $20M allocated for HVAC improvements

Tom Odgers provided an update on the Portland Clean Energy Community Benefits Fund (PCEF) funds.
He provided an overview of where the District is utilizing the funds. There were questions about the
timeline and working with the Portland Association of Teachers.

Update on the Request for Proposals for Bond Management

Liz Large shared that there were three proposals received and it was expected that a decision would be
made the following week. The next step would be contract negotiations before it goes to the Board for
approval.

Update on Center for Black Student Excellence Due Diligence

Director La Forte stated her frustration with not being able to get the Center for Black Student Excellence
on the agenda earlier and not being informed that Board Leadership had decided it should be moved to a
work session. She stated willingness to move it off of the agenda and wait until the Work Session to have
the update.

Future Agenda Topics and Next Steps

There was a request for the Committee to tour Jefferson High School and to discuss Roosevelt Facilities.

Adjournment

Director La Forte adjourned the meeting at 6:12 pm.
