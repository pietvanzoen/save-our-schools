Connect. Inspire. Enroll
Marketing and communications play a
critical role in enrollment by shaping
how families see and experience PPS.
As we work to recruit, retain, and
recover students, our strategy is
designed to both build pride in the
district and strengthen connections at
the neighborhood level.
This approach is carried out through
two complementary campaigns, one
global and one localized, each rolled out
in phases to maximize impact.
Together, these campaigns create a
uniﬁed message while meeting families
where they are.



                                            6
Campaign Theme and Concept
Global Message: "Legacies Are Made at PPS“
Celebrates the long-standing impact of a PPS education. This message
connects to pride and trust by reminding families that PPS is where
futures are shaped, talents are nurtured, and lifelong success begins,
honoring our alumni while inspiring current and future families.


                                                                               Whether a student becomes a civic leader, an artist, a
                                                                              business innovator, or a ﬁrst-generation graduate, PPS
                                                                                     plays a foundational role in their journey.




                                                           Localized Message: "Your Opportunity. Your Community.“
                                                           Focuses on neighborhood schools and the value of staying local. It emphasizes strong
                                                           relationships, community ties, and the convenience of quality education close to home, directly
                                                           addressing declining enrollment trends by encouraging families to rediscover and invest in their
                                                           local schools.

                                                               Greatness doesn’t require leaving your zip code, it’s found in the
                                                            strength, relationships, and opportunities within your own community.

                                                                                                                                                              7
Phased Approach (Global)
Phase 1: Research & Insights
 o Gather honest feedback to understand family perceptions, pain points,
    and priorities.
 o Launch newly branded PPS website as the primary marketing tool and
    voice of PPS

Phase 2: District Proﬁle Books
 o Highlight each school’s strengths and offerings.
 o Deploy to real estate agencies, businesses, wraparound service
    providers, PPS partnership meetings, career fairs, neighborhood
    associations, faith-based organizations, and community centers.

Phase 3: Digital Strategy
 o Paid social media campaigns, SEO strategy, and geo-fencing to reach
    targeted audiences.

Phase 4: Paid Media and Brand Activation Events
 ○ Targeted ad placements based on research-driven insights.
 ○ Brand activation events to bring the message to life through community
    experiences and face-to-face engagement.                                8
Phased Approach (Localized)
Phase 1: Prioritize by Data
 ○ Target top 5 lowest-enrollment zip codes (97206, 97202, 97211, 97203,
    97214).

Phase 2: School Toolkits
 ○ Launch customized marketing toolkits (brand assets, visuals, fact sheets,
    messaging) and best practice toolkits (open houses, tours, community
    engagement).
 ○ Prioritize school website launch for these speciﬁc schools as their key
    marketing platform. (Oﬃcial launch date for school sites is January 2026, high-decline sites will launch this fall)

Phase 3: Paid, Targeted Outreach
 ○ Alumni-focused social media ads tied to each school.
 ○ Targeted outdoor ads in neighborhoods surrounding schools.

Phase 4: Brand Activation & Expansion
 ○ School-level brand activation events in partnership with FACE, featuring
    alumni.
 ○ Expand to mid-level decline schools (next 5 zip codes: 97219, 97266, 97213,
    97215, 97229), replicating the same strategy until all schools are reached.                                           9

Principal Outreach Efforts
Kindergarten Connect
For our youngest learners, we start the
drive for kindergarten enrollment in the
spring.


Kindergarten ramp up is a way to make
connections with families and students
through summer meetups, play dates,
and visits to their school. At the start of
the year, parents get to meet their
teachers, see their classrooms, and make
1:1 connections that build a strong
community.
                                              14
Principal Outreach Efforts
Back to School Nights

In the fall, Back to School Nights are a
way to continue to foster relationships,
inform parents of upcoming events, and
allow them face to face time with
teachers.


Rigorous courses, student supports, and
community connections are all ways to
leverage successful back to school
nights.

                                           15
Principal Outreach Efforts
Heritage Events

Portland schools are rich with diversity,
and so each month schools celebrate
their communities, welcoming not only
current students, but future ones too, to
enjoy food, music, and culture.
As we open our schools, families can see
the many ways that PPS helps to foster
welcoming communities.




                                            16
Principal Outreach Efforts
Community Outreach

Neighborhood schools can be a fun hub
for the community, welcoming in our
current and prospective students through
fun and engaging community activities.
As we foster our community within
schools, we garner a positive reputation
and make public schools a premier place
to build strong school bonds.

Principal Outreach Efforts
Information Nights - Heading to High School
High School Fact Sheets
allow prospective students to
see what offerings each
school has to offer.
From courses, to athletics,
band, to the ways that
students are supported and
the little things that make
each school unique and
special.

Principal Outreach Efforts
Heading to High School -
Shadow Days
Shadow days are an innovative way to
get non-PPS students to experience a day
in the life at a PPS school. Helping to
disrupt myths, foster positive
interactions, and build connections as we
reconnect students to our great school
system.

Jefferson Dual
Assignment
For over a decade, Portland Public Schools
(PPS) has implemented a dual assignment
policy for Jefferson High School, allowing
students in designated zones to choose
between Jefferson and neighboring
comprehensive high schools. Resulting in:
 ● Under-enrollment at Jefferson
 ● Overcrowding at other schools
 ● Confusion for families
