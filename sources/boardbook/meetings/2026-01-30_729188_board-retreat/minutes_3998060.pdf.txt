Introductions and Stage Setting

The Retreat was called to order at 12:09 p.m. by Board Chair Eddie Wang.

Facilitators from Bombastic Communications introduced the purpose of the retreat and agenda for the
day. Board members and the superintendent participated in an icebreaker activity, and facilitators
reviewed meeting norms, emphasizing the importance of creating a safe space for discussion,
collaboration, and constructive dialogue.

Board Roles and Responsibilities

Board members and the superintendent reviewed the Oregon School Boards Association (OSBA)
Standards for Boardpersonship and reflected on current practices, areas of strength, and opportunities for
growth. Participants identified community engagement, vision-directed planning, accountability, and data-
driven improvement as areas of collective strength. There was discussion regarding how the board can
adapt its work to remain effective in the context of anticipated staffing reductions. Additional discussion
addressed governance models, approaches to consensus and conflict resolution, and the importance of
deliberation as part of effective decision-making.

Board members discussed the Science of Student Achievement article and shared reflections on how
student-outcomes-centered governance should focus on classroom experiences, student learning, and
data-informed decision-making. Participants expressed interest in revisiting and updating the district’s
strategic plan. The discussion also addressed how board agendas could better reflect a student-centered

focus while maintaining attention to facilities and bond-related priorities. Board members considered how
to balance staff workload, improve the structure and purpose of committees, clarify processes for
requesting information, and strengthen agenda-setting practices to align more closely with district goals.

Building Coherence

Facilitators introduced the Trust Matters framework, highlighting the importance of shared responsibility
and collaboration among board members. Participants briefly reflected on how board work aligns with the
five facets of trust.

Conclusion and Next Steps

Facilitators proposed several next steps, including developing clearer processes for submitting and
prioritizing information requests, reviewing committee structures and charters, strengthening
understanding of board policy, expanding participation in agenda-setting meetings, and coordinating
facilitation efforts with upcoming board self-evaluation activities.

The retreat was adjourned at 5:14 p.m. by Board Chair Eddie Wang.
