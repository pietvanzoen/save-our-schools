"Oversight Versus Overstep"
scenarios
         Is this oversight, overstepping,
       'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
1. During a board meeting, several Board Members have
questions about expenditures that were in the budget
they approved, including the purchase of new technology
for the high school. Board Members are curious about how
the money was spent, and they want to make sure that
teachers are receiving professional development so they
know how to teach with new technology.
         Is this oversight, overstepping,
       'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
2. A community member stopped you in the store because
they received an email from another Board Member sent
from the school district email account, supporting a
speciﬁc board candidate in the upcoming election. The
community member wondered if this candidate's support
was a stance the entire Board had taken since it came
from the school email account.
          Is this oversight, overstepping,
        'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
3. As the ﬁrst budget was presented to the public, the room
ﬁlls with youth and adults. It becomes clear that the
proposed cut to the baseball program is going to create
some upset folks. During the meeting, the Chair requested
the Superintendent and business manager present a
budget outlining various options other than baseball by
the next budget work session that was on the schedule for
next week.
          Is this oversight, overstepping,
        'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
4. A parent stops a Board member in the supermarket
and accuses his child's school of not providing the special
education services that his special needs child is entitled
to. The Board member suggests that this parent contact
the special education director to discuss their concerns.
          Is this oversight, overstepping,
        'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
5. Your Board just voted, and it was a split vote. You were
not on the prevailing side of the issue. When you leave the
meeting, a community member stops you and asks you
how you feel about being on the 'losing' side. You indicate
it was a horrible decision, and you will not be quiet about
your disapproval of this decision.
         Is this oversight, overstepping,
       'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
6. The Board is discussing the upcoming budget process at
a work session. A board member brings up that there is no
mention of district goals or the strategic plan in the
process. They remember that during a board training
presentation on budget, the budget should be tied to the
district's strategic plan.
          Is this oversight, overstepping,
        'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
7. A community member contacts a Board member
accusing some employees of inappropriate and illegal
conduct and demands that the Board member ﬁre those
employees immediately. The Board member indicates that
they will 'take care' of this issue as soon as they can meet
with the superintendent.
         Is this oversight, overstepping,
       'no big deal' or just not a good idea?
8. A Board member wants a detailed, comprehensive
report, including a school to school comparison, on
programs that increase achievement at the elementary
level and the data to show their effectiveness.
