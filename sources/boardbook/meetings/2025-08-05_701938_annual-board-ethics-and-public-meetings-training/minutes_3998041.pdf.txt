Annual Board Ethics and Public Meetings Training

Presenter: Mike Porter

The Meeting was called to order by Board Chair Eddie Wang.

Mr. Porter provided a training on public records and ethics laws, including public meeting requirements,
serial meeting restrictions, public records obligations, and gift and use-of-office laws. The training
emphasized the intent of the law, noting that legal requirements represent minimum standards and that
board members should err on the side of transparency when situations are unclear. Key topics included
which bodies are subject to public meeting laws, what constitutes a meeting or serial communication,
appropriate use of electronic communications and shared documents, handling of confidential records,
and limitations related to gifts and benefits. It was also noted that Oregon law requires board members to
complete an approved ethics training. Board members asked questions and discussed scenarios related
