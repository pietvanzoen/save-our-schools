                  PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
                  School District No. 1J, Multnomah County
                  Board of Education

                  April 11, 2026
                  Community Engagement Session: School Rightsizing

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Portland Public Schools Board of Education Community Engagement Session was held at
the Kellogg Middle School, 3400 SE 69th Ave, Portland, OR 97206, and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Board Members Present: Christy Splitt, Patte Sullivan, VIrginia La Forte, Stephanie Engelsman, Eddie
Wang, Student Representative Ian Ritorto

Staff: Margaret Calvert - Assistant Superintendent; Dr. Dan Carpenter - Principal


Call to Order and Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 2:07 pm.

Welcome + Grounding + Framing
Margaret Calvert opened the session and Principal Carpenter welcomed those in attendance. Board
Members and Student Representative Ritorto introduced themselves and shared what they hoped to take
away from the engagement session.
Margaret Calvert provided some context for the need to consolidate schools, including enrolment data
and trends.

Interactive Polling
Participants were asked to share what schools and grade levels they were representing, and student
graphics information

Small + Whole Group Discussion
Participants moved into small groups for discussion, and took interactive polls about their individual
experiences.

Neighborhood Gallery Walk
Participants walked to district maps placed around the room that showed each school with their
enrollment numbers and utilization rates.

Ranking the Factors
Participants were asked to rank which of the following factors were important to them: pathways,
demographics, boundaries, use of space, balanced programs, and impact. Participants had a chance to
ask clarifying questions and state what some of their priorities were.

Closing
Board Members closed with sharing what they heard from the participants in the engagement session.
Next steps on the timeline for decision and implementation, and information on additional engagement
sessions were provided.
