                                 Informal Meeting Minutes
In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Board Retreat of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at Dr. Matthew
Prophet Education Center - Board Auditorium, 501 N. Dixon St, Portland, OR 97227 and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Call to Order and Introductions

The Meeting was called to order at 9:15 am by Board Chair Eddie Wang.

Retreat

Time: 9:16 am

The Board and Superintendent participated in introductory activities and reviewed the meeting agenda.
Facilitators lead the Board and Superintendent through discussion on how to better structure Board
meeting discussions, including establishing consistent participation practices, clarifying when and how
questions should be asked, and identifying the Superintendent as the primary point of contact for
clarifying questions during a meeting. The Board agreed to consider revising their operating agreements
to reflect updated practices at a later date.

Break: 10:35 – 10:38 am

Facilitators summarized prior retreat themes and introduced concepts related to trust and governance.
Board members discussed their role in fostering public trust, balancing accountability with maintaining
confidence in the district, and identified effective meeting structures and communication practices. The
Board’s role as a bridge between the district and the community was emphasized, along with the
importance of transparency and accessibility, including considerations related to public comment and
sharing information with the public.

Lunch Break: 11:56 am – 12:17 pm

There was discussion focused on maintaining a future-oriented approach and strengthening collaboration
among Board members. Strategies were shared for staying focused on outcomes, supporting one
another’s effectiveness, and engaging in productive dialogue. There was initial discussion on improving
communication practices with staff, including clarifying expectations for information requests and aligning
processes to support efficiency and transparency.

Break 12:56 pm – 1:06 pm

The Board continued discussion on communication and information request processes, considering
structured approaches for submitting and tracking requests, including prioritization, timelines, and
expectations. Emphasis was placed on ensuring efficient responses while respecting staff capacity. Board
members also discussed coordination in responding to community communications, with a focus on
consistency, transparency, and reducing duplication of efforts.

Adjourn

The retreat was adjourned at 2:40 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.
