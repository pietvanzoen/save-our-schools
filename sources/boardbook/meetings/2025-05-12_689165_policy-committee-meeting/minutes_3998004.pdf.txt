Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Policy Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at
Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center, 501 N. Dixon St., Portland, OR 97227 and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Committee Members: Directors Julia Brim-Edwards (Committee Chair), Michelle DePass, Patte Sullivan;
and Student Representative JJ Kunsevi

District Student Council Member: Ian Ritorto

Staff: Liz Large – Contracted Senior Legal Advisor; Emily Courtnage – Director, Purchasing and
Contracting; Dr. Emily Glasgow – Senior Director, PK-5 Core Academics

Call to Order / Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 4:40 pm by Committee Chair Julia Brim-Edwards. She noted two
changes in the materials, along with a shift in the agenda order.

Public Comment

None

4.10.020-P Compulsory Enrollment: Age and Grade Level at Entrance

Time: 4:43 pm

Staff: Emily Glasgow

Committee Chair Julia Brim-Edwards stated she would not be recommending the draft policy to the Board
for further consideration due to a lack of support. She raised concerns about its current form, and
suggested focusing on improving enrollment practices. Staff shared initial efforts to make enrollment more
welcoming and inclusive, including early kindergarten outreach and a broader enrollment campaign.
There was discussion regarding creating uniform information sharing, with staff noting the difficulties in
ensuring uniform information provided to families due to the various first point of contact for families.
There was a request for a map of pre-K and Head Start sites with details about funding type for each site.

Draft Proposed New Policy x.xx.xxx-P: Solicitation Registration and Reporting

Time: 5:15 pm

Staff: Liz Large and Emily Courtnage

Staff provided an overview of the newest revisions to the policy, indicating the revised policy was posted
just before the meeting. Board members asked questions about how the policy would regulate contractor
and solicitor interactions, including the registration processes, alignment with state laws, and implications
for current and future contractors. There was discussion on how different types of purchases would be
categorized under the policy.

Draft Revisions to 1.20.012-P Student Representative and District Student Council Policy

Time: 6:05 pm

Student Representative JJ Kunsevi provided a summary of the two proposed changes to the policy which
allow for two representatives per school instead of one and an alternate, and lowering the meeting
attendance requirement for credit from 90% to 80%. It was noted that the changes were reviewed by high
school leadership staff, who raised no concerns. A request was made to add the revised policy to the May
19 board agenda for a First Reading, pending approval from the Board Chair.

    Actions:

        •      The Committee unanimously referred the policy to the full board for a First Reading.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 6:10 pm by Committee Chair Julia Brim-Edwards.
