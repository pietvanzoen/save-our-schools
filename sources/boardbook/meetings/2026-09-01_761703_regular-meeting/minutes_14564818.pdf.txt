                PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
                School District No. 1J, Multnomah County
                Board of Education

                September 1, 2026
                Regular Board Meeting



                                            Meeting Minutes

                                            Adopted 10/6/26

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Regular Board Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at
Grant High School, 2245 NE 36th Ave., Portland, OR 97212 and streamed live at:

https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance
Present:

Chair Eddie Wang; Vice-Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller; Director Michelle DePass; Director Stephanie
Englesman; Director Virginia La Forte; Director Christy Splitt; Director Patte Sullivan; Student
Representative Sandell; and Superintendent Kimberlee Armstrong.

Call to Order / Opening
Chair Wang called the meeting to order at 6:09 pm.

Superintendent's Report

Staff provided the Superintendent’s Report, sharing photos and highlights from the first week of the
2026-27 school year and announcing an Arts and Education Week celebration and proclamation reading
scheduled for September 15.

Student Representative's Report
Student Representative Sandell welcomed the start of the school year and the return of fall sports, and
acknowledged the challenges to the year ahead.

Student and Public Comment
    ●   Jody Scheer – Urged the Board to adopt a policy limiting digital device and screen use in schools.
    ●   Rosy Boyer – Asked the Board to establish and publicly share a clear, equity-centered framework
        for Rightsizing decisions before individual school closures are proposed.
    ●   Matt Landon – Raised concerns about the conduct of community bike-ride groups near PPS
        properties and requested that the District pursue trespass restrictions.
    ●   Frances Chewning – Raised concerns about the accuracy of school-size research being cited in
        the Rightsizing discussion.
    ●   Matt Coolidge – Stated concerns that the Rightsizing process has felt rushed and lacking in clear
        criteria, and asked the Board to consider enrollment-growth strategies before closing schools.
    ●   Rion Lyle – Winterhaven parent who asked the Board to look at waitlists and retention rates,
        when developing Rightsizing criteria.
    ●   James Powell – Urged the Board to keep Winterhaven open, citing its STEAM program and staff
        stability.
    ●   Kimberly Grilc – Asked the Board to preserve focus-option programs and to consider schools’ full
        educational and community value, not just building utilization.
    ●   Sam Bullick – Asked the Board to evaluate Winterhaven based on its total education rather than
        building utilization alone.
    ●   Chelsea Riordan – Asked for greater transparency about the goals and anticipated benefits of the
        Rightsizing process.

Comments from our Union Partners
Portland Association of Teachers (PAT) President Alicia Chavez raised concerns about the proposed
Superintendent contract extension in light of recent educator furloughs, workload and layoffs.

Report to the Board: Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC) Local Option Levy

Chief Financial Officer Michelle Morrison presented the Community Budget Review Committee’s annual
Local Option Levy report citing a decrease in local option levy collections which contributed to the
District’s 2025-26 budget shortfall. The Board thanked Director Sullivan for her leadership on the
Committee.

Racial Equity and Social Justice Student (RESJ) Support Service Strategy: Overview and Update
Chief Equity and Accountability Officer, Renard Adams, provided a follow-up to the RESJ partner
contracts memo shared with the Board in June. Staff described plans to strengthen partner-impact
measurement through pre- and post-service surveys aligned to each strategy, noted improvements in
attendance and on-track graduation indicators among partner-served students, and discussed an
increase in reported discipline incidents attributable largely to newly tracked minor-infraction categories.
Board members asked clarifying questions regarding partner evaluation criteria and accountability.

Break from 7:14 pm - 7:56 pm.

Update: Rightsizing
Chief of Operations Jon Franco and Executive Director of School Planning and Modernization Margaret
Calvert provided an update on Rightsizing, and asked the Board for direction on the magnitude of
scenarios that staff should continue to develop.

Board members offered a range of perspectives on magnitude: several Directors expressed support for a
more comprehensive approach intended to stabilize the system for a longer period, while Director Splitt
favored a more targeted, incremental approach, and Director La Forte asked to see a fuller range of
options. Director Englesman stated she did not yet have sufficient information – particularly a cost-impact
analysis – to weigh in on magnitude, and asked that such analysis be provided before any vote. Staff
indicated that community engagement, including school site visits, will continue through the fall and into
January, with a further work session scheduled for September 15.

Superintendent Contract Extension (Resolution 7371)

Public Comment

    ●   Tiffany Koyama Lane - raised concerns about the timing of the proposed increase given recent
        educator furloughs and layoffs
    ●   Angela Bonilla - shared concerns about Superintendent contract extension
    ●   Maya Pueo von Geldern - spoke in support of the extension, citing comparable superintendent
        salaries in other districts and the costs and disruption associated with a superintendent search.
Actions:

    ●   Director DePass moved, and Vice-Chair Chase-Miller seconded, adoption of Resolution 7371,
        Resolution to Authorize Superintendent Contract Extension. The motion was put to a vote and
        passed (5 yes – 2 no – 0 abstain).

        Chase-Miller: Yes; DePass: Yes; Engelsman: No; La Forte: No; Split: Yes; Sullivan: Yes; Wang:
        Yes.

Consent Agenda: Resolutions 7367 through 7370, and 7372 through 7376
Four resolutions – relating to school reading-intervention results and three Catapult Learning West
contracts – were initially pulled from the Consent Agenda for separate consideration pending additional
information from staff. After Chief Academic Officer Glasgow confirmed that the outstanding information
had been resolved, all four items were returned to the Consent Agenda prior to the vote.

Actions:

Director Splitt moved, and Director DePass seconded, the motion to adopt the Consent Agenda:
Resolutions 7367 through 7370 and 7372 through 7376. No members of the public signed up to comment
on the Consent Agenda. The motion was put to a vote and unanimously passed (7 yes – 0 no – 0
abstain).

Note: Because Resolutions 7372 through 7375 were returned to and adopted as part of the Consent
Agenda, separate votes on those resolutions (Agenda Items 12 through 15) were not conducted.

Other Business, Committee Referrals, and Future Agenda Items
    ●   Chair Wang will circulate a request for committee liaison assignment preferences for the coming
        year.
    ●   Director Englesman raised ongoing community concerns regarding bird strikes at McDaniel High
        School and requested a future update from staff.
    ●   Director Englesman suggested that information currently tracked in the Board’s internal question
        tracker be made available to the public where appropriate, and asked to discuss the pros and
        cons of doing so at a future meeting.
    ●   Director Englesman requested a full-Board update on the bond program; staff confirmed this
        update is scheduled for the September 15 Board meeting.
    ●   Director Englesman indicated she plans to circulate a draft resolution regarding a review of the
        District’s management structure and span of control, for discussion at a future meeting.

Adjourn
The meeting was adjourned at approximately 10:36 pm by Chair Wang.

                                              RECITALS

   A. Oregon law authorizes the Board of Education to employ a superintendent of schools, fix the
      terms and conditions of employment, and compensate for a contract term that does not exceed
      three years.

   B. Board Policy 1.10.010-P provides that the Board recruits and hires a superintendent as chief
      executive and evaluates the performance of the superintendent in accordance with the District's
      adopted mission, core values, and strategic objectives.

   C. Board Policy 1.20.011-P provides for the duties of the Board chair, including signing instruments
      as the Board may authorize.

   D. The Board of Education has determined that the District is well-served by extending her contract
      through June 30, 2029.

                                            RESOLUTION

Superintendent Armstrong’s contract through June 30, 2029, on the terms set forth in the amended
employment agreement.
