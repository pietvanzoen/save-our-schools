                                      RESOLUTION No. 7345

                                  Appointment of Financial Auditor

                                             RECITALS

   A.​ To comply with the requirements of Oregon Revised Statutes (ORS), the Portland Public Schools
       Board of Education shall appoint a financial auditor for the 2026-27 fiscal year.
   B.​ ORS 328.456, 327.137, 297.405 require the appointment of a financial auditor for the School
       District.

                                            RESOLVED

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education appoints Talbot Korvola & Warwick (TKW) to serve as
the financial auditor for the 2026-27 fiscal year.
