                                  Informal Meeting Minutes

Attendance

Chair Eddie Wang; Vice-Chair Michelle DePass; Directors Rashelle Chase-Miller, Stephanie Engelsman,
Virginia La Forte, Christy Splitt, and Patte Sullivan.

Student Representative Ian Ritorto: Absent

2025-2026 School Board and Superintendent Work Focus Areas

The meeting was called to order at 8:24 pm by Chair Wang.

Chair Eddie Wang opened the discussion by highlighting the focus areas identified by board members,
including creating a teaching, learning, and enrollment committee and strengthening trust and
communication with school communities. A draft work plan was introduced as a starting point for
discussion, with the expectation that it will be revised as needed.

Superintendent Armstrong outlined her priority areas, which included progress on the Albina projects,
boundary adjustments (particularly in the Jefferson cluster), strategic budget reductions, preparation and
alignment in bargaining, construction transparency and cost efficiency, alignment of goals in the District
Improvement Plan, and a culture of excellence in service across departments.

Board Members asked questions about the superintendent’s focus areas and offered feedback on the
draft work plan. Discussion topics included informational budget sessions, balancing workload with
capacity, focusing on academic achievement, the distinction between board and district work and whether
the Board goals should be reviewed. There was also discussion on the Board Protocols. It was noted that
the Board Protocols will be revisited at the upcoming retreat. Staff will incorporate feedback into an
updated draft work plan.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 9:32 pm by Board Chair Wang.
