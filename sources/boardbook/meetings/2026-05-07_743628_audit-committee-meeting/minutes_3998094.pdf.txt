                  Audit Committee Meeting


                                 Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, the Audit Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at
Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center , 501 N. Dixon Street, Portland, OR 97227 and streamed live at:
https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Committee Members:
Directors Sullivan (Committee Chair), Engelsman, and Splitt; Jennifer Samuels - Community Member;
Eben Cole - District Student Council Representative.

Other attendees;
Director La Forte; Sara Norman - Executive Director - Procedeo; Lien Luu - Sjoberg Evashenk
Consulting; Cathy Brady - Sjoberg Evashenk Consulting; Michelle Morrison - Chief Financial Officer;
Andrea Schiers - Legal Counsel; Mary Catherine Moore - Performance Auditor; Janise Hansen - Senior
Performance Auditor; Nuthathai Nicolaescu - Controller; Rosanne Powell - Senior Board Manager.

Call to Order / Introduction

Committee Chair Sullivan called the meeting to order at 5:03 pm.

Public Comment

There was no public comment.

Annual Bond Performance Audit

Cathy Brady provided an update on the Bond Performance Audit. She will have an internal draft report to
staff and an exit conference before providing a final report. She stated she cannot share the draft until it
is final.

Sarah Norman provided an update on the status of the implementation of the Bond Audit
recommendations. There was discussion about the status of implementation recommendations and
Committee oversight, open audit audit recommendations, and the accuracy of the materials provided by
the Internal Performance Auditor. She also provided an update on the RFP for the bond performance
auditor and reminded the Committee that the scoring committee is determined by Purchasing and
Contracting.

Student Body Funds Audit - Status of the Implementation of Recommendations

Staff provided an update on the implementation recommendations. There was discussion on staff training
and how a new Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system will have an impact.

The Committee agreed to keep its work plan. The Internal Performance Auditor shared a proposal to
change the Policy which requires the Board to approve an annual Audit Plan, but to instead shift it to
three years and to produce two audits per three year period. She suggested a risk assessment, an
increase in audit staff, and oversight of external audit functions. There were questions about her
requested Policy changes, and the timeline for revisions.

Discuss Audit Committee membership

The Committee approved the recommendation to re-appoint Caroline Zavitkovskias and Jennifer Samuels
as Community Members on the Committee.

OIPA Activities Status Update

Janise Hansen provided an update on the 2022-23 Audit Plan, which the Board approved in April of 2023.
They are wrapping the field work portion of the M98 Audit, but have not begun work on the CTE or School
Building Security Audits.

Michelle Morrison shared she would be sending information on the 2025-26 Supplemental Budget to
Adjust Appropriations .

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

There was a request to have a discussion on the process for evaluating the external auditor.

Adjourn

Committee Chair Sullivan adjourned the meeting at 7:08 pm.
