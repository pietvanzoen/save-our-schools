                                 Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Audit Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at Dr.
Matthew Prophet Education Center - Board Auditorium, 501 N. Dixon St, Portland, OR 97227 and
streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Call to Order / Introduction

The meeting was called to order at 5:03 pm by Committee Chair Patte Sullivan. She summarized the
meeting agenda and attendees introduced themselves.

Public Comment

Time: 5:05 pm

    ●   Lisa Jackson: Emphasized the importance of mental health, along with the importance of audits,
        a need for greater appreciation and support, and recognition of Renard Adams for their excellent
        work.

Status of the implementation of the Hardship Petition Transfer Audit recommendations

Time: 5:09 pm

Staff: Judy Brennan and Janise Hansen

Staff provided an overview of the remaining audit recommendations, with updates shared on progress
toward implementation. It was noted that while work is underway, some items were not yet ready to be
marked as complete. Board members asked questions related to data tracking, enrollment transfer
processes, timelines, and privacy considerations. Staff acknowledged that current capacity limits follow-
up in some areas.

Status of the implementation of the Contracts Audit recommendations

Time: 5:31 pm

Staff: Dr. Renard Adams and Michelle Morrison

Staff presented an update on the implementation of contracts audit recommendations, including
background information and details on progress made towards implementation. It was noted that most
audit components were complete, with ongoing development of systems to better measure the impact
and alignment of services. Plans were shared to implement revised processes alongside upcoming
contract renewals, and it was noted that annual reporting would continue. Questions and feedback were
provided, with acknowledgment that full approval of new systems would occur once development is
complete.

Discussion and recommendation to approve the 2026/2027 budget for the Office of Internal
Performance Audit

Time: 5:48 pm

Staff: Janise Hansen and Michelle Morrison

Staff shared the proposed budget for the Office of Internal Performance Audit, detailing some of the
specific budgetary needs, such as training requirements. Departmental benchmarking data was shared. It
was noted that the final approval of the budget would be done by the full Board as part of the broader
budget approval process. Board members expressed general support for the proposed budget.

Status Update on Approved Audits and Discussion of Proposed Audit Plan

Time: 6:06 pm

Staff: Janise Hansen

A status update on current internal audits was provided, including timelines for completion and plans for
upcoming work. Staff shared that audits on the approved plan that have not yet begun are being reviewed
for potential scope revisions, with any proposed changes to be brought back to the Committee for review.
It was noted that three additional topics are being researched and will be brought back to a future meeting
to determine which two would be moved forward.

Status update on the 2025-2026 Audit Committee Work Plan and Proposed Meeting Agenda

Time: 6:13 pm

The committee reviewed upcoming meeting plans and discussed scheduling adjustments, expressing
support for the next meeting to start earlier to accommodate a longer meeting agenda. Future agenda
topics were outlined, including follow-up on prior audit recommendations, and potential coordination with
additional staff and committees.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 6:16 pm by Committee Chair Patte Sullivan.
