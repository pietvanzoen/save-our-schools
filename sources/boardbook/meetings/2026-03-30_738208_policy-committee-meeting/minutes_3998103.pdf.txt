Vice-chair DePass noted that the revised Purchasing and Contracting Policy received its first reading on
March 10, 2026. No feedback has been received to date, and public comment remains open. The Board

Review of 7.00.000 Community Policies:

Committee Chair Michelle DePass noted that originally the Committee was going to look at the 1.00.000
level policies, but as there is interest in reviewing the 1.00.000 level policies as the full board, the
committee will move to the 7.00.000 level policies. Staff shared that the proposed revisions primarily
modernize the policy language, noting that they have reviewed comparable policies at neighboring school
districts and identified differences.

It was noted that the Districtwide Advocacy and Fundraising Policy 7.10.020-P will not be reviewed except
for the language modernization since it’s been revised recently.

7.10.010-P School-Site Councils

Time: 4:49 pm

The Committee reviewed proposed policy revisions. Consideration was given to adding a value statement
supporting student participation in school-site councils, as well as clarifying the use of “will” in place of
“shall,” with a recommendation to address this clarification within the Policy on Policies. It was noted that
further revisions, including proposed student language, would be brought forward for future consideration.

7.10.030-P District Foundation

Time: 5:07 pm

There was a request for a Foundation’s Articles of Incorporation and a memo summarizing what happens
when a Foundation is resolved.

7.10.040-P Parental and Family Involvement in Support of Academic Achievement

Time: 5:12 pm

Staff recommended the Parental and Family Involvement in Support of Academic Achievement
7.10.040-P Policy for recession, noting that the policy is old and all relevant information is included the
District Site-council Policy. Committee members expressed support for the recommendation and agreed
to the policy to the Board for rescission.

7.20.020-P Volunteers

Time: 5:13 pm

Committee Chair DePass summarized the proposed changes to Policy 7.20.020-P Volunteers. There was
a question about what kinds of background checks are required, noting conflicting information in this
policy and Policy 5.10.140. Staff will review the language in question and provide more information at an
upcoming meeting.

7.20.040-P Citizen - Community Sponsored Contests

Time: 5:25 pm

Staff recommended the recession of 7.20.040-P Citizen - Community Sponsored Contests, noting that it
is out of data and the purpose of the policy is unclear. Committee okay with moving forward with the
recession.

DePass summarized that the Committee supported moving all but the Policy 7.10.010-P School-Site
Councils and 7.20.020-P Volunteers.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items
