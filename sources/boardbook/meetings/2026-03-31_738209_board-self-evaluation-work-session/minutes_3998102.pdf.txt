Call to Order and Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 5:04 pm. Chair Wang introduced the work session and the facilitator.
Board members, Superintendent and Chief of Staff introduced themselves.

Board Self-Evaluation

Dr. Kristen Miles noted that the self-evaluation tool is based on years of research finding that when board
members are doing the indicators well student achievement increases. Board members provided initial
observations on the evaluation. The facilitator summarized the areas Board Members are doing well and
areas identified that need improvement.

Board members provided thoughts or questions on items where there was incongruency. Board Member
suggestion to include the data as part of the next round of onboarding new Board Members.

There was discussion regarding what the Board wants to do with the self-evaluation information, and
reviewed suggested goals for improvement. There was discussion about governance versus
programmatic question/decision making, and what a monitoring calendar could look like, as well as how
to implement work on board goals and protocols.

Dr. Miles wrapped up the discussion noting that building a monitoring plan will be crucial. Chair Wang
asked if there was interest in continuing the conversation at either a board retreat or another work session
