PROBLEM STATEMENT: 
We are subsidizing K-8 middle
grades programs with 21 FTE to
ensure core programming. 
Students across different middle grade models
face inequitable access to high-quality
programming, specialized educators, elective
pathways, support services, extracurricular
options, and strong educator teams. As a result of this system-wide work, the
following is now true in all middle grades
programs: 
●   Grade-level standards-aligned core
curriculum 
●   Access to Advisory 
●   Standards-based grading 
●   Supported transitions 
●   Aligned schedules* 
Due to variable enrollment and staffing
models, the following is not yet consistent
across middle grades programs: 
●   Enrichment & extracurricular
opportunities/pathways 
●   Access to academic intervention and
acceleration, as needed 
●   Collaborative teacher teams

results, do not show significant differences
between 6-8 and K-8 schools. 
This aligns with national research: 
“NWREL looked at the available research on grade
configuration and concluded that little evidence
existed to determine a cause-and-effect
relationship between grade configuration and
academic achievement.”

Academic Intervention 
Due to low student numbers, smaller   K-8
schools   frequently cannot staff targeted
academic intervention courses, leaving
eligible students without necessary support. 
Larger   6-8 schools   have greater capacity to
staff targeted intervention courses for
students in need of this support. Summary and Questions 
We are currently using 21 non-formula
FTE or   ~$3 million   to supplement
middle school programming in K-8
buildings. 
Middle grades students in K-8 buildings and 6-8
buildings have disparate access to specialized
educators, elective pathways, and intervention
opportunities. 
Educators in K-8 buildings and 6-8
buildings have disparate work loads and
access to collaborative educator teams. 
The proposed scenarios expand access to
comprehensive middle school programming,
reduce non-formula FTE subsidies, and free up
space for well-resourced elementary programs.
