MLC Enrollment Process
Students enter MLC through two procedures:

1. Annual School Choice Lottery
       i. Online application
      ii. Initial lottery result in January
     iii. Waitlist in place for follow-up offers
     iv. All families encouraged to visit

2. Reconnection Services Referral
       i. Students and families discuss goals, interests, and needs with an
          Outreach Coordinator
      ii. Families meet with school staff before a ﬁnal decision is made
