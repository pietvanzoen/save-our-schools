Students For Peace Policy Proposal (proposed by students)

Director Wang shared that he and Director Sullivan had met with a group of students at Lincoln High
School who had formed a group called “Students for Peace”. The students provided testimony on their
proposed policy in response to the District’s AD on Political Speech, which resulted in Palestinian flags
being removed from the school. They expressed sentiment that it felt like a targeted political issue. They
shared examples of where other materials deemed political speech were allowed to remain posted.

Board Members expressed appreciation for the students who brought forth the policy proposal. Director
Engelsman asked if any legal review of the policy proposal had taken place. Committee Chair DePass
suggested asking the legal team to review the proposed policy, before scheduling it to come back to the
Committee for further discussion and consideration. Director Wang asked if the Committee could also
discuss banned symbols at that time. Sharon Toncray responded that she would work with the students.

Review Proposed Revisions to the Public Contracting Rules

Emily Courtnage shared proposed changes to the Public Contracting Rules. These changes included
removing the duplicative amendment reporting requirements for contracts with amendments of 125% or
more over the initial contract amount, as these already require Board approval and to bring the Offer of
Judgment Rule into Alignment with Board Policy on Liability Claims, which allows the Superintendent to
settle up to $75,000 without extra reporting.

Director Engelsman requested data on amendments between 100% and 124% of initial contract amounts.
The Committee referred the proposed policy to the full Board for a First Reading at the March 10 board
meeting.

Review of 7.00.000 Community Policies

Committee Chair DePass requested that Committee members review the 7.00.000 Community Policies
and be prepared to discuss at the next meeting.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

Director Engelsman stated she is okay with the suggested use of shall and will in policy language moving
forward. She would like to add in a section about the language at the front of policies.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 5:53 pm by Committee Chair Michelle DePass.
