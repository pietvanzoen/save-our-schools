                                                                     STUDENT PROPOSAL

(This draft policy is intended as an addition to 3.30.035-P, which would require
renaming the title of the Policy; or it could be considered as a separate policy)

Overview:
Portland Public Schools is committed to providing a student-led, positive, and
productive learning and working environment. Posted materials in both classrooms and
public spaces should be appropriate, while uplifting student and teacher voices. This policy
ensures that displays support academic achievement, foster respectful and civil discourse,
and contribute to a safe and welcoming school environment for all students. PPS values
centering equity, inclusion, student voice, and high-quality learning in both classrooms and
shared school spaces.



Definitions (DRAFT):
Educationally appropriate​
- Materials, displays, or postings that are:
    A.​ A direct or strong relation to curriculum, instructional objectives, or school
        sponsored activities etc.
    B.​ Age appropriate, factually accurate information.
Conducive to learning:
    A.​ Encourage and be likely to aid education and learning.
Materials, displays, or postings that:
    A.​ Support and promote student engagement, focus, and academic achievement.
    B.​ Contribute to a safe, orderly, and respectful educational environment.
Inclusive
    A.​ Helping students to feel welcome in the classroom space.
            a.​ May include materials promoting minority or otherwise voices unlikely to be
                heard.
Educational discourse
    A.​ Civil conversations improving understanding of diverse perspectives.
    B.​ Reasonable relation to curricular, academic, or contemporary concepts.
Affected and Relevant perspectives
    A.​ Individuals or groups directly impacted by the contested material(s)



Policy:
   1.​ Teachers may post materials in the classroom that are educationally
       appropriate, conducive to learning, or inclusive.
2.​ Posted materials in schools must be in alignment with State laws, local laws,
    and PPS Policies, including:
       a.​ Policy: Student Anti-Harassment and Teen Dating Policy 4.30.060-P
       a.​ Policy: Anti Racist and Anti-Oppression Learning Communities
           4.30.070-P

2.​ For materials posted in the hallways and public spaces in the school, principals
    shall determine a process for determining what is taken down. That process
    should strive to promote educational discourse, be inclusive of affected and
    relevant perspectives, democratic, student led, and carried out in a timely
    manner.
