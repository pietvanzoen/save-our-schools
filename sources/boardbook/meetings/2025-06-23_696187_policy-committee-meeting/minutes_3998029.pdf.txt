Call to Order / Introductions

introduced themselves.

Public Comment

None

Draft Proposed New Policy x.xx.xxx-P: Solicitation Registration and Reporting

Time: 4:38 pm

Director Brim-Edwards summarized the proposed policy revisions. Committee Members confirmed they
have reviewed the changes closely and had no further questions. It was noted that construction contracts
follow a detailed request for proposals process that is outlined by statute. There was discussion regarding
the development of a reporting process to be shared on the website.
            Actions Taken:

            •   The Committee recommended the revised policy to the full board for a first reading.

Draft amendment to Ethics and Conflicts of Interest Policy 5.10.066-P

Time: 4:52 pm

Director Brim-Edwards provided an overview of the history of the proposed policy revisions, including that
that the policy was in the public comment period and was sent back to the committee by the full board for
additional revisions. There was discussion regarding potential revised language, including how narrow the
language should be and whether the policy revisions were necessary. No decisions were made; the policy
will remain in the committee.
