PRIORITIES
The CBSE staff collaboratively developed a community coalition strategy aligned with the CBSE vision. This
collaborative effort involved extensive engagement and deliberation over several months.


To personalize student outcomes, a series of collective community conversations were facilitated, and will continue
to be facilitated, with Principals from Harriet Tubman Middle School and Boise Eliot-Humboldt Elementary. These
discussions aimed to understand the unique needs and aspirations of students within each school community.


The development of multi-sector partnerships was guided by principles of academic research, innovation, and
liberatory design. This approach prioritized the expansion of capacity, power, and resources within the community to
collectively implement strategies that advance educational equity especially for Black students within the PPS
district.

Guided by community vision, The CBSE staff is working tirelessly to find a home for the Center for Black Student Excellence. In
reflecting that vision, PPS staff, community partners, and data regarding the desired student outcomes, has supported us in
envisioning a space that includes the following:

The CREATE & INNOVATE Lab
A dedicated pedagogical environment for students to engage in experiential
learning within the entrepreneurial, mechanical, and creative industries.
The Center for Culture and Community Collaboration
A centralized space for students to engage in intercultural understanding and
appreciation of history & culture within the Portland Public School community.
The Center for Wellness and Excellence in Learning
A holistic approach to supporting Black students, families, and educators. By
centering the needs and experiences of these individuals, and welcoming and
affirming all visitors regardless of background or identity, the Center will
prioritize healing justice, addressing historical trauma, and promoting
collective liberation.
The Multi-Purpose Center
These flexible spaces that are designed to empower student engagement,
community building, and diverse learning experiences through a wide range of
activities.
