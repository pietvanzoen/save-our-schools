                                       RESOLUTION No. 7058

                        Resolution to Approve a Senior Chief Salary Schedule

                                              RECITALS

A.   The Superintendent desires to promote two leaders to Senior Chief positions--Kristina Howard and
     Jon Franco, effective July 1, 2025.

B.   The District's salary schedule does not include the Senior Chief position, and the Superintendent
     recommends adding a range for that position of $224,000 to $239,000.

                                            RESOLUTION

The PPS Board of Education approves a range of $224,000 to $239,000 for the Senior Chief position.
