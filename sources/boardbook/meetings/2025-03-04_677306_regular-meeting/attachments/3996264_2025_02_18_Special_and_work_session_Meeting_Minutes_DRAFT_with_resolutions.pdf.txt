Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Special Meeting and Work Session of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was
held at Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center - Board Auditorium, 501 N. Dixon St, Portland, OR 97227
and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Director Julia Brim-Edwards: Present, Director Michelle DePass: Present, Director Herman Greene:
Present, Director Gary Hollands: Present, Director Christy Splitt: Present, Director Patte Sullivan: Present,
Director Edward Wang: Present. Present. Student Representative JJ Kunsevi: Present.

Call to Order

The meeting was called to order at 6:45 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.

Consent Agenda (Resolutions 7045-7049)

Time: 6:48 pm

There was discussion regarding the international field trip included in Resolution 7046, including its
duration, criteria for exceptions, cost and funding sources, and ensuring continued learning for non-
traveling students. There was a request to include the criteria for an exception to trip duration in the
Administrative Directive, as well as an update on the status of implementing an 8th-grade capstone
activity.

        passed (7 yes – 0 no).

        Directors Brim-Edwards: Yes, DePass: Yes, Greene: Yes, Hollands: Yes, Splitt: Yes, Sullivan: Yes,
        Wang: Yes; Student Representative Kunsevi: Yes (unofficial)

Annual Multnomah Education Service District Local Service Plan Resolution (Resolution 7052)

Time: 7:01 pm

Sascha Perrins – Multnomah Education Service District (MESD) Assistant Superintendent

It was noted that the list of services available for use from the Multnomah Education Service District
(MESD) are options available to Portland Public Schools (PPS) and that the district may not choose to
utilize all the services available. There was discussion regarding outdoor school being scheduled in the
current year during religious and cultural holidays, which is in violation of the Religious and Cultural
Observances Policy 2.30.010-P. The MESD representative highlighted the complexities in the scheduling
of outdoor school, noting that principal’s have the option to request dates and indicates dates that don’t
work for them. The Superintendent and MESD affirmed they would work together to minimize scheduling
conflicts with religious and cultural holidays. There was discussion regarding the enforcement of Policy
2.30.010-P.

        Public Comment:

            •      Ariel Matasar: Urged the Board to enforce its policy on protected dates, noting their child
                   missed part of Outdoor School due to scheduling on Rosh Hashanah, which privileged
                   non-Jewish students.
            •      Mel Berwin: Urged the Board to enforce its holiday equity policy and ensure compliance
                   from district partners.

        Actions:

        Director Herman Greene moved and Director Michelle DePass seconded the motion to adopt
        Resolution 7052, Annual Multnomah Education Service District Local Service Plan Resolution.
        The motion was put to a voice vote and passed (7 yes – 0 no).

Vision for Special Education Services in Portland Public Schools

Time: 7:41 pm

Chief of Student Supports - Jey Buno

Staff presented an overview of PPS’s Vision for Special Education, including strategic goals such as
integration into neighborhood schools, student representation in Special Education, academic data, and
Special Education funding. Staff noted that while academic achievement data is not where they want it to
be, PPS is performing better than the state average. Key priorities include reengaging families,
professional learning for educators, and strengthening core programming. Board members asked
questions throughout the presentation. There was Board discussion regarding the rates of representation
of students of color, the need for additional administrative support in schools with high special education
enrollment, the supports in place during the transition to neighborhood schools and how those supports
were communicated to the community, the increasing need for special education services, and the need
for advocacy at the state level, particularly regarding funding caps. Staff outlined the next steps, including
further engagement with advisory committees and community input to shape future actions. Legislative
efforts to increase special education funding were also highlighted.

Update: 2025 General Obligation Bond

Time: 7:58 pm

An update on the 2025 general obligation bond was provided, noting that the board has established
parameters for reducing high school modernization costs, design advisory committees have resumed
their work, and the Climate Crisis Response Committee (CCRC) has expressed support for full
electrification of the new schools. A consultant and campaign manager have been hired, a website is in
development (yespdx.com), and student engagement underway for the campaign. The referral paperwork
will be submitted soon.

Adjourn

Chair Eddie Wang adjourned the meeting at 9:01 pm.

                                      RESOLUTION No. 7052

            Annual Multnomah Education Service District Local Service Plan Resolution

                                             RECITALS


A. The 2025-26 Multnomah Education Service District (“MESD”) Local Service Plan (“LSP”) is
   essentially an annual menu of options offered to the MESD Superintendents’ Council for the
   Council’s review, modification, and approval.

B. The services offered in the LSP require approval of the component districts’ boards annually. The
   Services offered in the LSP must be adopted by two-thirds of component districts’ boards.

C. A separate list of the specific services for Portland Public Schools, or the District Service Plan,
   will be included in the 2025-26 budget development process.

D. The actual selection and use of resolution funds to pay for selected services remains at the
   discretion of each individual district. This Resolution does not commit Portland Public Schools to
   each of the specific services offered by MESD; it affirms the overall services offered to all of the
   local component districts and contains the terms of the LSP offered by the MESD.


                                           RESOLUTION

1. The Board of Directors of Portland Public Schools, School District No. 1J, Multnomah County,
   Oregon, agrees to the conditions and provision of all programs and services, described in the
   2025-26 Local Service Plan – Multnomah Education Service District with no exceptions.

2. In the event that the required resources are not available, each and every program and service is
   subject to reduction or elimination at the discretion of the Multnomah ESD Board. If such
   reductions or eliminations are necessary, they will be made through contingency planning in
   cooperation with the Superintendents of the local component districts.
