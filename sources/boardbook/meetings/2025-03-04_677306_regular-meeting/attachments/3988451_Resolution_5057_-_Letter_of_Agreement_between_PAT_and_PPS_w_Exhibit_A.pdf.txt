                                        RESOLUTION No. 7057

                   Letter of Agreement between Portland Association of Teachers and
                            School District No. 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon

                                                RECITAL

On February 19, 2025, PPS and Portland Association of Teachers (PAT) reached a tentative agreement
on a Letter of Agreement (LOA) that identifies the terms and conditions of the current PAT collective
bargaining agreement that will apply to Restorative Justice Coordinators, subject to approval by the Board
of Education.

                                             RESOLUTION

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education adopts and approves the LOA between PPS and PAT,
attached hereto as Exhibit A, including approving payments made pursuant to the PAT collective
bargaining agreement. The Superintendent or designee is authorized to make technical corrections to the
Agreement to correct typographical and spelling errors, make formatting changes, or renumber
provisions, so long as the intended meaning of the Agreement is not thereby altered.
