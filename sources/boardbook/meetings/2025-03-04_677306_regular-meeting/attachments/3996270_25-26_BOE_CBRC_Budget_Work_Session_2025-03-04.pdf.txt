                 3            PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
Vision for Budget Engagement
Portland Public Schools is committed to
transparency, collaboration, and engagement in
shaping our district’s budget.

With a $40 million shortfall, we are working to make
strategic reductions while centering student
outcomes.

We will communicate early and often throughout this
process.                 4                PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS

     ○ RESJ Community Advisory Committee
     ○ Migrant Education Parent Advisory Council
     ○ Culture in the Classroom
     ○ Title VI Indian Education Indian Parent Committee
     ○ Community Budget Review Committee
     ○ Early Literacy Grant Advisory Committee
