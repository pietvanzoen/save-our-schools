2025-2026 Budget Development with the Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC)

Time: 7:09 pm

Staff: Dr. Renard Adams – Chief Accountability and Equity Officer; Michelle Morris – Chief Financial
Officer; Nichole Watson – Senior Director of Family and Community Engagement

Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC) Members: Caitlin Bice, Minyana Bishop, Karanja Crews,
Aaron Cronan, and Grace Groom

Participants introduced themselves. Staff provided an overview of the budget engagement process,
including past engagement efforts, key survey findings, and the development of an FAQ to address
community questions. Staff encouraged advocacy at the state level.

The Board and CBRC Members participated in a Notices & Wonderings activity, discussing key
observations and questions about the budget process and findings. Observations included the
prioritization of classroom staffing, the importance of school flexibility, and concerns about limited staff
engagement opportunities. Questions included how survey feedback may influence decisions, the need
for policy flexibility in staffing, alignment between budget cuts and Board goals, and ensuring community
input is reflected in final decisions. There was also interest in reviewing district-funded programs to
explore cost-saving alternatives instead of cutting classroom instruction.

Dr. Armstrong highlighted in-person meetings she held with school staff, noting that administrators
expressed a need for flexibility in allocations by school. As a result, schools were given a staffing model
with options for reductions to better reflect the needs at each school. If additional state funding becomes
available, allocations will be adjusted based on feedback.

Update on the Center for Black Student Excellence

Time: 8:16 pm

Staff: Nichole Watson - Senior Director of Family and Community Engagement and Quinn Peoples -
Program Manager

The Board received an update on the Center for Black Student Excellence, including recent
developments, priorities, pilot programs, measurement tools, and next steps. Staff shared the vision for
the center as a space to uplift and support students, with access available to all PPS students. Discussion
included funding for non-physical spaces, partnerships with affinity groups and Black Student Unions, and
alternative ways to measure student achievement beyond testing and grades. Board members requested
additional information on long-term financial planning, program implementation within school
communities, potential site locations, operational costs, and a specific timeline. There was also interest in
increasing student engagement and identifying community partners, as well as discussing how the Board
can support the initiative.

Expenditure Contracts (Resolution 7067 and Resolution 7068)

Staff: Dan Jung – Chief Operating Officer and Stormy Shanks – Senior Director, School Modernization

The Board discussed two contract approvals related to the Cleveland and Ida B Wells modernization
projects. A motion to combine the two resolutions was approved by a voice vote. Discussion including
how the contract approvals align with recently implemented cost-saving measures and the potential to
amend the contract scope to further reduce costs. It was noted that the pre-construction services are
covered by the 2020 bond. There was discussion on the contracting method, the setting of Guaranteed
Maximum Price (GMP), and reducing cost overruns. Staff explained that pre-construction work would not
be wasted even if the 2025 bond does not pass. Questions were raised about project timelines,
contracting approaches, and past project performance.

The question was called by Director DePass. An amendment was introduced requiring regular reporting
on the modernization process, leading to debate about whether there was enough time to review the
amendment or potentially bringing it back for consideration at a future meeting. Staff noted they could
provide smaller, less robust updates throughout the process, and the Board agreed to late submission of
meeting materials as long as they met legal posting requirements. A suggestion was made to postpone
the amendment for discussion at the next work session. The amendment passed by a voice vote.

The call to question was appealed, resulting in a tie vote. Chair Wang allowed discussion to continue,
using the Student Representative’s yes vote as the tiebreaker. Staff committed to sending a follow-up
email to answer final questions. The combined resolutions were adopted as amended.
