Presentation of the Year 6 - Bond Performance Audit results

Time: 11:08 pm

Bond Performance Auditor: Cathy Brady

Cathy Brady presented the Year 6 Bond Performance Audit results, highlighting progress, challenges, and
recommendations across the 2012, 2017, and 2020 bond programs. It was noted that most 2017 bond
projects are complete, with expected savings at Lincoln. There were recommendations around post-
project evaluations and clearer contract terms in response to the ongoing issues with the Benson High
School modernization. While there have been budget and schedule challenges with the 2020 bond
modernizations and the Center for Black Student Excellence, infrastructure and curriculum projects are
moving forward. Other recommendations included improved workload tracking, improved oversight
communication with the School Improvement Oversight Committee and the Bond Accountability
Committee (BAC), improvements in BAC meeting documentation, and a stronger role for executive
leadership in the implementation of outstanding audit recommendations.

Board members asked questions about project changes, communication improvements, recommendation
status updates, and plans for the reallocation of remaining 2017 funds that resulted from cost savings.
There was further discussion about the Benson modernization project, staffing plans, and how the Board
could help support in resolving audit recommendations. Director Julia Brim-Edwards noted that questions
regarding the Health and Welfare Trust remain unanswered and will be submitted for the record.

Statement for the Record

Director Julia Brim-Edwards:

Request for Health and Welfare Trust Audits from PPS District Staff

For more than a month, I have requested from PPS staff the audits of the Health and Welfare Trust and
been denied access to them or been questioned about the request.

By way of this submission, I am requesting the audit again as well as additional information. I also asked
if the Superintendent and/or any senior staff (who are not Trustees) reviewed the Trust audits prior my
raising the issue and have not received an answer.

Since I have not received the audit, here are follow up questions related to the Trust Audits and the Trust
Governance Document that was provided:

    1. Have there been any amendments made to the Trust documents since it was signed in 2001? If
        yes, please send it to me. Are there bylaws, please send them to me.
    2. It appears to be registered in the state of Oregon, can you tell me the name that it is
        registered under? Is it registered with the Sec. of State and DOJ? And, is there only one entity?
    3. Who is the plan "Administrator"?
    4. Who is the current secretary of the Trust? Other officers?
    5. Over the last five years, please share the benefits that have been added, increased, decreased or
        terminated in whole or in part (Article 5.9)
    6. What reports has the Trust required (Article 5.12) over the last five years? Please share them?
    7. Article 6 says that the Trust shall maintain accurate records and accounts of all
        transactions which shall be available at all reasonable times for inspection by "any
        Party". According to the definitions (2.17) the District is a party. As a District director I am
        requesting the opportunity to inspect the records.
    8. Article 6 also states that there is an annual Valuation of Assets available to the district. Please
        share the 2024, 2023, and 2022 valuations.
    9. Article 9.1 relates to the annual certified audit and it says it shall be made available to the District.
        I am requesting the 2019, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 audits that were made available to the District
        or that should be available to the district.
    10. What accounting firm conducted the audits?
    11. Who is the Trust's attorney who is denying access to the audits; is the attorney being paid by the
        Trust?
