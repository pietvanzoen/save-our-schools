Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 4:33 pm. Board Members and Senior Staff introduced themselves.

Public Comment

Time: 4:35 pm

    ●   Emily Barrett: Requested a single, consistent high school pathway for French language
        instruction so Le Monde Charter School students can continue through French 9 and 10 at one
        centralized High School.
    ●   Emily Teplin Fox: Expressed concerns that the ACCESS School model contributes to
        underenrolled neighborhood schools, limits diversity, and disproportionately benefits higher
        socioeconomic families.
    ●   Michelle DuBarry: Urged the district to close ACCESS Academy to redistribute funds to
        neighborhood schools; and requested an audit of ACCESS academic outcomes.
    ●   Jocelynn Haley Cambier: Advocated for additional staff in the Dual Language program at Rose
        City Park, and raised concerns about the school’s Continuous Improvement Plan, lack of
        communication regarding the School Climate Committee, and restorative justice practices.

Charter School Overview

Time: 4:46 pm

Staff: Dr. Isaac Cardona, Sunita Sandos, and Korrina Wolfe

Staff provided an overview of district and state sponsored charter schools, including instructional models,
demographics and charter school funding structures. The presentation outlined the Board’s oversight as
sponsors, including responsibilities related to fiscal, academic, and operational performance. Board
discussion focused on the monitoring and evaluation of underperforming schools, funding mechanisms,
and support for students, including special education and historically underserved groups.

Talented and Gifted Services

Time: 5:15 pm

Staff: Aaron Ferguson, Emily Glasgow, and Darcy Soto

Staff presented an overview of the district’s Talented and Gifted program, including identification
requirements, services, corrective actions, and staff training. ACCESS Academy was highlighted with
information on enrollment, admissions, demographics, and academic offerings. Board discussion topics
included identification practices, equity and representation in TAG, access to services, and distinctions
between district identification and enrollment at Access Academy.

Jefferson High School Academic Program Expansion

Time: 5:55 pm

Staff: Margaret Calvert and Dr. Filip Hristić

Staff shared initial concepts for expanding academic programming at Jefferson High School, building on
existing strengths and community partnerships. The overview highlighted the academic planning pillars,
engagement strategies, and next steps, such as course development and family outreach. Discussion
focused on advanced academic options, including advanced placement (AP) courses and dual credit
pathways, preparation for advanced coursework, and implications related to weighted grade point
averages.

Adjournment

It was noted that the Board will further discuss the Jefferson enrollment scenarios at the December 16th
Work Session. The meeting was adjourned at 6:23 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller.
