Jefferson HS Academic
  Program Expansion
Context and Background
- Jefferson has a strong history of academic success and
  community partnership.
- Modernization (2027–2029) and PPS’s consideration of
  sunsetting dual-assignment create a major opportunity.
- PPS is exploring an expanded academic program that
  matches—and exceeds—what is offered in neighborhood high
  schools.
- Final decisions will be shaped through engagement with
  students, families, staff, and partners.
