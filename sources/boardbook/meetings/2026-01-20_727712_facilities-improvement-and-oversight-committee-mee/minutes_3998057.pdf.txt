Bond Audit Recommendations

Time: 4:50 pm

Staff: Janis Hansen and Dr. Jon Franco

Staff provided an overview of the Bond Accountability Committee Charter, including its oversight and
accountability role and proposed areas for updating the charter. The presentation highlighted the auditor
recommendations related to transparency and governance, including documentation practices, clarity of
                                               Page 1 of 2
responsibilities, committee composition, and meeting frequency. It was noted that the recommendations
were intended to initiate discussion.

Board members asked questions and provided comments, with discussion focused on the scope of bond
audits, the role of the contract manager, and the appropriate process for revising the charter. Feedback
emphasized that revisions should be staff-led, reviewed by the BAC committee, and approved by the full
Board, with interest in balancing clarity and simplicity and aligning the charter with voter intent. Staff will
draft proposed revisions based on committee feedback and bring them back for further review.

Office of School Modernization Updates:

Time: 5:43 pm

Sarah Norman, Jennifer Eggers, TJ Hopkins

Staff provided updates across several bond-related topics, including progress on bond audit
recommendations, the status of seismic grants and upgrades, Portland Clean Energy Fund initiatives, the
2025 bond procurement schedule, and major athletic projects. The update noted that seismic projects are
in the design phase, with a mix of comprehensive and targeted retrofits, and that grant funding is being
pursued to support the comprehensive projects. Staff also reported progress with Portland Clean Energy
Fund awards, including preparatory work, compliance with grant requirements, coordination with partners,
and advancement of both building upgrades and student-led projects.

Staff outlined the 2025 bond procurement approach, highlighting the use of phased and pooled
procurements to support efficiency and timely delivery across projects. An overview of major athletic
projects was provided, noting that projects are generally in early design and permitting stages, with
timelines dependent on regulatory approvals and contractor selection. Board members asked questions
and provided comments related to project prioritization, site selection, procurement strategies, and the
overall timeline and status of athletic and capital projects.
