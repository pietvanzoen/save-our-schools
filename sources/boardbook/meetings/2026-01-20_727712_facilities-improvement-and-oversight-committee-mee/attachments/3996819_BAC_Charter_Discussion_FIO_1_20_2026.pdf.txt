Bond Accountability
Committee (BAC)
• Advisory committee to the PPS Board
  of Education (Board)
• Roles, responsibilities, and committee
  charges are detailed in the PPS Capital
  Construction Bond Accountability
  Charter (BAC Charter)
• The BAC Charter is approved by the
  Board
• Members of the BAC are appointed by
  the Board

The purpose of a charter is to formally establish and authorize an
entity (like the BAC) by defining its fundamental purpose,
structure, goals, scope, and governing principles, acting as a
foundational agreement that grants rights, sets boundaries, aligns
stakeholders, and provides a reference for operation and
accountability

Recommendation from the Bond Performance Audit

“Revisit how best to use the BAC and strengthen the committee’s role and
  involvement in oversight to enhance bond program and project delivery
  through BAC’s technical feedback and insights on project details. This could
  include PPS providing the BAC with the same or similar bond data and staff
  analysis/memos currently provided to the SFIOC, as well as other
  documents such as contract templates, detailed schedules with critical path,
  or project priority criteria for review, in addition to involving the BAC with the
  annual bond performance audits.”

Additional Recommendations in the Year 6 Bond Performance Audit
              (related to the BAC) for consideration:
 Year 6 – Recommendation #19: Work with the BAC to establish protocols for voting and
  formalizing any relevant recommended advice at its meetings that can be presented in BAC
  quarterly reports to the Board or provided in real -time by direct BAC -led presentations or
  OSM-led presentations to the Board.
 Year 6 – Recommendation #20: Ensure written BAC minutes include attendance, summaries
  of discussions or presentations, action items, and recommendations made, as well as
  establish protocols to distribute meeting materials at least 12 days in advance of BAC
  quarterly meetings to align with distribution protocols for board meeting materials.
    Consider adding: Requests for information or documentation from the BAC should be clearly
       documented in the meeting minutes
 Year 6 – Recommendation #21: Formalize the BAC recruiting and application process that is
 employed when a vacancy occurs or as soon as it is known a member is leaving to best retain
 full membership.

                       Concluding Reminder

The advice provided by the BAC is for the PPS Board. The BAC Charter can
be updated to reflect the monitoring, oversight and accountability measures you
want to see; and the BAC reports can include the advice you need to inform
your decision making around the Bonds.

If what you need from the BAC does not fall neatly within the charter, the Board
could pass a resolution identifying specific advice requests, and/or monitoring,
oversight and accountability responsibilities for the BAC.
