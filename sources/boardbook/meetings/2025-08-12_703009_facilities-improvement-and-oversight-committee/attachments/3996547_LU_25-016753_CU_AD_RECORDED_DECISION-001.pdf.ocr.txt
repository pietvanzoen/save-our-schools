ll. ANALYSIS

Prior to the record closing public comments raised issues with the tone and tenor of
interactions between neighbors at Grant Park Neighborhood Association meetings. The
Hearings Officer, having participated in neighborhood association meetings and having
served on boards, declines to wade into those turbid waters. What is evident from the public
comment received is there is strong community support for Grant High School and for adding
lights to the field. The purpose of this decision however, is to analyze whether the Applicant’s
proposal meets the applicable approval criteria. When engaging in that analysis, the number
of public comments received do not move the scale up or down if they fail to address the
applicable approval criteria. Much of the public comment received did not address the
approval criteria, rather highlight personal feelings.

Based upon substantial evidence in the record, the Hearings Officer agrees with the findings
and analysis of PP&D and adopts and incorporates those findings from the Memo dated June
6, 2025 (Exhibit 30 - which modifies Exhibit 9 Staff Report and Recommendations) in this
decision.

The approval is subject to the following conditions:

A.

As part of the building permit application submittal, each of the required site plans and any
additional drawings must reflect the information and design approved by this land use

review as indicated in Exhibits C-1 and C-2. The sheets on which this information appears
must be labeled, "Proposal and design as approved in Case File # LU 25-016753 CU AD.”

The existing trees around the Grant Bowl field shown in Exhibit C-1 and subject to the tree
protection plan in Exhibit A-14 must be preserved unless determined by a certified arborist
to be dead, dying, or hazardous. If any of these trees are removed for one of these
allowable reasons in the future, it must be replaced with a new tree meeting the minimum
planting requirements in Zoning Code Section 33.248.030.C.1 in the same general area of
the site.

School sporting events in the Grant Bowl field must end no later than 9:30 p.m., with field
lights around the Grant Bowl field turned off no later than 10:00 p.m.

Non-school-related use of the Grant Bowl field through Portland Parks & Recreation must
end no later than 9:00 p.m., with field lights around the Grant Bowl field turned off no later
than 9:30 p.m.

Within and adjacent to the Grant Bowl field, signs advertising a business may not face
outward toward the street. Signs which face inward toward the field are not subject to this
requirement.

. Portland Public Schools (PPS) custodial staff (or other responsible parties) must remove

litter from the Grant Bowl field area after every scheduled PPS game.

With PPS's cooperation as to location, Portland Parks and Recreation must place and
regularly service at least two additional trash/recycling receptacles at the Grant Bowl field.

No amplified sound source may be used on or adjacent to the Grant Bowl field after 7:00
p.m., except for school games and events as limited by Condition H and I, below. This
