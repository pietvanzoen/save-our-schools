Organizational Topics:

Time: 4:59 pm

Committee Charter
The committee discussed the draft Committee Charter, with topics including, the Long-Range Facilities
Plan, committee work vs staff work, allocation of work to other committees, oversight responsibilities, and
continued review of the charter.

Work Plan
Staff: Jon Franco

The Committee reviewed and discussed the first draft of its work plan. Staff noted that the plan provides
access points for discussion throughout the year and ensures alignment with the work of the Bond
Accountability Committee (BAC). Discussion included the frequency of seismic updates and whether the
BAC should report to the Committee or directly to the full Board. It was noted that the BAC could present
to the full Board while the Committee reserves time on the agenda to address any follow-up items as
needed.

Update on 2020 and 2025 Bond Projects

Time: 5:33

Staff: Stormy Shanks

Staff provided an update on the 2020 and 2025 Bond Projects, including high school modernizations,
health and safety projects, infrastructure improvements, and planning for the 2025 bond program. They
reported on completed and near-complete projects such as water quality, lead paint remediation, and
radon mitigation, and suggested allocating the remaining health and safety funds to ADA accessibility
improvements. Discussion included the status of the Roosevelt Grandstand expansion, options for use of
remaining 2020 bond funds, integrating seismic upgrades with roofing projects, and how facility work
aligns with potential school consolidations.

Facility Improvements

Time: 6:03 pm

Athletic Field Projects - Grant Bowl Field Lights
Staff shared provided an update on the Grant Bowl lights project, indicating that with an aggressive
timeline they could have lights up by December 1st. They suggested Board members put pressure on
outside entities, such as the City of Portland and Portland Parks and Recreation, to expedite the
permitting and approvals process. There was discussion regarding the possibility of making progress on
other athletic fields improvements at the same time as the other facilities improvements, with a request for
an athletics projects sequencing and prioritization timeline by the next meeting.

Seismic Improvements
Staff reported that the seismic report has been received and noted that additional time is needed to
review it before being presented at the next meeting. There was a request to invite the structural engineer
to attend that meeting if available.
Future Agenda Topics and Next Steps

Time: 6:20 pm

Committee Chair La Forte will work on the agenda for the next meeting.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned by Committee Chair Virginia La Forte at 6:21 pm.
