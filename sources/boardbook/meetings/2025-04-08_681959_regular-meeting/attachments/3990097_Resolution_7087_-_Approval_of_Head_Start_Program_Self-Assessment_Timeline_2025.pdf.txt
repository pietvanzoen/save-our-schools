                                        RESOLUTION No. 7087

                        Approval of Head Start Policy Council Recommendation

                                               RECITALS

A.   Federal requirements call for the Governing Board of a Head Start program to approve
     recommendations for the program.

B.   The Board of Directors for Portland Public Schools serves as the Governing Board for the PPS Head
     Start Program.

C.   The annual Self-Assessment is a central tenet of continuous quality improvement for Head Start
     programs. The goal of the Self-Assessment is to meet Head Start Program Performance Standards
     and move toward program excellence in serving children and families. The process provides
     programs the means to regularly assess their own management systems and program operations in
     order to continually strengthen the program and the services and supports delivered to children and
     families.

                                             RESOLUTION

The Board of Directors for Portland Public Schools, School District No. 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon,
approves the Head Start Policy Council recommendations as stated above.
