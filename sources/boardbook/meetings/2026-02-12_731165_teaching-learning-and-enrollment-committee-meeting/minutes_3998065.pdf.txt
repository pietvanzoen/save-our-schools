Curriculum Deep Dive Presentation

Math
Filip Hristic provided an overview of the districts adopted math curricula, covering grades K-5, 6-8, and 9-
12. A video was played that showed a classroom using the curriculum. There were questions about
teacher feedback and staff provided examples of where they were able to incorporate feedback
given. Director Chase-Miller asked questions about the challenges of implementing a new curriculum
                                                   Page 1 of 2
with fidelity with current class sizes. Staff shared that lessons were set up to provide daily, consistent
small group work allowing the teacher to work with students who needed extra support. It was
acknowledged that any new curriculum implementation has a learning curve. There were questions about
sequencing lessons and how to support students who miss a day or more of school. There were
questions about assessments on how the district gathers data to assess how PPS is doing on the District
Continuous Improvement Plan (DCIP).

English Language Arts
Emily Glasgow provided an overview of the English Language Arts Curriculum used throughout K-12 and
the shifts made in literacy instruction. They showed a video of a classroom discussing and analysing the
book “Hatchet”. There were questions about supporting the science of reading and also about what
support is provided at the middle school level. Committee members expressed appreciation for the
curriculum and had questions about whether there was a requirement to adopt a new curriculum. Staff
shared that the state requires review of curriculum every seven years, but not to necessarily adopt a
curriculum. Director Splitt expressed a desire to end the practice of using non-standards based
assessments and would like to see less computer based assessments. There were questions about
which high schools have Advance Placement (AP)Language and Literature. It was shared that
International Baccalaureate (IB) schools do not offer AP courses.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 6:04 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller.
