Revised Comprehensive Master Plans

Dr. Franco provided a summary of the total projected budgets for the modernizations of Cleveland, Ida B.
Wells and Jefferson High Schools and shared that he was seeking Committee feedback. The
Comprehensive Master Plans for each of the schools is slated to be voted on by the Board on October
14th.

Jefferson High School
Steven Effros shared a revised master plan with the cost reduction study adjustments. This includes
reducing size, relocating the teen parent center, and moving the gym. He shared there would be a
Community Information Session on October 15th. There was a Committee discussion about the need for
Teen Parent Centers. The Committee also noted that they have achieved cost savings at the low end of
the projected savings range. The next update will be shared at the October 8 Committee meeting.

Cleveland High School
Erik Gerding shared a revised master plan, including updated athletic facilities. He also shared that they
are looking to have the stadium finished one year before the main building opens. He shared that the
funds needed to improve Powell Park are included in the overall project budget. There is a planned
Community Design Workshop on October 4th, and Special Education Educators are participating in the
design process as well. There was Committee Discussion about the timing of completing improvements at
Powell Park.

Ida B. Wells High School
Donna Bezio provided an overview of how they achieved a $5 million cost savings, and shared there
would be more information provided at the end of September. She highlighted that they were able to keep
the same number of parking spots and not move the athletic fields. There was discussion about finding a
health care provider for the clinic. There was discussion about the Hillsdale Farmer’s Market and the plan
to have them move to a temporary location during construction. There were questions about the need for
the use of the parking lot by contractors during the construction.

Athletics
Staff provided an update on the Grant Bowl lights and shared that there will be a more detailed athletic
improvement overview at the next Committee meeting. There will be an overview of plans for middle
schools in October and long-term facility plans for November.

Update: Athletics Facilities Improvement

There was an update on the timeline and next steps for getting additional staff help in the Office of School
Modernization. There was a request for information about meetings that had occurred between Procedeo
and staff.

Adjourn

Director La Forte adjourned the meeting at 6:34 pm.
