BACKGROUND

On September 9, 2025, the Board of Education approved Resolution 7165, authorizing execution of Purchase
and Sale Agreement for the proposed Center for Black Student Excellence Building. The approval of this
resolution begins a 90-day due diligence period of the property as outlined in the purchase and sale
agreement (PSA).

Staff will report due diligence activities and information through the Facilities Improvement and Oversight
Committee in an ongoing basis. Below is a summary of those activities and analyses timeline.
