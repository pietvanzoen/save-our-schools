                                Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Teaching, Learning, and Enrollment Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools
Board of Education was held at Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center , 501 N. Dixon St., Portland, OR
97227 and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Call to Order and Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 5:04 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller.

Public Comment

    ●​ Sebastien Telford-Rake - Advocated for a Policy that would guarantee that LeMonde French
       Immersion students would be assigned to PPS High School with the highest level of French
       offered.
    ●​ KD Parman - Roosevelt Principal who advocated for continued flexibility under House Bill 2056
       (Access to Linguistic Inclusion Act), which allows EL coursework to count toward World Language
       and Language Arts credits.
    ●​ Blake Goud - Advocated for additional public engagement meetings for the District Rightsizing
       Process.

Multiple Language Learners

Joanna Tobin, Will Flores, Alayna Papaiacovou, and Angela Yee provided an overview of the services
provided by the Multiple Language Learners Department, including terminology and framework, legal
