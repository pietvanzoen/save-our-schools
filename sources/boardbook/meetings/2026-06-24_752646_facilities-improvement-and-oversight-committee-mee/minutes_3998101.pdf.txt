                  PORTLAND PUBLIC SCHOOLS
                  School District No. 1J, Multnomah County
                  Board of Education

                  June 24, 2026
                  Facilities Improvement and Oversight Committee Meeting


                                         Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Facilities Improvement and Oversight Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools
Board of Education was held at the Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center, 501 N. Dixon Street, Portland,
OR 97227 and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendees
Committee Members: Virginia La Forte (Committee Chair); Michelle DePass; Rashelle Chase-Miller

Other attendees: Stephanie Engelsman - Board Member; Liz Large - Contracted Senior Legal Counsel;
Sara Norman - Executive Director, Procedeo, Tom Odgers - Chief of Integrated Operations; TJ Hopkins -
Senior Program Director, Procedeo

Call to Order/Introductions
Committee Chair LaForte called the meeting to order at 4:33 pm.

Public Comment
   ●​ Megan Steffek-Shared concerns about the delays in land use agreements between PPS and
       Portland Parks and Recreation.

Grice-Adair: Update on Master Planning Process
Provided an update on the Grice-Adair Center project and shared that the goal is to have the scoped
budget in August.

Update: Portland Parks and Recreation
Staff shared that they are in the final stages of negotiations with Portland Parks and Recreation.

Update: Grant Bowl Lighting
Director La Forte would like to have the Grant Community involved in the planning of the “Flip the Switch”
celebration to commemorate the lighting of the Grant Bowl. She also raised concerns about OSAA Safety
and Crowd control requirements and requested that they open the time capsule that was buried in 1923 at
the Grant Bowl celebration.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items
There was a request to avoid duplicative presentations and to align updates to the Board with the Bond
Accountability Committee reports.

Adjourn
Committee Chair LaForte adjourned the meeting at 4:56 pm.
