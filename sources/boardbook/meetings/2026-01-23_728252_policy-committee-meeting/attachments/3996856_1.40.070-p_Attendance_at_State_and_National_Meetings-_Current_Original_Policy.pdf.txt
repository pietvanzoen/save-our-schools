            membership in various associations of school boards and from the

            organizations shall be authorized to attend meetings of such organizations
            without prior Board approval. In addition, it shall be the policy of the Board           "
            to designate delegates to various conventions and meetings as may seem
            desirable.

            representatives shall be acted upon by the Board. Board members
            attending such meetings according to the above guidelines shall be
