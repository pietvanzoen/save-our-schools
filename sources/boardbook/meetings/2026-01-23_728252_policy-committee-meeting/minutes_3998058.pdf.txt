Call to Order / Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 12:00 pm by Committee Chair Michelle DePass. Attendees introduced
themselves. Vice-Chair DePass provided a summary of the agenda.

Time: 12:09 pm

    ●   Blake Goud: Requested the Committee review the policy on school consolidations and closures
        and capital investments to ensure equitable practices during upcoming consolidation
        considerations.
    ●   Dr. Effie Greathouse: Outlined a proposal by Safe Indoor Air for Schools Network which included
        adding three indoor air quality subgoals and revised environmental education goals to the Climate

    ●   Kylie Menagh-Johnson: In support of the Safe Indoor Air for Schools Network proposed revisions
        to the Climate Crisis Response, Climate Justice and Sustainable Practices Policy 3.30.080-P.

Policy Review and Potential Revisions/Rescissions of Section 1.00.000-P

Time: 12:05 pm

Sharon Toncray and Emily Courtnage

Staff provided an overview of the considerations used to review and propose revisions to all policies in
the 1.00.000-P level of Board Policies. It was noted that the majority of the proposed revisions are
discretionary changes intended to clarify and modernize the policies and are not statutory requirements.
Committee members heard public comment. There was discussion regarding how to provide feedback on
the policy proposals. Committee members will further review the proposed revisions and provide

                                              Page 1 of 2
feedback and questions to staff by February 6, 2026. The Committee will discuss the batch of policy
revisions at the next meeting for potential referral to the Board for a First Reading.

There was discussion regarding the legal ramifications of changing the word “shall” to “will” in many of the
policies. The Chief Legal Officer stated that, within policy language, the two words hold the same weight,
while “will” is more modern and accessible to the general public; however, further legal clarification will be
provided at the next meeting.

Other Business

Committee Members discussed revisiting the Climate Crisis Response, Climate Justice and Sustainable
Practices Policy 3.30.080-P and the Student Use of Personal Electronic Devices in Schools Policy
8.60.039-P in response to recent public comment heard on the topics. There was general interest in
further discussion and informational presentations on the topics. It was noted that the Facilities
Improvement and Oversight Committee will be hearing a presentation on air quality by the Safe Indoor Air
for Schools Network and the Teaching Learning and Enrollment Committee will be hearing a presentation
on computers and technology in classrooms. There was discussion regarding the impacts of technology
in classrooms.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 12:44 pm by Committee Chair DePass.
