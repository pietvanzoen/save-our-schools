SUBJECT:         Preliminary Financial Update for the Month Ended January 31, 2026


This Preliminary Financial Update is an overview of Portland Public School’s financial operations
for the Month Ended January 31, 2026.
This report provides an overview of financial operations of District funds for the period of July 1,
2025 through January 31, 2026. Amounts reported are from the financial records of the District
and are unaudited.


As you review the January 2026 Financial Schedules, please keep the following in mind:

         The “Annual Forecast” column for each fund includes known activity through January and
          projected revenues and expenditures based on historic annual averages, current year
          trends, as well as co-created projections with operational departments.
         The District operates on a modified accrual basis of accounting, which means most
          revenues and expenses are recorded as received and expended.
         Typically, the final audited financial reports for the prior fiscal year are completed in
          December, however due to a government shutdown and delayed issuance of OMB
          guidance on federal grant reporting requirements this year, the annual audit report filing
          timeline was extended to early February 2026. The FY2025 Financial Audit is complete.
         January close was delayed significantly due to the final audit completion and necessary
          final audit adjustments recording in the Month of January.
         Beginning balances reflect audited numbers and all final adjustments have been
          recorded based on the completed Fiscal Year 2025 financial report.
         This information is utilizing the best information available at this time.
         Additional reports are included with this report to provide additional context and a high level
          summary of the financial update.
