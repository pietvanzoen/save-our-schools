SUBJECT         Amendment of the 2026-27 Budget Calendar



BACKGROUND
Staff is requesting that the Board resolve to amend the 2026-27 budget calendar at the Board’s April 14,
2026 meeting. The date and location for the public hearing on the Proposed Budget has been updated
to May 13, 2026 at Benson High School.

RELATED POLICIES/BEST PRACTICES
Action is being taken to:
    ● allow sufficient time to adopt the budget by June 30, as required by ORS 294.408,
    ● comply with Oregon Local Budget Law,
    ● leverage Government Finance Officers Association (GFOA) best budgeting practices,
    ● ensure the budget incorporates accurate data inputs,
    ● meet Meritorious Budget Award (MBA) requirements, and
    ● update the adopted calendar to align with current planning.

ANALYSIS OF SITUATION
The Board may choose to amend the budget calendar as presented, amend it with revisions, or not
amend it. It includes steps of budget preparation and is built around mandated requirements to allow
sufficient time to adopt the budget by June 30, as required by ORS 294.408.

FISCAL IMPACT
No further impacts beyond those identified in the Analysis of Situation section above.

COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT (IF APPLICABLE)
The leadership of the CBRC have received the draft amended budget calendar. Community engagement
will continue with the budget process.

TIMELINE FOR IMPLEMENTATION / EVALUATION
If approved, staff will work on developing the budget for 2026-27 as scheduled.
