         Major Religious Holidays and Cultural Events 2026-27
Schools work to avoid scheduling special school events on major religious holidays and cultural events to be inclusive of all
students. This includes the scheduling of field trips, back-to-school night, outdoor school, assemblies, major tests, PTA and site
council meetings, student performances, etc. This list of holidays/events does not include all students’ traditions and there
may be other dates to avoid scheduling conflicts for students.
