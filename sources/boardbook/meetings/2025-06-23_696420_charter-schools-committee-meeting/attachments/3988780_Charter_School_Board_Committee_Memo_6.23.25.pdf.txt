Subject:​       Charter School Performance Frameworks 2023-24 and school updates


The PPS Charter Schools Office provides an Annual Performance Framework and Report (“Report”) on
each charter school during each school year, reporting on the formal performance framework from the
previous school year. The Report covers Academic, Financial, and Organizational measures, and is the
primary evaluation tool used to assess overall charter school performance both annually and when
contract renewal decisions are made.

Because many of the sources of data used in the Report are not available until late fall or winter (state
assessment data, Oregon Report Card, Municipal Audit and financial reports), there is a necessary delay
between the end of the school year and the completion of the Report. This memo is intended to
provide the Charter Board Committee with general information about each charter school at the end
of the school year, while awaiting data needed to complete the 2024-25 Annual Performance
Framework and Report.

The following is a summary of 2023-24 for each of the five charter schools that PPS authorizes, as well as
any alerts and updates for the current 2024-25 school year. The general summary information provided
in this memo supplements the attached individual 2023-24 annual performance report for each charter
school.

Portland Village School Waldorf-inspired, standards-based education
