Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Charter Schools Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was
held at Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center - Board Auditorium, 501 N. Dixon St, Portland, OR 97227
and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

5:34 pm

themselves.

Annual Charter Schools Update

Time: 6:38 pm

Staff: Sunita Sandoz and Korrina Wolfe

Staff provided an overview of the Portland Public Schools (PPS) Charter School Office and PPS-
sponsored charter schools, highlighting school-specific focus areas, enrollment figures, and assessment
data. The discussion included the impact of leadership turnover on outcomes at KairosPDX and their
strategic improvement plan. Board members asked questions about academic performance, discipline
and dropout rates, and the meeting cadence for charter school oversight. There was interest in identifying
successful practices for broader application and enrollment practices targeted at improving outreach to
ensure diverse student enrollment.
