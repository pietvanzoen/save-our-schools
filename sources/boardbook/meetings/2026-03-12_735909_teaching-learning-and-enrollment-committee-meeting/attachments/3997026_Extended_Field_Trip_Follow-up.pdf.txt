Continuity of Instruction
Overview
Portland Public Schools recognizes
that certain learning experiences,
such as Dual Language Immersion
(DLI) Capstone trips, Outdoor
School, and other extended ﬁeld
experiences, may involve groups of
students being off campus for
multiple days.
Overview
During these periods, schools are
expected to ensure continuity of
instruction for students who remain
at school. Site leaders work with
grade-level teams to plan
instructional coverage that
maintains alignment to grade-level
learning goals and classroom
routines.
Approval for Field Trips
Schools carefully plan for extended
learning experiences that take place
off campus, such as Dual Language
Immersion (DLI) capstone trips or
Outdoor School.
