Contracts Audit: Status of the Implementation of Outstanding Recommendations

Janise Hansen began by sharing her justification for the focus of contracts managed by the Racial Equity
and Social Justice team. The Committee members had questions about the timing for data used when
evaluating their contracts, how staff monitors progress on the contracts and suggestions to update the
cover memo template to include sharing information about what changes in practice may have occurred
to help meet the goals of the contract. Dr. Adams shared that the SMART goals have been revised to
align with the District Continuous Improvement Plan.

Dr. Adams shared that principals were able to review the contracted partners in their buildings prior to
their contracts being eligible for renewal.

There were questions about how they monitor progress. There were questions about how to include
contract management into an employee’s evaluation. Dr. Adams reminded the Committee that a manager
could not share an employee’s evaluation to prove compliance with such a requirement. There were
questions about whether their team and partners have what they need. He also confirmed that his team
performed site visits at each school every year.

He shared that Principals were able to review the partners before they considered whether or not to
renew a contract.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 5:34 pm by Committee Chair Patte Sullivan.
