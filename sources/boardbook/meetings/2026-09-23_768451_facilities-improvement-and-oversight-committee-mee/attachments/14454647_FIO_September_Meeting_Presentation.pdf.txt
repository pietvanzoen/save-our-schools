Team Wins
●   Furniture and Moves
     ○ In the last school year, the move coordination team worked with community partners to
        obtain over 770 pieces of donated furniture for school and admin use, providing an average
        value savings to the district of $206,640
     ○ Move requests
●   Nutrition
     ○ Meals Served in 2025-26 - During the 2025-26 school year, our teams served a total of
        1,727,075 breakfasts and 3,585,467 lunches. That was an average of 10,404 breakfasts and
        21,599 lunches provided to students each day. In partnership with our afterschool SUN
        partners, Nutrition Services provided 136,529 snacks across 41 eligible after school programs.
     ○ Summer meals - Because hunger does not take a vacation, our team works with several
        partners to provide meals during the summer break. We provided meals for Summer
        Scholars, Summer Acceleration Academy, SUN sites and at 15 parks sites across Portland.
        Meals in the parks is a partnership with Portland Parks and Rec and provides free lunches to
        anyone in the community ages 1-18.
