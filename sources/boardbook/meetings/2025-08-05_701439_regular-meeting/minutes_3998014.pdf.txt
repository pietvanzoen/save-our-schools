Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Regular Meeting of the Portland Public Schools Board of Education was held at Dr.
Matthew Prophet Education Center - Board Auditorium, 501 N. Dixon St, Portland, OR 97227 and
streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendance

Chair Eddie Wang; Vice-Chair Michelle DePass; Directors Rashelle Chase-Miller, Stephanie Englesman,
Virginia La Forte, Christy Splitt, and Patte Sullivan; and Student Representative Ian Ritorto

Call to Order / Opening

The meeting was called to Order by Board Chair Eddie Wang at 6:08 pm.

Superintendent's Report

6:09 pm

Superintendent Armstrong provided her report, including highlighting the winner of the Manilow Music
Project award, as well as noting upcoming District events, such as Project Community Care and the
Leadership Institute.

Student Representative's Report

None

Student and Public Comment

None

Comments from our Union Partners

None

Board Committee and Conference Reports

Time: 6:16 pm

    ●   Audit Committee – Committee Chair Patte Sullivan shared that she had an agenda setting
        meeting with the Senior Internal Auditor, noting they decided that even though there are currently
        seven meetings scheduled the committee will only hold meet five times.

    ●   Policy Committee – The first meeting is scheduled for Aug 25th, however there has been a
        request to change the date. She noted that the Committee will have a full work plan for the year.

    ●   Bond Accountability Committee (BAC) – It was noted that the BAC is a team of civic experts that
        provide recommendations on the bond. The BAC is looking for new members, anyone interested
        should contact Stormy Shanks. At the recent committee meeting, members extensively went
        through the bond audit.

    ●   Facilities Improvement and Oversight – Committee Chair Virginia La Forte shared that the first
        meeting will be held on August 12th, noting that the agenda includes looking at the Committee’s
        work plan, and that they will focus on facilities updates, seismic projects, a location for the Indian
        Education program, and modernizations in the coming year.

There was a request for Committee Work Plans to be shared with the full Board once completed.


Approving a School District Boundary Change between Portland Public Schools and the
Beaverton School District (Resolution 7148)

Time: 6:33 pm

The Board discussed the potential fiscal and transportation impacts of the proposed boundary change, as

Finance Focus

Time: 6:58 pm

Michelle Morrison – Chief Financial Officer and Jey Buno – Chief of Student Support Services

Staff presented an overview of district finances, including funding sources, historical funding trends, and
the annual financial cycle and process. Board members asked questions throughout. Discussion included
the state school fund equalization formula and average daily membership, weighted (ADMw), and it’s
impact on funding, with a request to review longer-term historical data.

Student Use of Personal Electronic Devices Policy Implementation 8.60.039-P

Time: 7:26 pm

Staff: Dr. Isaac Cardona – Chief of Schools and Dr. Jon Franco – Senior Chief of Operations

Staff provided an overview of the changes to the implementation plan for the District’s Personal Electric
Device Policy that were made in response to the governor’s recent technology mandate. It was noted that
Principals are working within their cohorts to determine how to implement the policy, and the
Communication team will send information to the community and staff.

Board Members asked questions and provided comments, with topics including concerns about searches
and potential bias, impacts on instructional time, school-level flexibility and consistency in implementation
across the district, potential funding avenues for tools like yonder pouches, and the importance of clear
expectations and communication with families.

Review Draft Board Work Plan

Time: 8:01 pm

The Board discussed whether to address the work plan or wait until the retreat and the meeting material
distribution timeline. The Board reached consensus to wait until the retreat. Dr. Armstrong noted that she
hopes to begin the work plan as soon as possible.


Consent Agenda: Resolutions 7159 through 7161

Time: 8:08 pm

Jey Buno – Chief of Student Support Services

Board Members asked questions regarding the special education services and medical care contracts,
including whether any of the costs are reimbursable by the high-cost disability fund, and whether contract
renewals require a Request for Proposal (RFP). Staff provided an overview of the contracting process. A
request was made for a public procurement and contracts training.
