                  Teaching, Learning, and Enrollment Committee Meeting


                                 Informal Meeting Minutes

In accordance with ORS 192.650, the District’s official School Board Meeting Minutes are maintained via
video recording and may be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation

Pursuant to notice made by posting to the Board’s public notices webpage and emailed to persons on the
mailing list, a Teaching, Learning, and Enrollment Committee Meeting of the Portland Public Schools
Board of Education was held at the Dr. Matthew Prophet Education Center, 501 N. Dixon Street, Portland,
OR 97227 and streamed live at: https://www.youtube.com/@ppsboardofeducation/live

Attendees:

Committee Members: Virginia La Forte, Christy Splitt, Eve Thompson (District Student Council
Representative)

Other Attendees: Patte Sullivan - Board Member; Mary Kate - Senior Legal Advisor; Kristina Howard -
Senior Chief of Academics.

Call to Order and Introductions

Director La Forte called the meeting to order at 4:36 pm.

Public Comment

    ●​ Emily Barrett - would like to see a policy which guarantees a path for students to go from
       LeMonde to the high school with the highest level of French offered.
    ●​ Miranda Rake - shared concerns about iReady and data privacy and advocated for screen and
       education technology policies.

District Continuous Improvement Plan Quarter 4 Report and Attendance Focus

Staff provided an update on the District Continuous Improvement Plan. While all goals were not met,
there was an increase in a sense of belonging and promising practices shown for increasing attendance.
There were questions on what resources and approaches were being used to increase attendance, and
strategies used to meet literacy goals.

Alternative & Focus School Overview

Staff provided an overview of the Ed Options Policy, including the various types of programs and the
lottery process used to access those programs. There were questions about class sizes comparisons
between focus option schools or programs and neighborhood schools.

K-12 Health Curriculum Adoption

The committee reviewed the recommendation for the K–12 Health curriculum adoption, including the
adoption process and the associated costs. Committee members expressed appreciation for the
presentation and recommended forwarding it to the full Board for consideration at the July 23, 2026,
Board meeting.

Other Business and Future Agenda Items

Director LaForte would like to understand how schools allow for Seniors to take less than a full load of
classes. She would also like to have an update on Jefferson's sunsetting dual assignment to be an
agenda item.

Adjourn

Director LaForte adjourned the meeting at 6:22 pm.
