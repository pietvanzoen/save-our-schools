  Subject: ​      Update for Sunsetting Dual Assignment: Jefferson is Rising

On January 13, 2026 the Board of Education voted to sunset the dual assignment zone (Resolution No. 7228) that
was shared with Grant, Jefferson, McDaniel and Roosevelt High School. A second resolution was passed that also
established an attendance area (Resolution No. 7229) for the Jefferson High School to a comprehensive
neighborhood high school starting in the 2027-28 school year. This is the first annual update on implementation.
We are sharing updates in the following areas: Academics, Athletics and Engagement.

Academics
Jefferson staff are working intentionally to build academic programming that will move the school from a focus
option to a comprehensive neighborhood high school. Academic Programs staff have partnered closely with
Jefferson High School administrators to develop a long-term vision for academic programming aligned with
projected enrollment growth.

A central element of this work is a 2.0 FTE investment designed to strategically build programming in anticipation
of increased student enrollment. Jefferson will introduce Pre-AP for ninth graders and AP Seminar for tenth
graders. Smaller classes at these levels will afford greater student support allowing students to develop the skills
and knowledge to access college level coursework as juniors.

Jefferson remains committed to a strong dual-credit partnership with Portland Community College (PCC). Offering
both Dual Credit and AP pathways allows Jefferson to better meet the diverse needs, interests, and postsecondary
goals of our students.

Jefferson will launch a Construction Carpentry program in 2026-27, creating a new Career and Technical Education
pathway for students interested in the skilled trades which will include beginning, intermediate, and advanced
levels allowing students to become CTE completers.

Key activities to date include:
      ●​ Collaborative planning meetings with Jefferson administrators to review enrollment and forecasting data,
           evaluate existing course offerings, and identify priorities for the 2026-27 school year.
      ●​ Implementation planning for the addition of AP Seminar and a Construction CTE pathway for the
           2026-27 school year, including course sequencing, staffing considerations, and alignment with district
           priorities.
      ●​ Administering YouScience aptitude and career interest assessments at both Jefferson HS and Harriet
         Tubman MS to provide student-centered data that will inform future pathway development and
         academic planning.
      ●​ Engaging external partners, including higher education, industry, and non-profit organizations, to explore
         innovative approaches to pathway design and career-connected learning opportunities for Jefferson’s
         students.
      ●​ Drafting a comprehensive academic programming framework that identifies college preparation, Career
         and Technical Education, visual and performing arts, and strategic partnerships as foundational elements
         of Jefferson’s future academic model.
      ●​ Identifying students accessing Special Education services in the feeder schools and working with the
         Special Education department to prepare classrooms, and outlining physical and instructional resources
         needed to provide a full continuum of services in the 2027-28 school year.
