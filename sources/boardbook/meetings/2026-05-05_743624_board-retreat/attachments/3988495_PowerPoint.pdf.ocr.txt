1. THE TOOLS: POLICY & PROCEDURE & GUIDELINES

Policy establishes a standard by
which an institution manages its
affairs.

Procedure/protocol is the
description of the operational
processes necessary to implement

policy.

Guidelines are general
non-mandatory recommendations
that offer background or information
on how to achieve a particular aim.
