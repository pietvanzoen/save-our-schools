Attendance

Board Members Present: Christy Splitt, Michelle DePass, Patte Sullivan,Rashelle Chase-Miller, Virginia
La Forte, Stephanie Engelsman, and Eddie Wang.

Staff: Kimberlee Armstrong - Superintendent; Deborah Kafoury - Chief of Staff; Rosanne Powell - Senior
Board Manager

Facilitators: Sandy Hayes - Bombastic Communications; Amy Cast - Bombastic Communications

Call to Order​
The Board Retreat was called to order by Chair Wang at 5:35 p.m.

Governance Discussion​
Sandy Hayes and Amy Cast asked Board Members and Superintendent Armstrong to reflect on how they
were feeling and where they wanted to be as a governance team. They introduced the Governance
Ecosystem concept and led a discussion about where policy design belongs within that framework. The
Board reviewed the distinctions between policy, procedure, and guidelines and discussed how each
contributes to effective board governance and organizational functionality. There was also discussion
about guardrails, the School Board’s purpose, roles and responsibilities, the difference between being a
trustee versus an advocate, and the role of shared values.

Values Exploration​
The Board participated in a values exploration exercise to identify the core values they share. The goal
was to establish a common foundation that can guide future policy development and Board
decision-making.

Policy Review​
The Board reviewed Policy 1.10.010-P – Role of the Board of Education to determine whether it reflects
the Board's identified values. During the discussion, members considered revisions to the Board's mission
statement and identified several updates for the Policy Committee to consider, including adding language
related to collaboration, teamwork, and accountability. There was also discussion about accountability
and how it should be reflected within Board policy. There was also discussion on how operating protocols
are considered. The Board emphasized the importance of grounding future work in its shared values,
including updates to the Rightsizing Resolution and the Racial Educational Equity Policy.

Future Actions/Next Steps​
The Board discussed next steps and how to apply its shared values to future Board goals, prioritization of
work, and policy development. The Board Office will develop a menu of professional development
opportunities for the board, including the possibility of attending a conference together.
