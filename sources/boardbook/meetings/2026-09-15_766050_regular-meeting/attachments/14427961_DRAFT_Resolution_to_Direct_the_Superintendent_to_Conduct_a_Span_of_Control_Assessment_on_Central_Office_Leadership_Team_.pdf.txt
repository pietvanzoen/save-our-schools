                                         Resolution No. XXXX


  Resolution to Direct the Superintendent to Conduct a Span of Control Assessment on Central Office
                                           Leadership Team



                                               RECITALS

   A.​ WHEREAS, Portland Public Schools (PPS) is committed to student success and equitable
       access to ensure every student can reach their full potential; and

   B.​ WHEREAS, the Board of Education has established specific priorities of 3rd Grade Reading, 5th
       Grade Math, 8th Grade Readiness and High School Graduation, to accelerate achievement and




                                                        T
       eliminate persistent achievement gaps; and

   C.​ WHEREAS, increasing costs and decreasing student enrollment necessitate a formal review and
       action plan to align district resources with the Board’s vision; and
         AF
   D.​ WHEREAS, the Board of Education finds it essential to maintain transparency and fiscal
       responsibility by providing clear direction to ensure resources are used first and foremost to our
       students' benefits.


                                             RESOLUTION


NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, the Board of Education directs the Superintendent to conduct a
Span of Control assessment of Central Office Leadership and report findings to the Board at a public
meeting no later than December 2, 2026.
