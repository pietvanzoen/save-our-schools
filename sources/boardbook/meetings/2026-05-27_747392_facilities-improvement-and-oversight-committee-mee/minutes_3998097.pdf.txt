Overview: Pool RFP Process


Paul Williams shared that this practice is not a new process for the District, but is not commonly used.
This will allow for more efficiency for the volume of contracts required to do the same category of work.
There was discussion about the ability to do competitive pricing with this process given the constraints of
Oregon laws. It was confirmed that vendors submit their pricing matrix with the Request for Proposal
(RFP). Staff explained that pricing information is obtained during solicitation and project assignment
phases and that assignments are made using a workload and qualifications-based matrix intended to
support delivery of multiple projects across the bond program.

Timeline for Athletic Facility Master Planning Process

Committee members discussed future considerations for the Grant Bowl, including controlled access and
fencing to support athletic event operations. Staff noted that fencing is not required by OSAA but may
support event management and ticketed contests.
Staff reported that ongoing lease negotiations with Portland Parks & Recreation have created schedule
risk for projects. There was discussion about communications with Portland Parks and Recreation
(PP&R).

Athletics Swing Site Plan

Staff provided an update on the swing site plans for high schools under construction and confirmed that
the middle school sports programs will use the swing sites for their feeder high schools. Jefferson
athletics remain largely on-site during construction, Cleveland athletics will relocate to Marshall, and Ida
B. Wells programs will share Jackson facilities through staggered scheduling.

Update on the implementation of the Bond Audit Recommendations

Sarah Norman shared updates on the recommendations from the Bond Audit, noting that Year 2,
Recommendation # 10 is closed. Year 4, Recommendation #5 was reopened by the external bond auditor
in April which the RISE team is reviewing, and the Year 6, Recommendation #13 was reopened by the
external bond auditor, and staff is working to provide the information they requested.

Review revisions to the Bond Accountability Committee Charter

Greg DiLoreto, the Bond Accountability Committee (BAC) Chair, commented that the BAC had reviewed
the proposed charter revisions and had recommended it be sent to the Facilities Improvement and
Oversight Committee for review. Discussion included whether the BAC should receive copies of
program-level and project-specific Program Management Plans containing key performance indicators.
Staff indicated they would confirm that BAC members have access to those materials There were
questions regarding KPIs referenced in the Committee Charge section, and whether bond expenditure
testing should be included in the district's financial audit process. Director La Forte stated the BAC
Charter would go to the Board for approval at the June 9th meeting.

Adjourn

Chair LaForte adjourned the meeting at 5:36 pm.
