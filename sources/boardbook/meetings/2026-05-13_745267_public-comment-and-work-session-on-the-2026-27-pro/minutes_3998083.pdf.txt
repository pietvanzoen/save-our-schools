2026-27 Budget Work Session

Board members' discussion focused on understanding the impacts of proposed reductions, identifying
opportunities for additional budget transparency, and exploring alternatives to planned cuts. Members
requested more information on the impact of reductions to Racial Equity and Social Justice (RESJ)
initiatives. There were questions about transportation reimbursement funds, the status of Career and
Technical Education (CTE) reductions, and the potential unintended consequences of ACCESS students
returning to their neighborhood schools due to transportation issues. There were requests to consider
delaying transportation cuts while exploring co-location or alternative service models, and examine
whether transportation flexibility offered to ACCESS families could also apply to Dual Language
Immersion (DLI) programs. Board members also sought updates on a furlough day for Service Employee
International Union SEIU) employees, and Indian Education engagement efforts.

Chair Wang adjourned the Board as a Budget Committee at 8:19 pm.
