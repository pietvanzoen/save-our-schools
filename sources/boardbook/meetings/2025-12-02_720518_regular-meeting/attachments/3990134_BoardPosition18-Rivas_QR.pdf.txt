1. Describe in your own words the mission and goals of OSBA.
   From what I have experienced, OSBA supports all districts with policy and law changes, also provides training to school board members.




2. What do you want to accomplish by serving on the OSBA board of directors?
   I would like to be an avenue of support for our region and working with board of directors to reach our goals and advocate for our districts. I
   would also like to build stronger relationships with my region and other districts.




3. What leadership skills do you believe you bring to the board of directors? Give an example of a situation in which
   you demonstrated these skills.
   I currently hold a leadership role in my place of employment, one area that I pride myself in is being available for my team and listening to
   their voice when sharing positive and not so positive information. I always take the time to listen with empathy even when our values might
   not align.

   This is also something I apply during our school board meetings and while listening to our student board members when they report out on
   what is happening at the schools or any issues that they want to bring forward.
