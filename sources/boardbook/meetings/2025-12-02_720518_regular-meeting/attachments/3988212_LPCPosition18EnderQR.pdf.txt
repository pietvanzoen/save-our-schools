1. What do you want to accomplish by serving on the Legislative Policy Committee (LPC)?
   I want to explore alternative state revenue mechanisms to increase school funding and support school safety initiatives for all students.




2. What leadership skills do you bring to the LPC? Give an example of a situation in which you demonstrated these skills.
   I listen for understanding and shared values, and can motivate a group past decision gridlock. While organizing a community soccer
   tournament, I guided the group towards a values-based decision about how to welcome people of different genders to participate.



3. What do you see as the two most challenging legislative issues faced by OSBA?
     Adequate school funding. Scrutiny of the state department of education policies and practices from the federal level.



4. What do you see as the two most challenging legislative issues faced by your region?
    Underfunding of special education and high-cost disability services. Adequate state funding for the increased costs of education.




5. What is your plan for communicating with boards in your region about legislative issues?
   My plan is to communicate by email to my region's boards about LPC and legislative action quarterly. I will collaborate with the other LPC
   members in my region or in other regions to connect with board chairs or the LPC liaison for each board.
