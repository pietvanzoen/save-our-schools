SUBJECT:         Preliminary Financial Update for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2025


This Preliminary Financial Update is an overview of Portland Public School’s financial operations
for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2025.
This report provides an overview of financial operations of District funds for the period of July 1,
2025 through September 30, 2025. Amounts reported are from the financial records of the
District and are unaudited.


As you review the First Quarter Financial Schedules, please keep the following in mind:

         The “Annual Forecast” column for each fund includes, for the most part, our current
          budgeted revenues and expenditures, as the year has just opened and no adjustment to
          assumptions have been made since budget adoption.
         The District operates on a modified accrual basis of accounting, which means most
          revenues and expenses are recorded as received and expended.
         At the start of each year, revenues earned and expenses incurred as of June 30 are
          recorded in the prior fiscal year and are not reflected in the new year reports as the prior
          year’s books remain opened for a minimum of 60 days.
         Beginning balances are reported, however, they remain unaudited draft numbers until
          the final financial report is completed in late December.
         This information is utilizing the best information available at this time.
         Additional reports are included with this first report of the fiscal year to provide additional
          context and a high level summary of the financial update.
