All Readers Need the Same Instruction!
Evidence and research-based reading
instruction, otherwise known as the Science
of Reading

Decades of neurological research across languages,
cultures, and writing systems has repeatedly
reaﬃrmed that all humans develop literacy in the
same manner and using the same neural pathways.

Students who have risk factors for dyslexia need the
same explicit phonics and phonemic awareness
instruction as everyone else. The only difference is
that they may need additional, multisensory
opportunities for repetition and practice and a
narrowed instructional focus.                          4

Oregon Dyslexia Requirements

 ORS 581-022-2445
 Universal Screenings for Risk Factors of Dyslexia
 Each school district shall ensure that every student is screened for risk factors of dyslexia using a
 screening test identified by the department when the student is in:

 (a) Kindergarten, if the student first enrolls at a public school in this state for kindergarten; or
 (b) First grade, if the student first enrolls in a public school in this state for first grade.

Oregon Dyslexia Requirements

 ORS 581-022-2440
 Teacher Training Related to Dyslexia
 Each school district shall ensure that at least one kindergarten through grade five teacher in each
 kindergarten through grade five school has received training related to dyslexia.

Teacher Training Related to Dyslexia

Over 350 educators across PPS have completed an
ODE-approved dyslexia training.
This training covers:
● Understanding and recognizing Dyslexia
● Using evidence-based practices to systematically and explicitly teach the
  foundational skills in reading; and
● Intensifying instruction to meet the needs of students with severe reading
  diﬃculties, including dyslexia
