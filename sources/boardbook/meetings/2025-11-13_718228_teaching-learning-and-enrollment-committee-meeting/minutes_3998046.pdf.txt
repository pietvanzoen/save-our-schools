Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 4:38 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller. Attendees
introduced themselves.

Public Comment

Time: 4:41 pm

    •   Rachel White: Expressed concern about the proposed Jefferson High School enrollment
        boundary scenarios and requested a three to four-year transition period.
    •   Laura Wetwood: Shared that the community developed an alternative scenario to the three
        provided by the Superintendent and advocated for Irvington to continue to feed into Grant.
    •   Christopher Boyce: Raised concerns regarding the proposed Jefferson plan.
    •   Stella Parker: Emphasized Sabin’s historic feeder pattern to Grant, requested additional
        engagement, and urged delaying changes until the new building and programs are fully in place
        to avoid family attrition.
    •   Steve Buel: Stated that teachers need stronger reading instructional supports.

Reading Instruction and Dyslexia

Time: 4:52 pm

Emily Glasgow and Elizabeth Martin

Staff provided an overview of dyslexia identification and literacy supports in the District, including
universal screening in early grades, ongoing monitoring, and districtwide use of literacy assessments.
They described training requirements for educators and progress in building staff capacity. Committee
members asked questions about state-approved dyslexia training, how teachers are supported in
completing assessments, and efforts to identify students who may otherwise be missed in the
identification process.

Required Instructional Minutes

Time: 5:11 pm

Joanna Tobin and Dr. Filip Hristić

Staff reviewed state requirements for instructional minutes, including how minutes are calculated, and
allowable exemptions. They explained districtwide compliance with the eighty percent threshold and
clarified when exemptions are required and at what level approvals occur. Committee members asked
questions throughout the presentation with discussion focusing on senior schedules, instructional time
exemptions, conference hours, study hall credit, and the distinction between schoolwide compliance and
individual student scheduling. Staff clarified legal requirements and current district practices.

Jefferson Is Rising: Enrollment Update

Time: 5:47 pm

Judy Brennan and Margaret Calvert

Staff presented an update on the Jefferson planning process, including timelines, use of an equity lens
and racial impact analysis, engagement efforts, and key themes from community feedback. They
discussed feeder pattern considerations, construction planning, and ongoing communication through
regular updates. Committee members shared observations from engagement sessions, asked questions
about student safety during construction, staffing, program continuity, and student experience. It was
emphasized that the process remains in a feedback and engagement phase with no final decisions made.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 7:02 pm by Committee Chair Rashelle Chase-Miller.
