1. 6:00 pm - Public Comment on the Proposed 2025-26 Budget
Ways to Provide Public Comment:
During the meeting
: During the meeting, public comment will rotate through school communities to ensure we hear from as many voices as possible. Because of this format, we won’t be able to confirm speaking spots in advance. Each speaker will have up to two minutes to share their remarks.
An option for providing comments virtually is available
. Please let us know you would like to attend virtually as soon as possible, and no later than 12:00 pm on 4/29, and a virtual meeting link will be emailed to you.
Outside of the meeting
: You’re also welcome to email your comments to publiccomment@pps.net.
