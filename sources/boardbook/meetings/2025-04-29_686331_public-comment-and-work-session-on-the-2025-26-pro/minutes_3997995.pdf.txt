2025-26 Proposed Budget Work Session

Time: 7:58 pm

Staff: Candice Grose – Chief of Communications; Deborah Kauforey – Chief of Staff; Michelle Morrison –
Chief of Finance

Board members asked questions and provided comments on the proposed 2025-26 budget, including
clarification and questions on potential reductions to the language access program, the use of reserves,
and strategies for state advocacy—such as the potential of calling a state of emergency on school
funding. Discussion also included upcoming seismic improvement needs, how they relate to the 2025
bond scope, and questions around PERS cost increases and the use of the PERS reserve fund. Board
members requested additional information on duplicated district services, vendor roles, and more specific
details on funding allocations.

Adjourn

The meeting of the Budget Committee was adjourned at 9:09 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.
