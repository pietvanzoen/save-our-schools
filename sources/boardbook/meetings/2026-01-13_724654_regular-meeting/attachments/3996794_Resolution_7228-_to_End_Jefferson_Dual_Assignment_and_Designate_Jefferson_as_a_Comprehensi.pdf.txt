                                        RESOLUTION No. 7228

                Resolution to End Jefferson Dual Assignment and Designate Jefferson
                                  as a Comprehensive High School

                                              RECITALS

A. In December 2022, The PPS Board of Education adopted Resolution 6627, which stated that “By the
   start of the 2026-27 school year, the Superintendent will initiate a process to develop a plan to
   increase student enrollment at Jefferson High School.”

B. Jefferson enrollment has been impacted for decades by transfer options, including voluntary busing
   to other schools in the 1980s and 1990s and No Child Left Behind in the 2000s. The largest driver
   of enrollment at Jefferson High School for the past fifteen years has been the dual assignment
   choice, endorsed by the PPS Board of Directors in resolutions 4236 in September 2010, 4358 in
   October 2010 and 4394 in January 2011. The assignment plan is unique to the Jefferson region and
   allows high school students a choice between Jefferson and either Grant, McDaniel or Roosevelt
   depending on their home address.

C. Resolution 4358 also designated Jefferson High School as a Focus High School. The initial plan
   allowed students within the historical Jefferson boundary to maintain guaranteed assignment at
   Jefferson while also providing dual assignment to one of the neighboring comprehensive high
   schools.

D. Since the implementation of dual assignment Jefferson enrollment has ranged from 391-670
   students. Over that same period, the comprehensive high schools that receive dual assignment
   students have seen significant enrollment growth, spurred, in part, by building modernizations
   completed at Roosevelt (2017), Grant (2019) and McDaniel (2021). Dual assignment has
   contributed to enrollment instability and decline at Jefferson, impacting program opportunities for
   Jefferson students.

E. In 2020 and 2025 Portland voters approved funding for full modernization of Jefferson High School.
   Groundbreaking is scheduled for May 2026, with the new building slated for completion in Fall 2029.

F. In Fall 2025, community members were invited to provide feedback on options for sunsetting Dual
   Assignment and returning to a standard school boundary assignment system. Feedback was
   received from more than 400 stakeholders.

G. To stabilize enrollment and bring equitable, comprehensive, comparable programming to the
   revitalized campus, the Jefferson Dual Assignment choice will end with the 2026-27 school year.
   Beginning in the 2027-28 school year, students will be assigned to Jefferson and surrounding
   schools through standard neighborhood boundaries, and Jefferson will operate as a comprehensive
   high school.

H. The Superintendent has developed a plan to convert dual assignment zones to standard school
   boundaries in time for implementation in the 2027-28 school year [see Resolution 7229].

                                             RESOLUTION

1. The Board of Education adopts the Superintendent’s recommendation to end dual assignment and
   designate Jefferson as a comprehensive high school beginning in the 2027-28 school year.
