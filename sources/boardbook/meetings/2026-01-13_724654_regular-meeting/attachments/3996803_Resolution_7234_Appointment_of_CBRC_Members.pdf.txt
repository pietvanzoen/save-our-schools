                                        RESOLUTION No. 7234

                    Appointment of Community Budget Review Committee Members

                                              RECITALS

A.   The mission of the Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC) is to review, evaluate, and make
     recommendations to the Board of Education (Board) regarding the Superintendent’s Proposed
     Budget and other budgetary issues identified by the CBRC or the Board. The CBRC receives its
     charge from the Board.

B.   In May 2024 through Measure 26-246, the voters of the Portland Public School (PPS) District
     passed a renewal Local Option Levy, which mandated independent community oversight to ensure
     tax dollars are used for purposes approved by local voters, and the CBRC serves that function for
     PPS.

C.   The CBRC is composed of eight to thirteen volunteer members. The Board appoints adult members
     to three-year terms with student members appointed to one-year terms.

D.   The Board recognizes that District employees and community members bring specialized knowledge
     and expertise to the CBRC and budgetary review process. The Board instructs all CBRC members
     to employ discretion, avoid conflicts of interest or appearance of impropriety, and exercise care in
     performing their duties.

E.   In fall 2023, three members were appointed to three-year terms through June 30, 2026: Aaron
     Cronan, Jen Gray-O'Connor, and Adriel Person. In fall 2024, Minyana Bishop was appointed to a
     three-year term through June 30, 2027.

F.   Recruitment of additional three-year term members yielded 18 applications to fill a maximum of nine
     positions. Five student applications were received.

G.   The CBRC pre-selection panel members were: CBRC vice chair Adriel Person, CBRC member
     Minyana Bishop, Board Director Patte Sullivan, and Board Director Stephanie Engelsman. The
     CBRC interview panel members were Board Director Patte Sullivan and Board Director Stephanie
     Engelsman.

H.   Ten adult candidates were interviewed. A student information session was hosted for interested
     student applicants.

I.   The CBRC Selection Committee recommends the Board appoint the following nine applicants to
     three-year terms concluding June 30, 2028: Andy Jacob, Anne Cherry, Angela Bonilla, Julia
     Plowman, Karanja Crews, Laura Hernandez, Rhonda Gray, Shain Corey, and Shannon Staley.

J.   The CBRC Selection Committee recommends the Board appoint the following five student
     applicants to one-year terms concluding June 30, 2026: Jiaying (Sirena) Chen, Emma Lee, Eli
     Posada, Keira Toll, Paxton Tomczyk.

                                             RESOLUTION

1.   Andy Jacob, Anne Cherry, Angela Bonilla, Julia Plowman, Karanja Crews, Laura Hernandez,
     Rhonda Gray, Shain Corey, and Shannon Staley are hereby appointed members of the Community
     Budget Review Committee for three-year terms through June 30, 2028.

2.   Jiaying (Sirena) Chen, Emma Lee, Eli Posada, Keira Toll, Paxton Tomczyk are hereby appointed as
     student representatives of the Community Budget Review Committee for one-year terms ending
     June 30, 2026.
