BACKGROUND

As needed when positions become available, the Board of Education (Board) appoints
Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC) members to assist in the annual budget
process. The CBRC reviews, evaluates, and makes recommendations regarding the
Superintendent's Proposed Budget and other budgetary issues identified by the Board. The
CBRC also monitors and advises the Board on the allocation and expenditure of Local Option
Levy funds.

The CBRC is currently composed of eight to twelve volunteer members appointed by the Board
and typically meets on a monthly basis during the course of the budget development season.
There are also student representatives.

Applications to join the CBRC were accepted through September 19, 2025. Staff received 23
applications, five of which were students.

Staff have submitted an amended CBRC charter to:
   1. Expand student membership in the committee and
   2. Increase the count of adult, three-year term members to up to thirteen.

Additionally, staff have submitted recommendations for CBRC appointees. The CBRC currently
has eight three-year positions available. Adoption of the charter amendment resolution and
appointment resolution would allow nine adult appointees and five student appointees to join the
committee.

ANALYSIS OF SITUATION

The process involved pre-selection scoring of the applications and subsequent interview of ten
adult applicants.

Applications were scored by a pre-selection panel consisting of:
   ● Adriel Person, current CBRC vice chair,
   ● Minyana Bishop, current CBRC member,
   ● Director Patte Sullivan, the Board representative to CBRC, and
   ● Director Stephanie Engelsman.

FISCAL IMPACT

No fiscal impacts.

CONNECTION TO BOARD GOALS

A primary goal of the CBRC is to review the budget through the lens of making progress on the
Board’s goals.
