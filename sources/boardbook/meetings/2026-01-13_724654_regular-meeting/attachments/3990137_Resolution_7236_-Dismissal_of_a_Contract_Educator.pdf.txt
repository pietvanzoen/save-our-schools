                                       RESOLUTION No. 7236

                                   Dismissal of a Contract Educator

                                              RECITALS

The Board has reviewed the evidence submitted along with the Superintendent’s recommendation for the
dismissal of a contract educator.

                                            RESOLUTION

The Board of Education affirms the Superintendent’s recommendation to dismiss the contract teacher
from employment with PPS. The District will notify the employee in writing of the Board’s decision.
