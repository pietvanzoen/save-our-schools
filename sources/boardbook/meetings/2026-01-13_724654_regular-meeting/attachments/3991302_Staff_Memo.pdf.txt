Subject:​      Jefferson assignment resolutions


The attached resolutions are provided for your review in advance of planned votes on January
13, 2026. The resolutions provide a path to grow and stabilize enrollment at Jefferson High
School, restoring it to a vibrant, high-demand school rooted in community, culture and
excellence while also sustaining robust programming at the neighboring high schools.
Furthermore, they return Jefferson to a comprehensive school status, ensuring comparable
access, enrollment clarity, and long-term stability for students and schools across
North/Northeast Portland.

The resolutions are the culmination of a robust engagement process in fall 2025, as described in
the December 4, 2025 Jefferson Dual Assignment Scenario Recommendation. They
demonstrate our commitment to providing students, particularly students who have been
historically underserved, well-resourced learning environments close to where they live.

End Jefferson Dual Assignment and Designate Jefferson as a Comprehensive High School
This resolution revokes the Jefferson High School focus school status and dual assignment
enrollment system established through Resolution 4358 in October 2010, designating Jefferson
as a comprehensive high school starting with the freshmen class in 2027-28. Staff will continue
to work with stakeholders to refine, plan and enact the vision shared during the Board work
session on December 16, 2025 which included an outline of a timeline, process and framework
for expanding the academic, athletic and extracurricular offerings at Jefferson as it transitions
from a focus to a comprehensive high school.

Attendance Boundaries for Jefferson High School
This resolution designates new high school attendance areas for elementary school
neighborhoods that are now in Jefferson dual assignment zones. Additionally, it clarifies that
Jefferson will continue to offer enrollment options for students who live outside the new
