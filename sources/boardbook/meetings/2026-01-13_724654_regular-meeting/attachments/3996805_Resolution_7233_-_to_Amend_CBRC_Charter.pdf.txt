                                        RESOLUTION No. 7233

                     Amendment of Community Budget Review Committee Charter

                                               RECITALS

A.   The mission of the Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC) is to review, evaluate, and make
     recommendations to the Board of Education (Board) regarding the Superintendent’s Proposed
     Budget and other budgetary issues identified by the CBRC or the Board. The CBRC receives its
     charge from the Board.

B.   In May 2024 through Measure 26-246, the voters of the Portland Public School (PPS) District
     passed a renewal Local Option Levy, which mandated independent community oversight to ensure
     tax dollars are used for purposes approved by local voters, and the CBRC serves that function for
     PPS.

C.   The CBRC is composed of eight to twelve volunteer members. The Board appoints adult members
     to three-year terms with student members appointed to one-year terms.

D.   The CBRC Charter was last updated in 2019 and outlined the mission, structure, charge, and
     protocol for selecting new members.

E.   A proposal to expand committee membership to up to thirteen members was developed.

F.   A proposal to include additional student members was developed.

G.   The charter revision adjusts the language relevant to student members to allow for additional student
     members and increases the adult membership to up to thirteen. There are no further revisions to the
     charter.

                                             RESOLUTION

1.   The Board amends the CBRC charter to allow for up to thirteen adult members and additional
     student representatives.
