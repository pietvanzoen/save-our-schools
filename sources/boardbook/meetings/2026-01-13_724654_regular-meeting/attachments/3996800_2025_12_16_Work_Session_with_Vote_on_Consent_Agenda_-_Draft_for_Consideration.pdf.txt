Attendance

Present:
Chair Eddie Wang; Vice-Chair Michelle DePass; Directors Rashelle Chase-Miller, Stephanie Engelsman,
Virginia LaForte, and Christy Splitt; Student Representative Ian Ritorto; and Superintendent Kimberlee
Armstrong

Absent:
Director Patte Sullivan

Call to Order / Introductions

The meeting was called to order at 6:12 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.

Consent Agenda (Resolutions 7226 and 7227)

Time: 6:13 pm

No items were pulled and there was no discussion.

    Actions:

    •   Director DePass moved and Director Splitt seconded the motion to adopt the Consent Agenda,

        Chase-Miller: Yes; DePass: Yes; Engelsman: Yes; La Forte: Yes; Splitt: Yes; Sullivan: Absent;
        Wang: Yes; Student Representative Ritorto: Yes (unofficial)

Comments from our Union Partners

Time: 6:14 pm

    •   Portland Association of Teachers (PAT)
        Angela Bonilla, PAT President, provided appreciation for teachers, and the Board Members who
        participated in Teacher for a Day. PAT Substitute Committee Chair Ian Mullin shared concerns
        being heard from substitutes. Concerns include large class sizes, too many substitutes competing
        for jobs which could cause subs to not qualify for health insurance in the subsequent year, lack of
        incentive pay for more challenging schools, and issues with the substitute job system Frontline.
        President Bonilla commented on incentive pay and the relationship to racism, noting that it
        supports filling schools that need additional support.

Data Dive Winter 2025: Student Achievement on MAP and iReady

Time: 6:33 pm

Staff: Dr. Renard Adams – Chief Accountability and Equity Officer; Dr. Isaac Cardona – Chief of Schools;
Kristina Howard – Senior Chief of Academics

Staff provided an overview of the results from the beginning of the year MAP and iReady benchmark
assessments, including overall performance by grade band and subject, and the actions steps that are
being taken to support students. It was noted that there some declines related to Multilingual learners that
the team is working to unpack.

Board Members asked questions and provided comments. Topics included curriculum imbedded
assessments, the science of reading, systems of supports for students, and culturally responsive
assessment.
2026-27 Finance Focus and Budget Development

Time: 7:20 pm

Staff: Michelle Morrison – Chief Financial Officer

Staff provided a finance update and shared plans to begin providing monthly financial reports in addition
to quarterly updates, noting that missing data related to the government shutdown may affect forecasting
accuracy. It was noted that the Annual Comprehensive Financial Report and Single Audit Report will also
be delayed due to the federal government’s delay in releasing required guidance for reporting. An
overview of budget development was also presented, including changes in the state revenue forecast, a
review of existing budget principles, and an outline of unfunded mandates impacting the district. Board
members discussed priorities and considerations for future budget work, emphasizing the importance of
understanding tradeoffs, maintaining alignment with Board goals, advocating against further state cuts,
and recognizing the student-facing impact of reductions in all areas of the district. Staff also summarized
ongoing engagement efforts with district and union leaders, principals, and the broader community
through regular communications and transparency tools.

Boys Volleyball

Time: 7:49 pm

Staff: Dr. Jon Franco – Senior Chief of Operations; Marshall Haskins – Senior Director, Athletics

Staff provided information on the Oregon State Athletic Association (OSAA) and boys volleyball, including
how many teams in the area elected to join OSAA and how many elected to remain club sports, as well
as summarizing the differences between the two options. Staff stated a commitment to coordinate play
from clubs around the area and potentially the state. They will reach out to other districts that have club to
coordinate schedules. Board Members provided comments and asked questions.

Staff stated a commitment to coordinating play from clubs around the area and potentially the state. Will
reach out to other districts that have club to coordinate schedules. Board Members provided comments
and asked questions, including the process for determining which OSAA sports PPS will offer, options for
freeing up funds for high school sports, and the benefits of playing club.

Jefferson High School Assignment Scenario Proposal

Time: 8:11 pm

Staff: Margaret Calvert – Assistant Superintendent, School Planning and Modernization; Deborah Kafoury
– Chief of Staff; Dr. Filip Hristić – Senior Director 9-12 Core Academics; Marshall Haskins – Senior
Director, Athletics; Drake Shelton – Principal, Jefferson High School

Chair Wang outlined the current engagement process and shared that a key theme from the community
was the desire for Jefferson to have a strong academic program. Staff presented early concepts for
expanding Jefferson’s academic and athletics offerings, describing the core pillars of the vision, potential
programming, and the planning timeline, while emphasizing that the work builds on Jefferson’s history of
academic excellence and will continue to be shaped with student and community input. Principal Drake
Shelton grounded the presentation by highlighting ongoing efforts already underway, including planning
for Advanced Placement expansion, career and technical education, and core course offerings to support
Jefferson as a neighborhood school.

Board members asked questions and provided comments on topics including enrollment forecasting,
student engagement, the upcoming District Student Council (DSC) recommendation, Advanced
Placement courses, and alignment with long-term district "right-sizing" plans. They emphasized ensuring
that academic programming and student supports are thoughtfully implemented.

There was discussion regarding next steps, deciding that the first step will be for the Board to take formal
action to end dual assignment and then to vote on a scenario. Board Members shared support for
scenarios and indicated whether they intend to bring forward any amendments. There were further
discussion regarding the scenarios and enrollment projections.

The Board discussed next steps and decided that the first step will be to take formal action to end dual
assignment before voting on a scenario. Board members shared support for the scenarios and indicated
whether they intend to bring forward any amendments. There was further discussion regarding the
scenarios and enrollment projections. Board Members should send any additional questions or potential
amendments to Rosanne Powell as soon as possible for purposes of planning for the next meeting.

    Public Comment:

        reconsider scenario F.
    •   Christinia Lively: In support of Jefferson modernization and investment in Jefferson, however
        requested to exclude Sabin from scenario C or to delay the process all together.
    •   Erin Brasell: Not in support of scenario C and does not want Sabin included in the Jefferson
        neighborhood boundaries.

Board Leadership

Time: 9:55 pm

Director Splitt introduced a Vice-Chair leadership mentor model, which includes pairing a Director with
Board leadership experience with one without experience, in co-vice-chair roles. The Board discussed the
co-vice-Chair mentor model and other potential models including Director who are not in leadership roles
rotating through leadership meetings. There was general support for Board Members taking turns
attending leadership meetings.

Committee and Conference Reports

Time: 10:08 pm

    •   Audit Committee
        The Audit Committee met to discuss outstanding audit recommendations. A new student
        representative from the DSC joined the Committee. It was noting that the December 17th meeting
        was canceled and that the Annual Comprehensive Financial Report (ACFR) presentation will be
        provided in January, due to a delay in guidance from the federal government.

    •   Facilities Improvement and Oversight Committee
        A full report will be provided at the next meeting, it was noted, however, that the Grant Bowl lights
        are in the process of being installed.

    •   Policy Committee
        The Committee will work through a review of all PPS Board Policies, starting with Section
        1.00.000 Board of Education. The next meeting is scheduled January 26, 2026

    •   Teaching, Learning, and Enrollment
        The Committee met on December 11, 2025. They heard public comment, and received a
        presentation on Charter Schools, which included the Board’s role as authorizers of Charter
        Schools, the TAG program, Division 22 requirements, and Access Academy, as well as a
        presentation on the academics plan for Jefferson High School Academic. There was a request to
        know more about French immersion high school pathways.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 10:19 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.
