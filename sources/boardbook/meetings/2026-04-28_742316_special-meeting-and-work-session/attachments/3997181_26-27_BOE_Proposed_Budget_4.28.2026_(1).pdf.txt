Bridging the Gap: Contingencies & Risks
The 2026-27 Budget assumes the following contingencies and
risks:
   ➔ Assumptions around negotiations and staﬃng costs
   ➔ Maintain a minimal 5% contingency (fund balance target)
   ➔ No reserves are available to support operational overages
   ➔ Continued restrictions on spending and Central Oﬃce hiring
   ➔ Delay of bus purchases
   ➔ Sale of ﬁxed assets
Any additional services or staﬃng not included in the budget will
require identifying an equal or greater offset.
The budget is a plan, if our situation changes, this plan would also
change.                                                                    46
