Superintendent delivers the 2026-27 Proposed Budget

Staff: Kristina Howard, Renard Adams, Michelle Morrison

The Board convened as the Budget Committee.Superintendent Armstrong delivered her budget
message.Staff presented the proposed 2026-27 budget. Board members expressed appreciation for the
detailed budget materials and the District's efforts to protect student-facing positions despite ongoing
fiscal challenges. They requested additional information in future budget documents, including data on
specialized programs, school-level student need and resource allocation, and actual student-to-teacher
ratios and class sizes. The Board also discussed the need for organizational restructuring and requested
a three-year staffing plan outlining anticipated staffing reductions, opportunities to streamline central
administration, and strategies to strengthen teacher and principal input in decision-making. Additional
discussion focused on director-level reductions, rising PERS employer costs, enrollment declines and
their impact on school funding, plans to rebuild District reserves, and advocacy for access to the
Educational Stability Fund at the state level. The Board as the Budget Committee adjourned.

Break: 8:24 pm to 8:31 pm
Bond Program Update
Staff: Sarah Norman, TJ Hopkins, Joanna Evenson

Staff provided an update on the 2017, 2020, and 2025 Bond Programs, including project progress and
budget status. Board members asked questions about project timelines, facility planning, and
implementation, and requested continued communication on major projects, future facility use, student
transitions during construction, and technology planning. Discussion also highlighted the importance of
community engagement and maximizing the reuse of District resources where appropriate.

Discussion: 7.10.020-P Districtwide Advocacy and Fundraising

Board members discussed the implementation of the Districtwide Advocacy and Fundraising Policy,
considering perspectives from Board members, student representatives, and community partners on the
role of local school foundations, equity, and fundraising. Discussion centered on the pace of
implementation, the importance of clear communication, and whether to pause the policy. While
acknowledging implementation challenges, Board members generally expressed interest in monitoring
the policy's effectiveness over time and considering future refinements, while reaffirming their
commitment to equitable access to resources for all students within the District.

Board Reflections: Rightsizing Public Engagement

Board members discussed feedback from the Rightsizing and Growing Great Schools engagement
process and identified several areas for continued exploration, including enrollment patterns, school
choice, facility investments, and program offerings. Members emphasized the importance of
understanding community priorities, ensuring broad and inclusive engagement, and maintaining a focus
on equity and accessibility as the process moves forward. The Board also expressed appreciation for the
engagement efforts to date and encouraged continued outreach and communication to support informed
community participation.

Other Business, Committee Referrals, and Future Agenda Items

Board members discussed revisiting Policy 7291, Public Contracting Rules, and requested additional
clarification regarding the process for reconsideration. Board members also discussed the Board Office
budget and wondered if any remaining funds could be used to help support Earth Stock. Chair Wang read
a statement in response to the proposed Multnomah County budget cuts to SUN Community Schools.
The Board stated support to sign on to a letter to urge the County to restore any proposed cuts.

Adjournment

The meeting was adjourned at 11:34 pm by Board Chair Eddie Wang.
