Funding for Schools
➔ $11.36 billion for the State School Fund
➔ Decrease in both Student Investment Account and High School
  Success Grants due to June state revenue forecast
➔ 20% reduction to Outdoor School funding
➔ Federal program funding is overall stable with some changes
  ◆ Title I funds are ﬂat and have been allocated to schools
  ◆ IDEA funding for students with disabilities is level
  ◆ Funding for professional development, preschool, migrant and
    multilingual learners is reduced, amounts TBD
