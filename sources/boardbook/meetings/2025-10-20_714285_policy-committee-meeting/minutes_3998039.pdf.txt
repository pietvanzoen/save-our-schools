Field Trips, Foreign Travel, and Other Off-Campus Activities Policy 6.50.010-P

Time: 4:54 pm

Board Chair Wang explained that the proposed policy revisions are intended to align with policy best
practices by reducing prescriptiveness, administrative burden, and address equity concerns. Committee
members discussed whether to delay the conversation on the policy revisions due to incomplete
materials, the purpose of the policy and what the revisions are intended to address, and how the original
policy requirements have contributed to process challenges. The committee ultimately agreed to move
the policy to the full Board for consideration, with one member indicating an intent to abstain due to
insufficient materials. A request was made to update the policy comparison document included in the
materials to reflect the most recent policy proposal.

Purchasing and Contracting Rules / Policy

Time: 5:35 pm

Staff: Emily Courtnage – Senior Legal Counsel

Staff provided an overview of the proposed changes to the Purchasing and Contracting Rules, which
include increasing the thresholds for Board approval, in order to reduce administrative burden, and better
align with inflation and practices used by similarly sized public agencies. Board members discussed
transparency and community trust, appropriate levels of Board oversight versus operational management,
comparisons to other large districts, and options for maintaining visibility into higher-value contracts
through alternative reporting methods.

The committee moved the policy to the full Board for further consideration.

Districtwide Advocacy and Fundraising 7.10.020-P (Discussion Only)

Time: 6:03 pm

Board members discussed the Districtwide Advocacy and Fundraising Policy 7.10.020-P at the request of
Director Engelsman, who outlined her concerns. The committee discussed the policy, including broader
commentary on school funding, noting that reliance on fundraising reflects ongoing underfunding of
education at the state level and places communities in competition for limited resources.

Liability of Employees of the District Policy 5.50.020-P

Time: 6:12 pm

There was no discussion. The proposed policy was moved to the full board for consideration.

Future Topic Discussion

Time: 6:11 pm

Committee members indicated policies they would like to consider for revision, including a request to look
at the policy covering interdistrict transfer, specifically to consider adding a clause that would allow for the
option to reconsider or revoke approvals due to poor attendance.

Adjourn

The meeting was adjourned at 6:13 pm by Michelle DePass.
