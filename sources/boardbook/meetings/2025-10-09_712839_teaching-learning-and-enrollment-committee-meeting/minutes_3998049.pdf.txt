Overview: Special Education Services

Time: 4:42 pm

Jey Buno and Dr. Kelly Clark

Staff provided an overview of the special education department’s key focuses over the past year,
including the shift to a neighborhood school model, expanded inclusion practices, and efforts to
strengthen instructional quality and collaboration between general and special education staff. The
presentation highlighted program data and enrollment trends by diagnosis categories and race. There
was discussion regarding disproportionality in student identification and common inclusion
misconceptions. Staff also described engagement with educators and families, key feedback themes, and
both immediate and long-term actions to improve service delivery, including stabilizing paraeducator
staffing, refining caseload models, providing professional development, and increasing communication
and transparency. Board members asked questions related to staffing and teacher recruitment, equity in
student identification, service delivery models, student outcomes, and funding limitations.
