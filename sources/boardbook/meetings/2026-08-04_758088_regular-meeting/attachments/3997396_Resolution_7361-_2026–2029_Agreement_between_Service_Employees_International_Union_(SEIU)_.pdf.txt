                                      RESOLUTION No. 7361

              2026-2029 Agreement between Service Employees International Union and
                         School District No. 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon

                                             RECITALS

The 2026-2029 Collective Bargaining Agreement between School District No. 1J, Multnomah
County,Oregon (Portland Public Schools) and the Service Employees International Union (SEIU) expired
on June 30, 2026.

                                           RESOLUTION

The Portland Public Schools Board of Education (Board) adopts and approves the 2026–2029 Collective
Bargaining Agreement between the Portland Public Schools and the Service Employees International
Union (SEIU), attached hereto as Exhibit A. The Superintendent or designee is authorized to make
technical corrections to the Agreement in order to correct typographical and spelling errors, make
formatting changes, or renumber provisions, so long as the intended meaning of the Agreement is not
thereby altered.
