2025-26 Program Investments
A Year of Districtwide Impact
Thanks to the generosity of donors across Portland, The Fund invested into student
success at the majority of schools districtwide this year.

High Impact Tutoring for K-3rd grade students                        400+
Math Supports for 9th-10th grade students                            7,000
Food Pantries in 20 Community Schools                                7,000
Total Student Support for 2025-26                 14,400+ students served

Every dollar was directed by the Parent Advisory Committee.
High Impact Tutoring is a Proven
Literacy Strategy Benefitting K-3 Students

With our investment of $660,000, 45 schools received in-person High Impact
Tutoring (HIT) in literacy skills this school year.

 ● 30 schools had tutors from Reading Results
 ● 15 schools had Educational Assistants, including bilingual tutors in Spanish
   DLI
 ● Tutoring was 3x/week - 30 minutes per session - 30 weeks
 ● Students were more likely to attend school on tutoring days
2026-27 Program Investments
Recommended by Parents and Students

On June 8, 2026, the Parent Advisory Committee recommended $152,000 for next
school year, and The Fund’s Board added $10,000 more for food assistance.

High Impact Tutoring - 7 additional schools          $144,000
Food Pantries - 9 Community Schools                   $18,000
Total Investment in PPS for 2026-27                  $162,000

With this investment, 47 PPS elementary schools will receive High Impact Tutoring
to more than 1,000 students districtwide. This is an important continuation of
services for most school communities.
