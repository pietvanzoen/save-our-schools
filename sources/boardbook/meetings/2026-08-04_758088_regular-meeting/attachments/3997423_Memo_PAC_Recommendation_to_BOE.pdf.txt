Subject:​   Recommendation for $152,000 Investment for 2026-27 School Year

The Parent Advisory Committee (PAC) for the Distribution of Donations for Staffing recommends
the following the allocations of the $152,000 available:

High Impact Tutoring - $144,000 - Seven Additional PPS Schools
With this investment, PPS will be able to serve seven additional schools with High Impact
Tutoring, which allows the district to maintain current service levels. PPS already has funding in
place to serve 40 Schools with High Impact Tutoring (31 schools with Reading Results plus an
additional 9 schools with Educational Assistants (English/Spanish). Learning Acceleration
requested additional funding to serve three to seven schools with a strong preference for
serving seven schools, thus enabling 47 PPS elementary to provide High Impact Tutoring to
over 1,000 students districtwide.


Food Pantries - $8,000
PAC representatives felt it was important to continue to support PPS Food Pantries, which they
invested $200,000 in 2025-26. With the allocation of $8,000, nine PPS Community Schools with
the highest amount of unmet need and a lack of other nutritional supports/partnerships will
receive $2,000/school for additional food support.
Also note that on June 4, 2026, The Fund for PPS Board of Directors voted to approve an
additional $10,000 for PPS Food Pantries from donations under their discretion, to be
added to the $8,000 recommended by the PAC, for a total of $18,000.

Background
The Parent Advisory Committee (PAC) for the Distribution of Donations for Staffing was
convened by Robyn Faraone, Director of Strategic Partnerships four times from April-June
2026. This committee includes an adult representative from each PPS Cluster plus two high
school students for a total of twelve representatives. More information about the committee and
links to all public meeting materials, minutes, and recordings are available at the committees
webpage: https://www.pps.net/departments/strategic-partnerships/parent-advisory-committee.
