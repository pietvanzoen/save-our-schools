Subject: ​   Recommended Ratification of Collective Bargaining Agreement with SEIU


We are pleased to present the attached tentative agreement for your ratification. Key
terms are:


 Duration                                     Three years - July 1, 2026 to 6/30/2029

 Wages & Step Movement                        2% increase effective 1/1/2027
                                              2.75% increase effective 10/01/2027
                                              3.00% increase effective 7/01/2028

                                              One step advancement will occur on July 1
                                              annually for employees who have not
                                              reached the top step. No other step
                                              advancements will occur regardless of
                                              wage effective dates.

 Insurance                                    Maintains the existing insurance
                                              contribution rates effective October 1, 2026.

 Substitute Reassignments                     Allows administrators to reassign a
                                              substitute when a job is cancelled 60
                                              minutes or less before the scheduled start
                                              time and the substitute has already
                                              reported.

 Administrative Updates                       Administratively updated language to
                                              more clearly reflect current practices on
                                              processes such as: grievance process,
                                              personnel files, employee evaluations, and
                                              overtime.
