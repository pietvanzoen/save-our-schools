                                        RESOLUTION No. 7359

                     Resolution to Rescind 2.40.020-P Student Participation Policy


                                               RECITALS

A.​ On April 27, 2026 and May 18, 2026, the Board of Education’s Policy Committee reviewed and
    considered the necessity and relevance of 2.40.020-P Student Participation Policy.


B.​ On June 9, 2026, the Board presented the first reading of each of those policies for rescission.


C.​ The public comment period was open for at least 21 days and no public comments were received.

                                             RESOLUTION

The Board hereby rescinds 2.40.020-P Student Participation Policy and instructs the Superintendent to
rescind and/or revise any administrative directives that are no longer accurate or relevant as a result of
rescinding these policies.
