                 Board Policy​                                                   2.50.020-P

                 Student Education Records
I.​ Education records are those records maintained by PPS that are directly related to a
    student.

II.​ The primary reason for the keeping and maintaining of education records for students is to
     help the individual student in their educational development by providing pertinent
     information for the student, their teachers, and their parents/guardians. These records also
     serve as an important source of information to assist students in seeking productive
     employment and/or post-high school education.

III.​ PPS will maintain confidential education records for students in a manner that conforms with
      state and federal laws and regulations.

IV.​ Information recorded on official education records should be carefully selected, accurate,
     and verifiable, and should have a direct and significant bearing upon the student's
     educational development.

V.​ PPS has an affirmative duty to provide records requested by another school district to
    determine a student's appropriate placement.

VI.​ PPS will comply with a request from parents/guardians or a student 18 years or older to
     inspect and review records without unnecessary delay. PPS will give parents of students
     with disabilities, and students 18 years or older with disabilities, an opportunity to examine
     all student education records in accordance with state and federal law, including all
     education records with respect to the identification, evaluation, and educational placement of
     the student and the provision of a free appropriate public education to the student.

VII.​PPS will give full rights to education records to either parent, or legal guardian, unless PPS
     has been provided legal evidence that specifically revokes or modifies these rights.
     However, once the student reaches age 18, those rights transfer to the student.

VIII.​A copy of this policy and related administrative directive will be made available upon
      request by parents/guardians, students 18 years or older or emancipated, and the general
      public.


Legal References: Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act, 20 U.S.C. § 1232g; 34 CFR Part
99 (2000). Assistance to States for the Education of Children with Disabilities, 34 CFR §
300.501 (2000); Individuals with Disabilities Education Act, 20 USC §§ 1400-1419; ORS
107.154; ORS 326.565; ORS 326.575; ORS 343.181; OAR 581-021-0220 to 581-021-0440.
