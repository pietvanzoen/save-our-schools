                                         RESOLUTION No. 7358

                                  Resolution to Adopt Revised Policies:


   I.​   2.10.010-P   Racial Educational Equity Policy
  II.​   2.20.010-P   Naming School District Property
 III.​   2.30.010-P   Religious and Cultural Observances
 IV.​    2.50.010-P   Public Access to District Records
  V.​    2.50.020-P   Student Education Records



                                                RECITALS

A.​ On April 27, 2026 and May 18, 2026, the Board Policy Committee reviewed and considered proposed
    revisions of:

   I.​   2.10.010-P   Racial Educational Equity Policy
  II.​   2.20.010-P   Naming School District Property
 III.​   2.30.010-P   Religious and Cultural Observances
 IV.​    2.50.010-P   Public Access to District Records
  V.​    2.50.020-P   Student Education Records



B.​ On June 9, 2026, the Board presented the first reading of the revisions to each of those policies.

C.​ Pursuant to District policy, the public comment was open for at least 21 days.

                                              RESOLUTION

The Board hereby adopts the revised policies:

   I.​   2.10.010-P   Racial Educational Equity Policy
  II.​   2.20.010-P   Naming School District Property
 III.​   2.30.010-P   Religious and Cultural Observances
 IV.​    2.50.010-P   Public Access to District Records
  V.​    2.50.020-P   Student Education Records


and instructs the Superintendent to amend any relevant administrative directives to conform to these
adopted policies.
