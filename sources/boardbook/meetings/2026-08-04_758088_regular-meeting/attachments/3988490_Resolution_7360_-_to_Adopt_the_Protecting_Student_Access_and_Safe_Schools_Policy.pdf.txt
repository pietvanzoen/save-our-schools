                                         RESOLUTION No. 7360

               Resolution to Adopt the Protecting Student Access and Safe Schools Policy


                                                RECITALS

A.​ On May 18, 2026, the Board Policy Committee reviewed and considered the proposed Protecting
    Student Access and Safe Schools Policy

B.​ On June 9, 2026, the Board presented the first reading of the revisions to each of those policies.

C.​ Pursuant to District policy, the public comment was open for at least 21 days.


                                              RESOLUTION

The Board hereby adopts the Protecting Student Access and Safe Schools Policy and instructs the
Superintendent to amend any relevant administrative directives to conform to these adopted policies.
