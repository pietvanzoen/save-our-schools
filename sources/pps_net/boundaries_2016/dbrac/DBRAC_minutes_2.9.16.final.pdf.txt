The meeting began at 4:36 pm.

Chair Jason Trombley presented the DBRAC enrollment balancing recommendations to Superintendent
Carole Smith. Superintendent Smith offered her deep thanks to the committee. DBRAC has been
responsive to many of the concerns they heard over the course of 18 community meetings. She stated
that an internal working group has been formed to look at implementation, as it is important for the
district to do this well as opposed to meeting specific timelines.

The committee and Superintendent exchanged questions and answers about several elements of the
DBRAC recommendations. She concluded by stating that she hoped to reconvene a critical mass of
DBRAC members to complete additional enrollment balancing work.

The meeting adjourned at 5:33 pm.
