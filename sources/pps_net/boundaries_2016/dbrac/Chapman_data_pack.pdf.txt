PART TWO: Issue summary
Chapman has grown by 150 students in the past four years, primarily due to new construction within the boundary.
Several small projects have occurred to maximize classroom space and other instructional space. Staff is currently
doubled up in large rooms located on the school's lower level. With no change, Chapman will likely need two more
classrooms next year, and additional classrooms in the future.
