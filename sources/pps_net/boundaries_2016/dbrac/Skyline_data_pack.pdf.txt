PART TWO: Issue summary
Skyline is a very small school serving a semi‐rural portion of the district. Because of the small size of the school, even minor
enrollment changes can have program impacts for all students. Skyline saw an unexpectedly large influx of new
neighborhood families this year. Due to urban growth boundary rules, PPS has few options for expanding classroom space
at Skyline. The school has been open to transfers in the past, and could limit those in the future. However, transfer limits
will not be enough to offset space needs for next year.
