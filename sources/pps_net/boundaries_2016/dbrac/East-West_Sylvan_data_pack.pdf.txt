PART TWO: Issue summary

West Sylvan is the largest middle school in PPS. For 10 years the school has operated on 2 campuses that are approximately
2.5 miles apart. It hosts a Spanish Immersion program for Lincoln and Wilson clusters. Neighborhood growth and transfers
caused the 6th grade population to outgrow the East Sylvan campus. This year 1/2 the 6th grade is located at West Sylvan.
The community has asked the district to find a way to bring the whole population to the West Sylvan campus. The greatest
challenge to meeting their request is the lack of common spaces and potential costs/timeline for major construction.
