Purpose of boundary review
• Establish strong, appropriately sized programs
  through an equitable, inclusive, transparent and
  on-going enrollment balancing process
• Equitably manage future enrollment growth
• Respond to community values and views on
  program offerings, enrollment & school
  configurations

School boundary characteristics
• Factors for consideration in policy and
  administrative directive
• Non-prioritized, non-exclusive




                                            5
Factors for consideration
• Stable feeder patterns
  – Most students move together to the next school
  – Elementary schools feed 1-2 middle schools, middle
    schools and K-8s feed 1-2 high schools
• Demographic diversity
  – Schools reflect the broader diversity in the district
  – Schools have space to accommodate students with
    different learning needs

                                                            6
Factors for consideration
• Compact assignment areas
  – Safe routes to school
  – A sense of community
  – Minimal transportation times
• Optimal use of existing facilities
  – Minimize facility changes
  – Conservation of natural resources
  – Student enrollment supports an adequate academic
    program

                                                   7
Factors for consideration
• Stable program and enrollment in surrounding
  schools
   – Avoid frequent changes
   – Minimize instability at nearby schools and programs
• Limit student impact
   – Affect smallest number of students possible
   – Avoid moving students more than once at a school level
   – Avoid separating small numbers of students at the next
     school level
