We want to start from the work that is already done and make use of the feedback;
an advanced starting point. Then we need to be targeted about the feedback we are
asking for and what we can use.

Committee feedback:
It (community input) can’t be wide open; we need to say what we need and not what
we don’t need, such as opening a new school.


By doing it piecemeal we can run into problems such as running up against the SE
border. If the Focus Option committee comes in the summer and says move SE
Focus Options that will impact what we’ve done.

We are not trying to create chaos for your children—but we are; it is no longer a city
where you move into a city and you expect to go to one particular school. We have to
seed the idea that we have to be more flexible. Things are going to change.

(Staff response) There are risks in localizing and globalizing; this is more piecemeal
and also is about people’s ability to process and manage change.

I don’t think it’s fair to say that we aren’t thinking of the impact at Laurelhurst or
Vestal; staff is thinking about what options do we have a Laurelhurst. It isn’t total
isolation in which we will make these decisions.

My ideal is we do it all at once. Otherwise it can come back around.

The pieces we have to move are big but not vast. Need to break it down for us.
Define the range of views.
