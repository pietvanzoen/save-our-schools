Grade progression rates (GPRs) measure the ratio of enrollment in a specific
grade to the previous year’s enrollment in the previous grade. Some
forecasters call them “cohort survival rates.” They incorporate migration,
school choice, dropouts, and retention. The “5 yr avg” columns are averages
of the most recent 5 years of grade progression rates, whereas the
“baseline” columns contain the future assumptions for GPRs excluding the
migration component. There is no baseline K-1 GPR because capture rates
are used in the model for both kindergarten and 1st grade.
