            West Side Boundary Proposal: 2b
Portland Public Schools and the community-led District-Wide Boundary Review Advisory Committee (DBRAC) are considering
a revised boundary proposal to address overcrowding at several schools on Portland’s West Side. Boundaries determine
which schools children attend.
PPS staff presented West Side Boundary Proposal 2b to a DBRAC subcommittee during a live simulcasted work session Jan. 26 at 4
pm at the school district offices. The meeting was promoted in advance on the PPS website, Facebook and Twitter.
View a video of the meeting: www.pps.net/departments/enrollment-transfer/10982.htm

The proposal draws from Scenario 2a and from additional options presented to the committee and the public at the Jan. 14
DBRAC meeting and detailed in a PPS email to West Side families Jan. 15.

pm, at Wilson High School, attended by more than 800 people.

                                     How soon could proposal 2b affect families?
If approved by the school board, the new boundaries could begin to take effect in fall 2016.
District policy says that students affected by a boundary change, and their younger siblings, can remain at their current school
through the highest grade. Fifth and 8th graders whose middle or high school assignments shift due to a boundary change,
however, attend the new middle or high school in the fall that the changes take effect. The school board could also decide to
adjust these rules if relief from overcrowding is required more quickly.
                                                             Next steps
The DBRAC subcommittee on West Side Boundaries endorsed, in concept, Scenario 2b on Jan. 26 and has drafted
recommendations. PPS and the subcommittee will present the scenario and the recommendations to the full DBRAC on Jan.
28. The committee is scheduled to vote on recommendations at the meeting.
DBRAC’s recommendations will go to Superintendent Smith, who will then make a final proposal to the board for balancing
enrollment district-wide.
                                                        Sharing feedback
Email to ppsgrows@pps.net . DBRAC committee will hear 15 minutes of testimony at the start of its Thursday, Jan. 28 meeting
at 5:45 pm, 501 N. Dixon St. The committee prioritizes hearing from at least one representative from each school community
(district-wide) that is in attendance and viewpoints or proposals that have not yet been heard.
There will be additional opportunities to comment when Superintendent Smith releases her proposal and during school board
deliberations.
