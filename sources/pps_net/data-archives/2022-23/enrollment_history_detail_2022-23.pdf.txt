Program type (i.e., elementary, middle, high, etc.) reflects the grade‐span of the Schools in that particular year. Schools changing grade‐spans and Program Types artificially inflate the Change
calculations. See School Detail and Footnotes for more information about the specific Schools that changed. In recent years enrollment counts are compiled on or about the first school day of
October. An enrolled student is defined as a student who attends one or more schools or programs within the District. Regardless of the number of schools or programs attended, each student is
counted only once; the counts are unduplicated. Schools and programs that are no longer in operation are designated as "closed."
