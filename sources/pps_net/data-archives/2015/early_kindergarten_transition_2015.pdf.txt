                              P R O G R A M AT A G L A N C E :

                              Early Kindergarten Transition
                                                                          HIGHLIGHTS

Early Kindergarten Transition (EKT) is a program that serves
incoming kindergarteners and their families.

Students who participated in the Early Kindergarten Transition
(EKT) program had higher attendance rates.

A larger proportion of EKT students met benchmarks for the
DIBELS test, a measure of early literacy.
