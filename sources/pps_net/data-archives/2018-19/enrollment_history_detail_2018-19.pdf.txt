 (1) Effective 2018-19, Boise-Eliot/Humboldt, MLK Jr, Irvington, and Sabin became K-5's, with the 6-8 grades from each school going to Harriet Tubman, a new middle school opened in 2018-19.

 elementary school, with K-5 students coming from Roseway Heights, including the Vietnamese Immersion Program. Two boundary changes were made which redirected some Rose City Park students to Lee and some Lee students to
 Rose City Park.
