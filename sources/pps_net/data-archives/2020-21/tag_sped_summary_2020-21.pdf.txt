October 2020 Enrollment Summaries are based on enrollment on October 1st as reported in the student information system (SIS).

LEP
Limited English Proficient (LEP) is the count of students eligible to receive English as a Second Language (ESL) or Bilingual Services. (Source: Synergy)

SpEd
Special Education (SpEd) is the count of students at each school with records flagged indicating participation in Individualized Education Plans (IEP), that is Special
Education program students. (Source: Synergy)

TAG
Talented and Gifted enrollment is the count of students identified as academically talented, intellectually gifted or TAG potential, as measured by Talented and Gifted
criteria. (Source: Synergy)

Gender
Starting 2018-19 Non-binary people, including intersex and gender-fluid individuals are identified in the student information system. Male and Female may not add up
to 100% for schools that have students that identify as non-binary.
