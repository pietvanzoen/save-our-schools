                                  2026-27 Budget Development
                                  Preliminary Recommendations
       Portland Public Schools is building a balanced budget for the upcoming
        school year that closes a projected $50 million General Fund shortfall.

    ➔​ 6% reductions are proposed for schools and centrally-managed services.
    ➔​ Although most areas of our district will be impacted in some way, many programs are maintained or only slightly reduced.
    ➔​ Community feedback is key; learn more and engage at https://www.pps.net/about/2026-27-budget-process.
