Meeting Called to Order
Chair Adriel Person greeted the members and called the May 4 meeting to order at 5:33 p.m. Group
discussed how to proceed given lack of seven voting members required for a quorum (only six were
present at the start of the meeting; two more (Andy Jacob and Vice-Chair Bonilla) joined later in the
meeting but two others (Co-Chairs Person and Crews) had to leave before the meeting was over.
Conclusion was that Q&A could take place, but no decisions / votes could be taken.

Two primary suggestions were brought forward and discussed as potential recommendations for the
committee’s report to the Board – increasing Budget transparency for better public understanding and to
build trust, and establishing benchmarking with local and peer district groups as well as the Council of
the Great City Schools (CGCS).

Staff and committee members reviewed and clarified sections of the budget in detail. Vice-Chair Bonilla
summarized overarching themes of:
    Transparency / legibility of the Budget for the public (e.g., differentiation between funding types);
    Feasibility of some budget assumptions;
    Establishing benchmarking with peer districts, including thoughts on defining a “peer district”
        (Seattle, Denver, San Francisco, Beaverton and Sacramento mentioned). Vice-Chair Bonilla shared
        the Secretary of State’s 2019 audit of PPS, updated in 2022, linked HERE;
    Analysis of blended classroom model;
