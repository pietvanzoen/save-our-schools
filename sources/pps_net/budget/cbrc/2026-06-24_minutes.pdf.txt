Facilitator Nichole Watson welcomed the Community Budget Review Committee and staff to the
meeting, which was called to order by Co-Chair Adriel Person at 5:02 p.m.

Housekeeping
Ms. Watson reviewed the meeting agenda and reminded the group of the CBRC question form and
tracking log, which are always available for requesting and obtaining information; all meetings are
recorded and posted, along with meeting materials on our website, and members are always
welcome to contact Cheryl Pitman (cpitman@pps.net) for assistance or logistical support. Ms.
Watson introduced Budget Director Tami Booth to discuss recruitment for next year’s committee.

CBRC Recruitment
Ms. Booth noted that a quorum had not yet been achieved, so will continue material / information
review in the hope that additional members will join. Per Ms. Booth all student members’ terms, as
well as four voting members’, expire on June 30. There will be an August-September application
window leading to October / November member appointment by the Board of Education, and staff
hope that current members will encourage friends, family and other interested community
members to apply. Any current members interested in assisting with the application process may
contact Cheryl Pitman.

Overview of Budget Milestones
Noting that it was informational, as a quorum still had not been reached, Ms. Booth shared
milestones of the 2026-27 budget process. This work culminated in adoption of the budget at the
previous evening’s Board meeting, at which this committee’s exemplary work was also called out.
Ms. Booth also noted that budget updates, for those wishing to continue to follow this work, are
posted monthly on the PPS Budget website.

Local Option Levy Data & Report Creation
Ms. Booth noted that no additional members had yet joined the meeting, which was therefore still
without a quorum, and that discussion would be informational unless more members joined. CFO

Michelle Morrison acknowledged the speed with which this year’s CBRC report to the Board was
completed, noting that this was the first year in which the Local Option Levy (“LOL”) work had been
separate from the Budget recommendation, and reported that this will be included as a “look-back”
in the District’s annual report.

Noting that a quorum had not yet been reached, Ms. Morrison noted that she and staff would be
happy to continue the meeting, to give time for additional members to join, and answer any
questions on an informational basis with a plan to schedule and additional meeting as soon as
possible. Another option could be to have two smaller groups meet separately to discuss the LOL
item, then get together to vote. Co-Chair Person opened it up to the group, which elected to
continue informational review of the slide presentation, send the materials out to the entire group
along with a link to the video of this meeting to review and work asynchronously, then schedule an
additional meeting to vote.

CFO Morrison noted that the LOL ballot measure language requires PPS to provide fiscal
accountability and independent oversight, which is the function of the CBRC. The language also
directs how funds may be used (link to ballot measure language).

Accounting Process Overview:
Ms. Booth continued the overview, inviting questions as they occur, and noting the broad categories
through which Local Option Levy funds are managed, ensuring that every dollar collected is used
correctly and reported accurately. Director Booth reviewed the Revenue and Expense Summary
Balance Sheet, as well as Journal Detail.

Budget Manager Alexandra Martin broke down Average Teacher Cost information and discussed
relating to Enrollment trends to ensure as accurate a budget forecast as possible, noting the
enrollment data is a snapshot in time. Both Ms. Martin and Ms. Booth discussed how teacher
costs are kept separate from administrative costs. CFO Morrison noted that the district would be at
least 20% short without this levy and expressed gratitude to the community on the District’s behalf
for its five-year renewal of the LOL in 2024.

Facilitator Watson led group discussion of next steps; Committee member Shain Corey noted that
the LOL draft recommendation is fairly complete but could refine, review with co-chair and send
final draft to the Committee for review, then call a final Committee meeting for a vote. The group
concurred on this path; Co-Chair Person commended Mr. Corey for strong work on a tight timeline
and expressed hope that this could be completed before her term ends on June 30.

CFO Morrison noted that Budget Manager Alexandra Martin has accepted a position at San
Francisco Unified School District; the group shared praise for Ms. Martin’s work and
congratulations on her move; the meeting was adjourned by Co-Chair Person at 6:01 p.m.
