Meeting Called to Order
Co-Chair Adriel Person greeted members, counted and acknowledged quorum of voting members
present, and called the May 4 meeting to order at 5:37 p.m..

Housekeeping and Reminders
Budget Manager Alexandra Martin reviewed the agenda, including links to the Question Intake form, Q&A
Tracking Log, and Committee materials and resources on the CBRC website. Upcoming meeting dates
were reviewed, as was the change in due date for the Committee’s report, from May 11 to May 8, was
discussed.

2026-27 Proposed Budget Review / Report Creation
Manager Martin introduced the proposed report document (linked HERE), including themes surfaced at
the Committee’s May 4 meeting, as a framework to be populated by the Committee.

Committee members expressed frustration with the compressed timeline and discussed ways to keep
their report high-level in order to maximize impact, even if it could not be as detailed as the group prefer.
Members suggested formatting the report as theme-based paragraphs.

Co-Chair Person and Manager Martin guided the group through the outline, proposing that the
Committee focus on key agreed-upon themes that can be expanded upon succinctly. The Committee
also discussed two sections drafted by member Anne Cherry, following the May 4 meeting. The group
agreed to begin the report with a brief opening statement regarding the shortened timeline.

Vice-Chair Bonilla linked the 2025-26 CBRC report (linked HERE) in the meeting’s Chat, and the group
discussed ideas and recommendations that might still be relevant, including:

   Compressed timeline; proposal to start earlier in future years, if public-meeting rules permit
   Areas of Inquiry the group didn’t have time to review.
   Transparency
   Long-term outlook and strategies to improve budget sustainability
   Unrealistic assumptions
   Proportionate reductions / Preservation of programs (possibly as an example under transparency)
   Efficacy of blended classrooms; the group expressed interest in a recent report from Dr. Adams’
    Research, Assessments and Accountability team (linked HERE)
   Revenue diversification / Alternate revenue sources, e.g.
        A Community Benefits Agreement incorporated into MODA Center renovation package
        In-kind donations to replace materials or services that PPS otherwise pays for
        Leasing rather than selling surplus properties, or developing low-income housing to both
         generate revenue and increase enrollment
   Reassessment of contracted services
   Advocacy efforts
   Request for a formal response from the Board of Education

The group discussed how to limit document-editing access to avoid creating a quorum and agreed to
limit direct editing access to Co-Chairs Person and Crews, Vice-Chair Bonilla, and Budget Manager
Martin. Remaining members will draft and email their responses to this group for compilation and
editing. The rest of the group will have view-only access, which will also be publicly accessible.

Co-Chair Person moved to approve the themes listed by the group for inclusion in the report, with the
understanding that drafting would occur over the next 24-48 hours in order to meet the May 8
submission deadline. Aaron Cronan seconded the motion, which was carried with six votes in favor,
none opposed and no abstentions, with student member Eli Posada voting in favor.

Manager Martin and Co-Chair Person recognized the service of members whose terms are concluding.
Laura Hernandez, who joined the Committee this year, has stepped down. The three-year terms of Aaron
Conan, Jen Grey-O’Connor, and Adriel Person’s conclude June 30, as will those of this year’s student
members. Student member Eli Posada stated that he joined the Committee with very little knowledge of
PPS’s budget or operations, and found the CBRC process educational, interesting and engaging. The
group thanked him for his participation, expressed deep appreciation for his perspective and shared their
hope that his comments would be reflected in the Committee’s report.

Manager Martin stated that if the report is completed early enough on May 7, she will email it to
Committee members by end of day, for review before submission to the Board by noon on May 8. If
later, and if time permits, she will send it for review by noon May 8, then submission by end of day.

Presentation of the report to the Board of Education is scheduled for May 12 during their 6:00-9:00 p.m.
meeting; more specific time will be shared when available. Executive Assistant Cheryl Pitman will
