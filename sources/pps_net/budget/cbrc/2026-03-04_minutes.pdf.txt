Meeting Called to Order
Presiding Officer Adriel Person greeted members of the Community Budget Review Committee (CBRC)
and called the March 4, 2026 meeting to order at 5:35 p.m.

Welcome and Introductions
Facilitator Nichole Watson welcomed members and reviewed the meeting agenda.

Housekeeping
Nichole Watson referenced the previous month’s discussion regarding ways for committee members to
share questions both during and outside of meetings. She reviewed the CBRC Questions Form and the
associated Q&A Tracking Sheet, which includes responses to questions submitted through the form, as




                                                                                                      1
well as questions raised during CBRC meetings. Committee members may also contact logistical
support staff member Cheryl Pitman (cpitman@pps.net) directly with any questions or for assistance.
Budget Director Tami Booth reviewed the committee’s meeting schedule and highlighted the upcoming
joint work session between the CBRC and the Board on March 31. Committee members may attend
either virtually or in person. Tami Booth asked members to notify staff if they plan to attend in person so
that adequate seating can be reserved.

PPS Equity Framework
Nichole Watson reviewed the PPS equity lens, with focus on transparent and student-centered decisions.

CBRC Leadership Selection
Nichole Watson reviewed three slides -- “Our Why,” the CBRC Charter, and Meeting and Committee Norms
– that provide context and guidance for members and outline expectations for meeting conduct. She
also outlined key leadership decisions before the committee at this meeting.

Nichole Watson also reviewed CBRC leadership duties in the context of the committee’s responsibility to
review, evaluate, and provide a report of recommendations to the Board of Education. As such, the
committee would need to determine whether to vote on leadership structure at this meeting or to defer
until the March 31 meeting.

Committee members discussed the committee’s role in reviewing and evaluating budget information in
order to develop recommendations for the Board of Education. Questions were raised regarding whether
the committee would have sufficient time to fully analyze information within the current timeline. Staff
noted that committee members have access to staff support and may submit questions or request
additional information as needed. Staff also acknowledged the compressed timeline and encouraged
members to submit questions early so that staff can gather relevant information.

Committee members also raised questions about the format and comparability of budget data across
years and expressed interest in access to underlying data sources to support analysis. Staff
acknowledged the request and noted that additional information could be provided where available.

Nichole Watson then led a discussion of committee leadership structure, including whether to vote on a
structure, elect members to leadership roles, or postpone the decision until the March 31 meeting. Adriel
Person, who served as a co-chair during the previous year, is currently serving as Presiding Officer until
the committee determines its leadership structure.

The Presiding Officer’s responsibilities include convening meetings, supporting facilitation, maintaining
meeting flow and adjourning meetings and, most importantly, supporting development of the
committee’s report by coordinating section assignments, assisting with synthesis of input, ensuring
progress toward completion, and supporting consensus-building among members.




                                                                                                            2
Committee members asked about leadership structures used in prior years. According to staff, previous
committees have used a variety of models, including co-chairs sharing responsibilities or a chair and
vice-chair structure, often reflecting the prior experience of committee members.

Members noted that the volume of work associated with the committee’s charge can be significant and
that a shared leadership structure may help distribute responsibilities among multiple members.
Suggestions included establishing co-chairs and additional vice-chair roles to help manage the workload.

The committee discussed whether to make a decision regarding leadership structure at the current
meeting or defer the decision until the next meeting. Some members expressed support for voting at this
meeting in order to establish leadership roles promptly and support the committee’s work within the
limited timeframe.​

During the discussion, a student member revisited a question raised at the February meeting regarding
the voting status of student representatives. Nichole Watson reported that she consulted with District
legal counsel and clarified that current policy does not permit student members of Board committees to
cast official votes. She noted that at the Board level, the student representative’s vote is not official but is
recorded in the meeting minutes. Nichole Watson encouraged student representatives to share their
perspectives and to vote for the record at CBRC meetings, noting that the issue has been documented
and may be revisited in the future.​

Committee members noted the limited timeline for completing the committee’s work and discussed the
value of establishing a leadership structure promptly in order to begin organizing the report development
process.

Angela Bonilla moved, and Andy Jacob seconded, a motion to establish a leadership structure consisting
of a chair and a vice-chair for the current year. After brief discussion, Adriel Person moved to amend the
motion for leadership structure to consist of two co-chairs and one vice-chair. The amendment was
seconded by Angela Bonilla.

Nichole Watson opened the floor for discussion. A member suggested that the committee proceed to
finalize the motion currently on the floor. Angela Bonilla then moved to close debate, and Andy Jacob
seconded the motion.

The motion to close debate was carried with eleven votes (including one student) in favor, none opposed,
and one abstention.

The committee discussed interest in leadership roles; several members expressed willingness to serve in
various capacities, including co-chair and vice-chair positions.

Members considered potential processes for identifying and selecting candidates, including providing an
opportunity for interested members to share their perspectives and relevant experience; suggestions
included using a form to gather candidate statements.

Staff noted that collecting written candidate statements would represent a new approach for the
committee, though in prior years interested members have informally shared their perspectives and
priorities before a vote. Members also discussed possible voting methods; however, it was noted that
electronic voting would not be appropriate within the format of a public meeting.

Angela Bonilla moved, and Adriel Person seconded, the nomination of Adriel Person and Karanja Crews
to serve as co-chairs of the CBRC for the current year. The floor was opened for discussion, and the
nominees briefly shared introductory remarks. Nichole Watson then called for a vote.

The motion was carried with twelve votes (including two students) in favor, none opposed, and one
abstention.

Review Questions and Answers
Committee members engaged in a question and answer discussion with district staff, including Dr.

   ●​ Counselor allocation
   ●​ Analysis of Educational Assistant allocation vs. overage pay

Additional CBRC meetings will follow in March, April, and May as the Committee prepares its
recommendations to the Board.
