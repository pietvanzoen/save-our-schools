Orientation to Committee Resources
Chief Morrison reviewed the ERS Budget Hold-Em planning tool, which can be accessed by signing in
with an email address. While presented as a game, it’s not intended to make light of budget issues but
can be a helpful resource for visualizing budget trade-offs; CBRC members are encouraged to take
advantage of it, as well as to share the link to the app with others who may be interested.

2026-27 Proposed Budget Overview and Orientation to Committee Resources
Chief Morrison stated that Superintendent presented the proposed 2026-27 Budget at the April 28 board
meeting and a press conference was held this morning. A budget training was presented to the Board at
its April 14 meeting (linked here, begins at 2:45 in the video; presentation is linked here). Delivering two
volumes on time was a monumental feat, and the volumes include many useful features including a
staffing “heatmap” and important ratios and breakdowns. Volume 1 is linked here, Volume 2 here, and
printed copies are available as well if desired. Committee members should submit any questions that
arise via their existing question form, in order to ensure same information available to all. The Budget
staff will also provide their Excel spreadsheets upon request. The sheets are view-only, but members can
make copies if desired in order to manipulate and again, for consistency, are encouraged to submit any
questions via form.

Ms. Watson opened the floor to questions / discussion. Several members expressed interest in prior
years’ CBRC documents (slide 8 in this evening’s presentation), then asked to review the 2026-27
Proposed Budget presented by the Superintendent. Chief Morrison reviewed with the committee, with
emphasis on Revenue and Expenditure Assumptions; staff noted that while the two Volumes are related
to each other they’re not redundant and there is no throughline. While members were again encouraged
to submit questions via the form to ensure everyone can access information, Director Booth also
expressed availability to connect directly if needed.

Chief Morrison clarified staffing classifications in the CSL slide. “Licensed” includes educators such as
counselors, multilingual teachers, and special education staff, as defined by state requirements.
“Classified” refers to represented support staff, while “non-represented” includes primarily specialized,
technical or managerial roles, largely based in the central office. Administrative roles may be licensed or
non-licensed and generally serve in executive or central office capacities. Additional details are included
in the appendix to the slide deck.

Members raised concerns around the potential for underestimating step increases and COLA, risking
future budget shortfalls or mid-year reductions, emphasizing transparency. Chief Morrison
acknowledged the inherent risk in projections, noting that assumptions have been shared publicly and
that the proposed budget is currently balanced.

Chief Morrison responded to a question on the Governor’s Executive Order requiring 2025-26 furlough
days to be made up in 2026-27, noting that Chief of Staff Kafoury is working closely with state officials to
clarify requirements and implementation; the 2026–27 budget assumes a full school year.

Co-Chair Bonilla reported that she and Vice-Chair Crews met with Budget Manager Martin to discuss
possible report creation process; Ms. Martin took extensive notes and is incorporating into questions in
the Tracking Log for all members to review. Ms. Bonilla recommended that committee members review
the tracking log and prior-year report material asynchronously, focusing on any threads from last year’s
report that should be pursued, prior to the next meeting, a working session, on May 4. Portions of the

Given that student members lack voting ability, Co-Chair Bonilla solicited their voice. Student member Eli

The committee briefly discussed how Athletics are funded and where they fit into the budget given
different funding sources; a similarly brief discussion around PERS was held; specific questions will be
submitted via the form.

Co-Chair Bonilla summarized by reminding members to review last year’s materials, consider any
threads for follow-up, think about overarching themes, and submit all questions via the form to ensure
information is brought forward accessibly for all. Facilitator Watson noted that this annual task is a
heavy lift and expressed gratitude on behalf of the Budget staff and the district. Co-Chair Bonilla
adjourned the meeting at 7:30 p.m.
