Equity Lens in Rightsizing
PPS is committed to transparent and
student-centered decisions.
Our district continues to direct resources based on
student need and uses data and community input
to inform decisions.
Key questions guided this process:
➔     Who is impacted?
➔     Does this recommendation reduce
      disparities?
As the District faces diﬃcult decisions to close the
budget gap, it is critical we hear from communities
to inform planning for future school years.
