Graduation & Cohort Completion Metrics Dashboard

A high school graduation cohort tracks students who entered the ninth grade together during a specific cycle. The four-year graduation rate represents the percentage of those students who earn a traditional or modified diploma within four calendar years. Longitudinal trend sheets from 2009 through the most recently certified cycles are accessible below:

Longitudinal Master Tracking Logs
  • Graduation & Completion Rates By Subgroup (2009-2026)
  • Graduation & Completion Rates By School Campus (2009-2026)
4-Year Cohort Trend Evaluations
  • Graduation Rate Trends By Accountable High School
  • Graduation Rate Trends By Student Race & Ethnicity
  • Graduation Rate Trends By Special Program Membership
ℹ️ Note on Five-Year Completion Rates: Students who earn alternative certificates, adult high school diplomas, or GEDs are explicitly accounted for within the five-year tracking datasets. Official student completion logs are certified and distributed by the Oregon Department of Education (ODE) across twelve accountable district high school campuses.
