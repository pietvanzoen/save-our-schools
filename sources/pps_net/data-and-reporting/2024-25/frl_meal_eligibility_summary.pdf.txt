This report shows how many students are eligible for free meals. The enrollment data is as of October 1st, whereas the
counts and percentages of students eligible for free meals reflect updates through November.

The federal government reimburses the District at a set rate for breakfasts and lunches through the National School
Breakfast Program and the National School Lunch Program, respectively. Breakfasts and lunches are available to all
students at no cost at a site based on two reasons (See Note column):
1. Schools identified as Community Eligibility Provisioning (CEP) schools. Through CEP, the federal government provides
meals at no charge to all students, including students who would not normally qualify for free or reduced meals.  CEP
was developed to reduce paperwork for families and decrease barriers to accessing school meals by using available data
to determine the school community's eligibility for this provision; therefore paper applications are not collected or
processed for CEP schools. For the 2024-25 school year, all schools and programs receiving service through the PPS
Nutrition Services Department have been designated CEP.

2.Head Start is only available to students from low-income, qualifying families, and all students are eligible for free
meals.
Free Meal by Direct Certification % is the percent of total students who receive free meals through Direct Certification.
Direct Certification status is determined by state agencies and shared electronically with school districts. Students or
families qualify for Direct Certification through a number of avenues such as eligibility for SNAP or TANF, Migrant Ed,
McKinney Vento, or foster care.

Starting in 2023, Medicaid was added as another way for families to qualify for free meals in Oregon. Students who
qualify for free meals via Medicaid are shown as Free Meal by Direct Certification.
