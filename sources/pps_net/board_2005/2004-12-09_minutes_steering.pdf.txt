      Portland Public School District 1J, Multnomah County, Oregon
            Board of Education’s Steering Committee Meeting
                           December 9, 2004

                              INFORMAL MINUTES

The meeting of the Board of Education’s Steering Committee convened at 12:10
PM at the call of Co-Chair Brim-Edwards in the Willamette Conference Room of
the Blanchard Education Service Center located at 501 North Dixon Street,
Portland, Oregon.

Present were:

      Board Members
      Julia Brim-Edwards
      Derry A. Jackson, Sr.
      Doug Morgan
      Lolenzo Poe
      Bobbie Regan
      David Wynde

      Staff
      Vicki L. Phillips
      Bill Farver
      Sarah Ames
      Julie Joel

      Others
      Paige Parker – The Oregonian

As Chair of the Community and Staff Relations Committee, Director Regan
presented and discussed the committee’s work plan and goals. She noted that
Student Representative Suleima Cortez would be joining her committee, as Ms.
Cortez’ schedule at school does not allow her to attend Student Achievement
Committee meetings. She stated cluster planning and the Strategic Plan were
also upcoming issues for her committee and other Board committees. (A copy of
the “Community and Staff Relations Committee – Proposed Work Plan, 2004-
2005” is attached to the minutes or may be obtained in the Board Support
Office.)

Co-Chair Brim-Edwards suggested that development of a legislative agenda be
added to the list for Community and Staff Relations.

As Chair of the Finance, Audit and Operations Committee, Director Wynde
referenced his list, “Strategic Issues – Balance of 2004-05.” (A copy of this
document is attached to these minutes or may be obtained in the Board Support

Office.) Discussion ensued among Board members and staff regarding the
committee’s issues.

As Chair of the Ad Hoc Personnel Committee, Director Morgan stated his
committee would come up with the framework and process for evaluating the
performance of the Superintendent. He indicated he contacted Board members
from other school boards that he met through the Broad Institute for sample
evaluation material. He said he would have a draft ready for review the following
week. Director Morgan noted it was important to link the work of the Board with
Superintendent Phillips’ leadership.

As Chair of the Student Achievement Committee, Director Jackson presented the
“Committee Action Plan” and “Timeline for SACOM Activities.” (Copies of these
documents are attached to these minutes or may be obtained in the Board
Support Office.) Discussion ensued among Board members and staff regarding
the committee’s issues.

In the absence of Director Williams, Chair of the Education Options and
Professional Development Committee, Director Regan, a member of that
committee, stated the committee would be holding hearings in January for three
proposed charter schools – Victory Middle School, an international middle and
high school, and a K-8 school. She said they would also be looking at a plan of
operation for the Franklin World Language Institute, and noted Odyssey (at
Hayhurst) also needed a plan of operation. Director Regan said the committee
would develop a plan and strategy for professional development, a strategy for
expansion of immersion and other focus option programs, and continue with a
review of the Student Enrollment and Transfers Policy. She added they would
look at the cluster planning process to see how focus options and charters fit in
on a city-wide basis.

Co-Chair Brim-Edwards asked Ms. Joel to prepare a template for committee
chairs to use to track issues, which would also assist Board staff in getting items
scheduled ahead for full Board meetings.

The meeting of the Board’s Steering Committee adjourned at 1:07 PM at the call
of Co-Chair Brim-Edwards
