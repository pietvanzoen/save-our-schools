                  2022-23 Elementary Schools Lottery Results Summary
Key Definitions
TR = Applicant who lives outside the host school's neighborhood
NH = Applicant who lives within the host school's neighborhood
Offered = Offers may be conditional upon meeting additional requirements.
Waitlisted = Student has been placed on a waitlist and may be offered a position if one becomes available before the end of September 2022.
Not Offered = Student was not placed due to waitlist limit.
Canceled = The application was canceled because the applicant was approved to a higher choice.
