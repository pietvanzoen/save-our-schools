Focusing the Phase 1 Recommendation
 To steer the work of the Guiding Coalition in December 2020, the Advisory Team clarifies that the Southeast Guiding Coalition Phase 1
 recommendation will only include enrollment and program balancing elements that will become effective for the 2021-22 school year. This thinking
 tool is a result of us examining our collective culture with the Southeast Guiding Coalition, and working to disrupt tenets of white supremacy culture
 showing up in our process. We’ve had robust discussions weekly that have helped us make adaptations to our process. We have a great opportunity
 to understand so many experiences and narratives from our work doing multiple levels of engagement, and now is our opportunity to take this in and
 synthesize this to bolster our process and strengthen our relationships as we move into making recommendations. These are:
    ● Identifying K-5 schools and programs that will feed to Kellogg Middle School

The Phase 2 recommendation will build upon the Phase 1 recommendation and address additional elements that will become effective starting in the
2022-23 school year. These are:
   ● K-12 neighborhood program feeder patterns for all southeast schools
   ● K-12 DLI/focus option program placement and feeder patterns for all southeast schools
   ● K-12 special education focus classroom placement and feeder patterns for all southeast schools
   ● K-12 neighborhood program boundary adjustments for all southeast schools
   ● Pre-K classroom placement for all southeast schools

During October and November, the Guiding Coalition reviewed fifteen scenarios that covered grades K-12 and numerous schools and programs outside
the potential Kellogg feeder pattern. Below are tools to support the December Kellogg-only focus:
    ● Table showing essential information about the potential Kellogg feeder schools included in prior scenarios
            ○ The 6-8 portions of Arleta, Bridger, Creston, Lent and Marysville K-8 schools
            ○ The 6-8 enrollment of Atkinson ES/Mt. Tabor MS and Woodstock ES/Hosford MS students
    ● Visuals of all Kellogg feeder patterns proposed to date

 Anaylzing a Kellogg-only scenario
 The Phase 1 Kellogg-only scenario will need to include as much certainty as possible, while also providing ample flexibility to align with Phase 2
 recommendations. Please consider:
    ● Prioritizing the needs of grades 6-8 students currently enrolled in K-8 schools
           ○ HS feeder patterns, changes to K-5 schools will be central to Phase 2
    ● Avoiding partial assignment to Kellogg: If a school has co-located programs, assign all or none to Kellogg in Phase 1
           ○ Co-located programs will be address in Phase 2
    ● Limiting proposed Kellogg enrollment in 2021-22 to no more than 700 students, leaving room for future adjustments if needed
    ● As part of implementation planning, allow any students who begin at Kellogg to remain there through 8th grade, even if the student lives in
       area or attends a program that shifts in the Phase 2 recommendation
