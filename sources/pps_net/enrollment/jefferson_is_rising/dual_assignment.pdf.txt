                         Jefferson High School Dual Assignment Process
                                          WHAT IS DUAL ASSIGNMENT?
Students in the Jefferson High School attendance area have a unique choice between Jefferson Middle College for
Advanced Studies and a comprehensive high school, based upon their home address. Use the School Finder tool to
verify your dual assignment options.

                                              HOW DOES IT WORK?
Families in a dual assignment zone are required to select a high school. It is advisable to explore program
opportunities at each school prior to making a decision. Begin by visiting each school’s webpage
(www.pps.net/8thgradeinfo), and feel free to reach out to them with any questions.

                                       HOW TO DECLARE YOUR CHOICE?
Currently attending 8th grade families receive an online survey in the Fall to designate their choices. Students who
are not currently attending can register with the school of their choice beginning in February for the next school
year.
Grade Level Specifics
Future 9th graders: Dual Assignment decisions are made in the Fall of 8th grade. Currently attending families
receive information packets and work with their 8th grade school counselors to designate their choice during the
High School Focus Option Lottery transfer cycle in November. By choosing their high school early, students can
sign up for classes, get to know their school and begin a successful transition to high school.
9th and 10th graders: Students are guaranteed placement at either school of choice.
11th and 12th graders: Students interested in Jefferson should call the school at 503-916-5180 to set up an
interview. You will meet with a vice principal to review your student’s school experience and determine whether
the school can meet your student’s needs and maintain the track for an on-time graduation. Your student may be
referred to your comprehensive high school for enrollment.



For more information, contact your dual assignment choice schools or the Enrollment and Transfer Center at
enrollment-office@pps.net or call 503-916-3205.
