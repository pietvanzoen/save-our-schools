Glossary of Terms | PORTLAND | Public Schools

Term Definition

The geographic boundary or attendance area that determines which

Catchment school a student is assigned to based on home address.

Career Technical Education. These are course progressions that help
CTE students learn about a field such as construction, culinary or health

sciences.

Dual Language Immersion; a program model where students learn
DLI content in two languages to build bilingualism, biliteracy, and cross-

cultural competency. Mandarin and Spanish are the two DLI programs
in the Jefferson cluster.

Historical policy allowing students to choose between Jefferson and

Dual Assignment another high school.

The goal of ensuring that all high schools in PPS have balanced
Enrollment Parity student enrollment numbers and equitable access to academic and
extracurricular opportunities.

A K-5, K-8 or middle school in a feeder pattern, or designated path for

Feeder School students to advance from one school grade grouping to another.

KPI Key Performance Indicators; metrics used to measure success.

A set of documents showing potential boundaries for Jefferson and
Scenario other high schools and the impact the change could bring to each
school.

SME Subject Matter Expert; district leader available for clarification.
