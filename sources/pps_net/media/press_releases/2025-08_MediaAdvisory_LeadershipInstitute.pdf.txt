                  PPS Superintendent, Dr. Kimberlee Armstrong, to Deliver Bold Call to Action
                                           at 2025–26 School Year Launch

August 4, 2025 (Portland) - Portland Public Schools (PPS) will officially kick off the 2025–26 school year with a
keynote address from Superintendent Dr. Kimberlee Armstrong, delivered before an audience of more than
1,000 educators, staff members, PPS Board Directors, elected officials, and a very special guest.

The event sets the tone for the year ahead, as Dr. Armstrong delivers a powerful send-off rooted in urgency,
unity, and an unshakable belief in what’s possible for Portland’s students.

Her remarks will unveil the district’s sharpened academic priorities and reaffirm PPS’s commitment to its
Continuous Improvement Plan, which centers on four high-impact focus areas: early literacy, middle-grade
math, attendance and enrollment, and on-time high school graduation. The plan serves as both compass and
commitment, grounding every school in data-informed strategies while centering the humanity of our
students.

WHEN: Wednesday, August 6, 2025, 8:15 AM – 9:15 AM (8 AM arrival is strongly encouraged)

**Media availability with Superintendent Armstrong immediately following.

WHERE: Benson High School, 546 NE 12th Ave, Portland, OR 97232

WHO: Superintendent Dr. Kimberlee Armstrong, Sr. Chief of Academics and Schools, Kristina Howard

Photo Availability: Superintendent keynote, principals leaving to breakout sessions (no filming while they are
in sessions) and a few moments with the special guest.

About Portland Public Schools
Portland Public Schools, founded in 1851, is Oregon’s largest PK-12 school district, with more than 43,000 students in 81
schools. PPS aspires to graduate compassionate, critical thinkers who are able to collaborate to solve problems, and lead
a more socially-just world. Central to this goal is affirming and operationalizing our deeply held community value of
racial equity, social justice and inclusion. Learn more at pps.net.
