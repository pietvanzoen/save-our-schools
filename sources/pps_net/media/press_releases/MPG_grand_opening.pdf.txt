          Grand Opening of the new hayu alqi uxyat building celebrates
      PPS's Multiple Pathways to Graduation alternative education programs

Sept. 18, 2024 (PORTLAND, ORE.) - We are excited to welcome you to the Grand Opening Celebration of
the new hayu alqi uxyat building that celebrates PPS's Multiple Pathways to Graduation alternative
education programs. The event is this Saturday, September 21st, 2024, from 10a.m. to 1p.m. at 1530 NE
Glisan St., Portland, OR 97232.

This new state of the art facility is now home to Alliance High School, DART/Kuumba School, Teen Parent
Services and Childcare, and Reconnection Services and Center. The 85,000 SF multi-story building unites
these programs in a central location, providing comprehensive support for students as they work their
way to graduation. hayu alqi uxyat is a Chinook word for Many Future Paths.

The Multiple Pathways to Graduation Mission is to provide educational options for all youth that
empower, engage, and prepare them for college, work training, and global citizenship while serving as a
vanguard for systemic educational change.

"PPS is committed to ensuring every student reaches their highest academic and personal potential," said
Superintendent Kimberlee Armstrong. "This new facility, designed with love and care, embodies our
belief that we never give up on learners. It stands as a testament that it's never too late to realize your
full potential."

An inspirational and transformative event
The event will include a special Ribbon Cutting Ceremony with inspirational and transformative stories
from MPG students and staff. The ceremony will be followed by self-guided tours of the innovative, all
electric, earth friendly building. Highlights include specialized CTE program facilities for automotive,
manufacturing, culinary, natural resources and digital media, a 500-seat gymnasium, a makerspace, a
rooftop deck, and a multi-age childcare center with an outdoor playground.

“What you will see in the hayu alqi uxyat building is the level to which the students, alumni, families, and
staff of all our programs had voice in designing the physical space. From the rooftop gardens to the
murals and quotes of our students throughout - we are very clear that our charge is to serve students for
whom school has been challenging and, at times, isolating. Working with tribal members from the Siletz,
Grande Ronde, and Chinook nations was instrumental to completing the selection process of our
building’s name hayu alqi uxyat, which means many future paths, in Chinook Wawa language. At hayu
alqi uxyat, we will be able to meet each student where they’re at and support these exceptional students
until they thrive.” said Korinna Wolfe, M.Ed, MSW Area Senior Director, Multiple Pathways to Graduation.

Innovative, Earth-Friendly Building
hayu alqi uyxat is an all-electric-powered building that has a solar array to generate some of its own
electricity. Its mass timber construction combined with natural daylight and increased energy efficiency
significantly lowers the school’s carbon footprint. Principles of trauma-informed design were centered
throughout the building to create an environment that supports the diverse needs of the MPG students.
To learn more visit the MPG project bond webpage

PPS Bond Program has significantly improved every school in the district
The hayu alqi uxyat building is the ninth PPS school to be modernized or rebuilt through the School
Building Improvement Bond program. The bond program has also provided health, safety and
infrastructure projects that have touched every school in the district. This has provided improved water
quality, security upgrades, new roofs, asbestos and radon remediation, lead paint stabilization, new fire
alarms and sprinklers, more accessibility, and seismic retrofits. The Bond Program’s projects employed
hundreds of workers from local companies.

PPS thanks the voters for their support as we continue to upgrade our aging schools, most of which were
built before WW2.

SUBJECT LINE: Media event: Jason Lee Elementary School to Receive Library Makeover 6/12

MEDIA ADVISORY:
Jason Lee Elementary School to Receive Library Makeover as Part of the Richard M. Robinson Literacy Champion Award
from Scholastic in Collaboration with Council of the Great City Schools

WHAT: Earlier this year, Portland Public Schools was named the winner of the Richard M. Robinson Literacy Champion
Award by Scholastic, the global children’s publishing, education, and media company, in collaboration with the Council
of the Great City Schools (CGCS), a coalition of 78 of the nation’s largest urban public-school systems. The award
recognizes a CGCS member school district for demonstrated progress in advancing reading achievement with the goal of
further supporting gains by increasing access to digital and print books, magazines, and materials within the district.

As part of this honor, Portland Public Schools has selected their very own Jason Lee Elementary School as the prize
recipient of a library makeover. As part of the library makeover, which will happen on Monday, June 12th, Scholastic will
donate more than 6,000 high-quality, age-appropriate books, a one-year site license to Scholastic Literacy Pro®, an
award-winning blended learning program that provides 24/7 access to more than 2,500 fiction and nonfiction ebooks
for purposeful independent reading, allowing personalized student choice while providing teachers with real-time
data to inform instruction. In addition, students will receive a summer book pack of 10 titles to take home and own,
encouraging independent reading beyond the classroom.
