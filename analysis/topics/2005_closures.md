# The 2005 closures (Smith, Edwards, Richmond neighborhood program): what the Board had in front of it

Sources: `sources/pps_net/board_2005/`. These are Board minutes, board books and memos from the old PPS site, recovered from the Internet Archive on 2026-10-19 (manifest there). The board books are scans, so quotes from them come from OCR and should be checked against the PDF.

## Decision
- On March 14, 2005 the Board adopted, by unanimous voice votes (6–0; Director Jackson absent), Res. 3250 (Ash Street boundary), 3251 (close Edwards, merge with Abernethy), 3252 (close Richmond's neighborhood program), 3253 (Rieke/Hayhurst boundary) and 3254 (close Smith) [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-03-14_minutes_board_action.pdf p.9].
- Timeline: proposals from the "Enrollment Data Analysis" went out Feb 11/14, 2005 [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-02-14_board_book_part1.pdf p.7]. Formal recommendations followed on March 7 after three Board hearings, and the vote was March 14 [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-03-14_minutes_board_action.pdf p.27]. On Jan 10 the timeline still pointed to a decision at "the late February meeting" [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-01-10_minutes_regular.pdf p.9].

## Which PSU forecast was used
- Each closure resolution recites that the District "currently enrolls 48,029 students with the Portland State University Population Center forecasting that enrollment will decline further to 44,373 by 2015" [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-03-14_minutes_board_action.pdf p.27].
- Both numbers come from PSU's **March 2004** report, built on October 2003 enrollment: "from present levels of 48,029 to 44,373 in 2015" [PPS:psu_prc/psu_prc_enrollment_forecast_2004-05_base_oct2003.pdf p.21].
- Analysis: the resolutions therefore used a forecast one cycle old. Even the "currently enrolls" figure is the October 2003 count. The October 2004 count was 46,823 [PPS:psu_prc/psu_prc_enrollment_forecast_2005-06_base_oct2004.pdf p.26], and PSU's report built on it was not published until August 2005, after the vote.
- Analysis: the March 2004 report's August 2004 school tables forecast Smith K-5 rebounding from about 200 (2007) to 284 by 2015 [PPS:psu_prc/psu_prc_enrollment_forecast_2004-05_base_oct2003.pdf p.112]. The Smith proposal does not cite that school-level forecast.

## The Smith case as presented (Feb 11, 2005 proposal)
- The rationale is current data: Smith had 219 students, down 19 from 2003-04. Its neighborhood capture rate was 73%. Of 79 neighborhood students attending other PPS schools, 17 went to Hayhurst for Odyssey. The proposal also cites building size (16 classrooms) and staffing formulas [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-02-14_board_book_part1.pdf p.9] [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-02-14_board_book_part1.pdf p.10].
- On projections it says only: "The data shows a continued trend of enrollment decline in most of the Cluster Schools" [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-02-14_board_book_part1.pdf p.11].
- Boundaries were to send "the bulk of the students to Markham and Capitol Hill since Maplewood is close to capacity" [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-02-14_board_book_part1.pdf p.12].
- Odyssey context: Odyssey moved from Chapman to Hayhurst in 2004-05, which "increased the enrollments by 112 students (From 212 to 353 students in grades K – 8)" [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-02-14_board_book_part1.pdf p.8]. Analysis: the figures don't add up (212 + 112 ≠ 353). The scan reads the same, so the inconsistency is in PPS's original.
- Savings stated in Res. 3254: $160,000 standard support plus about $148,000 operating, and an estimated $300,000 annual rental value. That gives roughly $600,000 a year, "somewhat offset" by first-year relocation costs [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-03-14_minutes_board_action.pdf p.28].
- Change before the vote: the district would keep the Smith building "in its inventory until the extent of future projected enrollment in that cluster becomes clearer" [PPS:pps_net/board_2005/2005-03-14_minutes_regular.pdf p.6]. Smith is still vacant today (see `rightsizing.md`).
