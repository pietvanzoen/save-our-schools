# Bond & seismic: contradictions, differing numbers, and gaps

Companion to `analysis/topics/bond_and_seismic.md`. Each item gives the conflicting figures with citations. Notes
marked "Analysis:" are my reading of the conflict.

## A. Numbers that differ across documents

1. **Educational & Technology subtotal: $288M vs $242M.** The staff memos and the Aug 2025 OSM update say
   "Educational & Technology Improvements - $288M" [BB:3988494 p.2; BB:3996542 p.3], but their parts add up to $242M
   ($176M + $10M + $56M), which is the figure in Exhibit A and the BAC memo [BB:3991930 p.1; BB:3992439 p.4].
   Analysis: $288M looks like an error that was carried forward. The $1,827,100,000 total only works with $242M.
2. **Physical Facility Improvements: $269M vs $290M.** The BAC planning memo heading reads "Physical Facility
   Improvements - $290M" [BB:3992439 p.2], while every other document says $269M ($190M + $79M)
   [BB:3991930 p.1; BB:3988494 p.1].
3. **Ballot summary vs bond plan on Jefferson.** The ballot title names only "Cleveland, Ida B. Wells (formerly
   Wilson)" for renovation or replacement [BB:3990971 p.1]. Resolution 7038 also covers completing Jefferson
   [BB:3990414 p.1-2], and $100,000,000 of 2025 money goes to Jefferson [BB:3996975 p.14].
4. **HS modernization targets vs outcomes.** The Dec 2024 targets were JHS $366M, CHS $360M and IBW $360M, a combined
   $1.086B that would leave ~$470M for elementary and middle schools [BB:3992439 p.6-7; BB:3992567 p.2]. The approved
   plans total $1,387,911,260 ($466,007,500 + $472,553,760 + $449,350,000)
   [BB:3993106 p.3; BB:3993120 p.3; BB:3990445 p.2]. The actual E&M remainder is $171,738,255 [BB:14383796 p.17].
   Analysis: about $298M less than the $470M that was promised as possible.
5. **Cost-reduction savings range: "$67 million to $33 million" vs "$67–$35".** The Apr 7, 2025 memo text says $67M
   to $33M, but its table sums to $67M–$35M [BB:3996343 p.2]. BAC Report #47 cites "$33M-$67M"
   [BB:3996453 p.3]. In July 2025 the BAC reported "$36M-$45M" [BB:3996656 p.3]. Savings stated in the approved
   plans: $24,745,297 + $11,091,090 + $5,650,518 = $41,486,905 [BB:3991107 p.19; BB:3989409 p.21; BB:3990440 p.20].
6. **Cleveland "original" budget.** The May 2024 comprehensive plan building budget was $468,644,850
   [BB:3993120 p.3]. The Year 6 audit says "approximately $481 million" including $20M planning, and footnotes $468M
   in internal reports [BB:3996481 p.27]. The BAC (Dec 2024) used $469M [BB:3992567 p.2]. The BAC (Jan 2026) says
   Cleveland is "budgeted at $449 million, up from the original budget of $442 million" [BB:3996976 p.3]. That
   appears to be the 2025-bond share only ($449,229,760 on Mar 10, 2026 [BB:3996975 p.14]), and the "$442M" figure
   could not be traced to any document.
7. **Cleveland budget table error.** In the Sept 2025 CHS presentation, "TOTAL HARD COSTS" for the 2024 plan shows
   $291,057,660, which is Jefferson's 2022 figure. The CHS subtotal directly above it is $383,318,991 + $1,650,000
   [BB:3989409 p.21].
8. **Split of HS budgets between bonds does not add up exactly.**
   - CHS: 2020 design $23,679,515 + 2025 $448,911,745 = $472,591,260, which is $37,500 above $472,553,760
     [BB:14383796 p.16-17]. The separate $137,500 swing-site line is not in the total.
   - On Mar 10, 2026, IBW was 2020 $25,318,534 + 2025 $432,000,000 = $457,318,534, above the approved $449,350,000
     [BB:3996975 p.13-14]. From May 2026 it reconciles: $20,000,000 + $429,350,000 [BB:3997258 p.17-18].
9. **2025 bond contingency jumped by $42,906,806.** It was $83,050,000 on Mar 10, 2026 [BB:3996975 p.14] and
   $125,956,806 "as of 4/1/26" [BB:3997163 p.14]. The program total rose from $1,827,100,000 to $1,869,896,806 and
   later to $1,870,006,806 [BB:14383796 p.17]. No explanation was found. Analysis: possibly the premium from the
   Feb 2026 bond sale, which PPS traditionally adds to contingency [BB:3992439 p.8]. This is unconfirmed.
10. **Remaining 2020 authority.** Res 7223 cites $343,055,000 of unused 2020 capacity [BB:3989962 p.1]. The staff
    report says a sale of up to $90,000,000 would leave "approximately $199,090,000" [BB:3988609 p.1]. Arithmetic
    gives $253,055,000.
11. **Procedeo contract value.** The memo gives base compensation of ~$55,066,000 plus unspecified incentives
    [BB:3996768 p.2]. The resolution lists $61,541,860 [BB:3996787 p.10]. The incentive portion is not broken out.
12. **Total voter-approved bonds.** The BAC says "$4.6 billion" in Jan and July 2026 [BB:3996976 p.3;
    BB:14498905 p.3] and "$4.7 billion" in Apr 2026 [BB:3997319 p.3]. The 2020 bond is also stated three ways:
    "$1.2 billion" [BB:3990414 p.1], "$1.206B" [BB:3996453 p.1] and $1,208,000,000 [BB:3989962 p.1].
13. **Seismic ROM totals (2024 Phase 1) differ between staff memo and Holmes v2.0.**

    | Item | Staff memo May 2025 [BB:3989487 p.2] | Holmes v2.0 [BB:3991109 p.14] |
    |---|---|---|
    | URM-only retrofit | $129,251,000 | $118,581,000 |
    | Full retrofit, URM schools | $340,050,875 | $326,594,375 |
    | Non-URM schools | $585,497,250 | $576,346,000 |
    | Total | $925,548,125 | $902,940,375 |

    Analysis: v2.0 likely drops sites (it excludes decommissioned, leased and in-progress sites). No reconciliation
    was found. School-level values in Appendix E are rounded compared with Appendix A (e.g., Beach $14,580,000 vs
    $14,577,500) [BB:3991109 p.12, p.172].
14. **Number of risk-scored sites.** Holmes says 75 [BB:3991109 p.3]. Parsing Appendix E gives 74 scored campus pages
    out of 96, with 22 marked "Not in Seismic Risk Assessment Scope" (my count, `seismic_facilities.csv`).
15. **Seismic budget source.** Res 7109 combines the $190M priority scope with HS savings ($366–398M total), sets
    aside a ~$100M reserve, and makes seismic the next priority [BB:3996420 p.26-27]. The Aug 2025 OSM update places
    seismic inside the $190M after the $100M reserve [BB:3996542 p.3]. The Mar 2026 memo says the seismic budget
    "is principally created by savings on other 2025 bond projects" [BB:3988546 p.1]. In Apr 2026 the BAC says staff
    are "close to identifying the $100 million" [BB:3997319 p.3]. No budget line labeled "seismic" appears in the 2025
    bond financial tables [BB:14383796 p.17]. Analysis: which line funds the ~$100.2M program, and whether the
    $100M emergency reserve was actually created, is undocumented.
16. **Seismic first tranche: "8–10 schools" vs what was funded.** Two full and seven targeted 2025/2020-bond
    projects, plus four 2017-bond roof projects, are counted as seismic [BB:3990267 p.9]. Analysis: only Fernwood and
    Rose City Park get full-campus mitigation, the kind Res 7109 contemplated. The other projects are partial, and the
    Board asked that future reports say whether a school's seismic needs are "fully addressed" [BB:3990212 p.3].
17. **Jefferson approval date.** The staff report says the Board approved the keep-students-on-campus approach in
    Dec 2023, then says "On April 2, 2023, the Board approved this project approach, with a $491M total project
    cost" [BB:3993106 p.1, p.3]. The Mar 2025 memo refers to Board materials of April 2, 2024 [BB:3996307 p.1].
    Analysis: "2023" is probably a typo for 2024.
18. **Resolution 7051 date.** The Cleveland and Wells staff reports say "On April 11, 2025 Board Resolution 7051
    adopted a Framework" [BB:3993120 p.1; BB:3990445 p.1]. Minutes show adoption on Feb 11, 2025 [BB:3996300 p.2].
19. **Opening dates.** The BAC (July 2026) says "All projects show a school opening of fall 2029" [BB:14498905 p.4].
    Project cards list "Tentative Sub Com: Fall 2030" for JHS and IBW [BB:14383796 p.19, p.24]. Analysis: these are
    consistent if fall 2030 means site and field completion after move-in, but the label is ambiguous.
20. **Beach retrofit timing.** Mar 2026 says Summer 2027 and 2028 [BB:3990267 p.13]. Apr/May 2026 say anticipated
    Summer 2028 [BB:3997163 p.33; BB:3997258 p.34].
21. **FIOC Nov 2025 slide date.** The deck is filed for the Nov 10, 2025 meeting but titled "11/3/25"
    [BB:3996723 p.1].
22. **Jefferson cost per the Year 6 audit vs staff.** The audit describes growth "from $366 million to $491 million
    as of December 2024" [BB:3996481 p.6]. The comprehensive plan table gives $490,752,797 for the 2023 pivot
    [BB:3991107 p.19].

## B. Gaps in the archive

1. **FIOC minutes are empty.** Every FIOC `minutes.md` (June 2025 – Sept 2026) is only a link to "Informal Minutes"
   with no content, e.g. [MTG:2025-08-12/703009], [MTG:2026-01-20/727712]. Committee discussion of seismic
   priorities and of consolidation (the stated purpose of Res 7110) cannot be reconstructed without video.
2. **No vote tallies found for:** Res 7380 (Grice-Adair CMGC
   exemption, Sept 15, 2026; reported approved [BB:14454647 p.12]); Res 7382 (bond audit responses, Sept 29, 2026).
   Draft minutes for those meetings were not in the archive.
3. **Formal seismic prioritization document.** The Board requested one on Mar 10, 2026 [BB:3990212 p.3]. No
   school-by-school ranked list with formula scores (deferred-maintenance score, enrollment flag) appears in the
   archive. Only the formula and the selected schools are published [BB:3988546 p.2-3].
4. **Seismic Risk Reduction Plan.** Res 7109 required one by Sept 1, 2025, with a multi-year schedule and costs
   [BB:3996420 p.26]. What was presented (Sept 9, 2025 and Mar 10, 2026) covers only the first tranche. No
   multi-bond plan for the deferred high-risk campuses (Jackson, Glencoe, Grout, Roseway Heights, Lee, Rieke, Buckman
   and others) was found.
5. **Facility Condition Index and capacity by school.** FCI is cited as a criterion [BB:3996658 p.2;
   BB:3996657 p.19] and the district FCI is "poor" [BB:3992439 p.2], but no per-school FCI table is in the archived
   bond/seismic material. The rightsizing decks use utilization and functional capacity maps, but the text layer
   holds no per-school values [BB:3997148 p.17]. The `capacity` and `fci` columns in `seismic_facilities.csv` are
   blank for this reason.
6. **Interaction of planned retrofits with closures.** No document says what happens to the Rose City Park
   full-campus retrofit (Summer 2028–2029) if Scenario A is adopted, or to the Beach targeted retrofit (both
   scenarios). Staff only said contracts are a flexible pool [BB:3990212 p.3].
7. **Grant conditions.** It is not documented whether SRGP awards (Marysville, completed Fall 2026; Ainsworth
   awarded; Fernwood submitted) [BB:3996450 p.2; BB:14383796 p.27] carry continued-use or repayment conditions if a
   school closes. Marysville closes in both scenarios [BB:14505788 p.2].
8. **Swing space for Fernwood.** The full-campus retrofit runs Summer 2027–Summer 2028 [BB:3990267 p.10] while
   Beverly Cleary becomes a K-5 receiving Irvington students [BB:14509719 p.2]. Whether students stay on site
   during the work is not stated.
9. **Use of the E&M modernization pool ($171.7M).** No allocation criteria or school list was found
   [BB:14383796 p.17].
10. **Contingency use and premiums.** Only the year-6 audit recommendation on contingency methodology is documented
    (resolution text in [BB:3996823 p.4]). No 2025-bond contingency drawdown log was found.
11. **Name and campus mapping uncertainties** used in `seismic_facilities.csv`:
    - "Bridger Creative Science" (scenarios) vs Holmes "Bridger" and "Creative Science / Clark".
    - "Clark" Mandarin immersion.
    - "Dr. Martin Luther King Jr." mapped to Holmes "King".
    - "Brentwood MS" has no Holmes page; Lane MS (Holmes score 9.0) is not mentioned in the scenarios.
    - "Boise-Eliot/Humboldt" spans two Holmes campuses.

    These should be checked against a PPS school-site list.
12. **Grice-Adair seismic.** The building needs a code-required seismic upgrade for E occupancy [BB:3996747 p.2]. No
    seismic cost is broken out within the $21–25M build-out estimate.
13. **High-school capacity analysis.** No PPS document reconciles 1,700-seat modernizations with the rightsizing
    1,100 HS threshold or with the TSCC's ~11,000 ten-year forecast [BB:3996394 p.5; BB:14505788 p.1]. Only the
    N/NE high schools are analyzed, in the Jefferson Scenario C memo [BB:3988475 p.4].
14. **Video evidence not used.** No YouTube transcripts were in the archive for this pass. Board members' individual
    positions in the Jan 7, 2025 referral, the Mar 10, 2026 seismic discussion and the Dec 2, 2025 Procedeo debate
    are known only from summary minutes.
