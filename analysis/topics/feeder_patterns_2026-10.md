# Scenario A/B feeder-pattern charts (posted 2026-10-22)

Compiled 2026-10-22. Data: `analysis/data/feeder_patterns_2026-10.csv`, with one row per (scenario, school) statement on
the charts. That is 235 rows; each chart labelled "A & B" is entered once under A and once under B.

## What was posted and how to read it

- On 2026-10-22 the rightsize Scenarios page gained two panels, "Feeder Patterns Scenarios A" and "Feeder Patterns
  Scenarios B" ("See proposed K-5 and K-8 feeder patterns"). They link 13 one-page PDFs
  [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/scenarios_2026-10-22.html](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/scenarios_2026-10-22.html).
  - Separate A and B charts cover four areas: Cleveland, Ida B. Wells, McDaniel and the citywide Dual Language Immersion
    (DLI) chart.
  - Five charts are labelled "Scenarios A and B" and carry a "No changes between Scenarios A & B" banner: Franklin, Grant,
    Jefferson, Lincoln and Roosevelt. Each is linked under both panels [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/scenarios_2026-10-22.html](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/scenarios_2026-10-22.html).
  - All 13 are stamped "Version 1.0 10202026" and "Proposal for community review"
    [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/scenario_ab_feeder_lincoln.pdf p.1](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/scenario_ab_feeder_lincoln.pdf).
- **Layout.** Each page covers one high school, with its middle schools below it and then the elementary schools.
  - A solid line means all students continue; a dashed line means only some do.
  - A yellow "Split" box says who goes where. A grayed box marks a school that feeds this middle school but continues to a
    different high school.
  - A red CLOSING box says where that school's students go. A NEW tag marks a change in the scenario, and on A charts a
    "Scenario B change" tag flags what B does differently.
  - A side panel ("What's changing") and numbered "Good to know" footnotes add detail
    [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/scenario_a_feeder_cleveland.pdf p.1](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/scenario_a_feeder_cleveland.pdf).
- **Context.** These charts are the scenario versions of the current-state feeder charts posted 2026-10-10
  [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/high_school_feeder_patterns_en.pdf p.1](https://www.pps.net/fs/resource-manager/view/b6a86fd0-fae1-4a0a-af46-72ee81348705#page=1).
  - The McDaniel, Grant, Jefferson and Roosevelt charts repeat the current-state note "Reflects the sunsetting of dual
    assignment, effective 2027-28 school year" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/scenario_ab_feeder_grant.pdf p.1](https://github.com/timaelliott/save-our-schools/blob/main/sources/pps_net/rightsizing/feeder/scenario_ab_feeder_grant.pdf).
  - Res. 7228 ended dual assignment in January 2026 [BB:3998053 p.3](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/3998053?org=915#page=3).
