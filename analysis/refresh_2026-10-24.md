# Refresh 2026-10-24: Willamette Week backfill

Willamette Week's schools section for 2026 (168 stories back to Dec 3, 2025) and its other news sections (scanned back to late December 2025 for PPS mentions) were read through wweek.com's content API from the repository owner's computer on 2026-10-23. Every PPS-relevant story missing from `sources/news/index.jsonl`, or held only as a search snippet, now has a metadata record. Per CLAUDE.md rule 8, each record has an own-words summary and at most two short quotes.

- **59 records added and 11 upgraded** from snippet-only to full. The upgraded stories are the Sept 15 audit, the Sept 17 recall, the Sept 19 Title I story, the Sept 21 letters, the Sept 22 Secretary of State referral, the Sept 30 Procedeo story, the Oct 2 audit response, the Oct 5 scenarios, the Oct 6 protest, the Oct 7 audit dispute and the May 27 resolution.
- **Skipped as not about PPS:** a statewide universities bill story, a Preschool for All story, Multnomah County chair-race items (including endorsements involving former director Julia Brim-Edwards), and city or state items with only passing PPS mentions.
- **Murmurs columns** are kept only for their PPS items and are titled by those items. Several repeat a standalone story from the same week, and both records are kept.
- **Dates and names.** Record dates follow the date in the URL; the API timestamps are UTC. Private individuals (parents, students, letter writers, and the plaintiff in the Oct 21 lawsuit) are described by role.
