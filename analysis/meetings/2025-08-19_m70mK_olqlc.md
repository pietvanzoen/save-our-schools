## Summary
- This was a governance retreat, not a policy meeting. No decisions or votes [YT:m70mK_olqlc@1:13:19].
- **TTI behavioral assessment** (Joseph) [YT:m70mK_olqlc@0:29:58–1:12:34]:
  - Profiles: 3 "persuaders" (named as Kimberly, "Relle" and Michelle), 2 "supporters" (Christy, Patty), 1 "conductor" (Virginia) and 1 "coordinator" (Eddie). There were no analyzers or implementers.
  - Joseph warned the team is prone to "initiative overload" and weak follow-through. He also noted a split between "command" and "collaborative" styles.
  - He advised a few high-level goals, written action plans, public dashboards and limits on after-hours email [YT:m70mK_olqlc@1:05:42].
- **Board self-assessment:**
  - Splitt said members share values and disagree mainly on "how". She cited the bond as an example [YT:m70mK_olqlc@0:57:20].
  - La Forte worried the board keeps leaving meetings "with no plan to do the work plan" [YT:m70mK_olqlc@1:00:21].
  - Wang (medium confidence): "there's still strong disagreement about what that looks like" [YT:m70mK_olqlc@1:03:23].
- **Governance relationship** (Jenkins): board vs. superintendent lanes; the "board as a whole" directs, not individual members. Also a Harvard case study on a board member who over-reached on principal hiring [YT:m70mK_olqlc@1:16:21–2:07:17].
- **NSBA effective-board standards; do-well/struggle exercise.** Groups reported struggles with:
  - communicating the vision;
  - community engagement (noting loss of community engagement staff);
  - data access;
  - unified governance [YT:m70mK_olqlc@3:05:06].

  One member proposed board goals cover "providing safe modernized schools", not only academics [YT:m70mK_olqlc@3:07:24].
- **Further-action list:**
  - a protocol for responding to constituent email;
  - data training;
  - work plan time;
  - a new strategic plan (the current one expires in 2025) and revisiting board goals;
  - "board briefs" [YT:m70mK_olqlc@3:10:36–3:14:25].
- **Legal duties briefing** (Toncray):
  - ORS 332.072/.075 are broad and dated.
  - The $150,000 superintendent contract-delegation threshold "hasn't been adjusted" for a roughly "$2 billion organization" [YT:m70mK_olqlc@3:18:12].
  - Serial-meeting rules and public comment at committees were also covered (per minutes).
- **Strategic planning** (Armstrong): the district continuous improvement plan has four goals to 2028 (attendance, literacy, math, 9th-grade success) [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:35:46].
  - Armstrong flagged that **in spring 2026 "we have budget reduction conversations. We have to talk about school optimization"**. She tied this to the 2030 plan and to completion of the high-school modernizations [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:38:03].
  - A member asked for sequencing of work through 2028/2030 [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:40:21].
- **Superintendent evaluation:** Wang wants assessments to be "learning tools" [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:42:44]. A member recalled a past pay-for-performance contract clause as "a disaster" [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:48:50]. There was interest in a board self-evaluation (Sullivan's contact; about $750) [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:49:36].
- **Pluses and deltas:**
  - Members wanted more time and quarterly retreats.
  - A member (unidentified) objected that the board/superintendent focus areas, deferred from 8/5 to this retreat, were again not discussed [YT:m70mK_olqlc@4:57:29].
