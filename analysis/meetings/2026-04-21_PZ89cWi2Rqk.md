## Summary

- **Invited report (Greathouse, SIAOS):** re-analysis of the district's airflow data for McDaniel HS. Many classrooms get roughly 2–3.5 ACH from HVAC alone. The second purifiers for most of 68 schools are or were in storage. She recommends deploying two purifiers per classroom and making their use required [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:10:28] [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:12:01] [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:14:35] [BB:3997154].
- **Staff/consultant presentation:** "indoor air quality is a safety issue, not an amenity". Modernized Cleveland, Wells and Jefferson are designed with higher ventilation, MERV-13 filtration, CO2-controlled dampers and operable windows in every classroom (about 1% more energy use) [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:23:56] [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:34:43] [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@1:01:01] [BB:3998092 p.1].
- Purifiers: more than 6,000 units delivered since 2020, about 2,500 in storage; deployment is "highly variable" and many have been sent back. Staff cited past teacher pushback about noise [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:40:23] [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:41:26] [YT:PZ89cWi2Rqk@0:59:30].
