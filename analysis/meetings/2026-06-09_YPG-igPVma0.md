- **Layoffs dominated public comment.** Most speakers came from the Sunnyside Environmental School community, and others were Whitman and Cleveland staff. The issues were seniority calculation, late notices and focus-school qualifications.
  - Armstrong said the structural deficit is over $160M, and so far the district has addressed only about 60% of it [YT:YPG-igPVma0@1:10:37] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YPG-igPVma0&t=4237s)).

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Not a formal agenda item.** Rightsizing came up only through the budget and transportation discussion.
- **Armstrong on fall 2026.** She expects the fall 2026 closure, boundary and program-move decisions to involve trade-offs that cannot be negotiated with every affected person. "Some things we're going to have to decide and then mitigate later" [YT:YPG-igPVma0@4:03:16] [YT:YPG-igPVma0@4:03:36].
- **Lottery and focus-school demand.** Public commenters said focus schools such as Sunnyside retain families in PPS. Sunnyside reported 126 kindergarten lottery applications for 26 seats [YT:YPG-igPVma0@0:26:43] [YT:YPG-igPVma0@0:43:27].
  - **Analysis:** this bears on the Reso 7307 principle of balancing choice programs against neighborhood schools.
- **Merger history.** One parent cited the Creative Science–Bridger merger as an example of a failed blending of teaching philosophies [YT:YPG-igPVma0@0:38:29].

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **BAC quarterly report (DiLoreto)** [YT:YPG-igPVma0@1:42:07] [YT:YPG-igPVma0@1:43:39]:
  - The committee now has 10 members.
  - 2012 bond is nearly 100% spent. 2017 is 95% spent, with about $49M to be reassigned. 2020 is 59% spent, with about $0.5B available. 2025 is 6% spent.
