## Summary
- **Rightsizing timeline unveiled** [BB:3996734]:
  - Fall 2025: review past decisions, including SEGC outcomes, and compile a current-state analysis.
  - Winter 2025-26: form an advisory committee and finish the current-state analysis.
  - Spring–Summer 2026: engage, develop a decision framework, and develop scenarios.
  - 2026-27 school year: scenario development, stakeholder engagement, and a recommendation.
  - Calvert: Jefferson dual assignment "dovetail[s] into what is going to come next around right sizing," including DLI locations and elementary boundaries [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@1:42:08] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JEfmEEMeU3Y&t=6128s)) [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@1:42:30].
- **Jefferson is Rising (sunset of Jefferson dual assignment):**
  - Six engagement sessions drew 401 participants, and 221+ feedback forms had come in [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@1:28:08] [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@1:28:53] [BB:3996734].
  - Upcoming: Dec 6 open house at Jefferson; Dec 8 public meeting with the final recommendation; Dec 16 board work session; board vote January 2026 [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@1:17:11] [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@2:24:21].
