## Summary
- **Leadership.** Wang was re-elected Chair (Res. 7136) and DePass Vice-Chair (Res. 7137), both 7-0 [BB:3998011 p.2].
- **Standing committees for 2025-26 (Res. 7138, 7-0).** The facilities committee was renamed the "Facilities Improvement and Oversight Committee" [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:27:01]. Wang stressed that committees develop recommendations and the full board decides [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:27:25].
- **Budget update (CFO Morrison)** [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:35:00]:
  - **State funding:**
    - The state school fund is set at $11.36B under a new current-service-level calculation.
    - Special-education funding requests were not funded.
    - The Student Investment Account (SIA) and High School Success grants dropped slightly.
    - Outdoor School was cut 20%, about $665K, equal to one day of the program [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:53:02].
  - **Federal funding:** Title I and IDEA (special-education) funds are flat. Federal "assurances" are changing, including Homeland Security compliance.
  - **New risk:** SB 916 (unemployment benefits for striking workers) is a new cost risk for a self-insured district.
  - **Reporting:** monthly financial reports to the board will start, with October enrollment and cost data informing projections [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:42:16]. Informational sessions are planned on revenues, cost drivers, PERS, capital projects and curriculum.
- **Board comments on the budget:**
  - Engelsman called for full state funding [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:46:24]. She also asked for a legal review of the Outdoor School cut.
  - A director asked for closer CBRC (community budget review committee)-board collaboration and clear bond-spending information flows [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:50:36]. The caption garbles the name the chair calls ("Dr. Sp."), so attribution is uncertain.
  - Wang floated parent fundraising and the Fund for PPS to cover the Outdoor School gap [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:53:37].
- **Lobbying Registration policy.** First reading [BB:3998011 p.3].
- **Audit Committee report (Sullivan).** The year-six bond performance audit found "disconnects" in bond oversight information reaching the board and the Bond Accountability Committee (BAC). Recommendations outstanding now total 43 [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:18:40].
- **Beaverton boundary.** Res. 7148, a PPS–Beaverton SD boundary change for a 12-home Washington County development, was **postponed** at La Forte's request pending a fiscal analysis [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:26:59]. The rest of the consent agenda passed 7-0.

## Rightsizing / closures
- There was no school-closure or consolidation item.
- **Res. 7148** is the only boundary item. It is an inter-district boundary cleanup, not an attendance-boundary change.
  - The staff memo (Mary Kane, Senior Legal Counsel) proposes aligning the PPS–Beaverton SD line with the county line for the Timber Ridge Estates area (12 homes) [BB:3988456 p.1].
  - La Forte questioned giving up property-tax and bond revenue [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:27:37].
  - CFO Morrison: operating funding is per-student through the State School Fund, so the operating impact is "kind of a wash". Future bond levies are the real consideration [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:29:15].
  - Sullivan (the zone director) said the issue came from a constituent [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:31:00]. Members also raised students' commute and social ties.
- **Analysis:** the new board's first boundary vote shows a disposition to demand fiscal due diligence, even on a very small change. That is relevant to how it may approach larger boundary and rightsizing proposals.

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **Bond audit (Sullivan, Audit Committee chair):**
  - Section 5: "bond oversight was provided although disconnects existed and certain information was not getting to the board or bond accountability committee". There are 9 recommendations (#14-22) on board roles and reporting.
  - Section 6: 20 prior recommendations (about 38%) remain in progress or not implemented.
  - Adding the year-six recommendations brings the total outstanding to 43 [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:19:03].
  - She plans at least two audit-committee check-ins and wants to coordinate with the facilities committee chair and the BAC liaison [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@1:20:58].
- **Bond reporting.** Bond spending will be reconciled in the monthly financial reports. Bond sales in tranches will come to the board [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:51:55].

## Budget & enrollment
- **Outdoor School:** a 20% state cut equals about $665K, roughly one of five days. PPS lacks funds to cover the gap.
  - Board members want to keep five days, possibly via the Fund for PPS.
  - Splitt reported a board letter to legislators [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:56:51].
- **Position control:** vacated central-office positions are no longer automatically refilled [YT:S_rBf9XK-JM@0:59:52].
