## Summary

- **Field-trip policy 6.50.010-P** (Wang's revision to cut prescriptiveness and the burden on DLI exchange trips): sent to the full board. Engelsman abstained because materials were posted the day of the meeting [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@0:19:49] [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@0:59:05] [BB:3998039 p.1].
- **Public contracting rules:** staff proposed raising the board-approval threshold from $150K to about $250K. Engelsman objected that the change would erode trust while the board is "trying to stop the hemorrhaging of students and families leaving our school district" [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:17:13] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XKKOGk4XJBo&t=4633s)). Wang warned against "performative oversight" [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:19:09]. Chase-Miller (medium) said the board "will be called on to make a lot of decisions this year that aren't going to be popular" and should not decide on optics [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:23:56]. Moved to the full board "for consideration, not recommendation" [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:28:40].
- **Districtwide fundraising policy** (discussion requested by Engelsman): Engelsman pledged to support the board's earlier decision (a districtwide fund) and urged families to get involved. DePass said the board and policy committee "could have done deeper engagement." Members framed foundations and fundraising fights as symptoms of state underfunding [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:31:13] [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:33:46] [YT:XKKOGk4XJBo@1:35:42].
