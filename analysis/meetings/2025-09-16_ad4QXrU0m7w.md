- **Revised comprehensive plans for the three high schools** (Franco). All start construction in summer 2026 and open in fall 2029. They are slated for a board vote on Oct 14 [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@0:19:18–0:21:37].

  |---|---|---|
  | **Jefferson** | **$466M** | "just under 25 million" saved, at the low end of the $25–33M range shown in April. CM/GC is Colas/Hoffman (captioned "Colas Hoffman") [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@0:34:42] |
  | **Cleveland** | Includes a $15M Powell Park allowance | About $11M saved; stadium to finish a year early (fall 2028); CM/GC Skanska [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@0:55:15–0:57:32] |

  - Wells: no teen parent center; a health clinic still needs a provider [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@1:09:01].

  - Middle-school field work is "pending funding" and depends on leftover money.
  - Haskins said the timelines depend on OSM staffing [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@1:30:14–1:39:23].
- **OSM leadership and Procedeo.** La Forte objected to a sole-source path ($149.5K initial contract, then about $500K) and cited competitive procurements elsewhere [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@1:41:41–1:45:29].

  - Turf advocates tied facility inequities to families leaving the district and "declining enrollment numbers" [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@0:07:41].

- **Seismic (Safe Structures PPS public comment):**
  - 49 building parts score ≥9 and 131 score ≥7.
  - The eight schools with the highest-risk parts (≥9.4) are Beverly Cleary K-8, Jackson MS, Rose City Park, Jason Lee, Buckman, Rieke, Kelly and Capitol Hill.
  - The group said PPS missed Res. 7109's Sept 1 deadline for full prioritization, and it supports Option C [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@0:13:58–0:16:16].
  - La Forte said Option C will be added to the deck and bimonthly seismic updates start in October [YT:ad4QXrU0m7w@0:16:16].
