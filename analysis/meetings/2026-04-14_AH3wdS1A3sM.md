## Summary

- **Resolution 7296 adopted 5–2** (Engelsman and La Forte no; Ritorto no, unofficial). It directs the superintendent to launch "phase three of Growing Great Schools", the districtwide rightsizing process [BB:3998077 p.3] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:17:59] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AH3wdS1A3sM&t=8279s)). The adopted text [BB:3998077 p.13] includes:
  - all schools and programs in scope;
  - an advisory committee of "union representatives, principals, and staff";
  - virtual and in-person community engagement and regular board updates;
  - implementation starting "no later than the 2027-28 school year", completed within three years;
  - Recital E as amended: "The result of this process **will** include school and program closures, consolidations, or boundary changes".
- Co-sponsor **Wang**: the board "has never really officially voted on this direction". He cited 7–12 months as best practice and said that excluding areas in SEGC (where "the board caved" to privileged areas) produced inequity [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@0:58:14] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@0:58:44] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@0:59:15] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:46:00]. Co-sponsor **Splitt** called it a kick-off, with criteria to follow in a later resolution after engagement [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:01:19].
- **La Forte/Engelsman guardrail amendment failed 3–4.** It would have required: board-approved decision criteria before scenarios; a standardized equity impact analysis per scenario; a published phased engagement plan; a detailed timeline; financial transparency (one-time and ongoing costs public before action); policy alignment. Yes: Engelsman, La Forte, Sullivan. No: Chase-Miller, DePass, Splitt, Wang [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:25:39] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:13:13] [BB:3990653]. "Boundary changes" was added instead (6–0–1, Wang abstaining) [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:00:21] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:15:53] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:17:26] [BB:3998077 p.4].
- Armstrong, after public comment that cited a "list": "there's no preliminary draft ... of a list", and that work begins "later this spring" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:08:32].
- Wang said Kellogg session participants were about "97% white" in a district roughly half students of color, which argued for waiting for more engagement. A N/NE session was being added [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:30:44] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:33:45] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:40:26].

## Rightsizing / closures

**Positions on Res. 7296 and the amendment (first round, 1:28:42–1:54:06):**
- **Chase-Miller:** likes the amendment, but pass the original now and add guardrails "reflective of what we what we heard from the community" later [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:28:42] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:29:12].
- **Wang:** content is good but needs staff vetting. The amendment was posted the day before; the original had a week [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:29:43] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:08:34].
- **DePass:** keep the advisory committee ("closest to the issue"). Guardrails after listening sessions [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:33:15].
- **Splitt:** strongly keep the staff advisory committee. Open to "may". Wants future drafting in committee because open-meeting law (HB 4177 veto threat) blocks deliberation outside meetings [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:34:46] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:35:18].
- **Engelsman:** the process has already started, so do one combined resolution later. Commitments must be memorialized because 2027 and 2029 elections could change the board [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:40:56] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:42:59].
- **Sullivan:** would vote for both; agrees with "may" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:49:33].
- **Ritorto:** would "like to see a scenario where no schools are cut". Supports the amendment [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:52:05].

**Second round:**
- La Forte won't vote yes "without the framework" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:54:06].
- Chase-Miller wants to hear "from more community" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:56:10].
- DePass: "I don't like when I hear people say I think we all agree", and worried about staff turnaround after the 4/27 session [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:58:15] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:59:16].
- Engelsman: the resolution process favors whatever "was written by one or two people in time" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@2:05:28].

**Advisory committee:** the revised D(b) replaced "community members, families, and staff" with "union representatives, principals, and staff". Wang said staff advised students can't be compelled to serve, and the change avoids "another SCGC" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:22:32] [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:23:33]. Engelsman had feared a parent committee of 80 [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:38:53].

**Analysis:** the 5–2 vote is the first formal board commitment to closures, consolidations or boundary changes. The split was over process (guardrails now vs after engagement), not over whether to rightsize. La Forte and Engelsman, the two "no" votes, were the strongest advocates of written equity and financial-transparency guardrails. Sullivan joined them on the amendment but voted yes on the final resolution.
