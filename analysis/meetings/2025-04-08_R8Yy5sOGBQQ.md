## Summary

- **Second modernization update under Res. 7067** [YT:R8Yy5sOGBQQ@2:13:17] [BB:3996343 p.2]:
  - Combined preliminary savings are $33–67M.
  - Total cost for the three high schools: $1.348–1.380B, of which $406M comes from the 2020 bond.
  - That leaves $176–208M of the 2025 bond's $1.150B modernization line for "additional modernization initiatives".
- **Project ranges (total project cost, including $20M of 2020-bond design money each):**

  - Ida B. Wells $439.5–452M; savings $3–15M [YT:R8Yy5sOGBQQ@3:02:32].
  - Cleveland $450–462M; savings about $7–18M, plus a separate $15M allowance for Powell Park fields [YT:R8Yy5sOGBQQ@3:34:42].
