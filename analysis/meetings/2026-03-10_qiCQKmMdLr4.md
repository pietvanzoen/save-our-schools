## Rightsizing / closures

**Staff framing (2:04:56–2:38:51).**
- Armstrong: Growing Great Schools is "an effort to rightsize our system so that our schools better match current and future enrollment"; decisions come late fall [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:04:56] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:05:26].
- Franco: "this is going to be difficult"; rightsizing "is not about money" but about well-resourced schools with consistent programming. Phase 1 was the enrollment campaign, phase 2 the Jefferson dual-assignment sunset, phase 3 this districtwide portfolio review [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:07:28] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:09:37].
- Calvert, current state: 45 K-5 schools ranging 160–560 students; 11 K-8s with median 446 and a range of about 403. The Jefferson process targeted ≤350 students' difference between largest and smallest high school as a proxy for equitable programming. She stressed the medians/ranges describe the current state, not an optimal size [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:14:12] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:15:44] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:18:20].
- Lessons from DBRAC (2014–17), the Southeast Guiding Coalition (2020–22) and Jefferson Is Rising: the district "ha[s] not always consistently reviewed the effectiveness of past decisions". The SEGC was meant to proceed SE → NE → SW but stopped after SE. Policy lists six unranked factors that may conflict [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:25:29] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:27:03] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:34:13] [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:36:47].
- Draft engagement calendar: listening sessions in April, possible board vote on scope in May, TLE touchpoints, September engagement, vote in December [YT:qiCQKmMdLr4@2:35:14].
