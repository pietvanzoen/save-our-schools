## Summary

- 13 people commented [BB:3997967 p.1]:
  - 6 on the Cleveland (4) and Ida B. Wells (2) modernization budgets
  - 5 for a Native American Student Success Center
  - 2 questioning the size and structure of the bond
- Cleveland and Wells speakers objected to the proposed cost targets: roughly $350M versus a roughly $450–460M design for Cleveland, and $340M for Wells [YT:ZYazmlim9R0@0:06:04] [YT:ZYazmlim9R0@0:07:34] [YT:ZYazmlim9R0@0:12:54].

- Native community members, including Indian Parent Committee leaders, asked for a bond line item for a Native Student Success Center. They suggested funding it from bond interest earnings, a longer term, or lower administration costs [YT:ZYazmlim9R0@0:19:57] [YT:ZYazmlim9R0@0:20:43].
