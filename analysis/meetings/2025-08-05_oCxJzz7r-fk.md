## Summary
- First regular meeting of the new board. There was no public comment ("We do not. That's a first.") [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:06:08].
- Committee reports [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:06:56]:
  - Sullivan (Audit) plans five meetings.
  - A board member, who said "I do enough policy by day" (Analysis: likely Splitt), is the new BAC liaison. She relayed BAC news [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:15:19]:
    - Mass timber and steel are "penciling out to be about the same cost".
    - LEED Gold is a state requirement.
    - The long-awaited seismic report was still being worked on.
    - The BAC felt the new board did not know it existed.
  - La Forte (new FIO chair) described the FIO work plan, below.
- **Beaverton boundary change (Res. 7148) postponed 7–0.** La Forte said the staff memo wrongly stated only 2 of 12 lots were developed, so the fiscal impact was understated [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:24:22].
  - CFO Morrison put the potential yield at about $45,000/yr across the homes (operating levy plus 2025 bond levy) [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:41:54].
  - Transport cost was put at about $8,000/yr [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:36:32].
- **Finance Focus (state school fund):** Morrison flagged:
  - a declining fund balance, with one-time money supporting operations [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:51:51];
  - a 2025-26 budget "propp[ed]" up by one-time transfers [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:52:37];
  - PPS's current use of "extended ADMw", a declining-enrollment cushion [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:12:28].
  A member warned "we're going to have a tough budget year next year" [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:13:15].
- **Cell-phone (personal electronic device) policy implementation:** Cardona described alignment with the Governor's July 2 executive order (number captioned as "2550"; not verified). Board concerns [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:21:33]:
  - searches and bias (Chase-Miller, Ritorto);
  - lost instructional time, and an "unfunded mandate";
  - inconsistent school-by-school storage methods (La Forte);
  - poor communication to elementary families that watches are included (Splitt).
- **Draft board work plan:** the board agreed to defer it to the 8/19 retreat. A member called the staff draft "more of a like to-do list" and volunteered to turn it into a real work plan [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:52:08]. Members also disputed the 4-day document-review window (protocol says 4 days; an unadopted draft said 12) [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:54:27].
- **Consent agenda 7159–7161 adopted 7–0.** Discussion covered high-cost disability fund reimbursement (Buno: reimbursed at "less than 40%") [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@2:01:18]. The board requested a procurement/contracts training [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@2:07:25].

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **BAC liaison report** [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:14:33–0:17:35]:
  - BAC reviewed the bond audit and gave "pretty meaty updates on all the modernization projects".
  - Mass timber is roughly cost-equal to steel, so the board could favor timber when costs are close.
  - A seismic prioritization diagram was shown; the seismic report was still pending.
  - The internal auditor proposed a joint annual bond-audit session (Audit + FIO + BAC).
- **FIO chair La Forte** [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:18:20–0:22:06]:
  - Asked staff for a full-year "work back" with project timelines and board-vote dates.
  - Asked for updates on 2020/2025 bond projects: Jefferson, Cleveland, Wells, Center for Black Student Excellence (CBSE), athletic fields incl. Grant Bowl lighting, the seismic plan, and Indian Education program relocation.
  - Proposed four project buckets: school modernizations; physical facility improvements (ADA, seismic, athletics); health and safety (lead, air quality, HVAC, security); district-wide strategic initiatives (CBSE, central office relocation).
  - Drafted the first FIO charter.

## Budget & enrollment
- **State School Fund** [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:50:19–0:57:10]:
  - SSF is about 34.2% of the general fund and local property taxes about 41.4%; property tax and SSF are equalized.
  - Weighted ADM: 42,281 resident ADM became 51,299 weighted.
  - The 11% special-education cap leaves about 1,267 students without the extra weight [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:01:04].
  - Per-ADMw amount for 2025-26 is about $11,812 [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:12:28].
- **Poverty count:** a member questioned the state's poverty count (~3,822, "6%") as far too low. Buno said it does not match direct-certification counts [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:04:52].
- **Reserves and one-time funds:** the fund balance target is 5%. Reserves are declining, and one-time funds support 2025-26 [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@0:53:22].
- **Planning timeline:** the 2026-27 membership and revenue estimate goes to ODE in December [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:10:11].
- **Extended ADMw** (declining-enrollment fallback) is in use [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:12:28].
- A member requested a longer (about 10-year) historical trend [YT:oCxJzz7r-fk@1:14:00].
