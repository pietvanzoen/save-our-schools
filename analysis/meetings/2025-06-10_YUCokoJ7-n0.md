## Summary
- **Optimization plan announced.** CFO Morrison said that, with the bond passed, "the district will develop and share a school optimization plan in partnership with the community". She tied it to shifts in where students live and to facility conditions [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:15:21] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YUCokoJ7-n0&t=921s)). This is the clearest public announcement of the optimization (later rightsizing) effort in this batch.
- **Structural deficit response.** Morrison listed the measures under way [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:12:41]:
  - blended 4th/5th grade classrooms
  - a high-school class minimum of 15
  - scaled-back supplemental staffing
  - a new ERP (enterprise resource planning) system
  - She expects at least $32M in further reductions after 2025-26 [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:14:28].
- **Closures as the next step.** A commissioner summarized "school optimization and school closures" as the next big step [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:19:16]. Morrison emphasized incremental efficiencies instead [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:20:08].
- **Kafoury's outlook.** Chief of Staff Kafoury: "what we're seeing right now might be as good as we get" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:21:15].
- **Franco on closures.** Asked about school closures, Chief of Schools Franco:
  - **Advantages:** lower operating costs (utilities, custodial, maintenance, transportation) and less deferred maintenance. Larger schools bring "more funding and potentially more robust pro programming" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:47:25].
  - **Disadvantages:** the community "ripple effect" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:48:52].
  - **Timeline:** a steering group starts talking in June 2025, with the work refined in fall [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:49:27].
- **Armstrong on process (attribution medium).** Closures and boundaries are "a community conversation" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:51:25]. She raised the possibility "that no schools are closed and it's more of a reconfiguration of the programs" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:52:58].
  - Example: dual-language immersion (DLI) sites with only 7 students in the neighborhood strand next to 20+ in DLI [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:52:06].
  - The Southeast Guiding Coalition (SEGC) "will not be the model". San Antonio is a positive example and San Francisco a cautionary one [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:54:01].
  - "we can't begin with the end in mind" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:56:56].
- **Enrollment data (Adams).**
  - Grade progression is stable. 5th→6th is the lowest progression rate, and 8th→9th has improved.
  - Kindergarten cohorts are smaller than graduating classes [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:35:06].
  - Modernized high schools see an "enrollment bump" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:45:52].
- **Enrollment campaign (Grose).** "Legacies are made at PPS" (districtwide) plus "Opportunities in your community" (aimed at high-attrition neighborhoods), launching in fall [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:41:32].
- **Seismic and facilities (Shanks).**
  - Full seismic correction would cost "a little over $1 billion" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:15:22].
  - PPS criteria will include whether "this school [is] planned for a consolidation ... from our school optimization ... efforts" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:16:49].
  - Average facility condition is rated "poor". About $150M a year would be needed just to keep it at poor [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:19:23].
- **Certification.** TSCC certified the 2025-26 budget with no recommendations or objections [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:24:39].

## Decisions & votes
- TSCC voted unanimously to certify the PPS 2025-26 approved budget with no objections or recommendations [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:24:39]. This is a TSCC action, not a PPS board action.

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Named workstream.** "School optimization plan" is now a named workstream, to be developed "in partnership with the community" after bond passage [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:15:21].
- **Staff framing.** Morrison said "student location" changes and facility conditions are the drivers [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:15:28].
- **Commissioner's question.** The commissioner who served on the SEGC (the chair) described that boundary process as "Not a great process" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:50:08]. She asked what factors will decide which schools close and how to mitigate the emotional toll.
  - She later said stabilizing under-enrolled schools would "make a huge difference" [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:55:08].
  - Her own neighborhood school's capture rate was 59-60% [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:37:55].
  - She described a "culture of optioning" toward focus-option schools [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:39:06].
- **Staff answers.** Franco (high) and Armstrong (medium):
  - closures save operating and deferred-maintenance costs and enable richer programs
  - but they hit "emotional tones"
  - the process will be community-grounded, data-driven and modeled on San Antonio
  - closures are not presupposed; program reconfiguration (e.g., DLI) is possible
- **Rita Moore** urged lessons from PPS's closures and boundary changes since 2005 [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:53:25].
- **Analysis:** these statements set out the administration's initial public framing of rightsizing:
  - fiscal efficiency plus program robustness
  - community process
  - no predetermined outcome
  - explicit rejection of the SEGC approach

  These are useful benchmarks for comparing later 2026 proposals.

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **Seismic prioritization.** Three steps; the second (engineer's prioritization) is under way. Step-three PPS criteria are not yet defined but will include consolidation plans from optimization, and whether a project can be done in an occupied building versus needing a swing site [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:15:01].
- **Funding the gap.** More seismic money is possible only by trading off other deferred maintenance from the $190M. There is a $100M reserve for end-of-life and emergency replacements [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:18:17].
- **Emergency management.** There is no current plan to coordinate seismic priorities with city/county emergency management [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:21:11].

## Budget & enrollment
- **Central office share:** about 5-6% of budget, or 2.58% counting administrative staff only [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:26:39].
- **Chronic absenteeism:** about 36%. Buno described ASERT (attendance support and engagement response) teams [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:58:39].
- **Staff leave and substitutes:** staff leave is up 250% in six years; the teacher sub fill rate is 92% [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:03:32].
- **Calendar:** only 16 of 38 weeks were full five-day weeks (a CBRC point raised by the chair) [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@1:12:06].

## Public comment themes
- **Cascade Policy Institute** (a written comment plus one in-person speaker) argued that PPS's race-conscious equity staffing formula violates ORS 659.850 and Title VI. It cited per-student funding of about $16.6K at Rosa Parks against about $8.5K at Richmond, and asked TSCC to withhold certification [YT:YUCokoJ7-n0@0:06:41].
