## Summary
- **Budget approved.** The Board, sitting as the Budget Committee, approved the 2025-26 budget (Res. 7116). The vote was announced as 5-0 [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:56:01]. The minutes record "4 yes – 0 no – 1 abstain", but their roll call lists all five present members voting yes [BB:3998006 p.3]. **Analysis:** the minutes contradict themselves. The video supports 5-0. Brim-Edwards said she had planned to abstain but then thanked colleagues after her questions were answered [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:52:12].
- **Superintendent's budget changes** since January [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:42:56]:
  - High-school staffing formula cut ($2.8M) restored
  - Interventionists, coaches and social-emotional supports added back
  - Half-time elementary assistant principals ($1.4M) restored
  - No special-education cuts
  - Core-enrichment teachers limited to two buildings
  - Levy funds pay for 718 positions
  - Title I's 25% holdback to be returned to schools after the state allocation
- **Sullivan raises closures.** Sullivan listed community ideas the board should consider, including: "Do we need to consolidate or close some schools to get the optimal number of students? ... Let's consider them." [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:10:34] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WPbTg28GB_o&t=4234s)). This is the most explicit board-member mention of closures in this batch.
- **Splitt on the budget.** Splitt called the budget "inadequate for our kids" because of chronic state underfunding, and urged constituents to lobby legislators [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:01:27].
- **Brim-Edwards' questions.** She pressed for line-item detail on about $3.4M in contracts, $2.2M in professional development and about $1M in travel cuts [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:14:48]. Figures she drew out:
  - about 240 FTE reduced overall, of which 157 are teachers [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:32:11]
  - status of the Southeast Guiding Coalition balancing commitment (see Rightsizing below)
  - no operating budget yet for the Center for Black Student Excellence (CBSE) [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:39:44]
- **Middle Grades Redesign update.** Covered 8th-grade success teams, advisory, the 5-of-7 schedule, field trips and capstones, and standards-based grading in 2025-26 [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:11:03].
  - Splitt asked whether the six remaining K-8s might convert. Staff said that is "outside this process". Armstrong said it is not on the work plan but could be agendized if the board wants [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:28:33].
- **Other items:**
  - Superintendent update on CBSE: an appraisal is due May 23, and the director post opens [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:02:07]
  - Consent agenda (5-0)
  - First reading of the Student Rep/DSC policy

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Sullivan.** Her consolidation/closure remark [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:10:34] is framed as a community idea to "consider", alongside a longer school year and restructured early-release days.
  - **Analysis:** this is the first explicit board-member statement in this batch (April–July 2025) that closures might be needed for "optimal" school size.
- **Southeast Guiding Coalition.** Brim-Edwards said PPS promised "10 um FTE for balancing for five years" to these schools after "the boundary changes, program closures, program moves", and said that set-aside was no longer reserved [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:43:14].
  - Chief Franco said the affected schools (Marysville, Bridger, Lent, Harrison Park) can still request set-aside FTE. Two spring rebalancings have been done and a third is planned [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:45:23].
  - Brim-Edwards: "the district made a promise" [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:49:16].
  - **Analysis:** this is a precedent for later rightsizing. Communities affected by earlier boundary changes and program closures hold the district to mitigation commitments.
- **Grade configuration (K-8 vs middle school):**
  - Splitt reported "whispers or downright shouts" from K-8s wishing they were not K-8s [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:28:33].
  - Staff said the redesign covers programming, "not um enrollment or boundaries" [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:28:43].
  - Armstrong (medium) said the new board and superintendent team has "not yet engaged in that conversation" [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:28:52].
  - Wang (medium) flagged K-8 program access and enrollment as a future topic [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:29:39].
  - Brim-Edwards (medium) recounted the 2017 board decision to address inequities in "underenrolled K8s" by reopening Kellogg and Tubman and creating Harrison Park feeder patterns [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:32:36].
  - Staff said 28 sites serve middle grades, and all must offer at least one world language [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:16:36].
- **Enrollment-based staffing.** Armstrong said staffing has "missed the mark in many places". PPS is adding a third spring staffing review and fall enrollment verification so schools are balanced before classes "collapse" after school starts [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:47:07].

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **CBSE building.** Brim-Edwards asked about operating costs for a potential CBSE building. Armstrong said a building with lease income could be "self-supporting", and that operating costs will come with a purchase proposal [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:42:10].
- **Print shop.** Staff say outsourcing projects about $500K in savings. PPS is negotiating with Hillsboro SD for printing [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:36:11].

## Budget & enrollment
- **FTE reductions.** About 240 FTE against filled positions and vacancies, or about 270 against the current-year snapshot. 157 are teachers and about 83 are non-teaching [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:32:11].
- **Professional development.** Central PD cuts include food and coffee at trainings [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:25:20].
- **Contracts.** Cabinet was asked to cut contracts 10%. RESJ (racial equity and social justice) contract cuts total $1.1M [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:21:20].
- **Board intent.** Brim-Edwards: the board's vote expresses "board intent", not just a spending ceiling [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@1:54:43].

## Public comment themes
- **CBSE.** The executive director of the Center for Black Excellence urged PPS to publicly identify a CBSE site by the end of June [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:05:19].
- **Latino Network.** A Benson student and Latino Network staff urged protecting culturally specific programs (Escalera) in the face of a 15% RESJ cut [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:50:27].
- **PAT** (teachers' union) President Bonilla welcomed the restored Title I funds. She described the strain on core-enrichment teachers and counselors split across two or three sites [YT:WPbTg28GB_o@0:56:25].
