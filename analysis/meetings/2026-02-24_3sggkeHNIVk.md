## Summary

- **MLC 9–12 discontinued administratively; this was the first school/program cut of the rightsizing era.** Staff presented the decision to end grades 9–12 at Metropolitan Learning Center after 2025-26 [BB:3996961 p.2] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:09:43].
  - Rationale: 40 students in 9–12 (less than half of 2016-17). Only 6% of MLC 8th graders continue there. Courses are heavily blended. Per-student cost is **$28,120 vs $8,729** PPS-wide 9–12. Projected savings **$1.1M**. Matching Alliance's staffing ratio would take 98 students; matching the PPS median would take 136 [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:13:16] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:19:07] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:21:18] [BB:3996961 p.11] [BB:3996961 p.13].
  - Origin: the item came out of principal "academic program design" budget conversations about the $50M deficit [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:10:28] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:54:46].
- Armstrong acknowledged PPS chose to "inform" rather than "engage" before deciding. **ODE confirmed MLC K-12 is a single school**, so legal staff say ending 9–12 is not a "school closure" under Policy 6.10.030 or state law. There is **no separate board vote**: the change is folded into June budget adoption [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:07:26] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:08:53] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@1:26:25] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@1:27:57].

## Rightsizing / closures

- **Staff case (0:09:43–0:25:37):**

  - Demographics: 35% of MLC 9-12 students receive special education services, vs 14.8% districtwide [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:14:47].
  - Sample student schedules showed blended courses [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:16:57].
  - The 2025-26 staffing table: 7.0 FTE allocated vs 2.6–2.7 at PPS high school rates [BB:3996961 p.12] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:20:35].
  - CBO and alternative options, with MLC students given lottery priority at Jefferson, Benson and Alliance [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@0:24:55].
- **2026-27 projections (if retained):** 38 students in 9–12 and 227 in K-8. 26 applicants for 15 9th-grade slots (11 waitlisted); every waitlisted student was offered a seat last year [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@1:11:59] [YT:3sggkeHNIVk@1:13:23].
