## Summary
- **Budget adopted.** The 2026-27 budget and tax levy (Reso 7326) passed **6–0 with Engelsman abstaining**. Engelsman moved it and DePass seconded [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:48:18] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yinJ7KK-Bqc&t=20898s)) [BB:3998082 p.4].
- **Engelsman's $13.12M amendment (Reso 7342) failed 1–6.** It would have restored $13,120,000 for school-based, student-facing positions after about 82–87 educator layoffs [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:47:04] [BB:3998082 p.5] [BB:3992230 p.1].
  - Chase-Miller's sub-amendment to draw reserves down to 4% (about $8.2M) failed 2–4–1 [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:41:38].
  - A motion to postpone the budget to June 30 failed 3–4 [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:46:26].
  - Engelsman's enrollment argument: $13.12M ÷ about $7,200 in state funding per student ≈ 1,822 students. If the cuts drive that many students away, the savings vanish [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@4:12:37] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yinJ7KK-Bqc&t=15157s)).
- **Armstrong tied the budget to rightsizing.** "we have several more uncomfortable conversations to go when we're talking boundary adjustments or program moves and school closures."
  - Her goal is to end recurring reductions "within the next 2 years".
  - The expected $65M cut for 2027-28 is meant to be "the stabilizing reduction" [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:33:20] [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:38:47] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yinJ7KK-Bqc&t=20000s)).
  - She also said all central-office administration costs less than $5M [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:36:45].
- **Budget amendments adopted:**
  - **Reso 7339:** a one-year freeze on discretionary out-of-state travel for the board and senior leadership, worth about $77K, passed 7–0 [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@2:42:58] [BB:3998082 p.4].
  - **Reso 7341:** a temporary pause on generative-AI contracts and expansion, with an inventory and assessment due in 120 days and board approval of GenAI contracts until a board AI policy exists, passed 7–0 [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@3:52:14] [BB:3998082 p.5].
  - **Reso 7340 (failed 3–4):** protecting Office of Internal Performance Auditor funding. Engelsman, La Forte and Splitt voted yes [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@3:09:13] [BB:3998082 p.5].
  - The OIPA debate turned personal between Wang and La Forte, and Sullivan objected to discussing a position in public [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@2:53:01] [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@3:04:30].

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Armstrong before the budget vote.** She listed simultaneous 2026-27 challenges: the ERP rollout, "a conversation about closures and boundaries and program moves", and "a $65 million deficit" [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@2:12:03].
- **Armstrong after the $13M debate:**
  - "several more uncomfortable conversations ... boundary adjustments or program moves and school closures" [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:33:20].
  - The 2027-28 $65M reduction is planned as the last structural cut. After that, reductions would only track enrollment decline [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:38:47].
  - Recruiting and retaining staff depends on ending layoffs [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:36:45].
- **Property sales:**
  - Chase-Miller proposed "Looking at property we can sell off" to rebuild reserves [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@4:32:28].
  - An unidentified staff member said "we are banking on some revenue from real property sales. It's already included in the budget" [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:32:34]. On 7/07, CFO Morrison confirmed one real-property sale of about $2.5M is in the budget [YT:r0e1Cx8ujLs@1:34:15] [YT:r0e1Cx8ujLs@1:35:24].
  - **Analysis:** this implies the adopted budget already assumes some surplus-property revenue before any rightsizing decisions are made.
- **Enrollment loss.** Engelsman said PPS has been losing about 700 students a year (and 500 before that). She warned that larger classes, blends and lost paraeducators could accelerate losses enough to cancel the savings [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@4:13:18].
- **MLC.** DePass suggested revisiting MLC programming, which the board had approved earlier, as a cost to reconsider [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@4:48:01] [YT:yinJ7KK-Bqc@5:10:00].
