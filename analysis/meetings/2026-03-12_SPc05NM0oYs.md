## Rightsizing / closures

- **Focus options:** Splitt does not want blanket closure. She wants to look at cases where "a focus option school less than a mile away" pulls 50 students from a shrinking neighborhood school, while protecting equity-serving programs like DLI. She floated hybrid models (neighborhood catchment plus lottery) [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:04:05] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:05:35]. Wang and Sullivan echoed the focus-option drain concern [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:13:17] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:15:51]. La Forte (medium) asked whether focus options disproportionately pull from North Portland's lower-income elementaries [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:08:43].
- **Consolidation vs closure:** Splitt defined consolidation as merging whole communities (or splitting one into two halves) rather than scattering a closed school's students across five schools. Chase-Miller agreed [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:24:47] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:30:27].
- **K-8s:** La Forte (medium) and Wang favor converting K-8s, with exceptions. Armstrong cautioned that remote schools like Skyline complicate uniform rules and suggested student focus groups [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:09:15] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:11:15] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:10:15].
- **Cohorts and feeders:** Engelsman wants whole elementary cohorts to move to middle school together. Student reps (Benson, Franklin) noted that cross-city mixing also builds community [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:16:22] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:21:02] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:22:07].
- **Communications:** Splitt warned that "growing great schools as a subject line might not get the attention that we need" [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:28:24].
- Calvert's synthesis: a balanced approach across the portfolio, with a possible mix of configurations, attention to unintended consequences and split feeders, and "everything is on the table" [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:31:59] [YT:SPc05NM0oYs@1:33:01].
- Analysis: compared with 3/10, the board shifted from "how fast/how broad" to criteria. Splitt's consolidation-over-closure and Chase-Miller's seismic-overlay ideas became recurring themes. Engelsman again emphasized lottery and choice effects over closure.
