## Summary

- **Public comment was entirely about Metropolitan Learning Center (MLC).** All five speakers opposed ending MLC's **9–12 high school program** next year (one also gave curriculum suggestions) [BB:3998065 p.1].
  - Speakers said families were told on Tuesday, Feb 10 at a school meeting that high schoolers would have to go to neighborhood schools or options like Benson [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:02:26] [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:05:19].
  - They said the district called it a "programming change" rather than a school closure in order to bypass closure processes [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:02:26] [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:06:02].
  - Low enrollment and the budget deficit were the stated rationale [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:04:34] [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:06:44].
- Chair Chase-Miller, an alternative-school graduate herself, announced a superintendent listening session for MLC families on **Feb 18 at 6 pm**. She said every MLC high school student will get individualized transition support (one-on-one counseling and help enrolling in other high schools), and tied the change to the $50M cut, part of "over hundred million in cuts over three years" [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:15:39] [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:16:21].
- Analysis: this is the first board-meeting appearance in this batch of a **grade-configuration / program closure** decision driven by small enrollment and budget. It was made administratively, not by board vote. The board took it up at the Feb 24 work session (see `2026-02-24_3sggkeHNIVk.md`). PPS reversed the decision about Feb 27 [NEWS:NEWS-20260227-wweek-1].
- Curriculum deep dive: K-5 math (i-Ready), 6-8 and 9-12 math, K-5 ELA (Wit & Wisdom), and 6-12 ELA (HMH Into Literature), with notes on technology use [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@0:21:33] [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@1:14:21].
- Chase-Miller questioned whether curricula can be implemented with fidelity "given class sizes". - Splitt asked PPS to move away from non-standards-based computer assessments like MAP [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@1:29:33]. Staff said the state's new accountability framework will require a K-8 interim assessment three times a year [YT:8y-3zX8JaWU@1:30:16].
