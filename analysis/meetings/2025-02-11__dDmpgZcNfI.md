## Summary

- The board adopted **Resolution 7051, "Framework to Build High Quality-High Schools in a Cost-Efficient Manner," 7–0**. The minutes describe a consensus wording amendment from Hollands; the video shows Wang naming Hollands as seconder [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:21:25] [BB:3997975 p.2–3].
- Brim-Edwards presented the framework, drafted with Hollands and Splitt (a non-quorum group) after the Feb 4 discussion. Its stated purpose is to redirect high-school savings to "aging elementary and middle schools" ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dDmpgZcNfI&t=138s)) [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:01:33] [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:05:21].
- **Key framework guidelines** [BB:3997975 p.6–7]:
  - About 295,000 sq ft per school, based on Franklin and Lincoln.
  - Meet the Ed-spec "area program" minimums.
  - Health center only if a provider commits; teen parent center only with demonstrated need.
  - All-electric required; equity in contracting retained.
  - No material reductions to exterior athletics.
  - Options other than LEED Gold; mass timber vs. steel to be evaluated; basement reduction options.
  - An additional $10M per-project reduction target.
  - Design Advisory Groups to reconvene.
- The framework directs the superintendent to bring near-term general-contractor cost options and mid-term contracting-method recommendations. Hollands added "current and" future contracting methods [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:08:22] [BB:3997975 p.3].
- Brim-Edwards noted that Cornerstone had admitted using 5% rather than 8% escalation, a roughly $45M error. She also listed Beaverton's differences: 1,500 vs. 1,700 students, 47 acres, no kitchen, no teen parent or health center, fossil fuels [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:15:18] [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:16:50].
- Jung "shooting from the hip" estimated $20–40M in savings. The captions say "per project"; the minutes say "approximately 20-40 million". He said savings would add to the bond's $190M for elementary and middle school infrastructure [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:18:22] [BB:3997975 p.2].
- Student Rep Kunsevi relayed Climate Crisis Response Committee students' support for sustainability and performing arts, and asked for more student voice in the bond [YT:_dDmpgZcNfI@0:19:53].

## Rightsizing / closures

- Not discussed directly.
