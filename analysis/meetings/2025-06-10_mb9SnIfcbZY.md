## Rightsizing / closures
- **Jefferson enrollment and boundaries.** This is the core rightsizing-adjacent item in this meeting.
  - **Armstrong's framing:** dual assignment "has unintentionally fragmented the student body" even as about $500M is invested in modernizing the school [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:44:31].
  - **Armstrong on urgency:** "do we risk that Jefferson maybe being 200 students or worse even lower or do we begin to make some adjustments and shifts now" [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:25:06].
  - **Implementation timing:** a possible one-grade transition starting 2026-27 or 2027-28, subject to board approval [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:14:22].
  - **Process (Calvert):**
    - three phases: review/initiate, engage, study/recommend
    - public meetings and a guided advisory committee
    - integrated with the "Legacies are made at PPS" and "Opportunity in your community" marketing campaigns [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:58:06]
- **Board positions:**
  - **Hollands** (high) objected that Jefferson's athletic fields are scheduled before the academic building, which sends a message at "a predominantly black school" [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:49:22]. Another director agreed [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:50:06].
  - **Splitt** (high) strongly supports ending dual assignment [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:05:56]. She also:
    - acknowledged that it will "upset particularly white affluent parents"
    - floated a phased catchment starting near Jefferson
    - relayed Irvington PTA interest in addressing "two underenrolled elementary schools right next door to each other" [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:10:11]
    - suggested recruiting experienced teachers to Jefferson with the teachers' union (PAT)
  - **Wang** (medium) argued for "one change" rather than a tiered approach, citing SEGC lessons and ripple effects on other high schools [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:12:42].
  - **Brim-Edwards** (medium):
    - "every student at Jefferson has chosen Jefferson"
    - tap alumni and middle-school athletic/music cohorts
    - market the Return to Albina preference
    - consider Benson as a pressure valve
    - ending dual assignment before the new building exists is "a harder [sell]" (captioned "cell") [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:18:50]
  - **A director, likely DePass** (low; not quoted in JSONL), urged partnerships with the alumni association, realtors and banks, citing Albina's history of displacement [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@3:02:08].
- **SEGC precedent:**
  - Brim-Edwards said PPS approved only 2.8 FTE of the promised 10 balancing FTE for SEGC schools [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:04:49].
  - She said collapsing Atkinson's neighborhood program into a single strand contradicts PPS's earlier reasoning when it closed Lent's neighborhood program [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:09:01].
  - She called the SEGC process "very traumatizing for a lot of schools" [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:19:10].
  - Hollands countered that one board's promises should not bind later boards in a fiscal crisis [YT:mb9SnIfcbZY@2:20:51].
- **Analysis:** this meeting marks the start of formal boundary work in North/Northeast Portland. It also shows the board's main fault lines on rightsizing-type changes:
  - equity urgency (Splitt, Hollands)
  - process caution and keeping promises to affected communities (Brim-Edwards)
  - "do it once" (Wang)
  - community-conversation framing (Armstrong)
