## Summary
- **First meeting of the re-formed FIO committee.** La Forte introduced herself as an advocate on lead paint, air quality and Grant Bowl lighting [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:00:49]. DePass cited 18 years in City of Portland construction management [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:03:06]. Chase-Miller cited her family's generations at Jefferson [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:03:51].
- **Public comment:** seven speakers. Five supported Grant Bowl lights. Safe Structures PPS asked about the seismic report and community input. Safe Indoor Air asked for 6 air changes per hour (ACH) [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:07:36–0:26:47].
- **Draft FIO charter** [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:27:32]. La Forte drafted it in response to the bond audit.
  - It covers "school modernizations, physical facility improvements, health and safety projects and other district-wide strategic initiatives included in the district's long-range facilities plan".
  - Members agreed the committee ensures *alignment with* the Long-Range Facilities Plan (LRFP) rather than writing it [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:30:34].
  - The prioritization factors include "current and projected enrollment trends" [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:32:05].
  - Target: full-board approval in October.
- **"Oversight" debate.** Supt. Armstrong said the board's oversight runs through supervising her. Committees should focus on large items needing deliberation [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:42:46]. Curriculum and technology bond oversight was steered to TLE.
- **Work plan** (Franco) [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:52:36]:
  - The three high-school comprehensive plans (Jefferson, Cleveland, Wells) come to FIO in September, then to the board.
  - Seismic updates are bimonthly, matching the board resolution's six per year [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:57:07].
  - The BAC will report quarterly to the full board, with FIO follow-up.

## Rightsizing / closures
- The **first explicit link in this batch between bond capital planning and "right sizing"/consolidation.**
  - The minutes record discussion of "how facility work aligns with potential school consolidations" [BB:3998016 p.2].
  - Shanks: facilities data will go to the rightsizing team; OSM will not make the decision [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@1:18:31].
  - Franco (medium confidence): "you don't want to invest in potentially a school or a building that could be that we're going to have conversations about in regards to right sizing" [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@1:19:16].
  - Seismic project selection will be aligned with "any of those decisions that are made about um right sizing our schools" and will weigh multi-summer construction disruption [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@1:29:15].
- Safe Structures PPS (public) noted the June OSM memo listed "school consolidation" as a seismic prioritization factor and asked how the community would be engaged [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@0:22:03].
- La Forte thanked staff for including "anticipated impacts of potential school consolidation" [YT:_1vhKE_wSC4@1:46:45].
- Analysis: by August 2025, staff were already treating rightsizing as a gating input for seismic and deferred-maintenance spending under the 2025 bond.
