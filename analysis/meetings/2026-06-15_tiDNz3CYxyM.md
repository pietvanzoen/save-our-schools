## Summary
- **Protecting Student Access and Safe Schools policy.** The new immigration-enforcement policy is required by HB 479 / SB 1538 and must be in place by Sept 30, 2026. It builds on PPS's existing toolkit. All three members gave a thumbs-up to forward it to the full board [YT:tiDNz3CYxyM@0:14:20] [YT:tiDNz3CYxyM@0:26:07].
