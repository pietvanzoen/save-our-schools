## Summary

- **AP access:** prompted by a 9th grader denied an AP class, Sullivan framed the issue [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:01:33].
  - Brida presented a districtwide AP review. Participation and scores are rising: average score 3.2, and students scoring 3+ up about 17%. There are persistent racial disparities and inconsistent course alignment across high schools [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:03:49] [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:06:49].
  - Next steps: stakeholder engagement, a possible administrative directive, a uniform course guide and family outreach, all by year end [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:09:52] [BB:3997982 p.1].
  - Brim-Edwards asked that IB and dual credit be included [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:19:52].
- **Policy 1.20.010-P vacancy fix:** referred to the full board for a first reading [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:25:59] [BB:3997982 p.2].
- **Ethics policy 5.10.066-P:** narrowed to bar *current* board members from applying for or accepting district employment, with no cooling-off period.
  - DePass called it "a board policy overreach" [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:31:11].
  - Referred for a first reading with DePass voting no [BB:3997982 p.2].
- **Kindergarten early entry (4.10.020-P):**
  - Brim-Edwards proposed easing the hard September 1 cutoff for September birthdays without testing. She argued the current rule pushes families to private kindergarten and loses students: "now we lost another student at PPS" [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:48:54] [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:51:59].
  - Glasgow cited equity, staff capacity after a $2.5M cut to Early Learners, and alignment with the state date [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:55:49] [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@0:57:21].
  - Staff will return with data on kindergarten enrollment declines, prekindergarten availability, and September-birthday first graders [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@1:18:36].
- **Solicitation (lobbying) registration and reporting policy:** introduced for discussion. Topics were thresholds, registration and reporting, and application to nonprofits and schools [YT:X3XzZAqUYSk@1:20:07] [BB:3997982 p.2].
