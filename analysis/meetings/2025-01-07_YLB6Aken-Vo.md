  - DePass (medium confidence) said "the most sustainable option is to use the buildings that we have currently" [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:59:01].

## Bond / seismic / facilities

- **Bond package** (staff memo by COO Dan Jung, updated 1/9/25):
  - $1.150B for the Jefferson, Ida B. Wells and Cleveland modernizations, including Cleveland off-site athletics.
  - $269M physical improvements: $190M deferred maintenance and priority scope, plus $79M athletics.
  - $176M technology, $56M curriculum, $10M PE spaces, $83M administration, $83M contingency.
  - Estimated tax rate held at $2.50 [BB:3988494 p.1–3].
  - The memo says reimagining the three high schools "could save as much as $470 million". The savings would be "reinvested into infrastructure investments at elementary and middle schools" [BB:3988494 p.3].
- **Scott's introduction** [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:04:10]:
  - The $1.15B modernization budget "has not decreased" and stays available [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:06:55].
  - Lincoln cost $225M; escalated at 8%/yr that is about $360M, the target for Cleveland and Wells [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:08:03].
  - The bond is needed because the state does not fully fund schools [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:09:11].
- **Staff on the cost-reduction process** (COO Jung, medium confidence):
  - The three project teams were asked to find reductions, including where designs exceed education specifications ("Ed specs") or code [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:14:21] [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:17:18].
  - The schools were at or near schematic design when paused. Final designs will return to the board, and there will be "at least a high level" design before the May vote [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:26:32].
- **Brim-Edwards (medium):**
  - Raised concern that the uncertainty was demobilizing the bond's "most fervent advocates" [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:12:43].
  - Noted the Bond Accountability Committee (BAC) had "no information" showing costs could reach the lower targets [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:27:19].
  - Asked that Cleveland off-site athletics be written into the materials [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:31:11].
  - Staff confirmed that the consolidated Cleveland line includes off-site baseball, softball and multipurpose fields, site still to be chosen [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:31:11].
- **Greene (medium):** wanted an independent "construction audit". He compared PPS costs with a Beaverton high school built for about $269M, and said "I don't want to cut a theater I don't want to cut band" [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:18:50].
- **Cleveland and Wells communities** turned out in force. Scott says they testified at the 5–6 pm bond listening session, a separate video [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@2:06:29]. A Cleveland 8th grader testified about inequitable softball field access and Title IX [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@0:24:33].
- **Albina Vision Trust / headquarters:**
  - Staff are building minimum criteria for a new headquarters and warehouse, likely at two sites.
  - Parking will be hard to replicate.
  - The board will approve the criteria [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@1:05:35] [YT:YLB6Aken-Vo@1:12:31].
