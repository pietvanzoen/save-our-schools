## Checked against the archive (Analysis)
- **The 80% figure.** PPS's Oct 20 deck says "8 in 10" of *all* students would be in a sustainable school under Scenario A ("7 in 10" under B), against "about half with no changes" [BB:14576326 p.8](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14576326?org=915#page=8). Her figure is for historically underserved students specifically. Her 24% baseline is not in the deck's text layer; the group charts are images, so it is unchecked here.
- **"Doesn't save meaningful money."** The district's Oct 20 figures, as the Oregonian reported them, put savings at about $1 million per closed building (about $14M under A), with about $18M in school-level cuts still needed [NEWS:NEWS-20261021-oregonian-1] (see `timeline.md`, 2026-10-21). "Not meaningful" is her characterization against a $65M gap.
- **The special-education cap.** Oregon's cap applies to the share of students funded at the special-education weight (11% of enrollment), not to "11% of SPED services". Her 18% figure is her statement. Analysis: wording, not substance.
