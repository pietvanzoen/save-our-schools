## Summary

- This was a governance-only meeting. Nothing touched rightsizing, the bond, facilities or the budget.
- Policy 1.20.010-P was to be revised so that a board vacancy must be filled from the departing director's zone, which conforms the policy to statute. The issue was found during Andrew Scott's resignation [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:00:46]. Action was expected in February.
- Brim-Edwards proposed ethics-policy (5.10.066-P) language barring board members from seeking district jobs while serving and for 365 days after [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:06:12].
  - Sullivan and DePass questioned what problem it solves.
  - The tentative landing point was no waiting period, but no applying while serving [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:39:59].
- Brim-Edwards raised zone-based board elections [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:30:46]:
  - DePass (medium) argued citywide elections have produced the most diverse board.
  - DePass also relayed Multnomah County elections' concerns about split precincts and the year-plus lead time [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:32:23].
  - Brim-Edwards will bring data [BB:3997965 p.2].
- Brim-Edwards floated a lobbying registration/transparency policy, modeled on Metro, for non-competitive expenditures such as curriculum, apps and Yondr pouches. Staff will draft it [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:47:44] [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:53:49].
- Sullivan asked the committee to take up a policy on AP class access for freshmen [YT:kd2kZ_ADP6M@0:54:34] [BB:3997965 p.2].
