## Summary
- **Chair's framing.** Chair Wang summarized his one-on-one calls with members into four common priorities [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:01:41]:
  - a new Teaching, Learning & Enrollment (TLE) committee, now established
  - student achievement and the achievement gap
  - a culture of central-office support for schools
  - school boundaries and enrollment, which he called "probably the most difficult one", to be done equitably [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:03:13] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZdKSNeHDaU&t=193s))
- **Superintendent's 2025-26 priorities ("A through E")** [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:05:52]:
  - **Albina projects:** Prophet Center relocation, Jefferson, Tubman, CBSE. Timelines and decision points due by June 2026.
  - **Boundaries:** a framework emphasizing "clarity and community voice", starting with the Jefferson cluster dual enrollment and moving toward "a district-wide framework" [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:06:46].
  - **Budget:** strategic reductions, "we don't want to just reduce in the name of reductions".
  - **Bargaining.**
  - **Construction:** timeline, transparency, tangible progress, and moving quickly to save money.
  - **District improvement plan:** academics and board goals.
  - **Excellence in service.**
- **Where the board's time will go.** Armstrong said the B's (boundaries, budget, bargaining, plus bond/construction) are where she will "lean on" the board most in 2025-26 [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:44:39].
- **Board feedback:**
  - adopt the A-E framework as the key focus areas
  - add academic achievement explicitly
  - add graduation and post-secondary readiness
  - track teacher absences and substitute fill rates in high-need schools (DePass)
  - track the successful-schools survey and the "catchment rate", which staff said would sit under Boundaries [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:30:28]
  - concern that the draft's language would give board leadership more power than the audit charter and the protocols allow; to be revisited at the August 19 retreat [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:48:22]

## Decisions & votes

## Rightsizing / closures
- The board and superintendent jointly named **boundaries** a top 2025-26 focus area:
  - the Jefferson cluster first
  - then "a district-wide framework that we can use to make decisions along the way" [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:07:06]
- Splitt expects boundaries to be "a huge conversation" that needs heavy board involvement [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:42:45].
- **Catchment rate.** A board member asked where "catchment rate" would be tracked. Staff placed it under Boundaries [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:30:51].
- **Analysis:** closures and "optimization" are not named in the draft focus areas or in the superintendent's priorities. The boundary framework is the vehicle. Compare with the June 10 TSCC statements about a "school optimization plan" [see 2025-06-10_YUCokoJ7-n0.md].

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- The draft key focus areas include "School Modernization Projects (Jefferson, Cleveland, Ida B. Wells, and Seismic Upgrades)" and the Prophet Center relocation [BB:3996524 p.1].
- Board members said the order should be explicitly non-prioritized. An email had complained that modernization appeared at the bottom of the list [YT:BZdKSNeHDaU@0:20:57].
