## Summary
- **Purpose.** Retreat on governance "coherence" (a symphony analogy: the board as conductor, the superintendent as first violin). The goal was a 2026-27 superintendent evaluation process [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@0:12:47–0:17:35].
- **Proposed process.** A year-long process with topic-by-topic feedback at already-calendared board meetings, a narrative "mastery-based" form without numeric ratings, and a year-end compilation. It was presented as evaluating "the system through the lens of the superintendent" [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@0:35:26] [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:54:12].
- **Friction about the existing tool.**
  - Splitt asked whether the board was "throwing away" the prior tool, which she thought had worked, and said she felt like no one had talked to the facilitators about where the board already was [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:05:17] [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:07:59].
  - Armstrong said the prior template covered only two years (2024-26) [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:09:21]. She also said last year's mid-year changes and public statements about her compensation departed from her contract and "really sets me up to be the bad guy in community" [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:31:40].
- **Rightsizing as an evaluation focus.** The board reached consensus to:
  - use the **District Continuous Improvement Plan (DCIP) domains** (literacy, math, attendance, 9th-grade on-track, with disaggregated focal-student data) rather than the expiring board goals [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:15:24–2:21:26] [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:39:04];
  - add **rightsizing as the 2026-27 "annual area of focus"**, judged on community-engagement quality, equity and demographic impacts, following the guiding principles, and the "financial health of the district" [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:40:25–2:50:39];
  - add operational areas for **financial stewardship** and **facilities/bond accountability** [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:54:05–2:58:11].
- **Timing.** Armstrong said the rightsizing "list of school closures" would be presented on 10/6, moved from 9/29 [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:07:16].
- **Open evaluation sessions.** Armstrong asked that the quarterly and final evaluations be held in open public session. Counsel noted that this is the employee's statutory right [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:20:52] [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@1:41:13].

## Decisions & votes
- No votes. The board reached informal consensus on evaluation content (DCIP domains, a rightsizing focus, financial stewardship, facilities/bond). Staff and leadership will calendar the topics, and the template will return to a public meeting [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@3:08:19] [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@3:30:06].

## Rightsizing / closures
- **How the board might judge the superintendent's rightsizing work** [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:40:25–2:54:05]:
  - which communities are engaged, and in what languages;
  - whether demographic impacts are considered;
  - whether the guiding principles were followed;
  - the outcome for the district's finances;
  - "exit tickets" at engagement sessions to measure whether people "felt heard".
- **Armstrong** asked for something "more rigorous" than counts of meetings: "it's not about just the number of places I go and who I talk to. it's not a count" [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:44:31]. She noted Engelsman had attended about seven of ten school community meetings [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:52:43].
- **Expectations for engagement.** A director said engagement should state up front that it sits closer to "inform and consult" on the participation spectrum, so that people are not later disappointed [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:51:20]. The speaker was not identified.
- **Facilitator's framing.** Rightsizing is "a crappy decision you have to make" and "the community is going to come at you hard". The board therefore needs a process evaluation it can point to [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:47:15].

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- Facilities and bond accountability are to be a separate evaluation area. Splitt suggested including short-term reports and long-term planning, plus items from the recent bond audit's 38 recommendations [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:56:09] [YT:gw0gfK9RiDQ@2:58:11].
