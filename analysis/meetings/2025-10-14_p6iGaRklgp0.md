## Summary
- **Superintendent ties rightsizing to program review.** Responding to Vietnamese DLI parents, Armstrong said "Growing Great Schools" requires looking at "our programming um our boundaries right sizing," and that "eventually" PPS must decide which programs to offer and how to support them [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@0:57:16] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6iGaRklgp0&t=3436s)) [BB:3998034 p.3].
- **BAC warns high schools are oversized.** BAC Chair Cara Turano: BAC members believe PPS is "building very large high schools without the student body to justify it," given declining enrollment and birth rates and a $465M project cost [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:47:15] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p6iGaRklgp0&t=6435s)). The BAC also called three simultaneous HS modernizations totaling $1.5B "a risky scenario" [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:44:42].
- Board pushback: Wang (medium) said 1,700-seat schools are really about 1,500 at an 85% "educational max," downsizing had saved little in a January exercise, and it would be unfair to shrink only the last three [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:51:13]; an unidentified member argued to "build for the future" like leaders did 100 years ago [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:49:59]; Student Rep Ritorto preferred a cushion [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:56:54].
- **Jefferson HS revised comprehensive plan approved 7–0** (Res. 7187): total budget $466M, about $25M below 2023 design; building construction 2027–summer 2029; students stay on site [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@2:08:48] [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@2:09:45] [BB:3998034 p.4].
- **Cleveland HS revised comprehensive plan approved 7–0** (Res. 7188): construction from June 2026, students relocated to the Marshall campus for three years, completion 2029; net savings about $1M; $15M 2025-bond allowance for Powell Park athletics [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@2:32:20] [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@2:33:14] [BB:3998034 p.4–5].
- **Seismic:** La Forte's FIO report relayed community fear that the sample priority formula underweights risk and calls for "consolidating into the safest buildings"; a member (low confidence: Splitt) stressed the formula is only a staff draft and the board will decide [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:08:26] [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:14:53].
- La Forte: deferred maintenance is "closing in on $2 billion" [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@1:11:48]. Analysis: the October 8 FIO meeting gave about $1 billion [YT:Z-mKpuZgLf0@1:08:16]; the figures conflict.
- **Finance Focus:** CFO Morrison said budget work compares pre-pandemic staffing and enrollment with today because programs and operations were never "reframed... to meet our enrollment decline." She is also benchmarking operations and "offerings at the schools" against like-size districts. PERS increases could add about $23M in 2027-29 [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@4:06:52] [YT:p6iGaRklgp0@4:06:07].
