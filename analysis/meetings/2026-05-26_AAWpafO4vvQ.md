## Summary
- **The board adopted Resolution 7307 (rightsizing Guiding Principles and Work Plan) 7–0.** Both student representatives voted yes [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:22:50] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAWpafO4vvQ&t=8570s)). Chase-Miller moved and La Forte seconded [BB:3998080 p.3].
- **The rightsizing timeline:** guiding-principle metrics and the framework come in late June or early July. Scenario engagement runs from late August into September or early October. The superintendent's recommendation is due by mid-November and the board vote in December, aligned with the budget, for changes starting in fall 2027 [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:44:06] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AAWpafO4vvQ&t=6246s)).
- **Revisions since May 12:**
  - "Safe" and "prioritize walkability and safe routes to schools" were added to the principles.
  - The principles now state "Neighborhood schools are the foundation of our system", linked to Policy 6.10.022.
  - The equity lens is defined, with its focal student groups.
  - A cohort-cohesion assessment was added to the implementation plan [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:41:01] [BB:3997273 p.1-2].
- **Walkability split the board.**
  - Chair Wang said making walkability the top priority would mean keeping every school open and doing nothing [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:51:07].
  - Chase-Miller warned that leaning on walkability could reproduce de facto neighborhood segregation [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:12:35].
  - Splitt framed the principles as aspirations with explained exceptions [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:06:24].
- **ACCESS Academy came up inside the rightsizing debate.**
  - La Forte asked that "right pathways" include ACCESS, and that the plan consider expanding it [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:46:56].
  - Engelsman floated a second ACCESS site [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@4:39:49].
  - Student Rep Ritorto endorsed expansion [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@3:04:42].
- **ACCESS busing "hub model".** Staff proposed cutting ACCESS busing from 10 routes and 125 stops to 5 routes and 13 school-based hub stops:
  - average ride time falls from 71 to 33 minutes
  - about $1M saved against the $1.5M cost of the current routes
  - about $500K in remaining cost before 70% state reimbursement
  - The board reacted warmly. A family meeting and a decision were set for June 9 [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:28:02] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:34:35].
- **Budget Committee approved the 2026-27 budget and tax levy (Reso 7308) 7–0** [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@3:16:18]. Wang called it a deliberately "risky" budget. He prefers setting a lower baseline after consolidations rather than over-cutting staff now [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:54:34].
- **The PAT 2026-27 one-year agreement (Reso 7306) was approved 7–0**, with a 2% COLA from January [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@0:56:59] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:01:14].
- **Bond update.** Jefferson, Cleveland and Ida B. Wells are on schedule for August 2029 openings. Jefferson breaks ground May 29 and Cleveland June 12. Engelsman challenged permit dates that moved since February; staff said the slide graphic was wrong and there are no delays [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@4:14:29] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@4:20:53].
- **Contracting rules (Reso 7291).** The board did not rescind. It voted to send the rules back to the Policy Committee with no amendments. The chair announced it passed 7–0 [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@4:34:03]. The draft minutes (OCR) say the motion "failed" [BB:3998080 p.5]. The video contradicts this.
- **Routine and ceremonial items:** student poetry performance; State of the Arts report; spring MAP/i-Ready data dive (reading up, math slightly down) [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@3:20:32]; consent agenda 7309–7310 approved 7–0 [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@4:35:12].

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Framing (Franco).** Franco presented rightsizing as a move "from short-term reactions to long-term sustainability", with a recommendation in November [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:32:52].
- **Engagement results (Calvert).** More than 600 families took part in board-session polling and about 1,600 more in an online Slido poll. Their top three factors were right pathways, right boundaries and right balance [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:34:54] [BB:3997282].
  - At Village Table and Roosevelt sessions, "right mix of students" (demographics) was a top priority [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:36:28].
  - Culturally specific sessions are continuing with Native and Black families, plus linguistically specific groups [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:37:15].
- **Southeast Guiding Coalition review.** Staff are working with researchers, including "Dr. Green", on the SEGC lessons. A final report is expected within about a week. Four work groups are drafting what a "well-resourced school" means, with drafts of all four principles hoped for by the end of June [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:59:12] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:03:57].
- **Board positions:**
  - **Sullivan:** the draft reflects what was heard; she supports it [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:46:23].
  - **La Forte:** ACCESS belongs in "right pathways" and should be expanded, not shrunk [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:46:56].
  - **DePass (medium confidence):** welcomed "safe" and cohort cohesion. Asked where retrofits or "tenant improvements" for a converted building fit in the timeline. Staff said they will add this, noting the seismic conversations [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:48:47] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:50:01].
  - **Wang:**
    - praised the clause protecting recently impacted and Title I communities
    - flagged the walkability contradiction [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:50:21]
    - Staff replied that trade-offs will be made explicit [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:52:14].
  - **Engelsman (medium confidence):**

    - urged fixing the harm to about 25 fifth graders a year split by SEGC decisions [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:52:41] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@1:55:43] [YT:AAWpafO4vvQ@2:00:52].
