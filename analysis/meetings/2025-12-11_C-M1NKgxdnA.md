## Summary

- **Charter schools overview:** there are seven charters within PPS boundaries (five PPS-sponsored, two state-sponsored), receiving about $20.8M this year ($19M state school fund pass-through, $1.3M SIA, arts tax, and a $275K equity allocation). Board policy sets 95% pass-through for schools serving 50%+ historically underserved students, otherwise 90% [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:12:30] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:19:37] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:20:48].
- **TAG services:** PPS has been under ODE corrective action for several years, continuing through fall 2027. 7,675 students (about 19%) are TAG-identified; staff said over-identification is a bigger issue than under-identification. A universal non-verbal screener moved to 1st grade; newcomer ELD students are also screened [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:44:44] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:48:55] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@1:00:13].
- **Access Academy:** about 325 students in grades 2–8, admitted by lottery from 62 home schools; 75 new students this year with 415 on the wait list. Eligibility rests solely on a 99th-percentile test score, which staff called "the exact opposite of tag identification" [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:56:47] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:57:26] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:59:05].
- Chase-Miller pressed on Access under-representation of girls and students of color; the principal acknowledged a gap and said eligibility criteria are being rewritten in an administrative directive with community input [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@1:13:17] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@1:14:17].

## Rightsizing / closures

- Public commenters argued that **Access Academy contributes to under-enrolled neighborhood schools** (e.g., 31 students from one Sabin/Irvington zip code; $1.2M on Access transportation; 75% of students from the 11 wealthiest zip codes). They asked the board to audit or close Access and redirect about $4M to neighborhood schools [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:04:06] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:04:43] [YT:C-M1NKgxdnA@0:06:22]. **Analysis:** this links program placement (focus/academic programs) to neighborhood-school enrollment, a theme relevant to later rightsizing decisions.
