## Summary
- **PERS overview (Morrison)** [YT:kIF9qrrMy6E@0:12:18] [BB:14330795 p.7]:
  - PERS is a defined-benefit pension. Employees contribute 6%; the assumed return is 6.9%, and employers cover any shortfall.
  - For an average educator, PPS pays about $22,578 a year toward PERS plus pension-obligation-bond (POB) debt service.
- **The 2027-28 budget cliff.**
  - PPS's 2002–03 POB side accounts are expiring. Their rate credits disappear while POB debt service continues, producing "the biggest budget challenge" in 2027-28 [YT:kIF9qrrMy6E@0:22:25] [YT:kIF9qrrMy6E@0:23:06].
