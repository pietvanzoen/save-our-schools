## Summary
- This was the last meeting of the 2024-25 board. Directors Brim-Edwards, Greene and Hollands and Student Rep Kunsevi were leaving.
- **Consent agenda (7-0).** It included Res. 7134, which accepts the county certification of the May 20, 2025 vote authorizing up to $1.83B in general obligation (GO) bonds [BB:3998008 p.4].
  - Board questions covered the Jigsaw/TeachTown special-education curriculum and staffing for the Reading Results high-impact tutoring contract [BB:3998008 p.2].
- **Climate Crisis Response Policy annual report** (Kat Davis; committee members). Highlights:
  - the climate dashboard
  - the decarbonization tool
  - student climate projects
  - a survey of 276 students
  - The committee thanked the board for "making sure that the modernized schools are going to be all electric" despite cost pressure [YT:UN2PCZyBB2Y@0:19:35].
- **Prophet Center relocation, Phase 2 staff engagement** (Kafoury, White):
  - A request for information (RFI) is out, and property evaluation with Albina Vision Trust (AVT) is beginning.
  - Staff listening sessions on June 11-12 drew about 250 people. Union listening sessions are planned through the summer.
  - Executive-session briefings to the board on candidate properties are planned for September through December [BB:3996480 p.3-5] [YT:UN2PCZyBB2Y@1:02:51].
- **Departing members recognized** [YT:UN2PCZyBB2Y@1:21:41]. Brim-Edwards' farewell recounted:
  - reopening Tubman, Kellogg, Harrison Park and Roseway Heights as middle schools
  - about $80M in the bond for equitable athletic fields
  - the view that leadership, "not the modernized schools or the selling of the headquarters", drives outcomes
