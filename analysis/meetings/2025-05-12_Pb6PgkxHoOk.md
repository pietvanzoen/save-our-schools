## Summary
- **Kindergarten entry age.** Chair Brim-Edwards withdrew her proposed change to Policy 4.10.020-P (age and grade at entrance) for lack of board support [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:02:14].
  - She kept her rationale: in a time of declining enrollment, telling families whose child just misses the September 1 cutoff to go elsewhere risks losing them for good [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:02:42].
- **Capture rate.** Brim-Edwards said PPS's "capture rate" has fallen into the 70s, below 80% for the first time she has seen. The capture rate is the share of resident school-age children who attend PPS. She called it a "sound the alarm moment" [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:32:19] ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pb6PgkxHoOk&t=1939s)).
  - She also repeated that PPS has lost about 10% of enrollment since 2019, against about 6% statewide [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:04:48].
- **Staff response (Dr. Glasgow).** Staff will develop talking points so any first point of contact can steer near-cutoff families toward pre-K and options [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:18:50].
  - PPS has 910 pre-K seats at 14 sites, compared with about 2,900 kindergartners [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:14:30].
  - The districtwide enrollment campaign is being led from the superintendent's office (Dr. Franco named) [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:28:45].
- **Why families leave.** Another committee member (unidentified) attributed declining capture to rising inner-city incomes and more school choice [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:30:54]. Brim-Edwards countered that the long COVID closures and the 2023 strike drove families out [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:31:50].
- **Solicitation policy.** The draft Solicitation Registration & Reporting policy (lobbying and vendor-contact disclosure; $100K placeholder threshold) was reviewed. No action was taken [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:33:34].
- **Student rep policy.** The committee unanimously referred revisions to the Student Representative and District Student Council policy (1.20.012-P) to the full board for a first reading. The changes allow two reps per school plus an alternate, and lower the attendance requirement for credit from 90% to 80% [BB:3998004 p.2].

## Decisions & votes
- Policy 1.20.012-P (Student Rep and DSC) was referred to the full board for first reading (unanimous) [BB:3998004 p.2].
- Policy 4.10.020-P change was withdrawn by the chair. No vote.

## Rightsizing / closures
- No closure or consolidation discussion.
- **Analysis:** the capture-rate discussion is relevant background. Board members were treating PPS's loss of market share, not just birth-rate decline, as an urgent enrollment driver in May 2025.

## Bond / seismic / facilities

## Budget & enrollment
- **Capture rate:** "in the 70s", against a historical rate above 80%. Brim-Edwards cites figures from the board's budget Q&A [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:29:28].
- **Pre-K demand:** pre-K demand exceeds supply at the county level. PPS pre-K seats are filling, and the drop points are at kindergarten and beyond [YT:Pb6PgkxHoOk@0:26:22].
