## Summary
- **Center for Black Student Excellence (CBSE).** The board voted 7–0 to enter a purchase and sale agreement for the One North building (Res. 7165).
  - Price: $16M against a $15.2M appraisal, from the $60M 2020-bond CBSE allocation [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:07:06].
  - A 90-day due-diligence period follows, with a final vote expected in December [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:07:51].
  - La Forte's amendment requiring an annual operating-cost analysis by Nov 4 was tabled 7–0 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@2:29:26].
- **Engelsman said explicitly: "We're going to be having conversations about closing schools."** She made the point while arguing PPS cannot fund CBSE operations [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:43:44]. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGAs0lyZ6kw&t=6237s
- **Outdoor School (Res. 7167) failed 2–5.** Engelsman and La Forte voted yes; Chase-Miller, DePass, Splitt, Sullivan and Wang voted no.
  - Middle-school and K-8 principals and the administrators' association (PAPSA) opposed diverting funds [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@3:16:42].
  - By consensus, the board directed a cost-only analysis [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@3:23:30].
- **Lobbying registration policy (Res. 7164) adopted 7–0** [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@3:35:37].
- **Splitt nominated to the OSBA Legislative Policy Committee (Res. 7174)**, 6–0 with Splitt abstaining [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@3:38:42].
- **Seismic study report** (Holmes Structures):
  - 92 campuses and 410 building parts scored 0–10 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:06:22].
  - Option A: full retrofits at the 8–10 highest-risk campuses, per the ballot language. Option B: the highest-risk building parts district-wide. Option C: a hybrid, which staff recommended [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:07:07–4:08:37].
  - Staff criteria explicitly include "anticipated impacts of potential school rights sizing" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:03:18].
  - **La Forte, DePass and Wang** all tied seismic choices to rightsizing and consolidation [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:10:56–4:23:14].
  - The decision was sent to the 9/16 FIO meeting, with board direction targeted for 9/30 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:27:02].
- **Budget brief (Morrison):**
  - Local option levy collections are about **$5M below budget**, "the equivalent of 33 licensed FTE" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:39:24].
  - The state forecast is negative ("prepare and plan not panic") [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:42:29].
  - **Reserves exhausted** in 2025-26 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:44:02].
  - About $100M of the $868M operating budget goes to PERS [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:47:06].
- **Procedeo contract amendment (Res. 7175) adopted 6–1** (Engelsman no). Engelsman moved to postpone, seconded by La Forte; the postponement failed 2–5 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@6:04:34].
  - The contract brings in interim leadership of the Office of School Modernization (OSM) after Sr. Director Stormy Shanks resigned, with six OSM vacancies [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:59:28].
  - An OSM senior project manager and an AECOM-affiliated parent testified against it [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:27:59–5:40:58].
- **Other items:**
  - Michael "Chappie" Grice Day proclamation [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@0:50:43].

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Engelsman (CBSE debate):** "PPS doesn't have a penny to spend on this. We're going to be having conversations about closing schools." She also cited schools closing for heat because of deferred maintenance and HVAC gaps [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:43:44].
- **Seismic and rightsizing link:**
  - Franco's process slide names "anticipated impacts of potential school rights sizing" as a PPS criterion [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:03:18].
  - La Forte: "it's hard for me to to look at this without thinking about the conversation that we're going to have this year about right sizing our schools … I'd hate to spend money on a facility that ends up being put out of commission" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:10:56].
  - DePass called for a decision tree. "We can't have this conversation in a vacuum because we've got 81 campuses because we're going to be kind of looking at boundaries in Northeast … we're not going to be inhabiting every single building" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:11:42].
  - Armstrong: "wondering if the um enrollment would be a key factor" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:13:17].
  - Franco: "we can't retrofit obviously any any building that potentially is going to be up for for any level of right sizing" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:15:35].
  - Wang: staff "would know" which schools are "definitely safe from any consolidation". Those that overlap with the highest seismic need should be priorities. He backed Option C [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:21:41].
- **Procedeo:** Franco said "the right sizing conversation is going to happen", and the interim OSM leader is expected to take part [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:01:44].
- **Integrated Grants report:** staff listed "Enrollment balancing and facilities" among 2024-25 barriers requiring "careful coordination and communication to manage transitional complexity" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@3:48:44].
- **Public comment:** a former board member argued that operating-cost analyses were never demanded for modernizations "despite enrolling declines that we all know exist" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@2:11:02].
- **Analysis:** by 9/9 the full board openly assumed rightsizing would happen in 2025-26. Members treated it as a gating factor for seismic retrofits and for any new facility commitments (CBSE), and some used the words "closing schools", "consolidation" and "put out of commission".

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **CBSE / One North** [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:06:19–2:35:37]:
  - Two buildings, about 75,000 sq ft, built 2015 (Holst), at Vancouver and Fremont in the Albina neighborhood. It has existing commercial tenants and fewer than 10 parking stalls [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:13:12–1:26:14].
  - Bond funds can be used for the building only, not programs or staff [BB:3998019 p.3].
  - La Forte checked the property tax at about **$317,000/yr** [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@2:14:09]. The legal advisor said leases are largely triple-net [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@1:36:06].
  - Due-diligence updates go to FIO on Oct 8, Nov 3 and Dec 9 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@2:32:33].
  - Engelsman pledged to apply the same fiscal scrutiny "for literally every project" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@2:34:07].
- **Seismic report:**
  - Retrofit capacity: about two full retrofits per summer, 68-day summer windows, and often two-summer projects. SRGP grants (the state Seismic Rehabilitation Grant Program) are up to $2.5M per building, need whole-building retrofits, and only one can be won per round (Jole) [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:13:17–4:20:10].
  - Splitt asked that Rieke Elementary (on the Ida B. Wells site) be considered for efficiency [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:16:20].
  - DePass wants resilience-hub mapping with equity data layers [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:23:14].
  - Safe Structures PPS questions were read into the record by La Forte [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:32:20].
- **Procedeo / OSM** [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:53:22–6:06:05]:
  - Armstrong cited six OSM vacancies, Procedeo's "billion dollar bond" experience and PPS's poor reputation with contractors. Only one firm bid the Jefferson CM/GC [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:12:38].
  - The goal is all three high schools open by fall 2029 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:45:30].
  - Staff said Jefferson comprehensive plan savings of about $25M would be shown at FIO [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:58:25].
  - The Procedeo final report on Jefferson is due Oct 22 [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:06:23].
  - Engelsman (medium confidence) opposed an out-of-state firm without an RFP [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:07:55]. La Forte said she would vote no on any longer-term contract without an RFP [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@6:05:20].
  - Sullivan, Chase-Miller, Splitt and DePass supported, citing past board delays and presumption of competence [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@5:59:58–6:03:46].
