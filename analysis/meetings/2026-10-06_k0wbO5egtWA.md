## Summary
- **Purpose.** Board policy requires an annual ethics and public meetings refresher. This year's training added more on public meetings and a section on FERPA and public records at board request [YT:k0wbO5egtWA@0:00:27] [YT:k0wbO5egtWA@0:01:05].

  - Potential conflicts: announce, then you may participate.
  - Actual conflicts: announce, then do not participate.
  - Prohibited use of office.
  - Gifts: limit of $50 from a single source per year, a new exception for up to $100 of swag from the public entity, and conference hospitality scenarios.
  - Penalties: up to $1,000 per violation [YT:k0wbO5egtWA@0:22:36].
- **2025 change.** Members with a conflict "simply within the budget" may still participate in and vote on adopting a budget. This contrasts with approving a collective bargaining agreement [YT:k0wbO5egtWA@0:10:38].

  - Members must complete a Government Ethics Commission-approved public meetings training during their term [YT:k0wbO5egtWA@0:24:25].
