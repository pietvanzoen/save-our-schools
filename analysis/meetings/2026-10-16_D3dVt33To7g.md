Analysis:
- **Phasing examples were not new.** The June 2028 dates for Sellwood and Marysville are already in the published per-school family guides. Sellwood: "building in 2028" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sellwood.pdf p.3]. Marysville: "Delaying full" implementation [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/marysville.pdf p.3]. So her answer that "some things" will be phased is not yet a new concession, and she rejected region-by-region phasing in substance by not taking it up.
- **School fund wording.** "our governor sets the state school fund" (also said on KPTV Oct 6 [YT:IF_vv3wXgAE@0:01:44]) is loose. The governor proposes a budget and the Legislature adopts it, as she herself noted ("they adopt their budget in June") [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:12:40].
- **"No blends" target vs what she promised.** She defined well-resourced as no blends [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:07:21], yet "Not all of our elementary schools will be at that ideal level" [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:10:23], and specific resources cannot be promised before state funding [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:12:33]. A Sellwood teacher's voicemail asked for a written class-size cap [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:07:44]. She did not commit to one; she committed only to a staffing plan.
- **Opt-out modeling.** She says the modeling includes an opt-out percentage. No such model appears in the archived Board materials as of Oct 16 (see `analysis/people/kimberlee-armstrong.md`).

## Positions & commitments

- **Committed to:**
  - a staffing plan attached to the early-November final recommendation [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:09:09];
  - reinvesting all closure and consolidation funds in schools [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:10:44];
  - presenting the opt-out analysis [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:13:43];
  - publishing engagement themes and initial thinking at the Nov 4 board meeting [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:17:47];
  - some phasing in the final recommendation [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:03:52].
- **Ruled out:**
  - Scenario C ("not into delaying the inevitable") [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:21:46];
  - slowing down as such ("what are we waiting for?") [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:02:23];
  - an outside audit of the enrollment data, in effect ("we don't um question those numbers") [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:05:57];
  - specific resource promises before the state school fund is known [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:12:33].
- **Leaning:** Scenario B with flexibility in "timelines" and "adjustments" [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:20:44] [YT:D3dVt33To7g@0:21:06]. She stressed that this was her own view, "independent of team processing or or where others may be at".
- **Not addressed:** adding schools to the lists; the requested class-size cap; the specific Sunnyside K-8 change; who would vote for B.
