- **Analysis:** this is the earliest reference in this batch to a named district "optimization" workstream running in parallel with seismic planning. It is likely a precursor to the rightsizing initiative and should be cross-checked against later meetings. Low enrollment is explicitly a factor that could deprioritize a high-risk building for retrofit.

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **Seismic prioritization method** [YT:GCPTxiAnThA@0:50:12]:
  - **Risk score:** based on construction year and type, adjusted for prior partial upgrades.
  - **Importance score:** based on the type of space affected (assembly versus storage) and Title I/TSI/CSI status. TSI and CSI are federal school-improvement designations.
  - **Low-cost, high-safety-return items:** for example, a single URM wall in an assembly area.
- **Prototype "Jones schools."** Similar outer-Southeast buildings (Harrison Park, Lent) were designed by the same firm. One cost study could inform seismic upgrades across the group [YT:GCPTxiAnThA@0:46:52].
- **Bond execution plan.** A plan with schedules and KPIs for modernizations, athletics, deferred maintenance and seismic is being drafted. 2025-bond reporting to the BAC starts in July [YT:GCPTxiAnThA@0:54:35].
- **Curriculum funding.** The 2020-bond curriculum budget rose from $53.4M to $63.3M using contingency to bridge the delayed bond [YT:GCPTxiAnThA@0:38:54].
