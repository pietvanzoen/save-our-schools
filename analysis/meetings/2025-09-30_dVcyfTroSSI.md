## Summary
- **Consent agenda (Res. 7178–7184) adopted 7–0.** It includes the appointment of three members to the Climate Crisis Response Committee (CCRC; Res. 7182), an employee-indemnification resolution (7184), off-campus activities (7181), contracts and minutes [BB:3998027 p.2] [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@0:02:19].
- **Fund for PPS annual report** [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@0:04:38]:
  - Champion PPS has invested $930K this year, mostly in high-impact tutoring [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@0:03:05].
  - Engelsman questioned the $1M fundraising goal vs. about $3.5M raised under the old local foundations, "in a significant budget crunch time" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@0:26:00].
  - Wang defended the district-wide foundation model [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@0:27:31].
  - Armstrong said local foundations had funded 26–52 teacher FTE per year, mostly at schools "without the designations", meaning not Title I/CSI (Comprehensive Support and Improvement) schools [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@0:29:03].
- **Finance Focus: contracts and procurement (Courtnage)** [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@1:11:39]:
  - Wang proposed raising the board approval threshold from $150K to $250K, which staff said is national best practice and the state formal-procurement threshold [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@1:24:38–1:26:09]. DePass noted the City and County have done so.
  - La Forte, Sullivan and Splitt asked about cooperative purchasing, contract monitoring, renewals and the "universe" of contracted-out work [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@1:26:57–1:44:42].
- **Draft 2025-26 board work plan** (Wang) [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@1:47:00–2:59:36] [BB:3996623].
  - Buckets: Albina projects (CBSE), **Boundaries** (Jefferson dual assignment monthly through a January action; board town halls Feb–Mar), budget and finance, bargaining, construction (bond), the district improvement plan, and governance.
  - Splitt asked for a committee layer and an engagement layer [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@1:50:05].
  - Engelsman wanted academic excellence and absenteeism added [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:02:19].
  - **La Forte asked that "right sizing our schools" be named explicitly** [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:18:25].
  - Wang said the board town halls under Boundaries are "about eventually right sizing" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:19:14].
- **Board stipends** [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@3:00:22–3:27:05]:
  - HB 2753 (2023) allows up to $500/month; ethics constraints shape three implementation options.
  - Splitt warned of a "rough news story" amid cuts and preferred Option 1 (future boards only) but would go with the majority.
  - Ritorto, Wang and a member citing her working-class family favored Option 2.
  - Consensus for **Option 2**, to return Oct 14. Estimated cost about $33,256/yr from the general fund [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@3:17:57].
- **Future topics:** Yondr pouches; a request (Engelsman, medium confidence) for a deep dive on **transportation costs**, including transport to non-neighborhood schools, because PPS will be "looking for pennies under every couch cushion". Armstrong: "we'll be bringing something in future months" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@3:28:36–3:29:21].

## Rightsizing / closures
- **The board's 2025-26 work plan treats rightsizing as embedded in "Boundaries".**
  - La Forte: "we don't have right sizing our schools on here which we know is going to come up … I think that the public wants to know what our plan is for the year so I do think you know it's okay to be clear" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:18:25].
  - Wang: the town halls are left vague because "that discussion progresses … the first town hall may be about like what are your community's biggest priorities? … what are things that are worth protecting? … yes, it's about eventually right sizing" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:19:14].
  - Wang: a district boundary vote ("this June or … probably January of next school year") will "backfill like so many boxes" of engagement, meetings and maps [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:15:25].
  - Wang: "Doing boundary work right … make sure that all our middle schools have … all the course offerings … consolidating our resources where they're needed" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:48:14].
- Armstrong: heavy engagement is coming on "budget and enrollment and school right sizing" [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:07:43].
- DePass recalled the "Southeast Guiding Coalition" (an earlier enrollment/boundary process) as very time-consuming [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:08:30].
- **Analysis:** this is the first board meeting in the batch where the timing of a district-wide boundary/rightsizing vote is discussed: June 2026 or about January 2027, per Wang. The draft work plan shows board town halls in Feb–Mar 2026 [BB:3996623].

- Adams: annual data cadence of OSAS (state test results) in October, current achievement in December, attendance in January, graduation in February, successful schools in March, growth in spring [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:42:05].
- The district continuous improvement plan (DIP) is coming to the full board on Oct 28 [YT:dVcyfTroSSI@2:36:47].
