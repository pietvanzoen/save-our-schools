## Summary
- **Certification.** TSCC staff found the 2026-27 approved budget "reasonable" and "in substantial compliance with budget law", with no recommendations or objections. The commission voted unanimously to certify it [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:18:08] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:18:50].
- **Rightsizing timeline and goal (Franco).** Franco re-stated the rightsizing timeline:
  - Guiding principles were adopted in May. Next come a decision framework and criteria, then scenarios in the fall.
  - The superintendent recommends in November and the board votes in December. Changes begin in fall 2027 [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:52:17] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:53:48] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eD_6WvmchGw&t=3137s)).
  - Research suggests "roughly a 15 to 20% savings in operational costs" per fully closed site, but "we are not doing this for the purpose of of cost savings" [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:55:46] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eD_6WvmchGw&t=3346s)).
  - Savings would first fund transition supports, not budget balancing [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:57:08].
  - Modeling is done in-house by four work groups [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:57:53].
- **Property sales.** A staff member (unidentified) noted that savings estimates exclude selling real property. Current policy routes sale proceeds to facility improvements, and staff want to avoid one-time funds for operations [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:56:25].
- **Commissioner Moore on past rightsizing.** Rita Moore said she began such work "in 2008". Her "perception of the Southeast uh process" was "same old same old same mistakes", and she offered to share lessons [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:58:43] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:59:03].
- **PERS is the main structural threat.** Morrison said that without the expected PERS rate spike PPS would have a "fully operationalized" budget after 2026-27 [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:08:18]. The PERS stabilization reserve was drawn to zero in 2025-26. The actual employer rate arrives in October [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:15:36] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:16:19].
- **Blended classrooms and enrollment.** Blended classrooms are rising, including first-time 1/2 blends. Staff said they are partly driven by low K-2 enrollment. Elementary class sizes were held [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:19:16] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:35:51].
- **Bond program management (Presidio).** The aim is on-time, on-budget delivery, not savings. About $4.56M of the $61M contract has been spent, as expected. Presidio covers the three high-school modernizations plus Grace Adair, while PPS staff run the "consolidated" projects (seismic, HVAC) [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:23:37] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:27:02] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:27:24].
- **Achievement (Adams).** ELA is back to pre-pandemic levels (about 58% proficient) and math is above them (about 51%) [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:28:52]. Armstrong credited cabinet stability [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:32:41].

## Decisions & votes
- **TSCC certification letter.** Moved and seconded by commissioners; carried unanimously by voice [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:18:50]. No PPS board action.

## Rightsizing / closures
- **Commissioner's question.** A commissioner asked about "boundary changes and closures of facilities" given declining enrollment and rising building costs [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:51:45].
- **Franco's answer:**
  - Drivers: declining enrollment, aging infrastructure, fiscal constraints [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:52:17].
  - The four guiding principles: well-resourced schools, healthy learning environments, educational options, equity lens. "Six" board engagement sessions were held [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:53:03].
  - Next steps: decision framework, then criteria, then modeling, then fall scenarios, then the November recommendation and December vote, with implementation in fall 2027 [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:53:48].
  - PPS consulted Oregon PTA and "Dr. Terren Green" (caption) of Racially Just Schools, who assessed the Southeast Guiding Coalition process. Franco said PPS historically had not studied its lessons learned [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:55:00].
  - Savings: about 15–20% of a site's operating costs if fully closed, before any property sale. "This is about consistency of student experience" [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:55:46] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:56:25].
  - Lesson from SEGC: success depends on supporting transitions, so early savings cannot balance the budget [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:57:08].
- **Analysis:** this is the clearest on-record staff estimate of closure savings in this batch. It is framed per closed site, not district-wide.

## Bond / seismic / facilities
- **Presidio management.** Presidio provides executive oversight of the three high-school modernizations (Jefferson, Cleveland, Ida B. Wells) and Grace Adair. Ida B. Wells groundbreaking is planned for August [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:24:21] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:28:00].
- **Accountability.** Presidio's PMP includes KPIs, with monthly board updates, quarterly BAC meetings and monthly FIO meetings [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:25:50].
- **Grace Adair Center for Educational Excellence** (former Center for Black Student Excellence site):
  - $43.7M of the 2020 bond allocation remains after the purchase.
  - The programming framework is called "Birth to Brilliance".
  - PPS is part of a Harlem Children's Zone superintendent cohort. No community partners have been named yet [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:59:29] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:00:14] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:03:18].
- **Security cameras.** More than 6,000 cameras have been deployed through the 2020 bond, and staff credited them with reducing vandalism [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:07:01].

## Budget & enrollment
- **Budget process.** This is the fourth year of central-office cuts, and about a third of FTE reductions fell on instructional support. Special-education staffing ratios are protected:
  - focal or specialized classrooms: 1 teacher : 11 students, plus 3 paraeducators
  - learning center model: 1 : 26 [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:09:27] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:17:56].
- **Principal supervisors** have fallen from 10 to 8 to 7 [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:13:35].
- **Contract growth.** Contract costs rose because of student transportation and utilities, which were under-budgeted in 2025-26, and the modernization program-management contract [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:20:07].
- **School days.** Each added school day would cost about $5M, which is not affordable [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:40:14].
- **Blends and enrollment.** Blends are "partially been driven by our student enrollment", which is significantly lower in K-2 [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@0:35:51].
- **Security.** Campus security cuts are focused away from schools. PPS is not returning to SROs [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:06:16] [YT:eD_6WvmchGw@1:08:28].
