## Summary

- **Most important for this archive:** the board adopted a Brim-Edwards amendment, 4–2 (DePass, Wang no), to the Cleveland and Wells pre-construction contracts resolution. It requires staged public updates on high school designs and cost reductions (March 18, April 8, April 15, and through May). Item 5 also directs that **"The Board will be provided an outline of a path to enrollment balancing across PPS high schools and a general enrollment strategy to address the reductions in enrollment PPS across the district"** [BB:3997983 p.5–6] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:19:28] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:42:27].
  - Item 5 was not discussed aloud; the debate centered on bond reporting.
  - Analysis: this is an early formal board directive toward high-school enrollment balancing, adopted as a rider on a contracts vote.
- **Pre-construction contracts** for Cleveland (Skanska) and Ida B. Wells (Hoffman) were combined into Res. 7067/7068 and passed 6–0, after a contested call-the-question appeal that tied 3–3 and was resolved by the student rep's unofficial "yes" [BB:3997983 p.4–6] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:43:13] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:46:17].
- **Greene pressed on CM/GC cost overruns:** "currently what we're doing doesn't work" and "no reason why we should be spending half a billion dollars on a single solitary High School" [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:05:41] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:17:09].
  - Staff (Jung): Lincoln and McDaniel finished on time and under budget. A roughly $50M contractor claim exists but is unresolved [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:12:29] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@3:15:37].
- **Public comment on capacity and closures:** a commenter who said he served on the board 2017–2021 opposed the bond as a high-school "overbuild" (captioned as capacity for "177,000" versus a projected "11,000 high school students in 10" years). He said he was "encourage[d] that uh Dr Armstrong has said she's going to look at school closures" ([link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GM0ozDVDOQ4&t=2503s)) [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:41:43].
  - DePass (medium) countered with new family housing in the Jefferson catchment and a PSU demographer's finding of teen-family growth in the Franklin and Jefferson clusters [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:53:58].

  - Survey of about 4,000 respondents: reduce class size; protect classroom instruction; eliminate a few programs rather than cut all [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@1:15:16].
  - Armstrong explained that schools got a staffing model with flexibility. Brim-Edwards and Splitt debated the trade-offs of flexibility (career coordinators, arts) [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@1:23:37] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@1:28:13] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@1:45:54].

## Rightsizing / closures

- **Enrollment-balancing directive (item 5 of the amendment):** "PPS high schools have wide divergences in enrollment numbers. The Board will be provided an outline of a path to enrollment balancing across PPS high schools and a general enrollment strategy to address the reductions in enrollment PPS across the district" [BB:3997983 p.5]. Adopted 4–2.
- **Public comment (former director, 2017–21):**
  - The final two high-school modernizations would overbuild; elementary and middle schools also need rebuilding.
  - He welcomed the superintendent's statement that she will "look at school closures". He framed the trend as falling birth rates [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:41:43] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:42:29].
  - Answering Hollands, he said he had tried in vain during his term to get staff to "link enrollment projections with modernization" [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:52:27] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:53:12].
  - Analysis: no board or staff statement in this meeting confirms the superintendent had said she would look at closures. Treat it as a commenter's claim.
- **DePass (medium) on Jefferson:**
  - Listed new family-size housing projects in the Jefferson catchment, including 75 units at Alberta/Williams and 62 at Carter Commons.
  - Cited a PSU demographer on growth of families with teenagers in the Franklin and Jefferson clusters [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:53:58] [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:54:45].
  - The commenter replied that bringing Jefferson to 1,700 would require far more units [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@0:55:30].
- **PAT President Bonilla (union comment):** "until our state can ensure that we have affordable housing in our city we will not have the enrollment to sustain this District" [YT:GM0ozDVDOQ4@2:07:21].
