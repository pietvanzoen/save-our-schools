## Key findings

- **Splitt's "Scenario D" is now known in detail** [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
  - **Closures.** Six elementaries forecast at 250 or fewer: Creston, Buckman, Woodmere, Sabin, Irvington, Peninsula.
  - **Routing.** Creston goes to Arleta, not Atkinson, and its DHH program moves with it. Part of Sabin goes to Vernon, so King keeps Mandarin. Part of Woodmere goes to Lewis.
  - **Spared for now:** James John, Beach, Rose City Park, Marysville, Lewis, Sellwood MS, Stephenson and Maplewood. Southwest instead gets a boundary process among eight elementaries.
  - **DLI.** Spanish DLI stays co-located pending a districtwide committee.
  - **What it leaves.** She concedes it leaves nine elementaries at 250–300 students and three under 250.
  - This fills in what she presented at the work session [YT:xByUlijpcnE@5:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xByUlijpcnE&t=18145s).
- **Engelsman's changes** [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html):
  - keep Skyline feeding Lincoln;
  - keep Sunnyside's middle grades;
  - start focus options in grade 2;
  - close no school with more than $250,000 of bond work;
  - move the vote to February or March: "If we push to February or March, communities and families can keep pushing on the legislature for a fix".
- **The Board vote date differs between sources.** The Oregonian gives Dec. 17 [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html). PPS's spokesperson, KGW and the Board calendar give Dec. 15 [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-wweek-1](https://www.wweek.com/news/schools/2026/10/20/parent-group-asks-pps-for-seismic-bond-spending-plan-amid-rightsizing/) [NEWS:NEWS-20261012-kgw-1](https://www.kgw.com/article/news/education/portland-public-schools-closure-parents-letter-governor-kotek-drazan/283-c323ba33-e225-420f-8e86-68828a08aba8) [BB:14511742 p.45](https://meetings.boardbook.org/Documents/DownloadPDF/14511742?org=915#page=45).
