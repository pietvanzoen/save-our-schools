## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-8 to neighborhood K-5; 6-8 moves to Ockley Green MS; Bybee Lake Shelter area moves to Astor.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/faubion.pdf p.3]:
> Faubion would change from a K-8 school to a neighborhood K-5 school. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to Ockley Green Middle School. The Bybee Lake Shelter school area would move from Faubion to Astor.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/faubion.pdf p.3]:
> Richer middle school options: Providing a full suite of middle school electives, athletics, and algebra pathways has proven challenging within this K-8 model. Focused early learning focus: Changing to a K-5 model allows Faubion to tailor its building and resources entirely to elementary development.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/faubion.pdf p.3]:
> Avoiding overcrowding: District teams considered adding extra elementary areas to Faubion, but it would have made Faubitoo large or disrupted nearby communities at Vernon and Woodlawn.
