## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes in 2028 (entire mechanical system replacement needed); Llewellyn-area students to Hosford; Duniway and Lewis areas to Brentwood; all continue to Cleveland HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sellwood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DDXzwTxlN46KRYVZvsNkzNDIIGHfYUcO&usp=drive_copy):
> Sellwood Middle School would close as a school building in 2028. Middle grade students from the Llewellyn boundary would move to Hosford Middle School. Middle grade students from the Duniway and closing Lewis boundaries would move to Brentwood Middle School. Middle and high school path: All areas would continue to feed into Cleveland High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sellwood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DDXzwTxlN46KRYVZvsNkzNDIIGHfYUcO&usp=drive_copy):
> Major building repairs needed: Sellwood Middle School faces major building concerns requiring an entire mechanical system replacement. Dropping enrollment: Expected enrollment at Sellwood drops to 350 students by 2031-32. Better building conditions: Moving its school pathway areas to Hosford and Brentwood Middle Schools brings together middle grade students into schools with more room for students and better building conditions.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sellwood.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1DDXzwTxlN46KRYVZvsNkzNDIIGHfYUcO&usp=drive_copy):
> Hosford proximity: Hosford offers closer proximity and better building systems for Llewellyn students compared to Sellwood.
