## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** No changes proposed; split MS pathway (Gray/West Sylvan) left in place because fixing it would hurt both middle schools.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridlemile.pdf p.3]:
> No changes are planned for Bridlemile at this time. Bridlemile would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school in its current school boundary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridlemile.pdf p.3]:
> Steady student numbers: Enrollment and classroom use at Bridlemile remain within healthy, stable ranges.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/bridlemile.pdf p.3]:
> Middle school pathways: District teams looked into fixing middle school split school pathways, but changing boundaries would negatively impact middle school enrollment at both Robert Gray and West Sylvan Middle Schools.
