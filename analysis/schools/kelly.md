## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; welcomes Lent neighborhood students previously assigned to Marysville; Woodmere CB classrooms move here; Marysville PPS Pre-K moves here; neighborhood middle schoolers shift from Brentwood to Harrison Park; Russian DLI continues to Brentwood.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/kelly.pdf p.3]:
> Kelly would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Kelly would welcome neighborhood students from Lent (previously assigned to Marysville). Special Education classroom(s) from Woodmere K-5 would move to Kelly. Neighborhood middle schoolers would shift to Harrison Park Middle School. Russian Dual Language Immersion (DLI) students would continue their middle school path at Brentwood.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/kelly.pdf p.3]:
> Organizing southeast pathways: Reorganizes elementary and middle school paths while protecting focused Russian DLI pathways.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/kelly.pdf p.3]:
> Regional balance: Shifting neighborhood students to Harrison Park balances middle school size across the Southeast region.
