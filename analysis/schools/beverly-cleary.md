## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-8 to neighborhood K-5; 6-8 moves to Beaumont MS; boundary expands to take Irvington K-5; seismic upgrades to be completed summer 2028; Irvington site may be a swing site for both communities in 2027-28.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beverly_cleary.pdf p.3]:
> Beverly Cleary would change from a K-8 school to a neighborhood K-5 school. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to Beaumont Middle School. Beverly Cleary's boundary would expand to include K-5 students from the Irvington neighborhood.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beverly_cleary.pdf p.3]:
> Central location and space: Beverly Cleary has plenty of space and a central location to serve elementary students. Major seismic construction: Planned seismic upgrades make changing to K-5 ideal to reduce the need for middle grade students to move to temporary buildings during construction.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beverly_cleary.pdf p.3]:
> Avoiding temporary moves: Moving middle grade students to Beaumont in Fall 2027 significantly reduces how many students need to move to temporary sites during construction. Chosen over Irvington: Beverly Cleary was selected over Irvington because it is more central and its planned building upgrades will create an improved space for Irvington students.
