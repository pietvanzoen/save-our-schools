## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes as K-5; K-5 to Beverly Cleary; 6-8 area moves from Tubman to Beaumont; building considered as temporary swing site for Beverly Cleary K-5 during summer 2027 seismic work.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/irvington.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1raDpPAw9-xT55_otAFK65Hw48WJCGsPf&usp=drive_copy):
> Irvington School would close as a neighborhood K-5 school building. K-5 students would move to Beverly Cleary K-5. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would from move Tubman to Beaumont Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/irvington.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1raDpPAw9-xT55_otAFK65Hw48WJCGsPf&usp=drive_copy):
> Low enrollment numbers: Irvington was expected to drop to just 223 students by 2031-32 (well below target levels). Updated buildings: Bringing together Irvington into Beverly Cleary expands student access to updated buildings and eliminates split middle school paths.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/irvington.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1raDpPAw9-xT55_otAFK65Hw48WJCGsPf&usp=drive_copy):
> Central location: Beverly Cleary offers a more central location than Irvington. Temporary construction site: Irvington is being considered as a potential temporary "swing site" for Beverly Cleary K-5 students during Beverly Cleary's Summer 2027 seismic upgrades.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's alternative "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would close this school, one of six elementaries forecast at 250 or fewer students next year [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
