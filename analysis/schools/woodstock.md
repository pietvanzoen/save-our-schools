## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; receives Mandarin DLI from Clark; Lewis Communication Behavior classrooms relocate here (Lewis closes only in A).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodstock.pdf p.3]:
> Woodstock would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school and receive the Mandarin Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program from Clark K-5.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodstock.pdf p.3]:
> Strengthening DLI learning Moving the Mandarin DLI program from Clark to Woodstock strengthens the DLI learning at a single school. Clark DLI enrollment is too small: Clark's current Mandarin DLI kindergarten class is fewer than 8 students, making a separate program too small.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodstock.pdf p.3]:
> Keeping the program going: Concentrating Mandarin DLI programs at Woodstock expands program support and supports teacher collaboration.
