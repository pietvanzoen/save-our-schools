## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-5 neighborhood school; welcomes Marysville neighborhood area and students (projection shows the merge in 2028-29: 224 in 2027-28, 536 in 2028-29).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/arleta.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1wFQwn4blhHaQuwKX320ymDPijEiYTtEC&usp=drive_copy):
> Arleta would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Arleta would welcome the entire neighborhood school area and student body from Marysville.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/arleta.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1wFQwn4blhHaQuwKX320ymDPijEiYTtEC&usp=drive_copy):
> Large school space: Arleta has 28 regular classrooms and can comfortably hold up to 653 students. This proposal keeps the enrollment well above current enrollment but well below the most students the building can hold. Major building upgrades: Arleta is getting a brand-new heating and cooling (HVAC) system in Summer 2027, has an elevator and access for people with disabilities, and has a full kitchen setup.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/arleta.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1wFQwn4blhHaQuwKX320ymDPijEiYTtEC&usp=drive_copy):
> Arleta chosen for building size: While building conditions at Arleta and Marysville were similar, Marysville was too small to fit both school communities together.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would merge Creston into Arleta instead of Atkinson [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
