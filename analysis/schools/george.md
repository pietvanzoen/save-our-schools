## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as 6-8 neighborhood MS and becomes the regional 6-8 Spanish DLI school; receives Spanish DLI from Ockley Green and César Chávez and Chávez 6-8; receives Peninsula-area neighborhood middle graders (from Ockley Green).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/george.pdf p.3]:
> George Middle School would remain open as a neighborhood 6-8 middle school and become the regional 6-8 Spanish DLI school serving the area. George would welcome Spanish DLI middle grade students from Ockley Green and César Chávez. George would welcome neighborhood middle grade students from Peninsula (shifting from Ockley Green). Students from the Peninsula boundary who are currently enrolled at Ockley Green could stay at Ockley Green through the highest grade.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/george.pdf p.3]:
> Single language program serving the area: Brings together Spanish DLI into a single school serving the area leading directly to Roosevelt High School. Recent building investments: George recently received bond-funded science lab upgrades, asbestos remediation (2025), and special education classroom upgrades.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/george.pdf p.3]:
> Space limits: District teams considered all middle grade students in the region, but George did not have the room to also house middle grade students from Astor K-8.
