## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-12 to K-8 alternative school; 9-12 students go to another alternative school, a focus option, or neighborhood HS; Odyssey K-8 co-locates in the MLC facility.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/metropolitan_learning_center.pdf p.3]:
> Metropolitan Learning Center (MLC) would change from a K-12 to a K-8 alternative (choice) school. High school (9th-12th) students would transition to another PPS alternative school, attend a focus option or return to their designated neighborhood high schools. The Odyssey K-8 program would co-locate onto the MLC school facility.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/metropolitan_learning_center.pdf p.3]:
> Matching grade structures: Changing MLC to K-8 matches district grade structures while creating space to co-locate MLC and Odyssey. Teaching models: Both programs center hands-on learning, allowing for the potential of shared resources.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/metropolitan_learning_center.pdf p.3]:
> Move timing: High schoolers would move in Fall 2027, while Odyssey moves when physical site preparation at MLC is complete.
