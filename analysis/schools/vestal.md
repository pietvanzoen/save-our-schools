## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open; receives Vietnamese DLI from Rose City Park.

**Scenario B:** No changes at Vestal (Rose City Park stays open).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vestal.pdf p.3]:
> Vestal would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school and receive the Vietnamese Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program from Rose City Park in Scenario A. In Scenario B, Rose City Park remains open so there would be no changes at Vestal.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vestal.pdf p.3]:
> Reaching the district’s school size goal: Vestal is below the district enrollment goal if there are no changes. Proximity for VDLI families: Vietnamese DLI community lives close to Vestal, and adding the program brings Vestal above the district enrollment goal.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/vestal.pdf p.3]:
> Building conditions and location: Building conditions and proximity were major considerations in this decision.
