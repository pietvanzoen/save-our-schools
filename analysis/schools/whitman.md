## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open; boundary expands to take 100% of Woodmere; new boundary -> Brentwood MS -> Franklin HS.

**Scenario B:** Stays open; takes 100% of Woodmere; a portion of Whitman boundary moves to Lewis; new boundary -> Brentwood MS -> Franklin HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/whitman.pdf p.3]:
> Whitman would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school and expand its school boundary to receive 100% of the student from the closing Woodmere k-5 school in Scenario A and B, in Scenario B, Lewis remains open and a portion of the Whitman boundary moves to Lewis. Middle and high school pathway: All students in this new boundary move to Brentwood Middle School and then to Franklin High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/whitman.pdf p.3]:
> Low neighborhood enrollment: Both Whitman and Woodmere are significantly under district enrollment goals, with Woodmere's enrollment remaining in the low 220s. Central school space: Whitman has room for 416 students, and receiving students from Woodmere makes better use of school space in a centrally located school with planned schoolyard improvements.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/whitman.pdf p.3]:
> Whitman chosen over Woodmere: Whitman was chosen over Woodmere because of being more central to where students live and having similar amounts of space and building conditions.
