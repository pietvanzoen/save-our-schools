## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open; welcomes Lewis neighborhood students; area west of McLoughlin Blvd moves to Llewellyn; all Duniway students -> Brentwood MS -> Cleveland HS.

**Scenario B:** No elementary boundary change; Duniway students go to Brentwood MS (Sellwood closes) then Cleveland HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/duniway.pdf p.3]:
> Duniway would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Duniway would welcome neighborhood students from the closing Lewis K-5 school in Scenario A only. Duniway's boundary area west of McLoughlin Blvd would move to Llewellyn in Scenario A only. Middle and high school pathway: All Duniway students would go to Brentwood for middle school and Cleveland for high school in both Scenario A and B.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/duniway.pdf p.3]:
> Central location and space: Duniway offers a central location, plenty of school space, and strong building conditions to welcome incoming Lewis students. Closer neighborhood access: Shifting the far west boundary to Llewellyn assigns students to the school physically closest to them.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/duniway.pdf p.3]:
> Duniway chosen over Lewis: When reviewing both schools together, Duniway was chosen to remain open because the building can support more students.
