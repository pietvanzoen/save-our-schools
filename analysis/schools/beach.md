## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes; Spanish DLI moves to César Chávez K-5; neighborhood students go to Chief Joseph (100% of area).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beach.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1l610ypmpRi24eZibkW8wJk1dUmkAQysF&usp=drive_copy):
> Beach School would close as a school building. The Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to César Chávez K-5. Students who live in the Beach neighborhood would now go to Chief Joseph.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beach.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1l610ypmpRi24eZibkW8wJk1dUmkAQysF&usp=drive_copy):
> The Beach building needs major repairs. The roof and building structure require urgent, expensive fixes to make it safe and modern. We want stronger programs in safer buildings. Moving students out of Beach fixes safety concerns while bringing students together into stronger, fully supported schools.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beach.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1l610ypmpRi24eZibkW8wJk1dUmkAQysF&usp=drive_copy):
> Chief Joseph is easier to reach: Chief Joseph is closer to where most families in the new school area live. Chief Joseph has plenty of space: Even though Beach is a larger building, Chief Joseph has more than enough room for all students. Chief Joseph is already updated: Chief Joseph recently received major improvements funded by local school bonds, including roof repairs and updated classrooms for general and special education.
