## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; boundary expands to take 100% of the closing Beach neighborhood area.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/chief_joseph.pdf p.3]:
> Chief Joseph would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Chief Joseph would expand its school boundary to welcome 100% of the closing Beach neighborhood school area.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/chief_joseph.pdf p.3]:
> Central location and updated school: Chief Joseph sits in a central location to serve both communities and features better building conditions than Beach. Better use of space: Receiving neighborhood students from Beach helps keep student numbers steady for both communities.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/chief_joseph.pdf p.3]:
> Chief Joseph chosen over Beach: Chief Joseph was selected as the receiving school over Beach because it is closer to where students live, even though Beach had a slightly more room for students.
