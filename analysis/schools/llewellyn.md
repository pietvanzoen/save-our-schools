## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open; boundary expands to take Duniway area west of McLoughlin Blvd; students go to Hosford MS instead of Sellwood.

**Scenario B:** No elementary boundary change ("No changes proposed"); Sellwood closes in both scenarios so Llewellyn-area middle graders go to Hosford.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/llewellyn.pdf p.3]:
> Llewellyn would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. In Scenario A, Llewellyn would expand its boundary to welcome students from Duniway living west of McLoughlin Blvd. Middle and high school pathway: Students would move to Hosford for Middle School instead of Sellwood.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/llewellyn.pdf p.3]:
> Closer neighborhood walk: Students living west of McLoughlin Blvd are by location much closer to Llewellyn than Duniway. Sellwood building needs: Sellwood Middle School would close in 2028 because of major building replacement needs.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/llewellyn.pdf p.3]:
> Logical boundary lines: McLoughlin Blvd forms a natural, boundary line for neighborhood assignments.
