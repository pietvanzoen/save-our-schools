## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; Spanish DLI moves to George MS; welcomes Faubion 6-8; former Peninsula area shifts to George; all students go to Jefferson HS (split pathway removed).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/ockley_green.pdf p.3]:
> Ockley Green would remain open as a neighborhood middle school. The Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to George Middle School. Ockley Green would welcome 6th-8th grade students from Faubion K-5. The former Peninsula school area would shift to George Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/ockley_green.pdf p.3]:
> DLI program serving the area: Moving middle grade Spanish DLI to George Middle School establishes a language program serving the area in North/Northeast Portland. One high school destination: Welcoming middle grade students from Faubion helps keep steady student numbers at Ockley Green and eliminates split route to high schools.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/ockley_green.pdf p.3]:
> Middle and high school cohesion: Fixes historical split route to high schools in the Jefferson cluster.
