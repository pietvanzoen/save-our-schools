## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** No changes proposed; distance to other schools makes boundary shifts impractical.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/forest_park.pdf p.3]:
> No changes are planned for Forest Park at this time. Forest Park would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school in its current setup and boundary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/forest_park.pdf p.3]:
> Distance between schools: Forest Park serves a separate area with no nearby schools close enough for practical boundary adjustments.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/forest_park.pdf p.3]:
> No viable options: Teams reviewed options to increase enrollment, but distance made boundary shifts impractical.
