## What the scenarios do

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sunrise.pdf p.3]:
> No changes are planned for Sunrise at this time. Sunrise would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school with its current boundary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sunrise.pdf p.3]:
