## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; welcomes 70% of the Maplewood boundary; a portion of Hayhurst boundary shifts to Rieke.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/hayhurst.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1BwsMTqcVksEx0je_XtPcti9pgFLBE_TP&usp=drive_copy):
> Hayhurst would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Hayhurst would welcome 70% of the school boundary from the closing Maplewood K-5 school. A portion of Hayhurst's current boundary would shift to Rieke.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/hayhurst.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1BwsMTqcVksEx0je_XtPcti9pgFLBE_TP&usp=drive_copy):
> Reaching a healthy school size: Without school boundary changes, Hayhurst’s enrollment was expected to drop to 266 students (below target). Room to welcome new students: Hayhurst has room for over 500 students, allowing it to comfortably welcome Maplewood families and help keep student numbers steady above 400.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/hayhurst.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1BwsMTqcVksEx0je_XtPcti9pgFLBE_TP&usp=drive_copy):
> Primary receiving school: Hayhurst was chosen as the primary location for Maplewood families because it is the most accessible option.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would replace the Southwest closures with a boundary process among eight elementaries including this one [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
