## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; welcomes Vernon 6-8; Irvington-area middle graders move to Beaumont (current Irvington-area students may stay through 8th grade); receives Beverly Cleary 6-8 Intensive Skills Classroom.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harriet_tubman.pdf p.3]:
> Harriet Tubman would remain open as a neighborhood middle school. Harriet Tubman would welcome 6th-8th grade students from Vernon as Vernon moves to a K-5 model. Middle grade students residing in the current Irvington school area would move to Beaumont Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harriet_tubman.pdf p.3]:
> Changing Vernon: Changing Vernon from a K-8 to a K-5 school requires a middle school location for 6th-8th grade students. Building a healthy student body: Harriet Tubman provides nearby classroom space, helping keep student numbers steady while ensuring students access a full range of middle school programs. Fewer split middle schools: The Irvington community would move to Beverly Cleary and to Beaumont to reduce split route to high schools in the region.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harriet_tubman.pdf p.3]:
> Option to stay: Policies would allow middle grade students currently living in the Irvington school area the option to stay at Harriet Tubman through 8th grade.
