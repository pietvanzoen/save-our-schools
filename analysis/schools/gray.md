## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; boundary expands to take Maplewood-area middle graders (from Jackson); sibling preference for families with older siblings at Jackson.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/gray.pdf p.3]:
> Robert Gray Middle School would remain open as a neighborhood middle school. Gray would expand its school boundary to welcome middle grade students from the Maplewood area.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/gray.pdf p.3]:
> Filling open classroom space: Robert Gray's enrollment was expected to drop below steady levels without boundary adjustments. Reaching the district’s school size goal: Receiving Maplewood middle schoolers brings Gray above 500 students and uses over 80% of building space. Recent building repairs: Gray received significant building repairs following the Winter Storm of 2024.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/gray.pdf p.3]:
> Sibling preference: Families with older siblings currently attending Jackson Middle School would be granted sibling preference. Building conditions: Robert Gray was chosen to receive Maplewood students because Maplewood students are proposed to go to Hayhurst and Rieke which already move to Robert Gray Middle School.
