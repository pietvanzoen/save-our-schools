## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Boundary expands to include the Sunnyside area; remaining Woodmere and Whitman sections move to Franklin; current Cleveland/Franklin students may stay through graduation.

**Scenario B:** Boundary expands to include the Sunnyside area; remaining Woodmere and Whitman sections move to Franklin; current Cleveland/Franklin students may stay through graduation.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/cleveland.pdf p.3]:
> Cleveland High School would expand its boundary to include the Sunnyside school area. Any remaining sections of Woodmere and Whitman would move into the Franklin High School boundary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/cleveland.pdf p.3]:
> Matching middle and high school paths: School boundary changes connect high school pathways with elementary and middle school changes across the region while balancing student numbers.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/cleveland.pdf p.3]:
> Students already enrolled: Students currently attending Cleveland or Franklin High School would be allowed to stay through graduation.
