## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Becomes "focused regional Spanish DLI K-5"; receives Scott Spanish DLI; PDF text says Rigler non-DLI neighborhood boundary shifts to Scott (but measures table keeps "K-5 neighborhood/language immersion" and projections rise; see discrepancies); Spanish DLI students -> Roseway Heights -> McDaniel.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rigler.pdf p.3]:
> Rigler would remain open as a focused regional Spanish DLI K-5 school. Rigler would receive Spanish DLI students from Scott K-5. Rigler’s non-DLI neighborhood boundary would shift to Scott K-5. Middle and high school pathway: All K-5 Spanish DLI students would move to Roseway Heights Middle School and then to McDaniel High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rigler.pdf p.3]:
> DLI program serving the area: Moving Scott Spanish DLI to Rigler follows the district model of creating focused Spanish DLI schools serving each area. Reaching steady enrollment: Welcoming these students brings Rigler's enrollment above steady target levels.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rigler.pdf p.3]:
> Regional DLI model: Follows the districtwide strategy for concentrated language program locations.
