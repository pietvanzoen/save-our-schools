## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes; 100% of boundary to Whitman; combined boundary -> Brentwood MS -> Franklin HS; Communication Behavior classrooms to Kelly.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodmere.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1VrGxkY8mrjTU6neXgKChfbYmXGah9Pm9&usp=drive_copy):
> Woodmere School would close as a school building. 100% of its neighborhood K-5 school boundary would shift to Whitman K-5. Middle and high school pathway: All students in this combined boundary move to Brentwood Middle School and then to Franklin High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodmere.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1VrGxkY8mrjTU6neXgKChfbYmXGah9Pm9&usp=drive_copy):
> Low enrollment numbers: Both Woodmere and Whitman face enrollment challenges below district enrollment goals, with Woodmere remaining in the low 220s. Central school space: Whitman is closer to where students live, has additional space and planned schoolyard improvements.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodmere.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1VrGxkY8mrjTU6neXgKChfbYmXGah9Pm9&usp=drive_copy):
> Whitman was selected as the receiving school over Woodmere because of its central location relative to where students live.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's alternative "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would close this school, one of six elementaries forecast at 250 or fewer students next year [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
- Under Scenario D, some Woodmere students would go to Lewis [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
