## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; boundary expands to take Lent and Kelly elementary areas; Atkinson neighborhood shifts to Kellogg; keeps Mandarin DLI (Bridger 6-8 also reassigned here per Bridger PDF).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harrison_park.pdf p.3]:
> Harrison Park would remain open as a neighborhood middle school. Harrison Park would expand its boundary to welcome elementary graduates from the Lent and Kelly school areas. Atkinson neighborhood students would shift to Kellogg Middle School. Harrison Park would continue to offer its Mandarin Chinese DLI program.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harrison_park.pdf p.3]:
> Connecting elementary and middle schools: Boundary adjustments make better use of middle school space and connect school pathways following elementary changes in southeast Portland. Protecting language access: Native Mandarin-speaking families have direct geographic access to Harrison Park and actively choose this program.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/harrison_park.pdf p.3]:
> Geographic sense: District teams explored reducing split school pathways to high schools, but moving the Bridger 6-8 area out of Harrison Park or Franklin did not make geographical sense.
