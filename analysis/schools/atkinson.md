## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-5 neighborhood school; Spanish DLI moves to Lent; welcomes Creston neighborhood K-5 students; whole new boundary feeds Kellogg MS then Franklin HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/atkinson.pdf p.3]:
> Atkinson would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. The Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to Lent K-5. Atkinson would welcome neighborhood K-5 students from Creston. Middle and high school pathway: All students in the new Atkinson boundary would go together to Kellogg for middle school and Franklin for high school.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/atkinson.pdf p.3]:
> Creating space for neighborhood students: Moving Spanish DLI to Lent creates the physical space needed at Atkinson to welcome neighborhood students from Creston. DLI Schools serving each area: Spanish DLI is moved into schools serving each area of the district. For southeast Portland, this is Lent. Closer to Kellogg: Creston students north of Powell live closer to Atkinson and Kellogg Middle School.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/atkinson.pdf p.3]:
> Atkinson's central location: Atkinson was chosen to stay open over Creston because it is larger and more central to where families live in the new boundary. Lent DLI school serving the area: Lent was chosen as the DLI school serving the area in the Southeast because it already functions as a full immersion school and families accessing Spanish DLI in this part of town live closer to Lent than Atkinson.
