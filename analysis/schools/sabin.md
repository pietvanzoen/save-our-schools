## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes (low enrollment; plumbing and heating replacement needs; leased early-learning space); full boundary to Dr. MLK Jr..

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sabin.pdf p.3]:
> Sabin School would close as a school building. Its full neighborhood K-5 school boundary would move to Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Elementary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sabin.pdf p.3]:
> Low enrollment and building risks: Sabin's enrollment was expected to remain low (235 students), and the building faces major plumbing and heating system replacement needs, alongside dependencies on leased spaces for early learning. Upgraded school features: Bringing together Sabin and Dr. MLK Jr. Elementary moves students to a building with a newly upgraded roof, larger grounds (4.9 acres), and strong school features.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sabin.pdf p.3]:
> Coordinated move: Moving Sabin to Dr. MLK Jr. Elementary coincides with moving Mandarin DLI from MLK Jr. to BEH, opening up the physical classroom space needed to welcome the Sabin community.
