## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; minor boundary change shifting the Bybee Lake Shelter area to Roosevelt HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/jefferson.pdf p.3]:
> Jefferson High School would remain open as a neighborhood high school. Jefferson would make a minor boundary adjustment, shifting the Bybee Lake Shelter area to Roosevelt High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/jefferson.pdf p.3]:
> Connecting elementary and high school paths: Adjusting attendance lines connects high school boundaries with elementary changes near Bybee Lake Shelter (which shifted to Astor K-8).

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/jefferson.pdf p.3]:
> Proximity to Roosevelt: Shifting the shelter boundary to Roosevelt keeps families closer to their assigned high school site.
