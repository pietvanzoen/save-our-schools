## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-8 to a focused K-5 regional Spanish DLI school; receives Spanish DLI from Beach, Sitton and James John; neighborhood (non-DLI) K-5 students move to Rosa Parks; 6-8 moves to George MS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/cesar_chavez.pdf p.3]:
> César Chávez would change from a K-8 to a focused K-5 regional Spanish DLI school. It would welcome Spanish DLI students from Beach, Sitton, and James John. Neighborhood (non-DLI) K-5 students would move to Rosa Parks K-5. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to George Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/cesar_chavez.pdf p.3]:
> Complex school setup: Hosting multiple complex programs (DLI, neighborhood K-8, special education) on one school created unbalanced class sizes and operational issues. Focused language focus: Moving to a single-focus K-5 DLI school creates a regional Spanish language program location.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/cesar_chavez.pdf p.3]:
> Regional domino effect: Changing César Chávez opens up the necessary space at Sitton and Chief Joseph to welcome neighborhood students from closing schools.
