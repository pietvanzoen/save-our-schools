## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes; full neighborhood boundary moves to Rosa Parks; middle-grade area moves from Ockley Green to George.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/peninsula.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15xt7PqKoHCdKNXu2tSzPph_CbsN3yJD2&usp=drive_copy):
> Peninsula School would close as a school building. Its full neighborhood K-5 school boundary would move to Rosa Parks K-5.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/peninsula.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15xt7PqKoHCdKNXu2tSzPph_CbsN3yJD2&usp=drive_copy):
> Low enrollment numbers: Peninsula’s current enrollment is 239 students and is expected to remain low at 236 students by 2031-32 (over 100 students below target levels). Updated building space: Bringing Peninsula into Rosa Parks helps keep steady student numbers at elementary enrollment across the Roosevelt cluster using an updated school with more room for students.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/peninsula.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=15xt7PqKoHCdKNXu2tSzPph_CbsN3yJD2&usp=drive_copy):
> Shared support models: Both Peninsula and Rosa Parks are Title I schools with SUN school and community support programs, food pantries, and support for students with and without disabilities. Building quality: Rosa Parks offers updated an building compared to Peninsula.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Splitt's alternative "Scenario D" (memo reported Oct 20) would close this school, one of six elementaries forecast at 250 or fewer students next year [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
