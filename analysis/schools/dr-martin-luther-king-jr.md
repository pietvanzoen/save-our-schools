## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-5 neighborhood school; Mandarin DLI moves to Boise-Eliot/Humboldt; boundary expands to take 100% of Sabin.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/dr_martin_luther_king_jr.pdf p.3]:
> Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Elementary would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. The Mandarin Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to Boise-Eliot/Humboldt (BEH). MLK Jr. would expand its neighborhood boundary to welcome 100% of the closing Sabin school area.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/dr_martin_luther_king_jr.pdf p.3]:
> Strong school features: MLK Jr. features a large 4.9-acre site, turfed field, large building (618 students), and a newly upgraded roof. Frees up room for Sabin: Moving Mandarin DLI to Boise-Eliot/Humboldt (BEH) opens up the physical classroom space needed at MLK Jr. to welcome Sabin neighborhood families.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/dr_martin_luther_king_jr.pdf p.3]:
> Fixing low enrollment: Expanding neighborhood boundary lines builds long-term steady student numbers for the school community.
