## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; receives Beaumont Spanish DLI 6-8; Vietnamese DLI stays; houses both DLI pathways (type of change listed as boundary change).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/roseway_heights.pdf p.3]:
> Roseway Heights would remain open as a neighborhood middle school. Roseway Heights would receive Spanish DLI middle grade (6th-8th) students from Beaumont Middle School. The Vietnamese DLI program would remain at Roseway Heights.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/roseway_heights.pdf p.3]:
> Middle school DLI school serving the area: Moving Spanish DLI middle grade students from Beaumont to Roseway Heights brings together middle and high school Spanish language pathways and matches middle school changes across the region.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/roseway_heights.pdf p.3]:
> Program strength: Keeping both DLI programs together was chosen because both language tracks continue to show student growth.
