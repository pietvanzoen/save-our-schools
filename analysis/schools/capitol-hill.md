## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open; welcomes 30% of the Stephenson boundary; Stephenson Communication Behavior classroom moves here; targeted seismic bond work Summer 2027 and Summer 2028.

**Scenario B:** No changes proposed.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/capitol_hill.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1YNJRSQEfCCJRjQIuUCmIP5YTmrdugLzq&usp=drive_copy):
> Capitol Hill would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Capitol Hill would welcome 30% of the neighborhood school boundary from the closing Stephenson K-5 school. Stephenson's special education classroom would move to Capitol Hill.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/capitol_hill.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1YNJRSQEfCCJRjQIuUCmIP5YTmrdugLzq&usp=drive_copy):
> Filling open classroom space: Capitol Hill was expected to drop below steady enrollment levels. Welcoming Stephenson students fills available space and helps keep student numbers steady at around 379 students. Planned building upgrades: Targeted seismic bond work is scheduled for Summer 2027 and Summer 2028.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/capitol_hill.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1YNJRSQEfCCJRjQIuUCmIP5YTmrdugLzq&usp=drive_copy):
> Capitol Hill's central location: Capitol Hill was chosen to stay open over Stephenson because it sits in a more central location to serve the broader community.
