## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** No changes proposed; surrounding schools lack space.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodlawn.pdf p.3]:
> No changes are planned for Woodlawn at this time. Woodlawn would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school with its current grades and school boundary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodlawn.pdf p.3]:
> Keeping the school community together: Although enrollment and program structures at Woodlawn remain below district enrollment goals, no changes are planned at this time.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/woodlawn.pdf p.3]:
> Surrounding space limits: Woodlawn would continue serving its neighborhood community because surrounding schools were not an option for boosting enrollment or consolidation because of limited space for students.
