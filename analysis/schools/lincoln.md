## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; boundary contracts as Skyline high schoolers move to Roosevelt.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lincoln.pdf p.3]:
> Lincoln High School would remain open as a neighborhood high school. Lincoln's school boundary would contract slightly as Skyline students move to Roosevelt High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lincoln.pdf p.3]:
> Shorter travel times: Reassigning rural Skyline high schoolers to Roosevelt significantly cuts daily transit distance across the West Hills for those families.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lincoln.pdf p.3]:
> Simpler travel to and from school: Adjusting the perimeter reduces long travel times for families in the far western areas.
