## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Building closes (severe building conditions; full seismic overhaul needed); 100% of neighborhood boundary to Scott; Vietnamese DLI to Vestal; middle-grade Vietnamese DLI stays at Roseway Heights.

**Scenario B:** Remains open.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rose_city_park.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1PuP0VaaG1rh4xC7Sjx4fkwHYGH_ZFJzH&usp=drive_copy):
> In Scenario A, Rose City Park Elementary would close as a school building. In Scenario A, 100% of its neighborhood K-5 boundary would move to Scott K-5. In Scenario A, its Vietnamese Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to Vestal K-5. Middle grade Vietnamese DLI students would remain at Roseway Heights Middle School. In Scenario B, Rose City Park would remain open.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rose_city_park.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1PuP0VaaG1rh4xC7Sjx4fkwHYGH_ZFJzH&usp=drive_copy):
> Urgent building repairs: Rose City Park faces major building risks, including very old building systems and critical maintenance failures requiring a full seismic overhaul. Avoiding temporary moves: Bringing together the school moves neighborhood students to Scott K-5 and moves Vietnamese DLI to Vestal K-5, avoiding expensive temporary construction moves.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rose_city_park.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1PuP0VaaG1rh4xC7Sjx4fkwHYGH_ZFJzH&usp=drive_copy):
> Primary driver: Severe building conditions were the primary reason considered for school closure in Scenario A.
