## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Building closes; 70% of boundary to Markham, 30% to Capitol Hill; Communication Behavior classroom to Capitol Hill.

**Scenario B:** Remains open.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/stephenson.pdf p.3]:
> Stephenson School would close as a school building in Scenario A. In Scenario A, 70% of its neighborhood school boundary would move to Markham K-5, and 30% would move to Capitol Hill K-5. In Scenario B, Stephenson would remain open.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/stephenson.pdf p.3]:
> Geographic isolation and low enrollment: Stephenson is isolated, with enrollment expected to drop to 261 students by 2031-32. Neighboring Capacity: Closest schools (Markham and Capitol Hill) offer more room to comfortably accommodate the student body.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/stephenson.pdf p.3]:
> Choosing the school students would move to: Stephenson was chosen for closure over Markham and Capitol Hill because of room for students and distance from where students live. As we look at who bears the burden of moving, we wanted to reduce the impact on students experiencing poverty and underserved communities and that is a contributing factor for moving Stephenson to Markham.
