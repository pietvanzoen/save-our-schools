## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Closes as K-5 school after 2027-28 (projection shows 300 students in 2027-28, closed from 2028-29; implementation delayed so SEGC-impacted students are not moved twice); pre-SEGC Marysville boundary to Arleta; pre-SEGC Lent non-DLI boundary to Kelly; PPS Pre-K to Kelly; building could become regional preschool/childcare/community space.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/marysville.pdf p.3]:
> Marysville School would close as a K-5 school. The pre-SEGC neighborhood K-5 school boundary would move to Arleta K-5, the pre-SEGC Lent neighborhood non-DLI boundary would move to Kelly. The PPS Pre-K program at Marysville would move to Kelly.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/marysville.pdf p.3]:
> Building size advantages: Arleta has room for 653 students (with 28 main classrooms) compared to Marysville's space of 410, allowing Arleta to comfortably house both communities. New uses for the building: Moving out of the Marysville building allows it to possibly serve as a regional preschool center, childcare center, or community partner space.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/marysville.pdf p.3]:
> Avoiding multiple moves: Delaying full implementation ensures students previously impacted by Southeast Guiding Coalition changes do not experience multiple school moves during elementary school.
