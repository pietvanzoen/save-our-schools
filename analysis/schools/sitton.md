## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as neighborhood K-5; Spanish DLI moves to César Chávez; boundary expands to take 100% of James John neighborhood students.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sitton.pdf p.3]:
> Sitton would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Its Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move to César Chávez K-5. Sitton would expand its school boundary to receive 100% of neighborhood K-5 students from the closing James John school.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sitton.pdf p.3]:
> Single-story accessible building: Sitton is a single-story, fully accessible school in better physical condition than James John, with a higher concentration of neighborhood students living nearby. Frees up room for James John: Moving Sitton’s Spanish DLI program to César Chávez frees up building space for Sitton to serve as a unified, accessible neighborhood elementary school.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/sitton.pdf p.3]:
> Building accessibility: Sitton was selected as the receiving school over James John primarily because its single-story layout provides total physical accessibility.
