## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Changes from K-8 to neighborhood K-5; 6-8 moves to Mt. Tabor MS and then Grant HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/laurelhurst.pdf p.3]:
> Laurelhurst would change from a K-8 school to a neighborhood K-5 school. Middle grade (6th-8th) students would move to Mt. Tabor Middle School and continue to Grant High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/laurelhurst.pdf p.3]:
> Balances enrollment: Balances enrollment at Mt. Tabor Middle School after significant impacts from the Southeast Guiding Coalition. Programs that can continue over time: Moving 6-8 students to a focused middle school will create sustainable programming. This means students will have more access to educational activities.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/laurelhurst.pdf p.3]:
> Direct Grant High pathway: Laurelhurst middle grade students join Mt. Tabor DLI student groups, leading directly to Grant High School.
