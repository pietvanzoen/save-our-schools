## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Building closes; neighborhood K-5 to Duniway; middle grades to Brentwood then Cleveland HS.

**Scenario B:** Stays open and receives a portion of the Whitman boundary; middle grades to Brentwood MS then Cleveland HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lewis.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=18liV8xTahqAn_NAnjl4B_1BwSqhRoXhE&usp=drive_copy):
> In Scenario A, Lewis School would close as a school building. In Scenario A, neighborhood K-5 students would move to Duniway K-5. In Scenario B, Lewis receives a portion of the current Whitman K-5 boundary. In both scenarios students move to Brentwood Middle School for 6-8 and then onto Cleveland High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lewis.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=18liV8xTahqAn_NAnjl4B_1BwSqhRoXhE&usp=drive_copy):
> Low enrollment numbers: Lewis enrollment was expected to drop to 201 students by 2031-32. Better building conditions and space: Duniway offers a central location, more room for students, and better building conditions to serve both communities.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lewis.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=18liV8xTahqAn_NAnjl4B_1BwSqhRoXhE&usp=drive_copy):
> Duniway chosen as receiving school: Evaluated both school buildings together; Duniway was chosen because it can support more students.
