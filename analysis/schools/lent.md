## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Remains focused K-5 Spanish DLI school for the area, welcoming Atkinson Spanish DLI; Lent neighborhood boundary moves to Kelly.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lent.pdf p.3]:
> Lent remains a focused K-5 Spanish DLI school serving the area, welcoming Spanish DLI students from Atkinson. Lent's neighborhood boundary would move to Kelly K-5.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lent.pdf p.3]:
> Language programs serving each area: Concentrates Spanish DLI into schools serving each area across the district to strengthen language immersion. Reaching steady enrollment: Moving Atkinson DLI to Lent brings Lent's student numbers above steady target levels (400+ students).

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/lent.pdf p.3]:
> Room for more students: Lent’s spacious building is ideally suited for a DLI program serving the area.
