## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Building closes; 70% of boundary to Hayhurst, 30% to Rieke; middle grades to Robert Gray instead of Jackson; K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom moves to Markham.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3]:
> Maplewood School would close as a school building. 70% of the neighborhood boundary would move to Hayhurst K-5, and 30% would move to Rieke K-5. Middle grade students would move to Robert Gray Middle School instead of Jackson. The K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom would move to Markham K-5 to rejoin the 3-5 classroom.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3]:
> Low Expected enrollment: Maplewood’s enrollment was expected to drop to 247 students against a building space of 465. Nearby Hayhurst and Rieke have available space to welcome these families.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.3]:
> Geographic split: 70% of students would move to Hayhurst because most of the Maplewood area is closer to Hayhurst. As we look at who bears the burden of moving, we wanted to reduce the impact on students experiencing poverty and underserved communities and that is a contributing factor for moving Maplewood to Hayhurst.

Analysis: how the letter's figures compare with the archive.
- **Enrollment.** Before Oct 13 the archive's latest official count was 287 (Oct 1, 2025) [BB:3997157 p.1]. The family guide projects 278 for 2027-28 [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/maplewood.pdf p.4]. The Sept 2026 staffing workbook later added to the archive counts **303** students in grades K-5 (spring projection 300) [PPS:staffing/2026-09-22_board-request/board_request_as_of_2026-09-22.xlsx#26-27 As of 923 Counts!R270-R275]. That is closer to the letter's 310 than to 288. The figure 288 matches PSU's July 2026 forecast of Maplewood's 2026-27 enrollment [PPS:pps_net/data-and-reporting/2026-27/psu_prc_enrollment_forecast_2026-27_to_2035-36.pdf p.39]. Analysis: this forecast is probably the source of the 288 the letter disputes. The September count came in 15 students above it.
- **$1.5M seismic.** This matches KGW's report of about $1.5M of roof-level seismic work at Maplewood [NEWS:NEWS-20261009-kgw-1]. The 2024 Holmes assessment lists "2015 roof-only seismic upgrades" [BB:3991109 p.108]. Maplewood's highest building-part risk score is 7.5 [BB:3991109 p.221].
- **"Major structural seismic improvements are complete… gym and cafeteria."** In the archived bond pages, this September 2026 wording is the update for **Vernon**, not Maplewood [PPS:sources/bond/pages/seismic_improvements.html]. The letter may have mixed up the two schools.
- **"Second-lowest outstanding retrofit cost."** In the Holmes cost summary, Maplewood's estimated complete retrofit is $3,435,000. Eight other sites on that table are lower, though some are not elementary or K-8 schools [BB:3991109 p.13]. The archive has no other ranking of 48 schools, so this claim cannot be confirmed from what is here.
- **Housing.** At the Oct 6 meeting, staff said Rieke rises to 311 "thanks to some housing that's being developed" [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:22:55]. The "net gain of 12 students" figure for Raleigh Crest does not appear in the archive.
