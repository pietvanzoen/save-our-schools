## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Boundary expands to take Skyline high schoolers (from Lincoln); also receives Bybee Lake Shelter area (from Jefferson, per Jefferson PDF).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/roosevelt.pdf p.3]:
> Roosevelt High School would remain open as a neighborhood high school. Roosevelt would expand its school boundary to receive high school students from Skyline.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/roosevelt.pdf p.3]:
> Shorter commute times: Reassigning the rural Skyline school area to Roosevelt connects high school routes to middle and high school with western neighborhood boundaries and makes better use of student enrollment.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/roosevelt.pdf p.3]:
> Travel routes: Shifting Skyline to Roosevelt provides more direct transit routes than traveling across the West Hills.
