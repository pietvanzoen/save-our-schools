## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-5 neighborhood school; Mandarin DLI (kindergarten below 8 students) moves to Woodstock.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/clark.pdf p.3]:
> Clark would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. The Mandarin Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program would move and bring students together at Woodstock K-5.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/clark.pdf p.3]:
> Low program enrollment: Enrollment in Clark's Mandarin DLI program dropped below 8 kindergarteners, making it difficult to keep as a stand-alone program. Focusing on neighborhood core: Moving DLI reduces operational complexity and lets Clark focus entirely on its neighborhood elementary program.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/clark.pdf p.3]:
> At one school language program location: Concentrating Mandarin DLI programs at Woodstock creates a single language program with enough students to continue on the east side.
