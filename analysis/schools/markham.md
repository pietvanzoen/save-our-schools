## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open; boundary expands to take 70% of Stephenson; receives K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom from Maplewood.

**Scenario B:** Measures table says "No changes proposed"; Maplewood (closing in both scenarios) K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom returns to Markham.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/markham.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Qx13JVd92AZdJotx-gADCdBr0LyczHyT&usp=drive_copy):
> Markham would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Markham would expand its boundary to welcome 70% of the students from the closing Stephenson K-5 school. Markham would welcome the K-2 Intensive Skills Classroom from Maplewood (uniting it with the 3-5 classroom).

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/markham.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Qx13JVd92AZdJotx-gADCdBr0LyczHyT&usp=drive_copy):
> Filling open classroom space: Absorbing 70% of the Stephenson boundary increases Markham’s classroom use above 90% and helps keep student numbers steadily above 500 students.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/markham.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Qx13JVd92AZdJotx-gADCdBr0LyczHyT&usp=drive_copy):
> Room for more students: Both Markham and Capitol Hill offer more room than Stephenson to comfortably house incoming students.
