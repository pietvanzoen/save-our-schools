## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-5 neighborhood school; welcomes half of Buckman (area north of Hawthorne and south of Burnside).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/abernethy.pdf p.3]:
> Abernethy would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Abernethy would welcome half of the student population from Buckman.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/abernethy.pdf p.3]:
> Enrollment is dropping: Abernethy can hold 441 students, but enrollment is expected to drop to 241 without changes. Filling open space: Welcoming Buckman students would bring Abernethy up to a healthy size and help keep student numbers steady.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/abernethy.pdf p.3]:
> Abernethy was chosen to stay open because the Buckman building needs urgent, major structural and repairs to make the building safer in an earthquake.
