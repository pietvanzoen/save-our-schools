## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Boundary expands to welcome 6-8 from Duniway and Lewis areas (and the combined Whitman/Woodmere area); Kelly neighborhood middle schoolers shift to Harrison Park; Russian DLI stays.

**Scenario B:** Boundary expands to welcome 6-8 from Duniway and Lewis areas (and the combined Whitman/Woodmere area); Kelly neighborhood middle schoolers shift to Harrison Park; Russian DLI stays.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/brentwood.pdf p.3]:
> Brentwood Middle School would expand its boundary to welcome 6th-8th grade students from Duniway and Lewis. Neighborhood middle school students from Kelly would shift to Harrison Park Middle School. The Russian DLI program would remain at Brentwood.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/brentwood.pdf p.3]:
> Building a healthy student body: Welcoming Duniway and Lewis elementary areas helps keep steady student numbers at Brentwood's enrollment while accommodating neighborhood elementary changes.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/brentwood.pdf p.3]:
> High school paths: Duniway and Lewis students would continue to Cleveland High School, while Whitman and Woodmere students would move to Franklin High School.
