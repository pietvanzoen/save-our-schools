## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Odyssey K-8 choice program moves from East Sylvan to the MLC facility; East Sylvan building closes.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/odyssey.pdf p.3]:
> The Odyssey K-8 special-focus choice program would move to the Metropolitan Learning Center (MLC) school. The East Sylvan building would close.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/odyssey.pdf p.3]:
> Shared teaching approach: Odyssey would move from the East Sylvan site to co-locate at MLC because both programs share hands-on learning models, creating opportunities for shared buildings and teacher teamwork. Closing an aging building: Moving the program closes the aging East Sylvan building.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/odyssey.pdf p.3]:
> Move timing: Physical move timing may occur in fall 2027 or fall 2028 depending on needed building preparation at MLC.
