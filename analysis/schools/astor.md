## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-8 neighborhood school; boundary expands slightly to include Bybee Lake Shelter area (from Faubion); stays K-8 because nearby middle schools lack room.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/astor.pdf p.3]:
> Astor would remain open as a neighborhood K-8 school. Astor would expand its boundary slightly to include students living near the Bybee Lake Shelter area.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/astor.pdf p.3]:
> Room for more students: Astor has space in its building to welcome additional students in the region. Serving local shelter needs: Including the Bybee Lake Shelter boundary makes sure those students go to a school as close as possible to where they live.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/astor.pdf p.3]:
> Keeping K-8 open: Astor remains one of the few K-8 neighborhood schools because nearby middle schools do not currently have room to take Astor's middle grade students.
