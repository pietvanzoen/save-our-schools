## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Boundary expands to take Sunnyside 6-8 and the Llewellyn area (from Sellwood); Duniway students moving to Llewellyn also attend Hosford.

**Scenario B:** Boundary expands to take Sunnyside 6-8 and the Llewellyn area (from Sellwood).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/hosford.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=16IF0FJU9HAl2spXle09ufYZsfukG8uaE&usp=drive_copy):
> Hosford Middle School would expand its boundary to welcome middle schoolers from Sunnyside as well as the Llewellyn area (from the Sellwood closure). Duniway students moving to Llewellyn would also attend Hosford for middle school.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/hosford.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=16IF0FJU9HAl2spXle09ufYZsfukG8uaE&usp=drive_copy):
> Changing Sunnyside: Changing Sunnyside to a K-5 neighborhood school (to welcome students from closing Buckman) requires 6th-8th graders to move to a comprehensive middle school. Sellwood building needs: Sellwood Middle School faces major building needs requiring an entire mechanical system replacement, leading to its planned closure in 2028.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/hosford.pdf p.3](https://drive.google.com/open?id=16IF0FJU9HAl2spXle09ufYZsfukG8uaE&usp=drive_copy):
> Hosford chosen over Sellwood: When evaluating both middle schools, Hosford has better building conditions and closer proximity to a larger number of students.

**Board-member alternatives and advocacy (Oct 20, 2026):**
- Engelsman proposes keeping Sunnyside's middle grades to avoid what she calls overcrowding at Hosford [NEWS:NEWS-20261020-oregonian-1](https://www.oregonlive.com/education/2026/10/2-portland-public-schools-board-members-pitch-alternatives-to-closing-14-schools.html).
