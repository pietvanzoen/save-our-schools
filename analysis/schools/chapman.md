## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** No changes proposed; below size goal but no nearby school close enough for a practical boundary change.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/chapman.pdf p.3]:
> No changes are planned for Chapman at this time. Chapman would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school in its current setup and boundary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/chapman.pdf p.3]:
> Geographic isolation: Although Chapman’s enrollment sits below steady target levels, there are no nearby schools close enough to make a school boundary change practical.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/chapman.pdf p.3]:
> No viable options: District teams explored ways to increase enrollment, but no practical boundary adjustments existed in the immediate area.
