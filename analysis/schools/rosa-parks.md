## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; takes 100% of the Peninsula boundary and César Chávez neighborhood (non-DLI) K-5 students.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rosa_parks.pdf p.3]:
> Rosa Parks would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Rosa Parks would expand its boundary to receive 100% of the closing Peninsula K-5 boundary. Rosa Parks would receive neighborhood (non-DLI) students from César Chávez K-5.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rosa_parks.pdf p.3]:
> Updated school setup: Rosa Parks is an updated school with more room for students, an early learning classroom, SUN school and community support programs, and an on-site food pantry. Filling open classroom space: Receiving students from Peninsula (site closure) and non-DLI neighborhood students from César Chávez increases building use and helps keep steady student numbers at elementary enrollment in the Roosevelt cluster.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rosa_parks.pdf p.3]:
> Maximizing modern space: Uses available building space at newly built Rosa Parks efficiently while building a strong student body
