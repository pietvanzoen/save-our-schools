## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as 6-8 neighborhood MS; welcomes Beverly Cleary 6-8 including the former Irvington area; Spanish DLI moves to Roseway Heights MS (then McDaniel HS).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beaumont.pdf p.3]:
> Beaumont would remain open as a neighborhood middle school (grades 6-8). Beaumont would welcome 6th-8th grade students from Beverly Cleary, including students from the former Irvington boundary. The Spanish Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program at Beaumont would move to Roseway Heights Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beaumont.pdf p.3]:
> Beverly Cleary is changing to K-5: Beverly Cleary is changing from a K-8 to a K-5 model, so middle grade students need a comprehensive middle school location. Avoids temporary building moves: Welcoming Beverly Cleary middle schoolers to Beaumont keeps students close to home and prevents them from having to move to temporary buildings during Beverly Cleary's planned summer 2027 seismic construction. Logical school pathways: Adding the former Irvington boundary creates clear, connected middle school pathways for all students in the area. Bringing DLI programs together: Moving the Spanish DLI from Beaumont to Roseway Heights builds a middle school serving the area for the Spanish DLI program that then moves to McDaniel for High School.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/beaumont.pdf p.3]:
> Direct neighborhood access: Beaumont provides nearby building space that allows middle grade students to stay in their local community.
