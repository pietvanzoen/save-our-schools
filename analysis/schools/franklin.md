## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Boundary adjusts to welcome the expanded Whitman area (former Whitman and Woodmere); full Sunnyside boundary shifts to Cleveland; small section of Vestal moves to McDaniel; current students may stay through graduation with sibling preference.

**Scenario B:** Boundary adjusts to welcome the expanded Whitman area (former Whitman and Woodmere); full Sunnyside boundary shifts to Cleveland; small section of Vestal moves to McDaniel; current students may stay through graduation with sibling preference.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/franklin.pdf p.3]:
> Franklin High School would adjust its school boundary to welcome students from the expanded Whitman area (former Whitman and Woodmere boundaries). The full Sunnyside boundary would shift to Cleveland High School. A small section of the Vestal boundary would move to McDaniel High School instead of Franklin.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/franklin.pdf p.3]:
> Matching middle and high school pathways: High school boundary tweaks connect high school pathways with elementary and middle school changes across the region while balancing student numbers.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/franklin.pdf p.3]:
> Legacy policy: Students currently enrolled at Franklin or Cleveland High School would be permitted to stay through graduation, with sibling preferences applied.
