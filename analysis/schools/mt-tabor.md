## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Boundary expands to take Laurelhurst 6-8; Laurelhurst students join Mt. Tabor Japanese DLI cohort on the path to Grant HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/mt_tabor.pdf p.3]:
> Mt. Tabor Middle School would expand its school boundary to welcome 6th-8th grade students from Laurelhurst as Laurelhurst becomes a K-5 school.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/mt_tabor.pdf p.3]:
> Home for Laurelhurst middle grades: Changing Laurelhurst to a K-5 model requires a middle school home for 6th-8th grade students. Space and direct pathway: Mt. Tabor provides nearby building space and connects elementary students with a direct middle and high school path into Grant High School.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/mt_tabor.pdf p.3]:
> Rebuilding student body size: Helps restore Mt. Tabor’s enrollment after past Southeast Guiding Coalition changes.
