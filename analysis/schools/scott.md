## What the scenarios do
**Scenario A:** Stays open as neighborhood K-5; Spanish DLI moves to Rigler; boundary expands to take Rose City Park neighborhood (non-DLI) students.

**Scenario B:** Stays open as neighborhood K-5; Spanish DLI moves to Rigler; enrollment falls below size goal (301 in 2031-32).

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/scott.pdf p.3]:
> In Scenarios A and B, Scott would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Its Spanish DLI program would move to Rigler K-5. In Scenario A, Scott would expand its boundary to welcome neighborhood (non-DLI) students from the closing Rose City Park K-5 school. In Scenario B, Rose City Park would remain open.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/scott.pdf p.3]:
> DLI program serving the area: Moving Spanish DLI to Rigler establishes Rigler as a focused regional Spanish language center. Neighborhood elementary home: Scott expands its footprint to serve as the neighborhood elementary home for incoming Rose City Park students in Scenario A.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/scott.pdf p.3]:
> Space balance: Moving Spanish DLI out created the exact room needed to welcome Rose City Park families.
