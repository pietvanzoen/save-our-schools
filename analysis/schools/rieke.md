## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open; takes 30% of Maplewood and a portion of the Hayhurst boundary; continues to Robert Gray MS and Ida B. Wells HS.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rieke.pdf p.3]:
> Rieke would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. Rieke would expand its school boundary to receive 30% of the students from the closing Maplewood K-5. A portion of the Hayhurst boundary would be adjusted to Rieke. Middle and high school pathway: Students would continue to Robert Gray Middle School and Ida B. Wells High School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rieke.pdf p.3]:
> Filling open classroom space: Both Rieke and Maplewood were projected below steady enrollment levels. Reaching steady enrollment: Receiving 30% of the closing Maplewood school boundary makes better use of school space and helps keep student numbers steady at Rieke. Connected boundary lines: Rieke receives a portion of the Hayhurst boundary to create a logical, connected boundary between Rieke and Hayhurst.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/rieke.pdf p.3]:
> Proximity to Wells HS: Rieke’s large school footprint and close proximity to Ida B. Wells High School make it a strong neighborhood school.
