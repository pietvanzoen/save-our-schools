## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Stays open as K-5 neighborhood school; receives Mandarin DLI from Dr. Martin Luther King Jr..

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/boise_eliot_humboldt.pdf p.3]:
> Boise-Eliot/Humboldt (BEH) would remain open as a neighborhood K-5 school. BEH would receive the Mandarin Dual Language Immersion (DLI) program from Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Elementary.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/boise_eliot_humboldt.pdf p.3]:
> Using open classroom space: Boise- Eliot/Humbodlt (BEH) has room for up to 632 students, but enrollment was expected to drop without adding programs. School building setup: BEH has the physical space and special-focus classroom setup ready to host an expanded language program.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/boise_eliot_humboldt.pdf p.3]:
> Frees up space at MLK Jr.: Moving Mandarin DLI to BEH creates the needed physical space at Dr. MLK Jr. Elementary to welcome neighborhood students from Sabin.
