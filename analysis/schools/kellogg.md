## What the scenarios do
**Scenarios A and B (same):** Boundary expands to take Atkinson and Creston neighborhood students; all SE Spanish immersion stays at Kellogg; Lent neighborhood students shift to Harrison Park.

PPS's wording, "What is being proposed?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/kellogg.pdf p.3]:
> Kellogg Middle School would expand its boundary to welcome neighborhood students from Atkinson and Creston. All Spanish Immersion in the southeast remains at Kellogg. Lent neighborhood students would shift from Kellogg to Harrison Park Middle School.

"Why is PPS considering this?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/kellogg.pdf p.3]:
> Connecting elementary and middle schools: Ensures Atkinson and Creston students move together into Kellogg and to Franklin High School.

"What else was considered?" [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/school_impact/kellogg.pdf p.3]:
> Maximizing modern space: Uses available building space at newly built Kellogg efficiently while building a strong student body.
