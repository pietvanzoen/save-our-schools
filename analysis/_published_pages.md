## Conventions for pages
- Every number and quote comes from the archive. Quotes are verbatim, caption repairs are in [brackets], and each links to its video timestamp.
- Members of the public are described, not named. Board members and staff are named.
- Interpretation is marked "Analysis".
- When a page depends on facts that change (e.g. the Nov 17 recommendation), republish it and update the date here.
