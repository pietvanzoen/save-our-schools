## 2. Board of Education: January to June 2025

**Attendance on Jan 7, 2025:**
- Directors: Julia Brim-Edwards, Michelle DePass, Herman Greene, Gary Hollands, Andrew Scott, Patte Sullivan, Edward Wang
- Student Representative: JJ Kunsevi

Source: [BB:3996217 p.2]

**Leadership elected Jan 7, 2025, both 7–0, for the period beginning Jan 8, 2025:**
- **Eddie Wang, Chair.** Moved by Brim-Edwards, seconded by Sullivan.
- **Michelle DePass, Vice-Chair.** Moved by Brim-Edwards, seconded by Hollands.

Source: [BB:3996217 p.2] (Resolutions 7025 and 7026). The minutes also record Chair Wang reporting "the upcoming Board vacancy" with three candidates [BB:3996217 p.2].

**Zone 1 vacancy, Jan 13, 2025:**
- Three candidates were interviewed: Jackie Wirz, Christy Splitt and Jazzmin Reece.
- The first round of voting was a 2-2-2 tie. In the second round Splitt received 3 votes, Reece 2 and Wirz 1, and the field was narrowed to Splitt and Reece.
- "The Board appointed Christy Splitt to fill the Zone 1 vacancy through June 30, 2025." [BB:3996229 p.3]
- Six directors voted: Brim-Edwards, DePass, Greene, Hollands, Sullivan and Wang. Scott did not appear [BB:3996229 p.3].
- Willamette Week reports that Splitt "was appointed to Andrew Scott's seat in January after he moved out of his zone and resigned" (https://www.wweek.com/news/schools/2025/05/20/splitt-chase-miller-la-forte-and-engelsman-lead-portland-school-board-races/).

**Zones of the 2024-25 board members who left in June 2025.** Willamette Week's report on the May 20, 2025 election (same URL):
- Zone 1: Splitt (appointed incumbent) led.
- Zone 4 (North Portland): Chase-Miller led incumbent **Herman Greene**, "the only current board member seeking reelection this cycle".
- Zone 5 (Northeast): La Forte led after incumbent **Gary Hollands** suspended his campaign and endorsed her.
- Zone 6 (Southeast): Engelsman led.

**Analysis:** by elimination, **Julia Brim-Edwards** held Zone 6 and did not run in 2025 (medium confidence; the article does not name her). Zones 2, 3 and 7 (DePass, Sullivan, Wang) were not on the 2025 ballot, which matches their 7/2023–6/2027 terms.
