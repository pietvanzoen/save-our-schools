## How people's positions developed in this window

**Rashelle Chase-Miller (TLE chair, Zone 4)**
- Her son will attend Jefferson, and she has a current Tubman 7th grader [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@1:10:14] [YT:JEfmEEMeU3Y@2:05:35].
- She announced the Jefferson engagement series and the planned January 2026 vote (10/9).
- At Policy (10/20) she said the board "will be called on to make a lot of decisions this year that aren't going to be popular" and should not decide on optics (medium).
- Reporting on the CGCS conference (11/4), she framed "school consolidations and enrollment challenges" as common urban problems.

**Christy Splitt (Zone 1, BAC liaison)**
- Draws on her legislative background: she lobbies on unfunded mandates (10/14, self-identified) and urged a board position for the 2026 short session (11/4, confirmed by minutes). She expects state cuts ("buckle up for small wins," 10/28, medium).

**Virginia La Forte (Zone 5, FIO chair)**

- She clashed with DePass and board leadership over the CBSE agenda notice (11/10), blaming leadership for pulling the item.
- On Jefferson she said families "across the white families... and the black families" share concerns about academic programming (11/13). She also challenged the instructional-time math for seniors (10/14).

**Michelle DePass (Vice-Chair; Policy chair)**

**Eddie Wang (Chair)**

- Supports dual-language immersion; his own child is in DLI (10/14).
- Insisted that farmers-market cost impacts on bond projects come to the board (10/8, medium).

**Stephanie Engelsman (Zone 6)**
