## Person-by-person observations

- **Eddie Wang (Chair)**
  - Consistently defends staff-led technical prioritization and warns that the "loudest voices" and organized communities distort equity:
    - Jan 27, on seismic selection: "turf wars".
    - Feb 24, on MLC: "zero sum game"; warns against bending policy when communities "come out in full force", citing outer Southeast and SEGC history.
  - Asked for a 10–15-year list of schools hit by program, enrollment and boundary changes.
  - Cuts framing: $30M, $48M and $50M across his three years.
  - Process-minded on serial-meeting law (Mar 8).
  - Analysis: the most consistent pro-staff, equity-of-voice voice. Likely to support a systemwide rightsizing process and resist one-off reversals.
- **Michelle DePass (Vice-Chair)**
  - Jan 27: strongly against seismic commitments ahead of consolidation ("rightsizing conversation should happen first"). Wants an equity lens and a full-board session. Cited the Buckman cafeteria beams.
  - Feb 24: an MLC alumna, yet accepted the cut as a trade-off ("we cut fat. We're now into muscle and bone"). Asked for historical data on closures and busing harms to communities of color.
  - Mar 8 (medium attribution): the board "changed our minds" under vocal parents; wants to "stand strong" and share burdens beyond "wealthier communities".
  - Analysis: favors systemwide, equity-centered consolidation over piecemeal decisions. Willing to close programs with personal ties.

  - Pragmatic and supportive of moving forward: seismic "we could keep talking about things forever"; MLC "sad" but must weigh all schools.
  - Repeatedly presses the legislature to release the Education Stability Fund.
