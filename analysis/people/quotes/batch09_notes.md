## Evolving positions on rightsizing, Aug to Oct 2026

### Stephanie Engelsman (Zone 6, SE): most consistently skeptical; hardened into opposition to the scenarios as drafted
- **8/4:**
  - Asked how many families PPS lost from the SEGC closures and boundary changes [YT:-qhL23TOZTs@1:10:49].
  - Championed the Creston/Hosford sports exception, which failed 1–4–1.
- **9/1:**
  - Wanted savings and layoffs per closure before endorsing any magnitude, and preferred to start with boundary changes [YT:5RMfvT-sAPU@1:45:09] [YT:5RMfvT-sAPU@1:57:06].
  - Re-introduced her cost-savings (pay and hiring freeze) resolution.
  - Voted against the superintendent contract (by elimination).
- **9/15:**
  - Said she had "just learned" that even about 20 closures will not make every school well-resourced [YT:uAf7l-ChjFo@3:38:02].
  - Said consolidation without smaller classes is unacceptable.
  - Her span-of-control resolution produced a staff commitment to report by early December.
- **9/23 and 9/29:**
  - Asked for an inventory of closed buildings.
  - Backed an internal bond auditor: "2 million out of four billion".
- **10/6:**
  - "to me this plan is mayhem."
  - Objections: school deserts, bus times and costs, SEGC schools hit twice, receiving schools far above 360, Sellwood→Hosford/Brentwood middle schools above 700.
  - Expects the board to amend the recommendation [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@2:56:43] [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@3:12:17].
- **Analysis:** she is a near-certain "no" on Scenario A as drafted. Her asks are concrete enough (transportation cost, Sellwood, Clark, Sunnyside middle grades) that amendments could move her, but she has never endorsed a magnitude.

- **9/15:**
  - Still uncomfortable with the magnitude but better understands why others accept it [YT:uAf7l-ChjFo@4:21:06].

- **9/29:** led the internal-bond-auditor side (the 4–3 majority).
- **10/6:**
  - "not ... the right scale for the moment" (24–27% of students move).
  - "Maybe our definition of community may have to change."
  - Will not vote for a package that adds schools not already on the list (caption reads "not on state"; repaired to [slate]).
  - **Said she is "slowly navigat[ing]" her way off the school board** [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@1:13:00]. Analysis: her term runs to 6/2027; this signals she will not seek re-election, and possibly an earlier exit. Verify.
- **Analysis:** she describes herself as a "common ground person". She is a likely vote for a reduced package (Scenario B or smaller) and against A as drafted.

### Virginia La Forte (Zone 5, NE): open to large change, but conditions it on cost data and neighborhood hubs

- **9/15:** asked why consolidated schools would not be well-resourced from day one.
- **9/29:** moved to strike "depend on budget availability"; wants "somebody in the building" auditing the bond.
