## Positions by person (within this batch)

- **Virginia La Forte (TLE chair).** She was the most skeptical questioner on DLI consolidation so far.
  - She asked whether the plan "started with converting DI programs to the whole school model" and whether alternatives were evaluated.
  - She demanded Lent before/after and comparative data, and wants to separate configuration effects from leadership effects.
  - She said James John "gave me significant pause" and asked for a James John vs Sitton building comparison.
  - She was surprised Rose City Park was back "on the table".
  - She warned against choosing sites for real-estate appeal (James John's commercial zoning).
  - She relayed families' charge of "[de facto] segregation" and ICE fears.
  - On middle grades she was supportive. She cited her own childhood K-8 → K-5 conversion and HS performing-arts teachers' views. She also said 500/360 are not "large".
  - Compared with 2026-09-08 (wanting "a map where every neighborhood is left with a school"), she is now challenging specific closures (James John, Rose City Park) while supporting 6-8 middle schools.
- **Christy Splitt.**
  - On DLI she questioned whether retention assumptions are realistic when hubs are far from current schools (Atkinson → Lent, Beach → Chávez), and she defended the 1/3-1/3-1/3 lottery balance (worried about Ainsworth).
  - On middle grades she was broadly supportive, citing her own sixth grader's experience. She offered constructive alternatives:
    - keep an arts/environment focus at a neighborhood Sunnyside;
    - phase middle-school changes;
    - give students with IEPs lottery preference at remaining K-8s.
  - She said "I don't love lottery schools" but is fine with focus schools.
  - She pointed out that the Southwest rebalancing (Maplewood → Gray, Jackson shrinking) leaves the West Hills (West Sylvan) out.
  - At IGA she floated a board position supporting new state revenue.
  - Consistent with 2026-09-08: supportive of rightsizing in general, skeptical of modeling assumptions.
- **Rashelle Chase-Miller (medium attribution).** She praised Rigler/Lent and said Chávez "needs the love". She asked what families leaving their schools gain. She left early to pick up her child.
- **Stephanie Engelsman.**
  - TLE (online): she wants concrete staffing (3 vs 4 classes per grade at Chávez) and route plans and costs with the Nov 17 recommendation. Staff said yes to both.
  - IGA: unfunded mandates should be costed (PE mandate, "80 teachers", which she said equals last year's layoffs). She wants a "sample proper budget" and invited community volunteers who are already analyzing rightsizing data.
  - Through Splitt she raised Sellwood MS timing (confirmed for the following fall, i.e. 2028).
- **Patte Sullivan.**
  - TLE: she asked how Robert Gray grows without moving West Sylvan students. Gray staff told board members they lack electives.
  - IGA: against unfunded instructional-hours mandates; wants Measures 5/50 addressed; "we just have to get more money. Period."
- **Staff.**
  - Calvert: DLI program count in North Portland "became a driver" of the design. James John vs Sitton was decided on single-story accessibility; Rose City Park on proximity, draw patterns and "significant facility concerns". She conceded that when programs move "not everybody ... decided to stay with the program".
  - Glasgow: framed moves as moving "children and families"; ELD staffing dilution in co-located schools.
  - Flores: lottery design; ICE training.
  - Hristić: K-8 subsidy of 21 FTE (~$3.4M); no OSAS difference by configuration.
  - Kefir (Lent principal): attendance 64% → 82%; ELPA proficiency 8% → 20%.
  - Coonrod: North Portland DLI routes are modeled.
  - Howard: Nov 17 recommendation.
