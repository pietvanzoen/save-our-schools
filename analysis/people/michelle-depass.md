## How her thinking changed over time

**2025: Jefferson advocate on capacity; consolidation anticipated.** In February 2025 she resisted downsizing high schools on Jefferson's behalf: "I don't think that we should base it on current enrollment because if we do then again Jefferson suffers because Jefferson has a really low enrollment" [YT:zxaXALjzumk@2:12:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zxaXALjzumk&t=7938s) By September she was already saying the district would not keep every building: "ideally we would it'd be great to have every single building in the portfolio, but we're not going to be inhabiting every single building, right?" [YT:HGAs0lyZ6kw@4:12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HGAs0lyZ6kw&t=15153s)

**Jan–Mar 2026: sequencing fight.** She opposed seismic commitments ahead of consolidation: "we have a couple of school communities who have basically been told that they're going to get seismic improvements meaning that they won't close." [YT:-NpJZaLR81U@1:35:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-NpJZaLR81U&t=5742s) and, after the MLC reversal, urged the board to "stand strong" against vocal parents: "we have just made a decision where we heard from some vocal parents, we changed our minds and so I think it'll be really important to stand strong." [YT:iY4k1Q8Y_yI@4:44:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iY4k1Q8Y_yI&t=17062s) *(medium-confidence attribution)*

**Apr–May 2026: equity-centered support; process deference.** She voted against the guardrail amendment, partly because "I don't like when I hear people say I think we all agree" [YT:AH3wdS1A3sM@1:59:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AH3wdS1A3sM&t=7141s), and described the goal as "how we can come together as a community to imagine a reconfigured right-sized Portland Public Schools where learning outcomes are not predictable by race or zip code" [YT:jBUIkfQZu-k@0:03:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jBUIkfQZu-k&t=189s)

**Sept–Oct 2026: escalation to "go big".** "I would say magnitude on a scale of 1 to 10, I would say 9.5." [YT:5RMfvT-sAPU@2:29:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5RMfvT-sAPU&t=8944s) By Oct 6 (medium attribution): "my north star is unapologetically those numbers of kids who are experiencing poverty." [YT:zOUH2Jq7Owo@4:10:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOUH2Jq7Owo&t=15032s) *(medium-confidence attribution)*

Analysis: DePass has moved from a Jefferson-capacity defender (2025) to the board's most expansive supporter of consolidation scale (2026). The constant is an equity and who-gets-heard frame. One smaller reversal: on policy reporting she called codifying a race-disaggregated reporting clause "overkill" on 4/27/26 [YT:5PEBDQJblME@0:32:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PEBDQJblME&t=1922s) but said "it's nice to have it codified" on 5/18/26 [YT:m5wWs7Qx8Ec@0:21:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m5wWs7Qx8Ec&t=1277s).
