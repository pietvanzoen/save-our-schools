## What changed

**FAQ rewritten.** The Oct 1 FAQ [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/faq.html] had 31 questions; the current one has 23
[PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/faq_2026-10-18.html].
- Removed: "Could closing schools push even more families to leave the school district?", whose answer said "more than
  93% of families stayed with PPS" after past changes [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/faq.html]. Also removed: whether some
  regions will be hit harder, how class sizes and staffing will change at remaining schools, and what happens to empty buildings.
- Added: an operational definition of a "well-resourced school"; "Could School Improvement Bond funds be redirected to cover
  operational expenses and prevent school closures…?" (answer: "No", bond money is legally limited to the ballot
  language); and how updates are distributed [PPS:pps_net/rightsizing/faq_2026-10-18.html].
- Analysis: the answer under "How will teacher transfers, job changes, or layoffs be handled?" talks about reusing closed
  buildings, so it looks misplaced; the staffing question has no real answer on the page as of Oct 18.

**2027 seismic projects (Res. 7390, Oct 20 consent agenda).** Staff ask to use an RFP for five 2027 seismic packages:
a full upgrade at Beverly Cleary–Fernwood (ROM $18.7M), partial upgrades plus partial roof replacements at Richmond
($6.5M) and Capitol Hill ($12.4M), and partial upgrades at Ainsworth ($2.3M, with a $1.5M state Seismic Rehabilitation
Grant) and Kelly (written "$1.6") [BB:14549641 p.2]. Analysis: none of the five closes under Scenario A or B; Capitol Hill
and Kelly receive students in Scenario A, and Beverly Cleary converts from K-8 to K-5 in both (`data/school_impact.csv`).
